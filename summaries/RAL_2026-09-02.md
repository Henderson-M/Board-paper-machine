## Top lines

[LEAD] The Royal Free group has delivered only £14.2m of a £37.2m year-to-date recurrent savings target — a £23m shortfall against a £151m annual improvement plan.
[Evidence] "Recurrent FIP underperformance remains the principal risk to the year-end position and three-year plan: although the trust has delivered savings in line with plan to date, **only £14.2m of the recurrent £37.2m year-to-date target has been delivered, a shortfall of c£23m** across all Health Units and corporate areas" — [Integrated Performance Report, Finance Summary]
[Evidence] "The key challenge remains delivery of the **£151.0m Financial Improvement Plan (FIP)**... leaving a £23m shortfall that requires rapid delivery and implementation of the planned savings schemes." — [Chief Finance Officer's report / IPR]

[LEAD] The on-plan £15.6m deficit depends on non-recurrent mitigations, and cash headroom is narrowing to a forecast £6.9m by year end.
[Evidence] "At the end of July 2026 (month 4) the Group reported a year-to-date deficit of £15.6m, against the Revenue Financial Plan Limit (RFPL) and excluding deficit support funding. This is an on-plan position (nil variance to the year-to-date RFPL plan). **The position is however dependent on the deployment of non-recurrent mitigations.**" — [Integrated Performance Report, Finance Summary]
[Evidence] "The Trust closed Month 4 with a cash balance of £68.7m, £18.3m above plan, driven by prior-year cash receipts, payment timing differences, and lower supplier payments, partly offset by the £23.0m non-delivery of recurrent FIP. Operating expenditure days remain stable at around 11 days. The current forecast is for a **£6.9m year-end cash balance in 2026/27 with no cash support required; however, cash headroom is narrowing**, and any deterioration in financial performance or further non-delivery of FIP could increase [the risk]" — [same]

[LEAD] The trust dropped to segment 4 of the NHS oversight framework on patient safety in Q4 after breaching thresholds on three separate bloodstream infections.
[Evidence] "the report recorded thresholds exceeded for MRSA bacteraemia, C. difficile and E. coli bloodstream infections, **resulting in the Trust moving to Segment 4 of the NHS Oversight Framework patient safety domain in Quarter 4**." — [Infection Prevention and Control report]
[Evidence] "Due to being over our Trust thresholds we decreased to segment 4 of the safety domain within the national oversight framework in Q4 of 2025/2026. We are now taking the opportunity to review and refresh our IPC plans across the Trust." — [same]
[Evidence] The NOF domain table shows the deterioration across the year: Patient Safety 3/3/3/**4**; Effectiveness and Experience of Care 2/2/3/**4**; Access to Services 4/2/2/2; People and Workforce 1/1/1/1; Finance and Productivity 4/4/3/2 — [Integrated Performance Report, Performance Summary — NOF Q4]

[LEAD] Three never events so far this financial year — wrong-site procedures and retained objects.
[Evidence] "**Three never events had occurred during the financial year to date, relating to wrong site procedures and retained objects.** While harm had been limited, these incidents prompted additional focus on audit compliance and the introduction of targeted human factors training in high-risk clinical environments." — [Minutes / Quality report]
[Evidence] "The board discussed the occurrence of never events. Julie Hamilton explained that investigations indicated compliance with safety checklists was generally strong; however, incidents were mul[ti-factorial]" — [Minutes]

[LEAD] The Royal Free says its NHS oversight segment is being held down purely by its finances.
[Evidence] "**The RFL remains in segment 3 overall due to our financial position. However, without this financial override we would position in segment 2** thanks to the improvements we have made in other areas" — [Integrated Performance Report, NOF summary]
[Evidence] "Finance and Productivity has improved, moving from a score of 3 to 2, with productivity placing us 20th among acute trusts." — [same]

[WORTH WATCHING] Bank and agency spend is £6.5m over the year-to-date cap and the trust's workforce metrics sit in the bottom national quartile.
[Evidence] "Workforce costs also remain under pressure, with combined bank and agency spend **£6.5m above the year-to-date cap**" — [Chief Finance Officer's report]
[Evidence] "Bank spend is £6.1m adverse to plan year to date (month 3: £4.6m) and agency spend £0.4m adverse year to date (month 3: £0.8m). While a proportion of this relates to the unplanned industrial action costs" — [same]
[Evidence] "workforce metrics remain in the lowest national quartile and continue to present a significant risk to financial delivery." — [Integrated Performance Report]

[WORTH WATCHING] The group has settled a £1.4bn contract with West and North London ICB, leaving only a £0.7m gap across all associate commissioners.
[Evidence] "[the trust] has secured agreement on its largest commissioner contract with West and North London ICB (**£1.4bn**) and all but two associate ICB contracts, leaving an outstanding gap of just £0.7m, with negotiations progressing positively. Urgent and Emergency Care (UEC) activity remains 7% above plan, generating £0.8m additional i[ncome]" — [Chief Finance Officer's report]

[WORTH WATCHING] Two board-level departures announced, plus the responsible officer's retirement.
[Evidence] "the Chair welcomed everyone to the meeting, and noted that **Barry Ray, Group Company Secretary and Vivek Ahuja, Non-Executive Director would soon be leaving the trust for new roles.** He thanked them both for their valued contributions" — [Minutes, item 1.1]
[Evidence] "The current Responsible Officer retired on 31.7.26 and a replacement was appointed." — [Medical revalidation annual report]

[WORTH WATCHING] A post-infection review at North Middlesex concluded an MRSA bloodstream infection was avoidable.
[Evidence] "At North Middlesex University Hospital (NMUH), a post-infection review (PIR) conducted by a multi-disciplinary team concluded that **the infection was avoidable, identifying gaps in line care practices and adherence to aseptic non-touch technique (ANTT)**. In response, immediate staff re-education and competency asses[sment followed]" — [IPC report, Appendix 5: MRSA blood stream infections]

[WORTH WATCHING] Maternity is holding a stable safety position while responding to Ockenden and Amos, but with named gaps.

[Evidence] On the national picture, the pack notes: "NHS England requires full implementation of the Maternal Care Bundle (MCB) by March 2027, covering five clinical areas: VTE, pre-hospital and acute care, epilepsy, maternal mental health, and obstetric haemorrhage." — [Maternity report]

## FOI leads

[FOI] **The three never events.** *Request from Royal Free London:* the never event categories and dates, the patient safety incident investigation reports or after-action reviews for each, the sites involved, and any correspondence with NHS England or the CQC. *Why it matters:* the pack confirms wrong-site procedures and retained objects but names no site, date or outcome.

[FOI] **The £151m Financial Improvement Plan, scheme by scheme.** *Request from Royal Free London:* the FIP scheme register showing the recurrent and non-recurrent value of each scheme, its RAG rating and its owning health unit, plus the list of non-recurrent mitigations deployed to month 4 to hold the on-plan position.

[FOI] **The segment 4 patient safety designation.** *Request from Royal Free London and NHS England (London):* the NHS Oversight Framework Q4 2025/26 scoring for the trust, NHSE's segmentation correspondence, and the refreshed IPC plans referred to in the report.

[FOI] **The avoidable MRSA bacteraemia at North Middlesex.** *Request from Royal Free London:* the full post-infection review, the ANTT and line-care audit results before and after, and the number of PIRs in 2025/26 and 2026/27 to date that concluded an infection was avoidable.

[FOI] **The West and North London ICB contract.** *Request from Royal Free London and NHS West and North London ICB:* the signed 2026/27 contract and its activity and income schedules, plus the two outstanding associate ICB contracts and the nature of the £0.7m gap.
