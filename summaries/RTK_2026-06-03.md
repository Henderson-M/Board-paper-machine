## Top lines

[LEAD] CMO's corridor care paper says the trust has not recovered from winter, has been in OPEL 4 for weeks with repeated business continuity, and is holding ED patients for over 48 hours — with NHSE "significant concern" about its distance from plan.
[Evidence] "Ashford and St Peter's have not recovered operationally in 2026 from the peak of winter pressures and have remained largely in OPEL 4 for several weeks now with repeated periods of Business Continuity. The impact of this is an overcrowded ED, with patients sometimes held there for over 48 hours" … "NHSE have expressed significant concern about our distance from plan currently." — p.2–3, [Improving Flow Plan and Corridor Care Overview]

[LEAD] Long-stay position is the trust's worst in over four years: 103 patients with a stay of >21 days on one day in May, the longest at 283 days.
[Evidence] "On Monday 11th May 2026 there were 103 adult inpatients with a length of stay > 21days, the longest of these being at 283 days (discharged later that day). … At its best the hospital has cycled in the range of 30-40 patients with LOS >21 days. This is the worst complex discharge position for at least 4 years." — p.6, [Improving Flow Plan and Corridor Care Overview]

[LEAD] Trust is rated amber/red for provider capability under the NHS Oversight Framework and remains in Tier 3 for performance.
[Evidence] "the Trust had been assigned a Provider capability rating of amber/red under the NHS Oversight Framework … The Trust remained in Tier 3 for performance, although there had been some improvement since the previous reporting period." — p.3, [Minutes of 25 March 2026 board, O-30/2026]

[LEAD] 2026/27 breakeven plan rests on a £36.7m CIP (6.8% of opex) of which £8.5m is still unidentified and £6m unscoped; NHSE now requires weekly CIP reporting.
[Evidence] "The CIP target of £36.7M (6.8%) for 2026/27 is a significant challenge … there is still significant risk in delivery with £8.5M yet to be identified and further work required to agree the detail of opportunities estimated at £6M." … "NHS England now require weekly reporting on our CIP progress. The committee were assured that this is not an unusual request." — p.3–4, [Finance and Performance Committee AAA]

[LEAD] 2025/26 breakeven was reached only with £3.3m late NHSE deficit support and £13.5m of one-off savings — flagged as a risk to the larger 2026/27 target.
[Evidence] "The Trust achieved its break even financial target … and as a result secured an additional £3.3M in NHS deficit support funding to deliver a year end surplus. £32.9M of CIPs were delivered in year, £13.5 of which were non-recurring which should be noted in respect of next years challenging target of £37.6M." — p.7, [Finance and Performance Committee AAA]

[WORTH WATCHING] Twilight medical cover leaves a single SHO covering 330 medical inpatients overnight — cited as a patient-safety and length-of-stay driver requiring investment.
[Evidence] "at present our ward based residents finish at 5pm on weekdays with 1 SHO and 1 Foundation doctor then covering 330 medical inpatients, including CAU. … This leads to many unresolved issues going into the night shift where there is currently only 1 SHO covering the same number of beds. This is impacting on patient safety, progression of patient pathways and hence increase in length of stay" — p.8, [Improving Flow Plan and Corridor Care Overview]

[WORTH WATCHING] Patient safety incident investigation backlog and a deterioration in Duty of Candour compliance reported at the previous board, alongside seven fatal harm cases.
[Evidence] "The CMO reported a backlog in the investigation of patient safety incidents and noted a deterioration in compliance with Duty of Candour requirements." … "Jane Dickson requested that the seven fatal harm incidents be prioritised, with particular attention given to communication with affected families." — p.4–5, [Minutes of 25 March 2026 board, O-31/2026]

[WORTH WATCHING] BAF people risk escalated from 12 to 16 and staff survey response rate fell from 51% to 42% (below the 47% national average), despite an improved overall ranking.
[Evidence] "the risk score for our strategic people risk on our Board Assurance Framework 'We do not create a culture where staff feel listened to, included, empowered and valued' was increased from 12 to 16" … "The Trust saw a decline in the response rate (42%) compared to the previous year (51%) and was below the national average (47%)." — p.3, [National Staff Survey 2025 Results]

[WORTH WATCHING] Group-wide governance overhaul nearly complete: 100 of 112 actions done across an External Governance Review (Audit One) and an NHSE Financial Governance Review, with the programme moving to closure.
[Evidence] "Of the total 112 actions identified across the reviews: 100 actions are complete; 12 actions remain outstanding." … "the formal governance review action plan transitions toward closure during the next reporting period." — p.2, p.4, [External Governance Review progress update]

[FOI] Request the 2025 External Governance Review (Audit One) and the NHSE Financial Governance Review reports in full.

Request both final reports, terms of reference and the full action plan from ASPH — they sit behind a 112-action remediation programme and an amber/red capability rating. — p.1–2, [External Governance Review progress update]
