## Top lines

[LEAD] Trust describes its out-of-area placement position as "unacceptable" and warns it could worsen, with BNSSG carrying nine OAPs against a zero target.
[Evidence] "The unacceptable out-of-area position has reached concerning levels and has the potential to worsen. This is driving additional costs and negatively impacting patient experience, particularly with service users being placed far from home." — p.144, [Month 2 Integrated Quality Performance Report]; "Out of Area Placements (OAP) – open at month end – Target 0/Actual 9" — p.138, [same]; "OOA numbers increased significantly in month, moving the in month position from partial to non-delivery" — p.189, [Finance Report Month 2]

[LEAD] Board raised its financial risk appetite to "Significant", citing below-average ICB funding for its mental health services and warning savings cannot be delivered without hitting service standards.
[Evidence] "With mental health services in both BNSSG and BSW receiving less of the overall ICB allocation compared to the national average, the Trust's ability to deliver the 2% efficiency ask and eliminate the underlying deficit will become very difficult without impacting service standards, hence the change in the risk appetite." — p.35, [Board Assurance Framework report]

[LEAD] A coroner has issued a Regulation 28 prevention-of-future-deaths report after an inquest jury found neglect in the death of a patient at HMP Eastwood Park.
[Evidence] "The Committee was informed of a Regulation 28 relating to a death in prison of a patient admitted to HMP Eastwood Park in June 2022 with autism and ADHD. The jury recorded death by suspension of ligature alongside neglect (not by AWP), with recommendations for the Trust to review processes for patients in prisons with mental health issues." — p.440, [Quality and Safety Committee Annual Report 2025-26]

[LEAD] Actions from a Mental Health Homicide Review and a linked Child Safeguarding Practice Review have slipped, with the board attributing delays to bed-capacity constraints.
[Evidence] "Further discussion had taken place regarding the recent Mental Health Homicide Review and Child Safeguarding Practice Review relating to a mental health homicide. While it had been noted that good progress had been made overall, some actions had slipped. The Committee had recognised that bed capacity constraints had contributed to these delays." — p.11, [Trust Board Part 1 minutes, 13 May 2026]. Separately an "Independent Homicide Review taking place in 25/26" is referenced — p.52, [BAF Appendix 1]

[LEAD] Three named seclusion facilities are recorded as not meeting CQC regulatory standards, with remedial capital works pushed into 2026/27.
[Evidence] "Seclusion suite environments at Bluebell Health Based Place of Safety, Ashdown PICU and Fairfax Low Secure do not meet CQC regulatory standards." Prioritised capital "to bring Bluebell and Ashdown Seclusion suites up to standard in 2026/27" — p.43, [BAF Appendix 1]

[WORTH WATCHING] A new corporate risk on recording of Mental Capacity Act assessments and best-interest decisions was added at a score of 16, and an MCA audit found recording of decisions was "an issue".
[Evidence] "Risk 3807 Recording mental capacity assessments/best interest decisions. Score 16" (added 11/05/26) — p.99–100, [Risk Report]; MCA audit "showed that MCA principles were generally understood by staff but the recording of the processes and decisions was an issue" — p.440, [Quality and Safety Committee Annual Report]; MHL Committee asks "Whether risks relating to patients being cared for outside an appropriate legal framework (including DoLS authorisation delays…) should be formally recorded on the Trust Risk Register" — p.110, [Mental Health Legislation Committee Chair's Report]

[WORTH WATCHING] The environmental ligature-reduction risk was escalated after an incident, rising from a score of 10 to 15.
[Evidence] "Risk 3680 Residual environmental ligature reduction work. Score 15, increased from 10." — p.98, [Risk Report]; "the Committee noted the escalation of the Trust Wide glazing risk following an incident, and that work had been initiated to apply safety film and remediate affected inpatient glazing" — p.440, [Quality and Safety Committee Annual Report]

[WORTH WATCHING] Deputy CEO appointment follows an executive departure and a "retire and return".
