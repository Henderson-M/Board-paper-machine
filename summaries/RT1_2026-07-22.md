## Top lines

[LEAD] Trust's children's mental health access is going backwards against a +10% target and it says it is shut out of the national funding that let rivals improve, because it does not run school mental health teams.
[Evidence] "Annual change in the number of children accessing NHS-funded mental health services has improved by 3% but remains negative at -1.0% against a +10% target" — p.119, [Integrated Performance and Quality Report, Item 6.2, Access domain]
[Evidence] "It was noted that other Trusts which achieved accelerated improvement, had done so primarily via the roll-out of school mental health teams. The Committee were advised that due to the Trust not providing these services, that the organisation was not able to access this funding." — p.107, [Business and Performance Committee Assurance Report, Item 6.1]

[LEAD] The trust's own committee gave "limited assurance" over confidence in hitting its efficiency target, and bank staffing spend is rising rather than falling against a plan that needs it cut 11%.
[Evidence] "Assign a limited assurance rating, given the level of confidence in achieving the efficiency target highlighted in the report" — p.107, [Business and Performance Committee Assurance Report, Item 6.1]
[Evidence] "we're required to reduce Bank spend by 11% & Agency spend by 45%-50%...
