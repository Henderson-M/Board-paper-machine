[LEAD] Trust's own fact-finding review reveals its new DaVita-built Peterborough dialysis unit was hit with a fire-service prohibition notice two months after opening, cutting capacity from 35 to 20 stations for six months, after the building's design code was switched without the trust being told.
[Evidence] "The design of the building was changed from HTM 05-02 to Approved Document B without the knowledge of anyone connected to the Trust. This caused huge issues as the Approved Inspector used by Davita (Building owner/landlord) ignored the comments made by CFRS at the consultation stage; this led to CFRS providing the building/Trust with a Restriction notice." — fire report p.27, [H2 — Fire Safety Management Annual Report]

"Some patients were required to travel to Boston or start dialysis as inpatients before transferring while awaiting a Peterborough slot." — fire report p.51, [H2 — Appendix E, Fact finding Review Report into the Peterborough Dialysis Unit]
[Evidence] "the restrictions increased inpatient length of stay at Glenfield Hospital and Peterborough City Hospital while patients awaited dialysis slots. There was also an adverse impact on UHL's reputation" — fire report p.52
