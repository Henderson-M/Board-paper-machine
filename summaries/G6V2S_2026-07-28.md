## Top lines

[LEAD] Named external firm BDO has run a well-led governance review that found the trust's board assurance framework was not working as its primary strategic risk tool, prompting a full overhaul of the BAF and corporate risk register.
[Evidence] "A review of the Trust's approach to risk management has been undertaken following recommendations arising from the BDO well led governance review. BDO recommended that a full review be undertaken of both the Board Assurance Framework (BAF) and Corporate Risk Register (CRR)... The review has concluded that whilst the current BAF provides a mechanism for recording principal risks, it is not consistently operating as the Board's primary strategic risk management tool." — p.61, [Strengthening Risk Assurance and Oversight and BAF Refresh]; board "considered the initial findings from the BDO Well-Led Developmental Review" — p.62.

[LEAD] Freedom to Speak Up concerns rose 46% in 2025/26, driven by sharp jumps in bullying, discrimination and worker-safety cases, alongside a doubling of grievances and near-doubling of employment tribunal cases during the merger/restructure period.
[Evidence] "149 concerns were raised, representing a 46% increase from the previous year" (from 102); bullying and harassment 7→33, discrimination/inequality 4→30, worker safety 14→31, sexual misconduct 0→2 — p.245, p.248–249, [Freedom to Speak Up Annual Report 2025/26]. "Grievance cases have doubled from 16 to 32, and disciplinary cases have risen from 23 to 44. Employment Tribunal cases have also increased from 11 to 21" — p.250; "Employment Tribunal cases involving BAME staff have increased substantially from 2 to 14" — p.251.

[LEAD] A coroner issued a Prevention of Future Deaths (Regulation 28) report on an inpatient death by ligature, criticising the trust for not implementing actions it had already identified.
[Evidence] "PFD issued relating to an inpatient death by ligature, Topaz Ward, September 2024: Concerns were raised about communication, risk management, and therapeutic engagement. The BLPI report had set out actions to address a number of these concerns, however, these had not yet been implemented, raising concerns from the coroner regarding the extent of implementation." — p.159, [Learning from Deaths Annual Report 2025/26].

[WORTH WATCHING] The CQC is mid-way through a fresh round of inspections at the trust, with a well-led review expected in the autumn — its first sustained inspection since early 2025.
[Evidence] "colleagues from the Care Quality Commission (CQC) began a series of Inspection visits across the Trust on 14 July. The CQC Team started by carrying out medicines reviews across our adult wards... We expect these visits to continue for some weeks... the anticipated CQC Well Led Review in the autumn." — p.37, [Chief Executive's Report].

[WORTH WATCHING] The merged trust is carrying a £35.8m savings requirement for 2026/27, profiled to land in the later months, which finance leads flag as high risk; it has imposed a broad vacancy freeze.
[Evidence] "The combined plan includes a CIP requirement of £35.8m... places increasing delivery pressure and risk on the later months" — p.234; "The risk of cash-releasing CIP delivery in the later months remains high for NLFT" — p.223; "Further specific measures to strengthen grip and control on both pay and non-pay, including a broad vacancy freeze, have been introduced" — p.235, [Finance Report Month 2 2026/27]. YTD deficit £3.2m vs £2.8m planned deficit, £0.5m adverse — p.223.

[WORTH WATCHING] The trust is running a three-year "Unlocking the Future" transformation affecting large numbers of clinical and corporate staff, with consultation and restructuring live across inpatient, community, rehab and corporate services.
[Evidence] "many staff currently in our corporate services mid-way through consultation and restructuring processes... Through our three-year 'Unlocking the Future' change programme" — p.34, [Chief Executive's Report]; "consultation activity and workforce redesign programmes affecting a substantial proportion of staff" — p.173, [Integrated Performance Report P2].

[WORTH WATCHING] NHS Talking Therapies expansion is under-delivering because promised ICB investment has not arrived and the extra posts have not been recruited.
[Evidence] "The target agreed for 2026/27 was predicated on both productivity improvement and additional investment from the ICB. The required investment has not yet been received, and recruitment of the additional posts has not commenced, resulting in under trajectory delivery." — p.175, [Integrated Performance Report P2].

[WORTH WATCHING] The trust is being asked to formally adopt the IHRA antisemitism definition and the Government's anti-Muslim hostility definition in response to the Lord Mann review, noting that contrary legal advice commissioned for Health Workers for Palestine has been circulated to NHS bodies.
[Evidence] "Organisations have also been asked to confirm by 31 July 2026 whether they have adopted the IHRA definition of antisemitism and the Government definition of anti-Muslim hostility... additional legal advice commissioned on behalf of Health Workers for Palestine has been circulated to NHS organisations relating to the definitions." — p.41, [Lord Mann Review – Position Paper].
