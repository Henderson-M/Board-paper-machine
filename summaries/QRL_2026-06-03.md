## Top lines

[LEAD] H&IOW system closed 2025/26 with an £81.43m year-end deficit against a planned £0.47m surplus — an £81.90m adverse variance.
[Evidence] "The System is reporting a final year end position of a deficit £81.43m, compared to a planned year-to-date surplus of £0.47m, so an £81.90m adverse variance to plan at financial year end." — p.2 (Financial Overview), [System Report M12]; also p.14 Table 2 showing ICS Total YTD actual (81,429) vs plan 468.

[LEAD] NHS England withheld £19.14m of the system's Deficit Support Funding because of its financial performance; the system received £44.02m of the £63.16m due.
[Evidence] "At Month 12 the System received £44.02m of Deficit Support Funding, with £19.14m withheld by NHS England due to the systems financial performance. This is a component part of the deterioration in the system forecast reported this year." — p.2; "Of the £81.43m deficit reported at year end, £19.14m relates to withheld Deficit Support Funding." — p.15, [System Report M12]

[LEAD] Three named acute trusts lost their Deficit Support Funding — UHS, Portsmouth Hospitals, and Isle of Wight Trust.
[Evidence] "providers had ended the Financial Year as expected and all apart from University Hospital Southampton NHS Foundation Trust, Portsmouth Hospitals University NHS Trust, and Isle of Wight NHS Trust retained their Deficit Support Funding and were working on their audits." — p.3, [Board Assurance Report: Finance Committee, 30 April 2026]

[LEAD] The 2026/27 ICB budget carries a £145.0m cost improvement programme, flagged by the board as a delivery risk.
[Evidence] "The Board noted the budget was not without risk including the scale of the £145.0m Cost Improvement Programme and timing of delivery in the context of the running cost restructure, and ongoing negotiations with out-of-system providers"; "The Board approved the 2026/27 Integrated Care Board Budget and noted the scale of the Cost Improvement Programme requirement and associated delivery risk." — paras 15.2, 15.5, [Minutes 1 April 2026]. Allocation £5.7bn; £19-per-head running cost requirement from April 2026 (para 15.1).

[LEAD] A major-incident fire at University Hospital Southampton (Southampton General) has created carried-forward cost pressures, bed closures and a drag on system elective recovery; an external regional review was undertaken.
[Evidence] "The Board requested clarity on the financial impact following the fire at University Hospital NHS Foundation Trust, particularly as providers would be carrying forward potential cost pressures... Detailed costings would capture the immediate response costs, recovery costs, longer term impact from bed closures, and the effect that would have on elective recovery across the system. The Chair noted that there was indication that additional regional support might be made available." — para 16.13; "a formal review following the fire had been undertaken by Region across the entire system" (para 17.3), [Minutes 1 April 2026]

[LEAD] System workforce ended the year 1,201 WTE worse than plan, and Hampshire Hospitals FT failed to submit its Month 12 workforce return to NHS England.
[Evidence] "Hampshire Hospital Foundation Trust was unable to submit its Month 12 Provider Workforce Return to NHS England and is therefore omitted from this report's Plan and Actual Whole Time Equivalent performance." Total Workforce 40,464 WTE, "1,201 WTE worse than nationally submitted plan." — p.2 and p.16, [System Report M12]

[WORTH WATCHING] The ICB is stepping back from accountability for system financial sustainability — it paused its top financial risk (4B, rated 25) pending clarity on its future role.
[Evidence] "The Committee discussed Risk 4B given that moving forward the Integrated Care Board would not necessarily be responsible for the Integrated Care System's financial stability. The Committee agreed that Risk 4B should be put on hold" — p.3, [Finance Committee report]; "Risk 4B had been increased to a score of 25 reflecting the current system financial position" — para 9.1, [Minutes]. ICB-as-statutory-body reported a year-end surplus (Finance Committee p.3).

[WORTH WATCHING] Hampshire and Isle of Wight Healthcare FT reported 2,295 overdue incidents and 79 of 109 safety actions overdue, plus rising prone restraint in CAMHS.
[Evidence] "the Trust report 2,295 overdue incidents and 79/109 safety actions overdue"; "high restrictive practice and use of force concerns: a high use of restraint is being reported by the Child and Adolescent Mental Health Service and prone restraint has increased." — p.4, [QPIC Board Assurance Report, 1 May 2026]

[WORTH WATCHING] H&IOW Community Healthcare FT's community mental health services were rated Requires Improvement by the CQC with nine regulatory breaches (Sept 2025 inspection).
[Evidence] "following the Care Quality Commission inspection (2–4 September 2025), the community-based mental health services achieved an overall rating of Requires Improvement and received nine breaches of regulation." — p.4, [QPIC Board Assurance Report]

[WORTH WATCHING] The ICB has de-commissioned nine procedures as "not normally commissioned"; the public asked specifically about bunion surgery funding.
[Evidence] "a question submitted in advance... asking whether funding for bunion surgery would be reinstated"; "Following the review nine procedures were removed and reclassified as 'not normally commissioned' and therefore, would not be routinely funded unless there was an exceptional need" — paras 20.1–20.2, [Minutes]

[WORTH WATCHING] 22.7% of beds are occupied by patients with no criteria to reside — ranked 40th of 42 ICBs (lowest quartile); 52-week RTT waits also ranked 40/42.
[Evidence] NCTR "increasing to 22.7% (compared to 21.4% in Month 11)... ranked 40 out of 42 Integrated Care Boards... 669 patients with no Criteria to Reside as at 12 April 2026, which is 23.00% of total G&A beds available (Lowest quartile)"; RTT 52-week waits "ranked 40 out of 42 Integrated Care Boards... with 2.1% (Lowest quartile)." — pp.5, 7, 8, [System Report M12]
