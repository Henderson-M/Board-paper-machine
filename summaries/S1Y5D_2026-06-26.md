# NHS Central East ICB board — 26 June 2026

- **Source:** https://www.centraleast.icb.nhs.uk/documents/
- **Pack:** 1 file (combined Board in Public pack, 71pp)
- **Analysed:** 2026-07-13T10:56:19Z

Context: this is the **first "Board in Public" of NHS Central East ICB**, the statutory ICB created on 1 April 2026 by the ICB (Establishment and Abolition) Order 2026. It merges the former Bedfordshire, Luton & Milton Keynes (BLMK), Cambridgeshire & Peterborough (C&P) and the Hertfordshire footprint of Hertfordshire & West Essex (H&WE) ICBs; West Essex went to the new NHS Essex ICB. It serves ~3.5 million people. CEO Jan Thomas; Chair Robin Porter. The pack carries this meeting's CEO report and committee reports plus, as papers, the draft minutes of the 27 March 2026 legacy meeting-in-common and the 1 April 2026 inaugural Central East board.

[LEAD] NHSE's forced merger of the three legacy ICB finance ledgers went wrong — ~5,800 Central East invoices affected, some wrongly flagged as approved and at risk of being paid without sign-off
[Evidence] "For CEICB approx. 5,800 invoices were affected... A number of invoices were transferred into the new ledger and had incorrectly been flagged as being approved when they had been pending the appropriate approval; leading to a risk that those invoices would proceed onto a payment run without having the necessary approvals in place... There have been no losses or control failures within the ICB. However, resolving these issues has had a significant operational impact on the ICBs finance staff." — p.58, [Audit & Risk Committee Chair's AAA Report, Item 9.1]

[WORTH WATCHING] Legacy systems closed 2025/26 in deficit before the merger — H&WE relied on £10m NHSE deficit support

## FOI leads

- **SBS ledger integration incident report.** Request from NHS Central East ICB (and/or NHSE/SBS) the report(s) being drafted on the ledger-merger incident that left ~5,800 Central East invoices affected, including how many invoices were at risk of payment without approval and the operational impact. *Why it matters:* names a control failure arising directly from NHSE's centralised ledger merger of the abolished ICBs. — p.58.
- **H&WE contract register "minimal assurance" internal audit.** Request the full internal audit report behind the minimal-assurance opinion on the Herts & West Essex contract register, plus the rationalised list of outstanding management actions Central East is inheriting. *Why it matters:* weak procurement controls, incomplete contract registers and "limited visibility of contracts" at a body that commissions £billions. — p.59.
- **CPFT Section 29A warning notice.** Request the notice and the ICB's summary paper on it from CQC / NHS Central East ICB, plus CPFT's improvement plan. *Why it matters:* formal regulatory action against a named foundation trust for care requiring "significant improvement". — pp.65–66.
- **Lampard Inquiry Rule 9 material.** The ICBs have received five Rule 9 requests for information/witness statements (three since the last update) including one on "the ICBs involvement in a provider led investigation". Request scope/subject of the Rule 9 requests and the ICB's submitted statements (note inquiry-sensitivity). *Why it matters:* points to a provider-led investigation the ICB was involved in. — p.43.
- **Private Priority Programme data + Dermatology recommissioning.** The Q1 stocktake states "Initial data analysis... is presented in the private section of today's meeting" and dermatology recommissioning "is subject to its own discussion" as a separate (private) board item. Request the private Item 8 pack and the dermatology paper. *Why it matters:* the substantive delivery data and a live recommissioning decision are being withheld from the public pack. — pp.45–47, 55.
- **MHIS understatement.** Request the H&WE Mental Health Investment Standard audit that "identified a £169,000 understatement". *Why it matters:* MHIS compliance is a statutory mental-health spend guarantee. — p.59.
