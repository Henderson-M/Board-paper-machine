## Top lines

[LEAD] North East London ICS closed 2025/26 with a £63.8m system deficit against a breakeven plan, even as the ICB itself ran a small surplus.
[Evidence] "At month 12, the Integrated Care System (ICS) is reporting a £63.8m deficit, which is an … adverse variance of £63.8m to the breakeven plan." / "The ICB planned to deliver a £2.5m surplus at Month 12; however, the actual year-end position was a £2.6m surplus, £0.1m better than plan." — p.17, [Minutes of 20 May 2026 board, Item 7.1 Financial overview]

[LEAD] The ICB booked £28.8m of termination benefits in 2025/26 and agreed 214 exit packages totalling £14.67m as it implemented nationally mandated staffing cuts.
[Evidence] "Termination benefits 28,809" (£'000) vs "261" the prior year — p.155, [Annual report and accounts 2025/26, Note 4.1 Employee benefits]. Exit packages: "Total 55 2,672,134 [compulsory redundancies] … 159 11,998,104 [other agreed departures] … 214 14,670,238" — p.153, [Annual report 2025/26, Table 1: Exit Packages].

[LEAD] First notices of compulsory redundancy were issued in the ICB's change programme this month, with the annual report citing "nationally mandated organisational changes across the NHS requiring 50% staffing cuts."
[Evidence] "Our ICB change programme is progressing with the first notices of compulsory redundancy being issued this month." — p.11, [Minutes of 20 May 2026, Item 4.1 Chair and CEO report]. "due to nationally mandated organisational changes across the NHS requiring 50% staffing cuts, to ICBs in 2025/26, the annual Staff Survey was replaced with a targeted pulse survey" — p.159, [Annual report 2025/26, Staff engagement].

[LEAD] The ICB breached the NHS England Very Senior Managers pay framework, agreeing a salary outside the recommended range without prior NHSE approval.
[Evidence] "In one instance, a salary was agreed outside the recommended pay range and prior NHS England approval was not obtained … The ICB recognises that this constitutes a departure from the framework" — p.149, [Annual report 2025/26, Remuneration of Very Senior Managers].

[LEAD] North East London had three chief executives across 2025/26: Zina Etheridge left in December 2025, Ralph Coulbeck acted up from 18 Dec to 15 Mar, and Nnenna Osuji started 16 March 2026.
[Evidence] "Zina Etheridge / Chief Executive Officer 210 - 215 … 01/04/2025 - 18/12/2025"; "Ralph Coulbeck … Interim Chief Executive Officer 145 - 150 … 18/12/2025 - 15/03/2026"; "Nnenna Osuji / Chief Executive Officer 10 - 15 … 16/03/2026 - 31/03/2026" — p.150, [Annual report 2025/26, Senior manager remuneration]. Etheridge's banded total £345k–£350k included pension-related benefits of £135k–£137.5k for under nine months in post.

[LEAD] Internal audit gave only "partial assurance" on the ICB's HR/staffing and procurement controls for 2025/26.
[Evidence] "HR staffing — Partial assurance"; "Procurement — Partial assurance" — p.145, [Annual report 2025/26, Head of Internal Audit Opinion]. Overall HoIA opinion: framework "adequate and effective" but "our work has identified further enhancements".

[LEAD] North East London's May 2026 baseline census recorded 47 adult mental health and 2 learning disability out-of-area placements; all current placements originate from one provider, NELFT.
[Evidence] "47 adult mental health out of area placements and 2 learning disability out of area placements identified … All current out of area placements originate from NELFT. This is reported to be due to the bed base seen at NELFT over recent years." — p.30/p.34, [MHLDA out of area placements paper, 11 June]. Only one placement (a learning-disability/autism patient at Priory Llanarth Court) is classed "Inappropriate" — p.34.

[LEAD] The ICB has invested c.£10.5m in inpatient capacity and is buying a 25-bed block contract from the Priory to cut reliance on out-of-area placements ahead of an NHSE March 2028 elimination deadline.
[Evidence] "North east London has invested £10.5m sustainable inpatient capacity" — p.31; "a 25-bed block contract with the Priory (2026/27)" plus "Investment of approximately £5m in 2026/27 to strengthen mental health crisis services" — p.35, [MHLDA out of area placements paper]. "ending all out of area placements and locked rehabilitation by March 2028" — p.30.

[WORTH WATCHING] NHS England has demanded a second assurance statement from the ICB confirming its new structure can discharge strategic-commissioner functions within the running-cost allowance.
[Evidence] "NHS England has requested that the ICB Board provides a further assurance statement, following the statement that was submitted in January 2026. This relates to confirming that the proposed organisational structure enables the ICB to effectively and efficiently discharge its functions as a strategic commissioner within the running cost allowance." — p.19, [Minutes of 20 May 2026, Item 11.0 AOB]. Response delegated to Chair/CEO/CFO/FPIC chair, deadline 29 May 2026.

[WORTH WATCHING] The ICB is dismantling its system-partner governance — disestablishing the Executive Committee, replacing the Population Health & Integration Committee with a Strategic Commissioning Committee, and confirming the statutory ICP will be abolished under the Health Bill.
[Evidence] "Disestablishment of the Executive Committee" — p.27; "A Strategic Commissioning Committee will be established as a Committee of the Board, replacing the current Population Health and Integration Committee (which will be disestablished)" — p.25; "The NHS Modernisation Bill (now widely known as the Health Bill), published in May 2026, confirms the anticipated position that the statutory basis for the ICP will be abolished" — p.27, [Governance update, 11 June].

[WORTH WATCHING] Mental health was the largest financial variance in-year, driven partly by "Right to Choose" activity and Section 117 placements.
[Evidence] "the mental health overspend is the largest variance, including a significant contribution from 'Right to Choose' activity, though not exclusively. Additional pressures include Section 117 placements and wider demand pressures." — p.18, [Minutes of 20 May 2026, Item 7.1].

[WORTH WATCHING] BHRUT's diagnostic data stopped flowing to NHSE after a November 2025 EPR go-live; full reporting was still being restored as of the May board.
[Evidence] "Barking, Havering and Redbridge University Trust implemented a new Electronic Patient Record … this meant there was an agreed interruption of data flowing to NHSE" — p.141, [Annual report, Data Quality]; "restoration of full diagnostic reporting is expected imminently" — p.18, [Minutes of 20 May 2026, Item 7.2].

[WORTH WATCHING] SEND demand is surging — c.25% average growth in EHCPs across the patch — as the ICB takes on the £1.8bn "Experts at Hand" commissioning duty with an unquantified funding ask.
[Evidence] "an average 25% growth in Education, Health and Care Plans (EHCPs) across north east London"; "Experts at Hand … Funded nationally at £1.8bn over three years … Local authority transformation funding of between £2m and £3.5m per borough per annum" — p.12, [Minutes of 20 May 2026, Item 5.1]. Board "Requested clarity on the financial implications for the ICB" — p.14.
