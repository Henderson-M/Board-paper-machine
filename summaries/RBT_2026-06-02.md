## Top lines

[LEAD] Mid Cheshire has brought in management consultancy Carnall Farrer to support its transformation/recovery, and is recruiting a Director of Transformation, after PAF recorded "limited confidence" in its large-scale recovery plans.
[Evidence] "BAF5 (Finance): Assurance rating reflected the significant risks in the Q1 position and identified gaps in controls, including contract income management, transformation/recovery and productivity and recurrent CIP delivery shortfall. There is limited confidence in the large-scale transformation plans, many of which are already behind plan. Mitigations include external support from Carnall Farrer and recruitment of a Director of Transformation." — p.331, [PAF Committee Chair's Assurance Report]

[LEAD] The trust faces a £39.4m planned deficit for 2026/27 and has warned that around £24m of Deficit Support Funding is at risk if it does not develop 100% of its savings plans by the end of June.
[Evidence] "Run rate pressures in pay and non-pay continuing, and budget overspends if not addressed, will exceed any mitigations that may be available and will risk payment of DSF c £24m" — p.360, [Finance & Efficiencies Report M01]; "Deficit Support Funding (DSF) for Q2 will be determine based on the CIP development – with 100% expected to be Implemented by the end of June 26." — p.360; "1 Financial balance £39.4m deficit" — p.133, [Medium Term Plan / Trust Strategy]

[LEAD] Only 60% of the trust's cost improvement schemes have been developed and the risk-adjusted savings forecast already shows a material gap; M1 CIP delivery was 61% of target, two-thirds of it non-recurrent.
[Evidence] "CIPs currently have a risk adjusted forecast of £16.9m, highlighting material delivery gap risk and this unchecked will impact cash as a result of deficit support funding potentially being withdrawn." — p.357, [Finance & Efficiencies Report M01]; "Only 60% of the CIPs are developed so far with a high level of risk on some of those schemes." — p.360; "Delivery of Cost Improvement Programme (CIP) savings in Month 1 is 61% against the planned target of £2.0m... A significant proportion of in-month delivery relates to non-recurrent savings (67%)" — p.363
