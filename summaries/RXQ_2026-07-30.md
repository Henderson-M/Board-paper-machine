## Top lines

[LEAD] Trust reports a Never Event involving wrong-site cardiac surgery, with committee minutes referring to "previous events" of the same type.
[Evidence] "Verbal update provided on a Never Event related to a wrong site cardiac procedure as well as plans to share learning from previous events to reduce risk of further incidents. Full Duty of Candour completed with relevant patient." — p.1 and p.7, [Chair Report, Quality & Clinical Governance Committee, 15 July 2026]

[LEAD] NHS England's South East regional director has written to the trust over deteriorating performance on four fronts and is visiting Stoke Mandeville in early August to discuss its recovery plans.
[Evidence] "We have received a letter from the NHS South East Regional Director regarding our performance across the following four areas: • Deteriorating urgent and emergency care performance • Elective performance remaining off standard • Cancer 62 days performance • Diagnostics activity levels behind plan… we are welcoming the South East Regional Director to Stoke Mandeville in early August to discuss our improvement plans." — p.3, [CEO Report July 2026, item 1.5]

[LEAD] A review of mortuary and post-death-care arrangements against post-Fuller/Nottingham national requirements identified "significant areas of concern"; the trust is nonetheless asking the board to approve a Board Assurance Statement to NHSE. (Note internal contradiction: an appendix on p.5 states "no significant areas of concern have been identified.")
[Evidence] "The Trust has reviewed its current arrangements against the national requirements and relevant guidance with significant areas of concern identified. However, further work is underway to strengthen internal procedures, complete relevant gap analyses…" — p.3, [Mortuary & Maternity Services Board Assurance Statement]. Mortuary "built to HBN 20 (superseded by HBN 16-01). Gap analysis underway to confirm specific areas of non-compliance" — p.4.

[LEAD] Paediatric audiology remains on the corporate risk register (Risk 851) with governance strengthened "in response to NHSE concerns" and patient look-back exercises still not due to complete until end September.
[Evidence] "Partially assured, noting: - Progress in completing look-back exercises with plans in place to complete these by end September - Strengthened governance and assurance arrangements in response to NHSE concerns - Appointment of a broadened substantive leadership team… - Ongoing challenges related to widespread workforce shortages and impact on capacity" — p.1, [Chair Report, Quality & Clinical Governance Committee]

[WORTH WATCHING] The trust sits in NHS Oversight Framework segment 3 solely because of its deficit-support override — unadjusted it would be segment 2 — and scored segment 4 (low performing) on finance/productivity, ranking 51st of 134 acute trusts.
[Evidence] "For Q4 2025/26 performance is Segment 3… This places the Trust 51 out of 134… Unadjusted performance (i.e. performance without the financial deficit override), would have placed the Trust in Segment 2. By exception, the Trust performed in Segment 4 for 'implied productivity level'… reported as loss in productivity of -3.44%." — pp.3, 6, [NHS Oversight Framework]. Finance and productivity domain scored "4 - Low performing" — p.7.

[WORTH WATCHING] The trust's 2026/27 capital plan has been cut by ~£5m after estates safety funding was confirmed at less than half the sum originally planned.
[Evidence] "The full year plan has reduced from £50.7m to £45.8m, due to confirmation of Estates Safety Funding of £4m compared to £9m previously planned." — p.1, [M03 Finance Report Front Sheet]. M3 deficit £(3.4)m vs planned £(3.5)m; pay £(1.5)m worse than plan; annual break-even relies on £1.2m deficit support funding.

[WORTH WATCHING] Neonatal safety and obstetric staffing are under pressure: the last MBRRACE report gave a red rating on neonatal outcomes, a new corporate risk on consultant obstetric workforce has been opened (Risk 1100), and maternity triage medical review times are "not yet to standard".
[Evidence] "further work was underway in key areas, including neonatal outcomes, following the red rating by the last MBRRACE report" — p.5, [Draft Board Minutes 28 May 2026]. "Risk 1100: Consultant Obstetric Workforce – new risk" — p.5, [Organisational Risk Report]. "BSOTS implemented; initial compliance c.75%. Medical review times not yet to standard — dedicated medical triage cover resource request in progress" — p.4, [Amos & Ockenden Reports — BHT position]

[WORTH WATCHING] A CQC inspection of medical and spinal services, medicines management and the emergency department has taken place; the trust is awaiting the draft report.
[Evidence] "the CQC were visiting us to inspect our medical and spinal services, medicines management and emergency department and linked services… We now await the draft report for review ahead of publication." — p.3, [CEO Report July 2026, item 1.4]

[WORTH WATCHING] The Freedom to Speak Up service's capacity was cut from 2.45 to 1 WTE during the year even as concern complexity rose, with cases requiring formal investigation including alleged racism, an information governance breach and matters requiring police involvement.
[Evidence] "in-year fluctuations of capacity reducing from 2.45 to 1wte" — p.4. "the FTSU team have received concerns that we have identified as requiring formal investigation relating to: - Colleague to colleague serious inappropriate behaviours, - Our modern slavery declaration; - An information governance breach - Failure to meet professional standards - Colleague to colleague bullying and harassment. - Alleged discriminatory practice / alleged racism" and "In certain types of cases police investigations are also required." — p.21, [Freedom to Speak Up Guardian Annual Report 2025-26]
