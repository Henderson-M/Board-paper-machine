## FOI leads

[FOI] **The independent Well-Led Review and the "DBTH Way in Action" review.** The pack tracks recommendations but does not reproduce either report. *Request from DBTH:* both final reports, terms of reference, the commissioning documents, the name and contract value of the firm(s) engaged, and the current RAG-rated action plan identifying the recommendation now recorded as off track. *Why it matters:* HSJ reported the bullying/blame-culture findings in January; the reports themselves have not been published and one recommendation has now slipped.

[FOI] **The NHSE cash support application.** *Request from DBTH and NHS England (North East and Yorkshire region):* the trust's revenue cash support application prepared/submitted in September 2026, any NHSE response and conditions attached, and correspondence about the month 4 deficit support funding that was not paid. *Why it matters:* the pack confirms the application exists but discloses neither the amount nor the terms.

[FOI] **The CQC visit to the emergency department.** The pack refers to "CQC recommendations actioned immediately in ED", an "Emergency Department CQC action plan" and a "CQC visit which highlighted issues with patient equipment in ED" — but publishes no findings. *Request from DBTH:* the CQC feedback letter or report from that visit and the trust's ED action plan.

[FOI] **The South Yorkshire ICB contract negotiation.** The finance report books a £6.485m mitigation against "Commissioner Growth and clawback", attributed to "Negotiation with SY ICB / NHSE CDC allocation". *Request from DBTH and South Yorkshire ICB:* correspondence and the agreed position on 2026/27 growth funding and clawback.
