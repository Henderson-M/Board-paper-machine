## Top lines

[LEAD] Merged LCH/LYPFT trust to be named "Leeds Partnership NHS Foundation Trust" — the Transition Committee's recommended name for the new organisation that goes live 1 April 2027.
[Evidence] "The final recommendation approved by the committee is to adopt the name: Leeds Partnership NHS Foundation Trust (LPFT)" — chosen from a four-name shortlist, "partnership" picked partly as "an important marker to distinguish us from acute trusts" — p.25–26, [Interim Chief Executive's Report, Item 5]

[LEAD] Merger timetable and new-board process now firm: full business case to NHSE in October 2026, a brand-new Trust Board to be appointed by end of October 2026, effective 1 April 2027.
[Evidence] "Draft full business case (FBC) to be reviewed by both Trust Boards at the end of September 2026… FBC submission to NHSE national transaction team and regional team October 2026… Process for the appointment of a new Trust Board to commence in August and conclude by end of October 2026 that will come into effect from 1 April 2027." Joint LCH/LYPFT board on 30 July 2026 to review due diligence. — p.26, [Item 5]

[WORTH WATCHING] Neurodevelopmental (autism/ADHD) waiting remains the trust's sole "extreme" risk — 1,154 families still on the Mind Mate ND triage waiting list — even as the CEO celebrates ND improvements elsewhere.
[Evidence] Risk 1383 scores 15 (extreme), static for 9 months: "As of June 2026, Northpoint reported that 1,154 families remain on the neurodevelopmental (ND) waiting list." Separately 512 CYP still wait >52 weeks for ND assessment. — p.125, [Significant Risks and Risk Assurance Report, Item 15]; p.77, [IPR, Item 12]

[WORTH WATCHING] Internal audit found a "significant disparity" between the trust's own Data Security & Protection Toolkit self-assessment and audit findings, notably on system back-up and restore.
[Evidence] "The Committee discussed the significant disparity between the self-assessment and the audit particularly around the back-up and restore of systems when failures occurred and the risks this posed to the Trust." Action plan/trajectory to full compliance due back to Audit Committee October 2026. — p.62, [Audit Committee Chair's Assurance Report, Item 10]

[WORTH WATCHING] 2026 Leeds SEND inspection found "systemic failures" in supporting families; full CQC/Ofsted report still awaited, LCH action plan not yet written.
[Evidence] "Leeds SEND Inspection Report 2026 - Discussed the outcomes of the recent SEND inspection, which identified systemic failures in supporting families, leadership challenges. An action plan is being developed to address inspection findings which will be presented to the next meeting in September." — p.45, [Quality Committee Chair's Assurance Report, Item 8a]

[WORTH WATCHING] Incident-management backlog remains at limited/partial assurance — only 20% of initial incident reviews completed within the required three working days, and 23 overdue patient-safety-investigation actions, some due since March 2025.
[Evidence] Assurance ticked "No assurance"/reported as Limited: "Compliance has increased from 10% reported previously to 20% at the time of reporting." 23 overdue PSII actions, oldest due March 2025. — p.49–51, [Incident Management & Investigation Oversight update, Item 8b]; p.96, [IPR, Item 12]

[WORTH WATCHING] Finance opens the year on plan — a £0.103m year-to-date surplus against a planned break-even, £8.56m savings target (incl. £2.46m carried forward), NOF Segment 3 maintained.
[Evidence] "the Trust is reporting a year-to-date surplus of £0.103m against a planned break-even position… forecasting a break-even position in line with plan." Savings target £8.560m for 2026/27. Ends 2025/26 in Segment 3. — p.81, [IPR, Item 12]; p.108, [NOF Briefing, Item 13i]

## FOI leads

- **Leeds 2026 SEND inspection report + LCH action plan.** The pack references but does not disclose the inspection findings ("systemic failures in supporting families, leadership challenges"). Request the full inspection report and LCH's SEND improvement action plan — from Leeds Community Healthcare NHS Trust (and/or Leeds City Council / West Yorkshire ICB as the local-area partners). Why: names systemic failure across a children's services system LCH provides into. — p.45

- **DSPT internal audit report.** Request the internal audit report on the Data Security and Protection Toolkit final assessment and the back-up/restore findings, plus the compliance trajectory — from Leeds Community Healthcare NHS Trust. Why: an audit that contradicts the trust's own cyber self-assessment. — p.62

- **New-organisation full business case / transaction papers.** The FBC and due-diligence (legal and internal) go to a private joint board on 30 July 2026. Request the FBC and checkpoint report to NHSE (4 Sept 2026) once submitted. Why: sets out the financial and workforce case for the LCH/LYPFT merger. — p.26
