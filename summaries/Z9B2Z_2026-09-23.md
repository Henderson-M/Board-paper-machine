# West and North London ICB annual general meeting – 23 September 2026

- **Source:** https://www.westandnorthlondon.icb.nhs.uk/how-we-are-run/policies-and-publications/navigate/1617/2410
- **Pack:** 3 files
- **Analysed:** 2026-09-15T10:30:54Z

Context for the reporter: this is a 30-minute online AGM (9.45-10.15am). There is no CEO report, finance report or risk register. The pack is the agenda plus the two final annual reports and accounts of the legacy organisations, North Central London ICB and North West London ICB, which merged on 1 April 2026 to form West and North London ICB. Both ARAs were approved by the legacy boards on 17 June 2026 and submitted to NHS England on 19 June 2026, so the documents are not brand new, but they are being formally laid before the public for the first time at this meeting and the merged-organisation totals below have not, as far as this scan can tell, been pulled together anywhere.

All page references are **PDF page numbers of the file as downloaded**, not the printed page number in the document footer (the ARAs run about two pages ahead).

## Top lines

[LEAD] The two ICBs that merged to form West and North London agreed 309 exit packages costing £25.3m between them in 2025-26, their final year of operation.
[Evidence] North Central London: "Total 121 £11,502,621", all of them "other departures agreed" and none compulsory, including 28 packages in the £150,001-£200,000 band costing £4,844,706 – p.153 and p.179, [NCL ARA]. North West London: "Total 9 £692,848 179 £13,095,604 188 £13,788,451", against 41 packages costing £2,783,308 the year before – p.103 and p.131, [NWL ARA]. [INFERENCE] The £25.3m combined figure and the 309 count are my addition of the two separately disclosed totals; neither document states a combined number.

[LEAD] A further £44.3m of redundancy cost is provided for or accrued and lands in 2026-27, on top of the £25.3m already paid.
[Evidence] "The ICB has recognised the sum of £14,398,000 Contractual redundancy provision in respect of staff at risk following merger with NHS North West London ICB", expected within one year – p.189, [NCL ARA]. "A restructuring provision of £13.2m has been recognised... The provision has been measured to include only those direct expenditures necessarily arising from the restructuring (compulsory redundancy)" and, separately, "In addition, voluntary redundancies were approved in accordance with the ICB's voluntary redundancy scheme, totalling £16.7m. These costs have been accrued in respect of these arrangements, with payments and employee departures occurring on 30 April 2026" – p.138, [NWL ARA]. [INFERENCE] The £44.3m is my addition of £14.398m, £13.243m and £16.7m. Note the NWL £16.7m voluntary figure does not reconcile to the £13.1m of voluntary packages shown as agreed in-year in the exit packages table, so worth asking the ICB what the £16.7m covers.

[LEAD] Chief finance officer Stephen Bloomer's disclosed remuneration across the two ICBs was £490,000-£495,000, while North Central London ICB reported its "highest paid director" at £150,000-£155,000 because joint roles were cost-shared.
[Evidence] Table "Total salaries and allowances of senior managers under the NHS North Central London ICB and NHS North West London ICB shared staff arrangements", 1 April 2025 to 31 March 2026: "Stephen Bloomer 235-240 252.5-255 490-495" and "Frances O'Callaghan 220-225 125-127.5 345-350" – p.137, [NCL ARA]. The reduction is due to North Central London and North West London ICBs having a joint executive team", a stated 39% fall, and "During the reporting period 2025/26, eight senior managers received remuneration in excess of the highest paid director/member" – p.132, [NCL ARA]. [FOI] Request the redundancy business cases and the NHS England assurance returns that sit behind the £25.3m already paid and the £44.3m still to come. To West and North London ICB, as successor body.
[Evidence] The North Central London remuneration committee record lists "approval of a business case to run a compulsory redundancy scheme as part of the organisational change programme", "noted the NHS England Redundancy Payment Assurance Return", "approval of the business case of the costs of the voluntary redundancy scheme following the consultation launched in December 2025", "approval of the remuneration for the Joint CEO position" and "noted the NHSE Redundancy Assurance Template (Q2, 3, and 4)" – p.107, [NCL ARA]. North West London's People and Remuneration Committee record contains the equivalents plus "E-Governance approvals: compulsory redundancies and CFO joint role" – p.69 to p.70, [NWL ARA]. None of these business cases, returns or templates is published. Ask for the compulsory and voluntary redundancy business cases, the four quarterly NHSE Redundancy Assurance Templates, and the NHSE Redundancy Payment Assurance Return, for both legacy ICBs.

[FOI] Request the auditors' Annual Reports for both legacy ICBs. To West and North London ICB, or to Deloitte and KPMG via the ICB.
[Evidence] Deloitte states: "Other findings from our work, including our commentary on the ICB's arrangements, will be reported in our separate Auditor's Annual Report" – p.114, [NWL ARA]. That report is not in this pack and contains the auditors' actual commentary on financial sustainability and governance, as distinct from the by-exception statutory opinion. Ask for both the 2025-26 Auditor's Annual Report and any ISA 260 report to those charged with governance for each legacy ICB.

[FOI] Ask what the four active legal cases behind North Central London ICB's £1.209m provision are, and why legal fees doubled.

[FOI] Ask for the register of losses and special payments behind North Central London ICB's 50 cases.
