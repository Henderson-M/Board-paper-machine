## Top lines

[LEAD] More than half of the savings North Cumbria has banked so far this year come from terminating its Carlisle PFI, not from operational efficiency — leaving a £36.6m operational savings task barely started.
[Evidence] "The CIP target for 2026/27 is a challenging £49.97m which represents 8.2% of the Trust's planned income." … "Of the £23.60m there is recurrent CIP of £19.31m (82%). Savings resulting from the voluntary termination of the PFI arrangements at CIC account for £13.42m." — p.225, [Finance Report May 2026, Item 5.2]. The same table nets PFI out to give an "Operational CIP" target of £36,552k, against which just £2,280k had been actioned by the end of May — p.225.

[LEAD] The Cumberland Infirmary PFI handback is the trust's single highest-scoring corporate risk, and further termination payments are not yet in the cash forecast.
[Evidence] "NCIC1217 CIC PFI Handback … 25" — top of the corporate risk register, p.52 and p.54, [Corporate Risks Report, Item 2.4]. "Further payments in respect of voluntary termination of the PFI at the Cumberland Infirmary are not currently reflected in the cashflow forecast." — p.227, [Finance Report May 2026]. The trust also books "£17.85m net benefit arising from the cost of voluntary termination of the Trust's PFI at Carlisle and the release of the associated PFI liabilities" as the driver of a £11.99m March 2026 run-rate surplus — p.214.

[LEAD] North Cumbria is £2.7m off plan at month two because NHSE has not yet paid any of the £16.43m deficit support its breakeven plan depends on — and that funding is conditional on five separate delivery tests including ambulance handover times.
[Evidence] "The Trust's financial plan for 2026/27 is a breakeven position supported by £16.43m of non recurrent deficit support." … "At the end of May the Trust is reporting a deficit of £6.47m against a planned deficit of £3.78m – i.e. an overspend of £2.70m." — p.203–205, [Month 2 2025/26 Finance Report, Item 5.2]. Conditions listed include "A fully developed and board-approved workforce reduction plan", "Levels of unidentified CIP to be minimal from month 1", and "Ambulance handovers to be delivered in accordance with plan but should not exceed the previous year's delivery for the same period" — p.204.

[LEAD] The trust warns last year's deficit support could be clawed back against 2026/27 ICB allocations if the 2025/26 audit worsens its position.
[Evidence] "Note that should there be any deterioration to the Trust's financial position following the audit of 2025/26's financial accounts there is a risk that some or all of 2025/26's Deficit Support Funding could be recovered against 2026/27 ICB allocations." — p.204, [Month 2 Finance Report]. Regional DoF wording quoted: "Where there is a deterioration in a system's forecast outturn after this period, there will be a national clawback of Q4 DSF in allocations." — p.205.

[LEAD] North Cumbria remains in NHSE segment 4 and is in the Intensive Recovery Support Programme, with NHSE representatives now sitting in on its finance committee.

"The Trust has secured external funding to establish, implement and embed a quality management system… agreed by NHS England as part of their support to us being placed in an intensive recovery programme." — p.27, [Chief Executive's update, Item 2.2].

[Evidence] "White applicants are 4.2 times more likely to be appointed from shortlisting than BME applicants" … "BME staff continue to report higher levels of bullying, harassment and discrimination from patients, colleagues and managers." — p.254, [Workforce Race Equality Standard Report 2026, Item 6.4]. Approved by People Committee 8 July 2026 for publication on the trust website.

[LEAD] The trust's board-level culture risk was escalated from 12 to 20 in June, and is now among its top-scoring corporate risks.
[Evidence] "NCIC1269 - There is a risk to the trust of achieving organisational objectives and priorities without a culture in place that aims to be compassionate, inclusive, and patient-centred… Increased score from 12 to 20 following discussion at People Committee." — p.51, [Corporate Risks Report, Item 2.4]. [Evidence] "For March 2026, the total funded nursing establishment across the services included within this review was 1,395 WTE compared with an SNCT-recommended requirement of 1,455 WTE, representing a deficit of 59 WTE." … "Workforce modelling to 2031 predicts a potential Registered Nurse shortfall of 265 WTE" — p.61, [Nurse Staffing Establishment Review, Item 2.5]. "Agency costs are £1.23m higher than planned; and Nursing & midwifery wte worked exceeds establishment by 100wte." — p.208, [Finance Report May 2026]. ## Notes and inference

- [INFERENCE] Netting the £13.42m PFI benefit out of the £23.60m actioned leaves roughly £10.2m of operational savings banked against a £36.55m operational target — but note the pack itself sets out the £36,552k "Operational CIP" line and the £2,280k YTD actioned figure on p.225, so this framing is derived from, not stated in, the paper.
- [INFERENCE] The combination of a segment 4 rating, Intensive Recovery Support Programme entry, NHSE observers joining the finance committee from July, and conditional deficit support suggests an intensifying oversight regime — the pack states each element separately but does not characterise them collectively.
- The finance paper is titled "Month 2 2025/26 Finance Report" (p.203) but reports May 2026, i.e. month 2 of 2026/27 — apparent typo in the paper.
