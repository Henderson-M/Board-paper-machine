## Top lines

[LEAD] Great Western Hospitals was downgraded to NHSE oversight segment 4 in the Q4 2025/26 National Oversight Framework, the only BSW trust to move down; RUH improved from 4 to 3, SFT held at 3.
[Evidence] "Quarter 4 National Oversight Framework segmentation confirmed RUH improving from segment 4 to segment 3, SFT remaining in segment 3, and GWH moving to segment 4." / Table: GWH "Moved down (from 3)" — p.27, p.31, [CEO report Item 3.2]

[LEAD] GWH's 18-week RTT performance has fallen to 58% (lower quartile nationally) and has "attracted regulatory attention and enhanced regional oversight," with a new recovery director appointed from May and 4,000 records under external validation.
[Evidence] "The waiting list has grown since July 2025 which has meant the current 18-week performance is 58%. This performance overall is in the lower quartile nationally." / "80% of the waiting list growth is in the non-admitted pathway... 4000 records are being validation through external company with a minimum of 25% expected to result in a clock stop." — p.160, p.164, [GWH Managing Director's Report Item 5.3]

[LEAD] The RUH's Emergency Department was rated 'requires improvement' by the CQC (report published 13 May 2026), with the trust required to submit an action plan; local MP Wera Hobhouse wrote a letter of support on the ED environment.
[Evidence] "The Emergency Department CQC report was published on the 13th May 2026, rated requires improvement... We have received a letter of support from local MP Wera Hobhouse following the CQC publication, specifically regarding the Emergency Department environment and the current occupancy of patients who no longer meet criteria to reside." — p.150, [RUH Managing Director's Report Item 5.2]

[LEAD] The RUH's Fire Authority has ordered the trust to resolve within two years a situation where inpatients in Block 47 would need to be evacuated across the roof in the event of fire; the trust's critical infrastructure backlog stands at £75.85m, with the full backlog programme around £120m and only £743,000 allocated to fire safety in 2026/27.
[Evidence] "The Fire Authority noted that inpatients remaining in Block 47 would require evacuation across the roof in the event of fire and asked the Trust to resolve this within two years." / "the current CIR cost at £19.7m and the total maintenance backlog at £75.85m, with the full backlog programme estimated at around £120m... £743,000 has been prioritised to the fire programme as the Trust's highest risk" — p.150-151, [RUH Managing Director's Report Item 5.2]

[LEAD] The BSW Hospitals Group is £17.1m in deficit year to date at Month 2 — £6.7m adverse to plan — driven by GWH (£4.7m) and RUH (£2.6m); Salisbury is on plan.
[Evidence] "Month 2 is materially off plan — YTD deficit is £17.1m, £6.7m adverse to plan, driven by positions at GWH (£4.0m) and RUH (£2.6m)." / slide: "The Hospitals Group position at M2 is an overspend of £6.7m, driven by adverse variances at GWH (£4.7m) and RUH (£2.6m)" — p.103, [Month 2 Group Finance Report Item 4.2]

[LEAD] GWH's managing director says there is "currently no clear path to deliver the full £47m savings" (9.6%) required for 2026/27; turnaround support started 13 April.
[Evidence] "The financial plan for 2026/27 is incredibly challenging with currently no clear path to deliver the full £47m savings 9.6%." — p.161, [GWH Managing Director's Report Item 5.3]

[LEAD] Salisbury's fully-identified £32.5m savings programme is described as "the principal organisational risk," and its segment 3 position was held only via a financial override; a key worker accommodation deal that would deliver its Q1 CIP and cash has been delayed by NHSE.
[Evidence] "£32.5m programme fully identified but remains high-risk and dependency laden in delivery against NHSE conditions... Non-delivery of Group schemes... are now the biggest risk to CIP delivery." / "National Oversight Framework for Quarter 4 position remained in segment 3 due to financial override." / "Key worker accommodation deal which will deliver CIP position and cash boost in M3/4 has been delayed by NHSE, undisclosed assessment of Novel, Contentious and Repercussive guidance." — p.127-128, [Salisbury Managing Director's Report Item 5.1]

[WORTH WATCHING] An NMC investigation found that applicant-screening systems and processes for the NMC register "had not been followed for 12 years," with 421 nursing and midwifery professionals required to provide further information (raised in RUH's June minutes, in the pack for approval).
[Evidence] "a Nursing and Midwifery Council (NMC) investigation had identified that systems and processes relating to the screening of applicants seeking to join the NMC register had not been followed for 12 years. The NMC had identified 421 nursing and midwifery professionals who were required to provide further information" — p.9 [DRAFT Public BoD Minutes 3 June 2026, BD/26/06/06]

[WORTH WATCHING] Staff survey results across all three trusts show declining advocacy/recommendation scores and falling perceptions of career development and feeling valued, flagged as "a significant early warning indicator of workforce dissatisfaction and potential retention risk."
[Evidence] "All Care Organisations have seen a reduction in advocacy scores. This represents a significant early warning indicator of workforce dissatisfaction and potential retention risk... Staff are reporting a decline in well-being, not lined to well-being offers but due to staffing levels, workloads, support and development." — p.113, [Update on Care Organisation and Group Staff Survey Actions Item 4.3a]

[WORTH WATCHING] GWH stroke services are underperforming with a SSNAP score of E (26.2 points) and are under review by the stroke network and region; the service is run by locum consultants, described as "an unsustainable model."
[Evidence] "Stroke services are underperforming with a SNNAP score of E with 26.2 points, this service has also been part of a review from the stroke network and region... The service is supported by locum consultants which is an unsustainable model." — p.161, [GWH Managing Director's Report Item 5.3]
