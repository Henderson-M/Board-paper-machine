# Salisbury NHS Foundation Trust board — 15 June 2026

- **Source:** https://www.salisbury.nhs.uk/media/u01onrzv/sft-v2-public-board-meeting-papers-june-2026.pdf
- **Pack:** 1 combined PDF (~27MB, public board meeting papers June 2026)
- **Analysed:** 2026-06-15T00:00:00Z

> Context: SFT is now one of three Care Organisations in the new **BSW Hospitals Group** (with Royal United Hospitals Bath / RUH and Great Western Hospitals Swindon / GWH), governed by a single shared Group Board going live **1 July 2026**. CEO Cara Charles-Barks signs as "Chief Executive, BSW Hospitals Group"; Nick Johnson is SFT Managing Director. Several papers (finance, governance, the NHSE letter) are group-level. Watch attribution carefully — some figures are SFT-only, some are group-wide.

## Top lines

[LEAD] NHS England accepted Salisbury's (BSW Group) medium-term plan only "with conditions", citing "material concern" over the credibility of its savings programme.
[Evidence] "Although the BSW Hospitals Group Trust has submitted a breakeven plan across all three years, there remains a material concern regarding the maturity and credibility of the efficiency programme. Progress in developing a robust, evidence-based efficiency plan has been limited." — Annex One, NHSE SW letter, 22 Apr 2026, [CEO Report appendix, Item 2.2]

[LEAD] NHSE warns Salisbury that failure to deliver the plan will trigger escalation, and imposed conditions including fortnightly de-risking updates and a formal Cash Management Committee.
[Evidence] "It is important to note if you are unable to deliver against your commitments, this will lead NHS England to consider the full range of regulatory options available to us as part of any subsequent escalation." — NHSE SW letter (Sue Doheny, Regional Director), 22 Apr 2026, [CEO Report appendix, Item 2.2]

[LEAD] Salisbury must deliver a £32.5m efficiency programme in 2026/27 — "more than double" its recent average — which its own finance committee warns could "unravel with relatively little warning".
[Evidence] "The SFT target CIP delivery is £32.5m in 2026/27, more than double the previous 3-year average delivery of c£15m." / "...compounding risks of CIP delivery ... and tight cash requirements, which could potentially conspire to 'unravel' with relatively little warning..." — p.2, [Month 1 Finance & Performance Committee Escalation Report, Item 2.4]

[LEAD] Salisbury closed 2025/26 with a £13.4m pre-audit deficit after losing £3.5m of national deficit-support funding in Q4.
[Evidence] "The Committee noted a pre-audit year-end deficit of £13.4 million..." / "...the impact of the Trust being unable to access the national element of deficit support funding in Q4 of £3.5m due to the confirmed forecast outturn of £13.5m with NHS England." — Month 11 & Month 12 F&P Escalation Reports, [Item 2.4]

[LEAD] Salisbury's 2025 staff survey scores fell across every People Promise domain — in several questions faster than the national decline — with the board paper itself flagging "significant national headwinds" and the BSW merger.
[Evidence] "SFT scores have fallen in all core People Promise domains and for staff engagement and morale and in some cases the falls have been higher than the national downward trend." / "Would recommend organisation as a place to work has fallen at SFT by 8% — while national average fall is 3%." — Staff Survey 2025 cover sheet & slides, [Item 3.1]

[LEAD] Staff free-text comments allege a "toxic", "clique-driven" culture of favouritism and deep distrust of the Bath/Swindon merger.
[Evidence] "There are explicit allegations of favoritism in promotions and training opportunities, with some staff describing the environment as 'toxic' or 'clique-driven'." / "Significant distrust exists regarding the merger with Bath and Swindon. Staff feel these decisions are 'done unto them' rather than 'with them'." — Staff Survey 2025 free-text analysis, [Item 3.1]

[WORTH WATCHING] Group sister trust GWH (Swindon) is under "external financial turnaround support" against a £47.2m efficiency requirement; RUH (Bath) closed 2025/26 £20.5m in deficit.
[Evidence] "GWH has commenced with external financial turnaround support arrangements to assist with the delivery of a challenging £47.2m efficiency requirement." / "RUH closed 2025/26 with a year-end deficit of £20.5m against a forecast £34m." — Public CEO Report, [Item 2.2]

[WORTH WATCHING] Salisbury received a level-1 MOSS maternity safety signal after three term stillbirths in Jan, Feb and March 2026; reviews found no themes and MNSI calls it "a cluster".
[Evidence] "MOSS had generated a level 1 signal ... following a stillbirth in each of January, February and March 2026..." / "...feedback from them independently that they are not seeing similar themes through cases and would describe cases as a cluster at present." — p.1–2 & Annex 1, [MOSS Critical Safety Alert, Item 5.5]

[WORTH WATCHING] HIOW ICB issued an Activity Query Notice over Salisbury's elective overperformance, and the trust has "several live disputes being actively pursued" with commissioners.
[Evidence] "HIOW ICB issued an Activity Query Notice on the overperformance (above contract envelope) on elective activity in Month 7." / "Contract finalisation discussions ongoing with several live disputes being actively pursued between SFT and commissioners." — Month 11 F&P Escalation Report [Item 2.4] & NHSE letter response [Item 2.2]

[WORTH WATCHING] The £45m "DASH" capital scheme is being recommended despite capital costs already exceeding national funding and with no approved business case.
[Evidence] "...the capital costs already exceed the national funding available, capital costs may increase as the design moves to RIBA Stage 3..." / "...target construction [only] price of £45m. Noting that the business case for the DASH project has yet to be received by the Board." — Month 1 & Month 11 F&P Escalation Reports, [Item 2.4]

## FOI leads

[FOI] Request the full NHSE South West "contract close down letter" (22 April 2026) and the SFT/BSW Group response, plus the CIP risk-weighting table — Request from NHS England South West and/or Salisbury FT. The pack quotes the conditions and the "material concern" but the embedded CIP maturity table renders as blank in the extracted text; the unredacted scheme-by-scheme risk weighting is the substance. Why it matters: shows exactly how fragile the £32.5m savings plan is judged to be.

[FOI] Request the cancer recovery plan and the "12-month rolling plan-related targets" the F&P Committee asked for — Request from Salisbury FT. Why it matters: 62-day standard ran at 56.4% in January (plan 78.4%) before recovering; the plan itself is referenced but not published in the pack.

[FOI] Request the SFT staff survey divisional A3 action plans and the full free-text thematic report — Request from Salisbury FT. Why it matters: the cover paper concedes "further work is still required to complete identified A3s"; the verbatim "toxic / clique-driven / favouritism" findings come from a summarised slide, not the underlying report.

[FOI] Request the DASH project Short Form Outline Business Case and the contract recommendation report naming MWD Healthcare / Willmott Dixon as principal supply chain partner — Request from Salisbury FT. Why it matters: £45m scheme proceeding with costs above national funding and "abortive business case" exposure the committee flagged.

[FOI] Request the keyworker accommodation business case and the Hyve / institutional-funder lease terms — Request from Salisbury FT. Why it matters: a long-lease sale-and-leaseback of NHS staff accommodation with IFRS 16 / CDEL accounting risk flagged by the committee; sign-off delegated below full board.
