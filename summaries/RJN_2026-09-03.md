## Top lines

[LEAD] Cheshire and Merseyside ICB has set up a task force on "Sustainable Hospital Services at East Cheshire Trust", led by its turnaround director, with the first programme board meeting this month.
[Evidence] "Along with the Non-Executive Directors, I met with **Jude Adams, Executive Director of Transformation & Strategy (Turnaround), Cheshire and Merseyside Integrated Care Board (ICB) who is leading task force work on Sustainable Hospital Services at East Cheshire Trust**, to discuss the progress made to date. **The first formal meeting of the Programme Board will take place in September 2026.**" — [Chair's Commentary, July and August 2026, item 6]
[Note] The pack does not set out the task force's terms of reference or scope. See FOI.

[LEAD] An arms-length review of East Cheshire's structural deficit has been commissioned with NHS England and the ICB.
[Evidence] "**Arms-length review of the structural deficit commissioned in agreement with NHSE and C&M ICB.**" — [Board Assurance Framework, financial sustainability risk, mitigating actions]
[Evidence] The BAF also records: "**External drivers of the deficit report completed**" and "Learnings from the **Drivers of the Deficit report** are being implemented including feeding into the workforce delivery group." — [BAF and Finance, Performance and Productivity Committee minutes]
[Evidence] "Detailed clinical financial model required" is listed as a gap in control. — [BAF]

[LEAD] The trust is in segment 4 of the NHS oversight framework, tier 1 of the national UEC framework and tier 2 for elective care.
[Evidence] "**The Trust is in NHS Oversight Framework Segment 4. The Trust is now segmented into Tier 1 of national performance framework for UEC. The Trust is now segmented into Tier 2 of national performance framework for Elective care.**" — p.95 and p.101 of 275, [Board Assurance Framework]
[Evidence] The reason is given explicitly: "The Trust is in NHS Oversight Framework Segment 4 **because of the impact of DCS data quality issues and non-allocation of deficit support funding.**" — p.104 of 275, [same]
[Evidence] Alongside: "CQC report – **Medical Care (Including older people's care) – rating Requires Improvement (July 2025)**"; "CQC report – **Urgent and Emergency Care – rating Requires Improvement (July 2025)**" — [same]

[LEAD] The trust drew down £2m of distressed revenue support in April because its deficit support funding had not been confirmed in time.
[Evidence] "**In month one 2026/27, the Trust received £1.3m of deficit support funding and drew down distressed revenue support of £2m for April (this was because of timing of confirmation of DSF).**" — p.247 of 275, [Finance, Performance and Productivity Committee minutes]
[Evidence] The BAF records the underlying position: "Adverse financial plan. **Cash balance has deteriorated and the Trust has requested distressed revenue support in 2025/26.** CIP Programme not fully developed." — [Board Assurance Framework, first line of defence]
[Evidence] "The Trust implemented stringent cash management measures throughout 2025/26 and continues to maintain these controls in 2026/27 to support financial stability and cash preservation." — [Finance report]

[LEAD] Quarter 2 deficit support funding is conditional on a fully developed savings programme — and a third of the programme is risk-rated out.
[Evidence] "**Deficit support funding for Quarter 2 2026/27 has not been confirmed and as guidance indicates that this is contingent on having 100% fully developed CIP plans; it remains a risk.**" — [Month 4 Finance Report]
[Evidence] "the Trust has a **£14.2m CIP requirement in 2026/27** noting that schemes totalling £14.2m have been identified to date, representing **100% of the target. However... this value has been risk rated to £9.7m**, and the forecast outturn position adjusted accordingly, and work is ongoing to address this gap." — [same]
[Evidence] "Other key risks remain in respect of the pay award, industrial action, medically fit mental health patients [and] unfunded support and data quality." — [same]
[Note] A separate, earlier passage in the same bundle states "The Trust has received deficit support funding for quarter one and quarter two [and] this is reflected in the position" — the papers are internally inconsistent on the Q2 position and this is worth checking with the trust.

[Evidence] "**Our CEO, Ged Murphy, took retirement from the Trust on 31st July 2026, after nearly 5 years loyal service.** Staff gathered to wish Ged well in his retirement and to thank him for his commitment, leadership and compassion during his time at the Trust. ## FOI leads

[FOI] **The ICB task force on Sustainable Hospital Services at East Cheshire Trust.** *Request from NHS Cheshire and Merseyside ICB and East Cheshire NHS Trust:* the task force's terms of reference, membership, work programme, options under consideration, and the papers for the September 2026 programme board. *Why it matters:* an ICB turnaround director leading a task force on the "sustainability" of a named district general hospital, alongside references to "future hospital archetypes", is the shape of a service reconfiguration story.

[FOI] **The arms-length review of the structural deficit.** *Request from East Cheshire NHS Trust, NHS England (North West) and C&M ICB:* the terms of reference, the identity of the reviewer and the contract value, and the completed "Drivers of the Deficit" report referred to in the BAF.

[FOI] **The PwC engagement.** *Request from East Cheshire NHS Trust:* the PwC contracts covering the controls report and the balance sheet review — value, term, scope and deliverables — and both completed reports.

[FOI] **The month 3 Finance Performance Recovery Meeting.** *Request from NHS England (North West) and East Cheshire NHS Trust:* the papers, attendance and note of the FPRM attended by Stephen Hay, PwC, NHSE and ICB colleagues, and the equivalent papers for months 1, 2 and 4.

[FOI] **The Q2 deficit support funding decision.** The papers give two different accounts of whether Q2 DSF has been received. *Request from East Cheshire NHS Trust and NHS England (North West):* the DSF award/withhold letters for Q1 and Q2 2026/27, the conditions attached, and the distressed revenue support drawdown of £2m in April 2026 and its repayment terms.

[FOI] **The DCS data quality issues** cited as one of two reasons for the segment 4 rating. *Request from East Cheshire NHS Trust:* the papers reported monthly to Private Trust Board on data quality, the quantified income and activity impact, and the remediation plan.
