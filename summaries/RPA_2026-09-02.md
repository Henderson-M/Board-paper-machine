## Top lines

[LEAD] Medway has the worst underlying deficit as a share of turnover of any provider in its system — £74.6m, or 13.5%.
[Evidence] "the system enters the period with an **underlying recurrent deficit of £334.1m excluding deficit support funding, while MFT starts from an underlying deficit of £74.6m, equivalent to 13.5% of turnover, the highest percentage deficit in the provider table.**" — [Draft system plan summary in the board pack]
[Evidence] Benchmarking does not close it: "the parallel exercise in benchmarking MFT (using national benchmarks) shows '**opportunities' of £114.9m could be realised, leaving a gap of about £30m i.e. our gap is greater than benchmarked efficiencies.**" — [same]
[Evidence] "The draft system paper also shows that **MFT is affected by several stacked financia[l pressures]**" — [same]

[LEAD] The trust is forecasting a £41m cash shortfall and modelling stretching £41.8m of supplier payments to stay above the minimum cash balance NHS England sets for it.
[Evidence] "**Forecast = cash shortfall for 2026/27 of £41m** (see 'creditor stretch' row), **£35.8m in year shortfall** plus cash holding required to operate into 2027/28." — p.170 of 571, [Month 4 Finance Report, 12-month rolling operating cash flow, Scenario 1]
[Evidence] "Mitigation: Working capital management, including review of creditor payment terms. See 'creditor stretch' row in the cash flow, **in total £41.8m of creditor payments would need to be stretched to ensure the minimum cash holding of £2.765m as set by NHSE is met at all times.**" — [same]
[Evidence] Even the improved scenario needs it: "Scenario 2: Increased CIP delivery... this would give a year end cash shortfall of £14.0m (£16.8m inc min cash balance required). **This would require creditor mitigation of c. 34 days, 64 day payment terms by March.**" — p.172 of 571, [same]
[Evidence] "Not achieving efficiencies will result in an I&E reforecast at which point **a revenue cash support application can be made to NHSE. A successful cash support application for the full shortfall of £41m would enable supplier mitigations to be reversed but would be at a cost of c. £1.5m PDC dividend per annum.**" — [same]
[Evidence] "A **loss of DSF would lead to a need for NHSE cash support (as last year).**" — [Month 4 Finance Report, Executive Summary]

[LEAD] NHS England has escalated oversight and put Medway on fortnightly performance monitoring, with a system turnaround director working on its recovery plan.
[Evidence] "Referral to Treatment (RTT) performance remains a significant concern. **NHS England has escalated oversight arrangements, placing the Trust on fortnightly performance monitoring. The System Turnaround Director is supporting the development and implementation of a comprehensive recovery plan across all clinical direc[torates]**" — p.121 of 571, [Integrated Performance Report / Chief Operating Officer's report]
[Evidence] Among the forecast risks the board is asked to note: "**RTT performance penalties and loss of DSF**" — [Month 4 Finance Report]

[LEAD] The trust went adverse to plan in July, £3.5m in a single month, and its savings programme is running at little over half the required rate.
[Evidence] "Following the Trust remaining 'on Plan' for the first quarter (Apr–Jun) **for July we are adverse to plan in month & YTD delivering a deficit of £1.7m, which is £3.5m worse than plan including the Deficit Support Funding ('DSF'). The YTD position recognises £11.8m of DSF.**" — [Month 4 Finance Report, Executive Summary]
[Evidence] "**CIP delivery for the YTD is £7.6m (Mth 1 - £1.2m, Mth 2 - £2.2m, Mth 3 - £2.2m, Mth 4 - £2.0m) against a plan requirement of £12.9m.**" — p.161 of 571, [same]
[Evidence] "**The efficiency target is £3.3m per month** – whilst the monthly reported delivery remains consistent this still falls short of the requirements. The Trust's expenditure **run-rate is higher than plan and poses a high risk to delivery of the control total in 2026/27.**" — [same]
[Evidence] "The Trust approved a **breakeven plan (deficit of £35.4m after adjusting out DSF)**. The Month 4 Provider Assurance Model is forecasting a **£13m variance to plan (after DSF)** for the best and mid-case" — p.167 of 571, [same]
[Evidence] "**Short term measures and technical adjustments in Q1 are not sustainable**; therefore, real cost improvement initiatives are required to retu[rn to plan]" and the trust "**is at high risk of failure to deliver the break even plan.**" — [same]

## FOI leads

[FOI] **The creditor stretch plan.** *Request from Medway NHS FT:* the month 4 cash flow forecast in full, including the "creditor stretch" schedule showing the £41.8m of supplier payments proposed to be delayed, the projected supplier payment days by month, and any assessment of the impact on suppliers or on Better Payment Practice Code compliance. *Why it matters:* a named trust modelling the deliberate delay of £41.8m of supplier payments to stay solvent is a strong standalone story.

[FOI] **The financial governance reviews.** *Request from Medway NHS FT:* the internal and external financial governance reviews undertaken over the last two years whose recommendations were consolidated into the Financial Governance Implementation Plan — including the reviewers' identities and contract values — the reviews' findings on "weaknesses that had contributed to previous adverse financial forecasts", and the consolidated implementation plan itself.

[FOI] **The NHS England escalation.** *Request from Medway NHS FT and NHS England (South East):* the correspondence placing the trust on fortnightly RTT performance monitoring, the notes of those fortnightly meetings, the System Turnaround Director's terms of reference and remit, and the recovery plan.

[FOI] **The system draft plan.** *Request from NHS Kent and Medway ICB:* the draft system plan showing a £334.1m underlying recurrent deficit and the provider-by-provider table of underlying deficits as a percentage of turnover, plus the £114.9m benchmarking opportunity analysis for Medway. *Why it matters:* the provider table ranks every trust in the system by underlying deficit.

[FOI] **The coroner and PSIR backlog.** *Request from Medway NHS FT:* the number of open coroner cases and patient safety incident investigations past their target completion date, by age, and the number of outstanding duty of candour notifications, at the most recent month end.

[FOI] **The nil by mouth incident.** *Request from Medway NHS FT:* the Prevention of Future Deaths report(s) received in the last two years, the trust's responses, and the investigation into the case in which a patient who was nil by mouth was given water.
