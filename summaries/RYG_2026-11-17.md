[WORTH WATCHING] A technical accounting adjustment (de-recognising a previously recognised liability) swung the trust's reported position from a roughly £3m surplus to a roughly £3m deficit.
[Evidence] "an accounting adjustment had been required following re-assessment of the technical grounds to de-recognise a previously recognised liability. [WORTH WATCHING] The trust expects to exit the year with an underlying recurrent deficit of about £5m, with the Cost Reduction Programme now forecasting slippage and rising reliance on non-recurrent savings.

[Evidence] "reports had now been published for Community Mental Health Services, Crisis Services and Older Adult Wards, with each service receiving a rating of Requires Improvement... the Trust's Well-Led inspection report had recently been published, with the Trust receiving an overall rating of Good" — Item 2.5, CQC Regulatory Report
