# Cornwall Partnership NHS Foundation Trust (RJ8) — Board of Directors (public), 9 July 2026

Mental health + community trust. Single combined pack (~19.4MB). Analysis applies HSJ editorial context. Page refs are pack-page numbers.

## 1. Top lines

**LEAD — Underlying £27m recurrent deficit sits behind a "breakeven" plan built on £22m of non-recurrent income and £18.8m of unconfirmed income; NHSE accepted the 2026/27 plan only "with conditions."**
> "The underlying financial position remains unchanged from plan, with a recurrent deficit of £27m." — p.110, [IPR Finance Executive Summary, Item 10]
> "The Trust's 2026-27 financial plan has been accepted by NHSE with conditions and targets delivery of a breakeven position. This is underpinned by a multi-year contract agreed with NHS Cornwall ICB; however, the agreement includes £22m of non-recurrent income, with a planned resolution via the national Deconstructing the Block exercise." — p.110, [IPR, Item 10]
> "Income risk: Approximately £19m included in the forecast position is not yet confirmed." — p.110, [IPR, Item 10]; "Assumes receipt of £18.8m income not yet confirmed." — p.220, [Finance Report M2, Item 11]

**LEAD — Recurrent cost-improvement delivery has effectively not started: just £36k of recurrent savings achieved against a £1.567m plan; all savings delivered to date are non-recurrent.**
> "Recurrent savings achieved are £36k against the plan of £1,567k - £1.5m deficit… Non-recurrent savings achieved are £3.5m against a plan of £1.9m." — p.227, [Finance Report M2, Item 11]
> "Notably, efficiencies delivered to date are entirely non-recurrent, increasing delivery risk for future periods." — p.110, [IPR, Item 10]

**LEAD — External auditors flagged TWO "significant weaknesses" — financial sustainability, and value for money — in the 2025/26 annual audit, and warned on reliance on one-off savings.**
> "The External Auditors noted that they had identified one significant weakness in relation to financial sustainability and one significant weakness in relation to improving economy, efficiency and effectiveness. As such they have raised two key recommendations… it noted the significant reliance on one-off and non-recurrent savings that put at risk the Trust's ability to sustain this position." — p.48, [Board Committee Summary Reports — Audit & Risk 19 June 2026, Item 8]

> "Cornwall Partnership NHS Foundation Trust are a regional outlier." — p.64, [Board Committee Summary Reports — People & Culture 24 June 2026, Item 8]

**LEAD — NOF ranking jumped 22 places in a year — from 49th/61 (segment 4) in Q1 2025/26 to 27th (segment 2) in Q4 — driven largely by two mental-health metrics; CEO Debbie Richards personally invited onto NHSE's new CEO Advisory Group.**

## 2. FOI leads

- **NHSE plan-acceptance letter and its conditions.** The pack states NHSE "confirmed approval of the Trust's plan, subject to conditions, in a letter attached as Appendix 1" and lists three conditions (savings de-risking; net-risk reduction with monthly milestones; workforce plan realignment). *Request from CFT (and NHSE South West):* the full NHSE plan-acceptance letter for 2026/27 and all monthly de-risking milestone updates provided to NHSE. Why: reveals the extent of regulator concern about a trust whose "breakeven" rests on £18.8m unconfirmed income. — p.237, [Finance Report M2, Item 11]

- **CQC "Must Do's and Should Do's" action plan.** The Estate BAF entry records a "Care Quality Commission action plan related to Must Do's and Should Do's signed off by Board" plus "Care Quality Commission relationship meetings," and states estate-related regulatory actions "remain outstanding with no identified capital on how these can be addressed." A CQC senior specialist was also observing board meetings. *Request from CFT:* the current CQC action plan (Must/Should Do items), the underlying inspection report, and minutes/notes of CQC relationship meetings. Why: identifies unresolved regulatory requirements the trust says it cannot fund. — p.271 area, [BAF Estate risk, Item 15]; p.199, [Minutes 14 May 2026]

- **"Deconstructing the Block" exercise / ICB contract.** £22m of non-recurrent income embedded in the multi-year ICB contract is to be "resolved" via a national exercise. *Request from CFT / NHS Cornwall & IoS ICB:* the multi-year contract, and correspondence on how the £22m non-recurrent element is to be made recurrent. Why: goes to whether the "breakeven" is sustainable. — p.219, [Finance Report M2, Item 11]

- **External auditors' two "significant weakness" recommendations.** *Request from CFT:* the External Auditor's Annual Report 2025/26 and the two key recommendations in full. Why: the pack summarises but does not quote them. — p.48, [Audit & Risk 19 June 2026, Item 8]

- **eRoster "limited assurance" internal audit report.** *Request from CFT:* the final Internal Audit report "eRoster System – Overtime, Net Hour Balances and Roster Finalisation" and management response. Why: overtime/roster-finalisation controls at a trust already reliant on premium locums. — p.46, [Audit & Risk, Item 8]

- **Talking Therapies re-tender.** ICB has notified CFT that Cornwall & IoS NHS Talking Therapies "may be subject to a re-tender process." *Request from NHS Cornwall & IoS ICB:* the procurement notice, timetable and incumbent-performance basis for re-tendering. Why: a live contest for an existing NHS-delivered service. — p.34, [Deputy CEO Report, Item 7]
