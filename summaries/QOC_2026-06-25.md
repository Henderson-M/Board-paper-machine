# Shropshire, Telford & Wrekin ICB board (in common with Staffordshire & Stoke-on-Trent ICB) — 25 June 2026

- **Source:** NHS STW/SSOT Integrated Care Board public agenda papers, meeting 25 June 2026 (published 18 June 2026)
- **Pack:** 1 combined pack file (QOC_2026-06-25.txt), ~10,480 lines
- **Analysed:** 2026-06-24

Note: this is a **meeting in common** of two ICBs — NHS Shropshire, Telford & Wrekin and NHS Staffordshire & Stoke-on-Trent — operating as a "cluster" under a single shared executive team. Most papers cover both ICBs/systems. Page references use the paper's own internal page markers where shown; cite by enclosure/paper title.

## Top lines

[LEAD] The cluster is heading for a full merger of the two ICBs by April 2027 and is seeking board sign-off to delegate its NHSE response to the Transition Committee.
[Evidence] "The Board are asked to confirm their outline support... for the direction of travel towards a full merger on the current Cluster footprint by April 2027." Also: "In order to meet the submission deadline for the cluster ICBs response to NHS England by 14th July 2026, the Boards are asked to approve delegation of the submission sign off to the Joint Transition Committee." — Chief Executive Report (Enc.05), s1.2 ICB merger, internal p.5

[LEAD] Transition Committee escalated a roughly 50% ICB workforce reduction as an active organisational-resilience risk, with continuing exits hitting delivery and staff wellbeing.
[Evidence] "Workforce capacity and organisational resilience risk due to scale and pace of change, with ~50% workforce reduction and ongoing exits impacting delivery and staff wellbeing." — Triple A Highlight Report, Transition Committee in Common (Enc.), meeting 22 April 2026, ALERT 1

[LEAD] The cluster is being reshaped to fit a 2026/27 running-cost budget of £19.55 per head of weighted population, with redundancy costs approved and double-running costs funded from the 2026/27 plan.
[Evidence] "...to enable the STW/SSOT cluster to function with an establishment within the 2026/27 running cost budget of £19.55 per head of weighted population. The Remuneration Committees approved the overall expected redundancy costs as modelled, recognising that funding for double running costs is secured within the 2026/27 financial plan." — Triple A Highlight Report, Remuneration Committee in Common, meeting 16 March 2026, ASSURE

[LEAD] Four ICB executives are leaving their roles at the end of June, alongside the substantive appointment of Vanessa Whatley as Cluster Chief Nursing Officer.
[Evidence] "We would like to place on record our appreciation and thanks to Paul Edmondson-Jones, Heather Johnstone, Chris Ibell and Tracey Shewan for the significant contribution each has made during their time in post." And: "I am pleased to confirm the appointment of Vanessa Whatley as Chief Nursing Officer following a robust external recruitment process." — Chief Executive Report (Enc.05), s1.3, internal p.5–6

[LEAD] The STW system's reported 2025/26 surplus rests on £92m of NHSE deficit support funding, including a late £8.2m top-up booked in month 12.
[Evidence] "The system reported a £10.6m full year surplus against a breakeven plan... largely due to additional £8.2m deficit support funding earned and received in month 12. The total deficit support funding received in 2025/26 was £92.0m." — Triple A Highlight Report, Finance Committee in Common Part 2 (System), meeting 28 April 2026

[WORTH WATCHING] SSOT system's 2025/26 efficiency programme finished £26.3m behind plan, almost entirely driven by UHNM.
[Evidence] "The reported system efficiency delivery YTD is £26.3m behind the submitted plan of £306.3m, this comprises of MPFT £0.3m and UHNM £26.7m. As a system this equates to 91% delivery YTD." — Triple A Highlight Report, Finance Committee in Common Part 2 (System), meeting 28 April 2026

[WORTH WATCHING] STW ICB continuing-healthcare spend ran £7.9m over budget in 2025/26 and the committee flagged a significant risk of further adverse spend in 2026/27.
[Evidence] "STW ICB 2025/26 had an end of year adverse variance to budget of £7.9m due to an increase in cost of commissioning care for residents eligible for NHS Continuing Healthcare... The committee noted that there is a significant risk of an adverse spend to budget for 2026/27 if no action is taken." — Triple A Highlight Report, Finance Committee in Common Part 1 (ICB), meeting 26 May 2026

[WORTH WATCHING] Quality committee logged diabetes (STW) as its only "extreme" risk and named an ophthalmology backlog at UHDB causing prolonged waits for Staffordshire residents, with harm currently unquantified.
[Evidence] "The Diabetes risk (STW) remains the only extreme risk for quality, safety and performance." And: "An Ophthalmology waiting list backlog at UHDB has been noted to be causing prolonged waits affecting Staffordshire residents. Harm is currently unqualified due to limited data due to associate commissioning arrangements." — Quality and Performance Committees in Common Highlight Report, meeting 27 May 2026, ALERT

[WORTH WATCHING] Rolling out Mounjaro (tirzepatide) prescribing in general practice could cost £6m–£7m a year for cohorts 1 and 2 alone, exceeding NHSE implementation funding and now on the risk register.
[Evidence] "If the full demand for cohort 1 and 2 were met, then that would cost between £6m and £7m per year and this cost would increase as further cohorts join over the years. The cost is on the risk register, as initial implementation funding provided by NHSE, does not meet the full cost." — SSOT Health and Care Senate Highlight Report, meeting 14 May 2026 (IMOG approvals, item b)

[WORTH WATCHING] A newly formed "Shropshire, Telford and Wrekin Community and Hospitals NHS Group" (SaTH/ShropCom) is now operating, with the place partnership chair calling for ShIPP's role to be reset.
[Evidence] "an overview was given of the newly formed Shropshire, Telford and Wrekin Community and Hospitals NHS Group and what it is intended to achieve. The Chair noted the need to reset ShIPP's role, membership and purpose, with a focus on becoming a decision-making forum." — ShIPP Joint Committee Highlight Report, meeting 16 April 2026, ADVISE

[FOI] Request the Sir Glen Burley / Sir Jim Mackey NHSE merger and strategic-commissioning correspondence referenced and partly reproduced in the pack, plus the cluster's draft 14 July submission.
[Evidence] "a letter from Sir Glen Burley, Deputy CEO of NHS England, is attached ahead of a decision in September on final ICB boundaries" (CEO Report Enc.05, s1.2) and "Sir Jim Mackey's letter (1 April 2026) requests each Integrated Care Board (ICB) to submit a single, system-aligned narrative..." (Strategic Transformation & Commissioning Committee Highlight Report, meeting 13 May 2026, ADVISE). Request from NHSE / the ICB: the full Mackey letter, the cluster's joint response and "clear set of national asks", and the draft merger submission whose sign-off is being delegated to the Transition Committee.

[FOI] Request the modelled redundancy-cost business case and voluntary-redundancy scheme detail behind the ~50% workforce reduction.
[Evidence] "The paper presented Committee members with information around the number of voluntary redundancy applications received and costs..." (Remuneration Committee in Common, meeting 1 April 2026) and the redundancy-cost modelling approved on 16 March 2026 to hit £19.55/head. Request from the ICB: the business case "Concluding elements of the formation of SSOT & STW Cluster 2025/26–2026/27", total WTE before/after, modelled redundancy costs, and double-running cost figure.
