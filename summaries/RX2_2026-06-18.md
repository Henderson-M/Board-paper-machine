## Top lines

[LEAD] Staff survey scores fell across almost every People Promise domain in 2025 and sit below the national average on all but flexible working.
[Evidence] "A deterioration across most People Promise domains"; engagement 6.49 (2024: 6.69) vs national 7.02; morale 5.71 vs national 6.12; "We each have a voice" 6.47 vs national 6.89 — p. [NHS Staff Survey Results 2025], lines 8589–8603

[LEAD] On benchmarked engagement Sussex Partnership is the lowest of the six South East mental health trusts the trust lists, despite its highest-ever response rate.
[Evidence] Staff Engagement: SPFT 6.49 vs Surrey & Borders 7.05, Berkshire 7.48, Oxford 7.21, Hampshire & IOW 6.87, Kent & Medway 6.65; response rate 58% (highest ever, up from 45%) — p. [NHS Staff Survey Results 2025], lines 8665–8708, 8577

[LEAD] Trust hit a £23k surplus / breakeven for 2025/26 but a third of its £33.9m savings were one-off, leaving the recurrent gap unresolved into 2026/27.
[Evidence] "The Trust has delivered the break-even plan for 2025-26, with a small surplus of £23k... £11.2m of the £33.87m CIP delivered is non-recurrent" — p.29 [IQPR M12 / Finance, Be Sustainable], lines 2956, 2968–2969

[LEAD] Temporary staffing ran £5.7m over cap for the year and the 2026/27 plan demands further cuts of 10% on bank and 30% on agency.
[Evidence] "Temporary staffing spend was over cap for the year by £5.7m... Bank will continue to be a pressure in the 2026-27 plan with further reductions of 10% required and 30% on agency respectively" — p.29 [IQPR M12 / Finance], lines 2960–2961

[LEAD] Trust unit costs are 19% above the national average, with IAPT (Talking Therapies) running at 150% of comparators.
[Evidence] "The recently published 2024-25 National Cost Collection Index (NCCI) for SPFT is 119... unit costs 19% higher than the national average... an index of 94 for Inpatient Services, 142 for Community... and 150 for IAPT" — p.29 [IQPR M12 / NOF Relative Difference in Costs], lines 2970–2972

[WORTH WATCHING] Trust ranks near the bottom nationally on several oversight-framework metrics: CYP access 66/70, over-60-day acute inpatient length of stay 45/47, relative cost 56/61.
[Evidence] "CYP Access -20.5%... 66 out of 70"; ">60-day Acute inpatient LOS 33.7%... 45 out of 47" (national mean 23.2%); "Relative difference in costs 118.86%... 56 out of 61" — p. [IQPR M12 exec summary NOF table], lines 1931–1972

[WORTH WATCHING] Mental health 111 crisis line left 54% of calls unanswered in January 2026, flagged as "a significant concern."
[Evidence] "NHS 111 select for MH / SMHL capacity to answer calls in a timely manner remain a significant concern (54% calls unanswered in January 2026)" — p.32 [IQPR M12, UEC Transformation], lines 3072–3074

[WORTH WATCHING] Six of ten corporate change programmes are rated "at risk" at year-end, with reporting on several metrics suspended after a February 2026 EPR go-live.
[Evidence] "four are on track... and six are at risk (Community Transformation; CAMHS Transformation, Inpatient Transformation, Combe Valley, Quality Priorities and Digital Transformation)"; "Due to the introduction of a new EPR in February 2026, reporting for some metrics is not yet available for M12" — p.30 [IQPR M12 exec summary], lines 1976–1978, 1857–1858

[WORTH WATCHING] Trust commissioned external consultancy Moorhouse on inpatient flow/length-of-stay improvement through 2025/26; £10m secured for 2026/27 waiting-time and flow investment.
[Evidence] "The Trust has worked with external consultancy Moorhouse through 2025-6 to develop and implement improvement plans aimed at reducing inpatient length of stay and waiting times"; "Since Month 12, £10m of investment has been secured for 2026/27 to support improvements in waiting times and system flow" — p. [IQPR M12 exec summary], lines 1886–1887, 1880–1881

## FOI leads

[FOI] Chief People Officer's private leadership-retreat company and SPFT liability waiver. The CPO, Funmi Onamusi — the executive accountable for the staff survey — declares she directs Breaking Hundred Circle CIC, a paid leadership retreat business for minority-ethnic women that SPFT staff attend, with the trust agreeing a liability-waiver clause to manage perceived conflict. Request from SPFT: any correspondence/agreement between the trust and the CPO on this arrangement, the conflict-of-interest assessment, and whether any trust funds or work time have supported staff attendance. *Why it matters:* potential governance/conflict issue at the top of the People function during a year of below-average staff survey results.
[Evidence] "I am the Director of Breaking Hundred Circle CIC... Following conversation with the CEO, we agreed I would share the current terms & conditions... Participants expressly release and hold harmless Funmi Onamusi, BHC, and SPFT from any liability, perceived conflict of interest, or professional disadvantage" — p. [Declarations of Interest Register], lines 354–378

[FOI] Moorhouse consultancy contract. Request contract value, term and deliverables for the Moorhouse engagement on inpatient flow/length of stay. *Why it matters:* named external firm on a core operational problem; cost not disclosed in pack.
[Evidence] "worked with external consultancy Moorhouse through 2025-6" — p. [IQPR M12], line 1886

[FOI] EY (Ernst & Young) People Services digitalisation contract. Request value/term/scope of the implementation contract awarded to EY. *Why it matters:* named firm; contract awarded and "fully onboarded" but value undisclosed.
[Evidence] "Successfully completed the procurement process, awarding the implementation contract to Ernst & Young (EY)... EY have been fully onboarded" — p.43 [IQPR M12, People & Culture Programme], lines 3881–3883

[FOI] Sussex Experience (patient) survey collapse. Response rate fell to 0.4% at year-end, undermining the trust's ability to monitor patient experience. Request the underlying data-quality remediation plan and EPR-linked recording failures. *Why it matters:* the trust itself says the data is unreliable for performance monitoring.
[Evidence] "The Sussex Experience Survey response rate remained very low at year end (0.4%)... This under-recording impacts the data's reliability and the Trust's ability to monitor performance" — p. [IQPR M12], lines 2930–2935
