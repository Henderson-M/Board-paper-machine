> Note: this is a joint "Boards in Common" meeting of Shropshire Community Health NHS Trust (SCHT) and The Shrewsbury and Telford Hospital NHS Trust (SaTH), run as an NHS Group under Group Chair Andrew Morgan and Group CEO Jo Williams. Most substantive material is SaTH's. Page refs are to the main papers PDF ("Board in common meeting papers - July 2026") unless stated.

## Top lines

[LEAD] SaTH flags potential need for up to £14.5m of extra cash support in Q3/Q4 as it runs a £30.49m pre-support deficit plan for the year.
[Evidence] "A number of scenarios have been developed for the Trust's cash flow for the remainder of the financial year indicating cash flow pressures in Qs 3 and 4, when support may well be required of up to £14.5m as a worst case." — p.55, [SaTH Finance Assurance Committee Key Issues Report, meeting 30/06/26]

[LEAD] SaTH already £1.8m adverse to plan two months in, with M2 deficit of £7.4m against a £5.6m plan, driven by industrial action and efficiency slippage.
[Evidence] "Month 2 showed a cumulative deficit of £7.4m against a plan of £5.6m, a deficit plan of £1.8m mainly as a result of Industrial Action and slippage on efficiency of £1m. The deficit plan for the year is £30.49m." — p.55, [SaTH Finance Assurance Committee Key Issues Report]

[LEAD] SaTH's £40m efficiency target is risk-rated at only £30m deliverable, and workforce cost savings were not achieved in month 2, leaving pay nearly £6m adverse to plan.
[Evidence] "Efficiency savings have £4.1m have so far been delivered, which is £1.8m below plan although the £40m is still expected to be delivered by year end albeit the risk rated calculation currently places this at £30m... While noting that workforce cost savings hadn't been achieved in M2, pay expenditure... is a deficit to plan by nearly £6m and splits by division." — p.55, [SaTH Finance Assurance Committee Key Issues Report]

[LEAD] SaTH is treating uncontracted activity overperformance as a live financial risk and putting "triggers" in place to pare back activity.
[Evidence] "Discussion around overperformance in activity levels highlighted the potential risk to its recovery where no contract had been agreed with commissioners including the danger of activity being performed at premium cost (excess activity and cost without the income)." Committee "was clear that a series of 'early warnings or triggers' were in place to drive quick decisions around confirmation of income, deferral of investments, the paring back of activity and the realisation of 'group' benefits." — p.55–56, [SaTH Finance Assurance Committee Key Issues Report]

[LEAD] An independent external review of SaTH's delivery of all 210 Ockenden maternity recommendations is due "later this summer"; the trust reports 196 of 210 (93%) evidenced and assured, with 8 actions now "not yet delivered" (up 2).
[Evidence] "an independent external review has been commissioned to validate our progress and confirm that all recommendations have been fully delivered... involving more than 700 individual actions and over 1,200 pieces of supporting evidence. The report is due later this summer." — p.31 [Group Chief Executive's Report] and p.210 [SaTH Integrated Maternity & Neonatal Report]; delivery table "Evidenced and Assured 196 (↓1) 93%... Not Yet Delivered 8 (↑2) 4%" — p.210

[WORTH WATCHING] SaTH reported a Never Event in ophthalmology in May 2026 and is running a combined patient safety investigation into two never events.
[Evidence] "Never Event reported in Ophthalmology May 2026. Ophthalmology Transformation Programme will include recommendations from the investigation. Plans are in place to incorporate recommendations from a combined PSI investigation into two never events" — p.100, [SaTH Integrated Performance Report]

[WORTH WATCHING] SCHT's own committee flags its NHS Staff Survey culture sub-score as below national average and its NHS Oversight Framework segment deteriorated from 1 to 2 in Q4.
[Evidence] "The Integrated Quality report demonstrated annual staff survey sub-score results as being below national average with recommendation for full action plan triangulating with Freedom to Speak Up returns" — p.48, [SCHT Q&S Committee Chair's Assurance Report]; "the Trust had received a NOF score of 2 in quarter 4, a deterioration from previous score of 1" — p.52, [SCHT Resource and Performance Committee Chair's Assurance Report]

[WORTH WATCHING] SaTH's SHMI mortality data is being reported with an explicit health warning over data-warehouse accuracy problems.
[Evidence] "Mortality rates (SHMI) remain below 100, but there is ongoing concern about the accuracy of data due to issues with the data warehouse" — p.100, [SaTH Integrated Performance Report]

[WORTH WATCHING] Group CEO Jo Williams was invited to 10 Downing Street on 9 June to meet the PM and Health Secretary over SaTH's elective waiting-time reductions; the trust reports zero English RTT waits over 52 weeks.
[Evidence] "On 9 June, I had the privilege of representing the Trust at 10 Downing Street, where I was invited to meet with the Prime Minister and the Secretary of State for Health and Social Care. The invitation recognised the significant contribution our Trust has made in reducing elective waiting times" — p.33, [Group Chief Executive's Report]; "English is 0 x 104 weeks, 0 x 78 weeks, 0 x 65 weeks, 0 x 52 week (adult)" — p.96, [SaTH IPR]

[WORTH WATCHING] SCHT logged 15 staff assaults and two mortuary-security breaches involving unauthorised access by funeral directors, plus an independent ligature audit finding environmental risks.
[Evidence] "15 staff assaults were reported during the period, and a deeper review has been commissioned... Mortuary security audits also identified two breaches relating to unauthorised access arrangements involving funeral directors, which were addressed immediately." — p.42, [SCHT Q&S Committee Chair's Assurance Report]
