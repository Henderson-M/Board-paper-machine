# Bristol, North Somerset and South Gloucestershire ICB and Gloucestershire ICB joint cluster board, 30 September 2026

- **Source:** https://bnssghealthiertogether.org.uk/events/ (meeting page: https://bnssghealthiertogether.org.uk/events/joint-cluster-integrated-care-board-icb-board-meeting-30-september-2026/)
- **Pack:** 13 files (11 supplied by the scan, plus 2 further papers found on the meeting page and read: draft minutes of 29 July 2026 and the BNSSG and Gloucestershire Winter Plan 2026)
- **Analysed:** 2026-09-28T21:45:00+01:00

Note: this is the joint cluster board of the two sovereign ICBs (BNSSG, ODS QUY, and Gloucestershire, ODS QR1), chaired by cluster chair Jeff Farrar with Shane Devlin as chief executive of both. Papers cover both ICBs; each top line says which ICB it refers to. A closed session (9.30am, "Motion to Exclude Press and Public") precedes the open board at 11.15am; no papers for the closed session are published.

## Top lines

[LEAD] The cluster is planning the BNSSG-Gloucestershire ICB merger for April 2027 but its Joint Transition Committee has ordered contingency work on a "prolonged transition period" because of "continued uncertainty regarding future organisational arrangements" and the risk of "further delay to the merger".
[Evidence] "six workstreams and an oversight group have now been established to support merger planning, with detailed deliverables and milestones being developed against the working assumption of implementation from April 2027." ... "Members explored the potential consequences of any further delay to the merger, including the operational and financial implications of maintaining separate organisational infrastructures, governance arrangements and corporate systems for an extended period. ... members concluded that further contingency planning is required to understand the implications of a prolonged transition period and commissioned additional work in this area." (p.3, [5 Chief Executive Report]). The four areas flagged for "continued Board attention" open with "the impact of national policy decisions on merger timescales" (p.5, same paper). Draft July minutes record: "NHS England (NHSE) would be advised that Gloucestershire and BNSSG would merge into one organisation as from April 2027." (p.4, [3 DRAFT Open Joint Cluster Board Minutes 29 July 2026]). [INFERENCE] The "national policy decisions" and "external policy environment" language points to a pending national position on ICB merger timetables; the paper does not say what decision is awaited.

[LEAD] Both ICBs still forecast breakeven at month 5 but the pack shows a £7.7m forecast prescribing overspend across the cluster driven by generic drug price concessions, a £4.6m ADHD overspend at Gloucestershire, a £6.4m all-age continuing care variance at BNSSG, and downside scenarios of a £12.3m deficit (Gloucestershire) and £7.5m deficit (BNSSG).
[Evidence] Gloucestershire: "YTD spend on ADHD is significantly over budget (£1.0m) with our forecast outturn (FOT) position having worsened in month 5 to £4.6m." ... "The Primary Care Prescribing budget is reflecting a forecast overspend of £5m driven primarily by exceptionally high generic drug price concessions. Between April and August, an average of 234 concessions per month generated a cumulative £2.5m pressure - around £500k per month - with further risk as demand for high-volume winter medicines rises." ... "The scenarios range from a deficit of £12.3m to a surplus of £4.3m. Our base case position is one of a small surplus." ... "Mitigations to date are primarily linked to reserves slippage" (cover p.2, [6 Finance Update, Month 5 (August) Cluster Finance Report]). BNSSG: "FoT variance is £6.4m (July - £6.1m)" on AACC; "The scenarios range from a deficit of £7.5m (M4: £7.8m) to a surplus of £8.5m (M4: £8.2m) with our base case shows a small surplus of £2.1m (M4: £2.6m)." (cover p.2, same paper). Summary table: Prescribing full-year forecast variance cluster "(£7.7)", BNSSG "(£3.1)", Gloucestershire "(£4.6)"; Continuing Care cluster "(£6.1)"; Mental Health cluster "(£5.2)"; Reserves/Contingencies cluster forecast underspend "£19.9" (slide "Summary financial position (£m)", same paper). BNSSG risk page: "ADHD / Autism – an extrapolation of current run rate would be a £4.5m overspend, around £0.8m has been recognised in the FOT." (slide "Risks & mitigations", BNSSG, same paper). [INFERENCE] The breakeven forecasts rest on reserves slippage and unphased running-cost savings; the paper says so for Gloucestershire ("Mitigations ... primarily linked to reserves slippage") and flags for BNSSG that "Running cost YTD is above plan".

[Evidence] BAS 2.2 (eliminate corridor care): "No. Corridor care remains a risk going into winter 2026. GIRFT corridor-care recommendations are being implemented within our acute partners ...
