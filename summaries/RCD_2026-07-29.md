[Evidence] "our turnaround programme is currently focusing on a recruitment pause alongside variable pay controls... use of temporary staff has reduced as a result of our controls by around 20%. [LEAD] Hazard Group 3 microbiology has been outsourced to a private London laboratory since June 2024, with lost samples, testing delays and patient-safety concerns flagged.

"Routine delays of one day compared to in-house testing, with an additional four-day delay for Friday samples" — supp p.10, [CRR #264, risk score 15]
