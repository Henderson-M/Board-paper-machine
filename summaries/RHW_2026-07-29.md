## Top lines

[LEAD] Board approves a 15-year Estates Masterplan built around the New Hospital Programme slipping to a decade-plus away, with trust-wide backlog maintenance now ~£175m.
[Evidence] "With the current timeline for a new Royal Berkshire Hospital 10 to 15 years away, we are progressing a comprehensive estate masterplanning programme… Backlog maintenance works across the Trust currently stand at £175 million (June 2026 works cost), with high and significant critical infrastructure risk elements accounting for a substantial portion of this. Full costs including design, decant and enabling works are considerably higher." — p.103, [Estates Masterplan 2026-2040, Item 9]

[LEAD] Trust warns that without mitigation its cash forecast turns negative in Q3, with sufficient cash only guaranteed to December 2026.
[Evidence] "Without mitigation, the forecast deteriorates to a negative cash position in Q3." / "Sufficient cash is expected to be available to the Trust until December 2026, assuming mitigations on spend. Financial Improvement Programme (FIP) delivery remains the critical lever." Risks: "FIP non-delivery — direct cash shortage risk with limited Q3/Q4 headroom." — p.70, [Integrated Performance Report — Cash performance, Item 7]

[LEAD] Savings running £14.1m short of target on current run-rate; underlying monthly deficit £4.15m once support funding and non-recurrent savings stripped out.
[Evidence] "At the current run rate of £2.77m per month, year-end delivery would reach approximately £29.6m, a £14.1m shortfall; monthly delivery needs to rise by around 47% from July. 45.3% of savings delivered so far are recurrent, against a 70% target." / "the underlying deficit for June is £4.15m. Closing this gap is the central purpose of the Financial Improvement Programme." — p.56, [Chief Executive's Report, Item 6]

[LEAD] Emergency department attendances up 17% year-on-year — a spike the trust says was not seen elsewhere in the South East and is hitting its finances.
[Evidence] "Emergency Department attendances had increased by 17% in comparison with the previous year. This level of increase was not seen by other trusts in the South East and this level of activity impacted significantly on the Trust's financial performance." — p.29, [Finance & Investment Committee minutes 20 May 2026, min 67/26]

[LEAD] Thematic review of all 10 Never Events over two years concludes the trust's safety barriers have not consistently worked.
[Evidence] "Whilst essential safety processes are in place in the Trust, they have not consistently functioned as effective barriers to patient harm from Never Events. The plan was for improvement focus to move to system redesign and standardisation across the Trust rather than individual actions." — p.44, [Quality Committee minutes 1 July 2026, key messages to Board; review at min 49/26 p.42]

[WORTH WATCHING] Perinatal mortality rate more than doubled year-on-year and sits above target, flagged as a national alerting metric, with a national audit body writing to the trust over poor haemorrhage data.

[WORTH WATCHING] Violence and aggression against staff rising; CEO commissions a Staff Safety Strategy — trust confirms it is pursuing prosecutions and was featured by HSJ on 23 July.

"The Health Service Journal published an article on 23 July 2026 highlighting the ways we are continuing to strengthen our response to abuse of staff… We are continuing to pursue a number of prosecutions and plan to have a senior presence from the Trust in court where cases come to trial." — p.54, [CEO Report]

## FOI leads

- **CQC Adult Inpatient Survey 2025 results.** Withheld under s22 (embargoed to Aug 2026). Request the trust-level results and any accompanying board narrative from RBFT (or CQC on national publication) — the pack says the trust "had achieved improvement with further areas to focus on," but gives no figures. Why it matters: named trust patient-experience benchmarking, and the board has already seen the numbers.
- **HFMS Ltd governance review.** An external governance review of the trust's subsidiary HFMS Ltd was received, with actions due for completion by September 2026 and a board discussion on the subsidiary's "future use." Request the review report, its recommendations and the board paper on HFMS Ltd's future. From RBFT. (min 59/26, p.17)
- **Final ISA 260 (auditor's report to those charged with governance).** Noted as completed with updates "included [s22 FOI Act]." Request the full ISA 260 / external audit report 2025/26 and the recommendations arising. From RBFT. (min 82/26, p.21)
- **Prior-year accounting error.** Deloitte and the CFO discussed a prior-period adjustment confirmed as "an error by the finance team… in relation to the level of materiality," to be discussed with NHSE. Request the correspondence with NHSE and papers describing the error and restatement. From RBFT. (min 56/26 May, p.16)
- **Counter-fraud / Worldpay scam.** A "fraudulent transaction scam involving Worldpay (UK) Ltd" was shared with the finance team; a local proactive counter-fraud exercise was delayed. Request the counter-fraud referral detail and the local proactive exercise findings. From RBFT. (min 53/26, p.15)
- **Significant contracts and single-tender waivers.** Multiple significant contracts named (Hydro-X £4.24m; Trustmarque/Ultima £1.05m; Trustmarque Business Solutions £1.17m; Molnlycke £977k) plus 12-13 single-tender-waiver awards per meeting, with committee concern that large-value STWs go to "a small number of contractors for estates work." Request the STW register, values and justifications. From RBFT. (min 63/26, 64/26, 88/26, 89/26)
