Assurance Level: Limited Assurance" – p.267, [Board Committees - Recent Meetings, Item 6.5]

[LEAD] Board is asked to approve a 12 to 18 month cultural programme responding to an NHS England-commissioned review of its own senior leadership and board culture, carried out by Workforce Challenge Partners, with 31 actions and an external expert panel overseeing sign-off.
[Evidence] "Workforce Challenge Partners® (WCP) were commissioned by the regional NHSE North-West team to undertake a comprehensive review of senior leadership and board culture at the trust collaborating closely with the Board. Governance: "the programme of delivery will be overseen for an initial 12 – 18 month period by a newly established Task and Finish Group reporting to the board, chaired by the Chair of the Board of Directors and will also be supported by an external Expert Advisory Panel" – p.200. [LEAD] A coroner has issued a Regulation 28 report to the Secretary of State over ADHD service demand and staffing after the death of a person who had waited nearly three years for a diagnostic assessment.
[Evidence] "following an inquest into the death of a service user who sadly took their own life using a ligature, Coroner issued a Regulation 28 to the Secretary of State regarding the demand, pressures and staffing for attention deficit hyperactivity disorder (ADHD) services. The service user was not under any Trust services. However, had been on a waiting list for an ADHD diagnostic assessment for nearly three years. The Secretary of State is required to respond to the coroner within 56 days and the Trust will receive a copy of the response" – p.26, [Safety Report, Item 2.1a]

[LEAD] Board is being asked to self-assess as Green in five of the six provider capability domains, months after its capability rating was upgraded on the strength of board leadership changes.
[Evidence] Proposed ratings: "Strategy and leadership Green / Quality of care Green / People and Culture Amber/ Green / Access and delivery of services Green / Productivity and value for money Green / Financial performance and oversight Green" – p.228, [Provider Capability Self-Assessment Submission, Item 6.1]. Submission due to NHS England North West by 2 October, signed off under delegated authority by the chair and chief executive (p.226).
