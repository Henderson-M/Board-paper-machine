## Top lines

[LEAD] Trust forecasts it will need £10m of PDC deficit support from December 2026 to maintain liquidity and meet statutory obligations.
[Evidence] "the Trust will require revenue. PDC support of £10.0m from December 2026 to maintain liquidity and meet statutory obligations" — p.88 (PDF p.88), [Financial Report April 2026, §4.2 CIP]; also flagged in exec summary "the Trust anticipates the need for £10m of deficit support from December 2026" — p.84 (PDF p.84)

[LEAD] Underlying deficit has worsened month-on-month to £3.5m in April, despite a "breakeven" 2026/27 plan, with one-off supports from 2025/26 no longer available.
[Evidence] "The underlying position has deteriorated from March 26, moving from a deficit of £2.6m to £3.5m driven by elective under performance and increasing pay costs"; "While in 2025/26 the Trust benefited from non-recurrent support funding and other one-off gains, these are no longer available" — p.85, [Financial Report, §2.1 / Executive Summary]

[LEAD] Only 29.9% of the trust's cost improvement programme is developed and deliverable; the cash forecast is "highly sensitive to CIP delivery".
[Evidence] "Only fully developed and deliverable CIP schemes have been included in the forecast, totalling £13.5m, which represents 29.9% of the total CIP programme"; "The forecast is highly sensitive to CIP delivery; failure to deliver the fully developed schemes would result in earlier and increased reliance on revenue support" — p.88, [Financial Report, §4.2/§4.4]

[LEAD] A trustwide review of theatre ventilation has created or escalated five significant risks across all three main sites, four flagged as infection-prevention risks from ageing air handling units.
[Evidence] New risks 3307 (Treatment Centre/Nightingale, RHCH) and 3308 (Heathcote, RHCH) both scored 16; escalated risks 570 Ventilation systems Trustwide (12→16), 3059 Main theatres BNHH (12→16); "If the theatre ventilation system... does not provide the number of air change handling cycles required, then there is an infection prevention and control risk"; gaps in controls: "Age of air handling units / Ability to replace air handling units" — p.48–50, [Risk Report, May 2026]

[LEAD] Trust is making its annual licence self-certification (CoS7) on a qualified basis for the third year running, formally warning the regulator it may not be able to provide commissioner-requested services within its funding.
[Evidence] "The Company Secretary believes that the Trust is able to make this declaration in line with statement 2, bringing to our regulator's attention that the Trust continues to operate within a constrained financial environment... The Trust has made similar declarations in the past 2 years"; declaration text: "there is a risk that the Trust will struggle to meet all its responsibilities, including its responsibility to provide CRS, within the available financial envelope" — p.277, p.280, [NHS England Governance Self-Certification 2026/27]

[WORTH WATCHING] Emergency care is already off-plan in month one of 2026/27, with April four-hour performance of 64.16% against a plan trajectory of ~70% and a March 2027 target of 78%.
[Evidence] "For Month 1 (April) we have delivered a modest improvement on Month 12 to 64.16%" — p.3, [CEO Report]; performance summary shows ED Op Plan 26/27 of 70.0%, target Mar-27 78% — p.94, [Performance Report]

[WORTH WATCHING] Diagnostic (DM01) performance of 30.4% in April sits below the 33.8% operating-plan figure and far below the 20% breach target.
[Evidence] "DM01 Performance — Apr 26 — 30.4% — Op Plan 26/27 33.8% — Target Mar-27 20%" — p.94, [Performance Report]

[WORTH WATCHING] Trust is over-established by 66 WTE against plan and pay is an adverse driver once a £1m one-off junior-doctor planning benefit is stripped out.
[Evidence] "the worked WTE position coming in above plan by 66 WTE's. The underlying pay position is above plan by 64 WTE's and £0.4m"; "The favourable reported variance to plan is inflated by one off planning benefit of £1.0m, for the back dated payment of junior doctors" — p.84/87, [Financial Report]

[WORTH WATCHING] Estates and Facilities staff/services will NOT transfer to subsidiary HHCS Ltd, following union engagement and a national NHSE consultation on subsidiaries.
[Evidence] "Estates and Facilities staff were informed via a letter from the Chief People Officer that the trust will not proceed with the proposed transfer of staff and services to Hampshire Hospitals Contract Services Ltd (HHCS). ## FOI leads

[FOI] Theatre-ventilation review — request the full report. The risk register repeatedly cites a "Trustwide review of theatre ventilation" as the reason for five escalated/new significant risks but the review itself is not in the pack.
[Evidence] "The risk has been escalated following a Trustwide review of theatre ventilation" — p.48–50, [Risk Report]. Request from HHFT: the theatre ventilation review report, scope/terms, air-change-rate test results by theatre, and the remedial works programme and cost.

[FOI] ICO referrals — request count, dates and subject matter. The DSP paper confirms referrals were made but withholds number and nature.
[Evidence] "In the last twelve months, the Trust made a small number of referrals to the Information Commissioner's Office in line with statutory requirements" — p.214, [Data Security and Protection report]. Request from HHFT: number of ICO referrals/breach notifications in the last 12 months, dates, and whether any related to patient or staff data.

[FOI] £10m PDC deficit-support requirement — request the supporting correspondence/modelling. The need is stated but the underlying liquidity forecast and any system/NHSE correspondence are not disclosed.
[Evidence] "PDC support of £10.0m from December 2026 to maintain liquidity and meet statutory obligations" — p.88, [Financial Report]. Request from HHFT/HIOW ICB: the cash/liquidity forecast, PDC bid documentation, and correspondence with NHSE on distressed/deficit funding.
