# Nottingham University Hospitals NHS Trust (RX1) — Trust Board (Open), 9 July 2026

**Source:** Trust Board Open Meeting pack, 9 July 2026 (635pp) — https://www.nuh.nhs.uk/download/trust-board-open-meeting-thursday-9-july-2026pdf.pdf?ver=41751
**Pack:** https://www.nuh.nhs.uk/board-papers
**Analysed:** 2026-07-06

This is the first ordinary board meeting after the 24 June 2026 publication of Donna Ockenden's Independent Maternity Review into NUH — the largest maternity review in NHS history. The pack is dominated by the review's fallout and its accompanying regulatory pressures (HTA mortuary inspection, Nottingham Inquiry, corporate context).

## Top lines

**[LEAD] Ockenden review found 444 women and 76 babies suffered potentially avoidable harm at NUH across 2012–2025 — the trust "failed those affected" and accepts responsibility.**
[Evidence] "The final report covers the review of the maternity care of more than 2,500 families between 2012 and 2025 and found that 444 women and 76 babies suffered potentially avoidable harm." / "The letter accepts that we failed those affected, and that we accept responsibility for our failings and that we did not always provide the right conditions for safe perinatal services and that had a significant impact." — p.96–97, [Publication of the Ockenden Report, NUH 6540]

**[LEAD] Ockenden set NUH 32 Local Actions for Learning plus 18 national Immediate and Essential Actions (50 actions total) — trust admits 5 of the 32 local actions are not yet mapped to any improvement pathway.**
[Evidence] "Of the 32 actions identified, 27 are mapped to a perinatal improvement pathway and 5 are not." — p.100, [Publication of the Ockenden Report, NUH 6540]. Verbatim indictment: poor governance/culture means "poor practice is not investigated; learning is not integrated; and mothers and babies are failed by an organisation they should be able to rely upon absolutely" (p.96).

**[LEAD] A new Learning and Improvement Board to oversee NUH's maternity response will be chaired by Michelle Welsh, MP for Sherwood Forest and the Government's Maternity Adviser, with a Families Board co-chaired by Donna Ockenden.**
[Evidence] "Establishment of a new Learning and Improvement Board which will be Chaired by Michelle Welsh, MP for Sherwood Forest and the Governments Maternity Adviser. This will provide independent check and challenge on the delivery of improvements... one representing families, co-chaired by Donna Ockenden and a family member, and one representing staff." — p.98–99, [Publication of the Ockenden Report, NUH 6540]

**[LEAD] Human Tissue Authority inspection of NUH mortuary services returned three critical and six major findings; CEO has apologised and committed to "rapid improvement with appropriate independent oversight".**
[Evidence] "On 22 June 2026, the Human Tissue Authority (HTA) published its report following an inspection of the Trust's mortuary services undertaken in March 2026. There were three critical findings, six major, one minor and 11 advisories." — p.44, [Chief Executive's Report, NUH 6537]. Note: the government has separately required the HTA to review NUH mortuary records 2015–2026 as an assurance exercise on incident reporting (p.98).

**[LEAD] NUH's year-to-date deficit hit £23.0m at Month 2 (May), £6.1m adverse to plan, with efficiency delivery already behind and no cash support expected until September.**
[Evidence] "The Trust's year-to-date performance to May was £23.0m deficit, £6.1m adverse to plan." / "Year-to-date: The Trust delivered £6.3m of savings against a year-to-date plan of £7.1m" / "the Trust is not expecting to require [cash] support until September, assuming efficiencies are delivering to plan." — p.283/339–340, [Integrated Performance Report, NUH 6546]. Prior-year context (March position) referenced in May minutes: "£12.2m deficit against a break-even plan" and efficiency delivery "£67.6m against an efficiency target of £97.7m resulting in under performance of (£30m)" — p.[May minutes].

**[LEAD] Trust commits to eliminating ED corridor care by end of September 2026 and all corridor care by end of October 2026, describing it as "unsafe, undignified and unacceptable, with clear evidence of harm to patients and staff".**
[Evidence] "The purpose of this paper is to present a single improvement plan to eliminate care corridors care across NUH... with the ambition of ED corridor care being removed by the end of September 2026... eliminate all care meeting the national definition of corridor care by the end of October 2026." / "Corridor care remains unsafe, undignified and unacceptable, with clear evidence of harm to patients and staff." — p.107–108, [Corridor Care Removal, NUH 6541]. Same-sex accommodation breaches "increased to 90 in May, linked to ED corridor care" (p.283, IPR).

**[WORTH WATCHING] Two SHMI diagnostic-group mortality outliers remain higher than expected and under monitoring — Fractured Neck of Femur SHMI 1.58 and Acute Myocardial Infarction SHMI 1.38 at end Q4.**
[Evidence] "two NHSE SHMI diagnostic group outliers remain higher than expected and under monitoring: Acute Myocardial Infarction and Fractured Neck of Femur. Acute Myocardial Infarction SHMI was 1.38 at end Q4... Fractured Neck of Femur SHMI was 1.58 at end Q4; a deep dive is underway with pathway review, ward-level mortality analysis, theatre access and pre-operative delay work." Overall NUH SHMI 1.1569, "close to the upper control limit." — p.131–132, [Mortality & Learning From Deaths, NUH 6542]

**[WORTH WATCHING] Three Regulation 28 Prevention of Future Death notices received by NUH in Q1 2026/27 (none in Q4 2025/26).**
[Evidence] "three Regulation 28 Preventing Future Death notices have been received in Q1 2026/27. They relate to different clinical conditions with no shared theme or recurrent clinical concern. One action is completed and two remain ongoing." — p.132, [Mortality & Learning From Deaths, NUH 6542]

**[WORTH WATCHING] Fit and Proper Persons annual submission to NHSE flagged a mitigation for one executive director whose DBS check had expired.**
[Evidence] "The submission to NHSE included details of mitigations put in place in respect to one Executive Director whose Disclosure and Barring Service (DBS) check had expired." — p.381, [Fit and Proper Persons Test, NUH 6549]

**[WORTH WATCHING] Freedom to Speak Up service reported 500 concerns in the period, with demand "stretching the current 2 WTE" team.**
[Evidence] "500 concerns were received by the FTSU Team, 127 felt confident to be open" / "The FTSU service remained busy with demand stretching the current 2 WTE." — p.[May minutes, FTSU Bi-Annual Report]

## FOI leads

- **HTA mortuary inspection report (three critical findings).** Request the full HTA inspection report of NUH mortuary services (March 2026 inspection, published 22 June 2026) and the trust's action plan — from the Human Tissue Authority and/or NUH. The pack quotes only headline counts (3 critical, 6 major); the substance of the critical findings is not disclosed. Also request the terms of the "independent oversight" the CEO committed to.
- **Confidential Nottingham Inquiry report to board.** The CEO report states "Further detail on the Trust's involvement in the Inquiry and the matters considered during the evidence hearings is provided in a separate report for Confidential Board" (p.44) — request the confidential board paper on the Nottingham Inquiry, and details of the disciplinary process outcomes NUH made public in advance of 27 May 2026.
- **The 5 unmapped Ockenden Local Actions for Learning.** Request from NUH the benchmarking/mapping document identifying which 5 of the 32 LAFLs are not currently mapped to a perinatal improvement pathway, and their assurance status.
- **Fractured Neck of Femur mortality deep dive.** Request the interim report to NUH's Learning from Deaths & Deterioration Group (due July 2026) on the Fractured Neck of Femur SHMI outlier (1.58), covering theatre access and pre-operative delay findings.
- **FPPT mitigation for expired DBS director.** Request the FPPT annual submission (dated 26 June 2026) and the specific mitigation applied for the executive director with the expired DBS check.

## Verbatim colour

- On the review findings: *"The report demonstrates what ensues when leadership, governance and culture are not robust: poor practice is not investigated; learning is not integrated; and mothers and babies are failed by an organisation they should be able to rely upon absolutely during a period of acute vulnerability in their lives."* — p.96, [NUH 6540]
- On systemic failures repeated from earlier reports: *"Dismissing the concerns of, not listening to and not respecting women and families, defensive practice... organisational reputation prioritised over safety, a poor safety culture with ineffective incident reporting... meant that avoidable harm to women and babies was not prevented."* — p.96, [NUH 6540]
- CEO on the critical incident: *"On Monday 29 June, the Trust declared a Critical Incident due to severe and sustained pressure on our services, particularly Urgent and Emergency Care. These services were impacted by the recent period of extreme hot weather. The Critical Incident ended on Wednesday 1 July."* — p.44, [Chief Executive's Report, NUH 6537]
- On national policy fallout: government has confirmed three immediate national actions in response to the review — *"Extension of Martha's Rule to all maternity and neonatal services"* and *"Extension of the Hillsborough Law Bill to apply to the independent maternity and neonatal reviews into Leeds Teaching Hospitals NHS Trust and University Hospitals Sussex NHS Foundation Trust."* — p.98, [NUH 6540]

## Pack assessment

**Not routine — high-significance pack.** This is the first ordinary board after the Ockenden maternity review publication, and it carries multiple genuinely publishable leads: the review's harm figures (444 women / 76 babies), the MP-chaired Learning and Improvement Board, the HTA mortuary critical findings, a widening £23m deficit, and a September/October corridor-care elimination pledge. NUH is already under intense national scrutiny (Ockenden, Nottingham Inquiry, corporate manslaughter context) and this pack advances several of those threads with named figures and verbatim board-level admissions.

Tier counts: **LEAD 6, WORTH WATCHING 4, FOI 5.**
