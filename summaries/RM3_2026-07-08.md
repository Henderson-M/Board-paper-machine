## 1. Top lines

**LEAD — NHS England has issued NCA formal enforcement undertakings over quality-governance, spinal and gynaecology services.**
> "Board of Directors is reminded that NHSE have issued formal enforcement undertakings based on concerns they have with the efficacy of quality governance arrangements across the organisation. NHSE also identified issues relating to the management of risk in spinal and gynaecology services, and with regulatory concerns set out in the CQC Section 29A Warning Notice issued in October 2025 in relation to Surgical Services at Salford Royal Hospital." — p.2, [Enforcement Undertakings]. NHSE requires monthly board oversight and monthly oversight meetings (first held 26 June 2026); NCA must share an Integrated Improvement Plan by 24 July 2026, "the delivery of which will be the basis of satisfying any conditions to exit undertakings." — p.4–5, [Enforcement Undertakings]

**LEAD — Independent "Oade Report" into spinal surgery patient safety published 28 May 2026; trust accepts findings, apologises, offers 3-year review to any of "Consultant A's" patients.**
> "The Trust recognises that patients and families experienced harm, distress and, in some cases, long-term and life-changing impacts." — p.2, [NCA Response to the Dr Oade Report]
> The Oade Report (commissioned by NHSE North West region, chaired by Dr Yvette Oade) "concludes that the reviews did not answer important questions and that approaches and/ or methods taken compromised the quality of the resulting reports… the ways matters were investigated have, in some cases, made the trauma which they have experienced, worse." — p.2–3, same paper. Any patient operated on by "Consultant A" can request an in-person review for a three-year period; "At the point of submitting this paper, two patients had established contact." — p.5

## 2. FOI leads

- **The enforcement undertakings document itself.** The pack references NHSE "formally issuing enforcement undertakings" but does not reproduce the undertakings text, the specific conditions, or the exit criteria. The Enforcement Undertakings paper carries FOI status "Confidential." *Request: the full NHSE enforcement undertakings notice served on NCA, its conditions and exit criteria, and the Integrated Improvement Plan submitted to NHSE on 24 July 2026 — from NCA and/or NHSE North West.*

- **The Oade Report in full and its terms of reference.** Board accepts six recommendations but the pack only summarises them. *Request: the full "Spinal Surgery Independent Patient Safety Investigation – Phase One: Diagnostic review of concerns raised" (Oade Report) and the draft ToRs (Appendix 2) for the further independent "controls review" — from NCA / NHSE North West.*

- **The CQC Section 29A Warning Notice (Salford Royal surgery, October 2025) and re-inspection findings.** Pack says "initial CQC feedback following the surgical reinspection indicates no significant concerns" but the notice and reinspection report are not published in the pack. *Request: the Section 29A Warning Notice and the CQC surgical re-inspection report — from CQC / NCA.*

- **External spinal and gynaecology reviews.** "externally commissioned reviews, with terms of reference agreed by NHSE… outputs are expected to be available in early autumn" (p.2, Enforcement Undertakings) — request the ToRs, the commissioned firm(s), and contract values.

- **Financial recovery trajectory / FSP dashboard.** DSF continuation is conditional on a recovery trajectory (p.2, F&P 29 June). *Request: the Financial Sustainability Plan assurance dashboard and Q2 recovery trajectory submitted to NHSE.*

- **Part 2 (closed session) minutes of 6 May, 26 May and 22 June 2026** (agenda Item 22) and Cyber Security BAF risks explicitly withheld from the public domain (p.4, BAF paper).

- **"Recent media coverage" re staff concerns** (CEO report §6) — worth identifying what has been published and whether it links to any FTSU / grievance / tribunal matters.

## 3. Verbatim colour

- On the harm caused by the reviews themselves: *"Even well-intentioned reviews can cause harm if not coordinated, trauma-informed and communicated with care, and this is an important learning point for the organisation."* — p.3, [NCA Response to the Dr Oade Report]

- On why the spinal action plan stalled: *"The circumstances and the approach taken has led to a lack of engagement in the action plan by consultants in the service. This has hampered progress and should be addressed to ensure sustainable improvement."* — p.3, [NCA Response to the Dr Oade Report]

This is creating a significant burden on clinical leaders and limiting capacity for frontline engagement and improvement."* — p.4, [Enforcement Undertakings]

- On finances only holding via one-offs: the plan is *"largely being achieved through non recurrent mitigations supporting unachieved CIP to date and industrial action and other emerging pressures."* — p.3, [F&P Committee report, 29 June 2026]

## 5. Pack assessment

High-signal pack. NCA is under an active NHSE enforcement-undertakings regime tied to quality governance, spinal and gynaecology services and a live CQC Section 29A Warning Notice at Salford Royal surgery — a strong document/regulatory-action story with a hard deadline (IIP to NHSE by 24 July). The published Oade Report into spinal surgery patient-safety failings is publishable in its own right, with quotable candour that the reviews themselves worsened patients' trauma. Finances are deteriorating: £10m Month 2 deficit (£14.8m ex-DSF), CIP delivery running at £6.2m of £104m, agency and bank caps breached, and an explicit warning that PDC cash support is likely in Q3. Leadership is on an acting basis (Acting CEO and Acting CFO) — worth confirming substantive-appointment status. Note both CEO and CFO are "Acting"; no named departure is disclosed in this pack, but the interim positions are a standing watch item.
