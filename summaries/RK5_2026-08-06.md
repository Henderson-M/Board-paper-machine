## Other notes

- **CQC 'Good' at King's Mill.** Report published 15 July 2026 following an unannounced inspection on 9–10 March 2026 under the national winter system pressures programme. All five domains rated good; "The rating of Kings Mill Hospital has changed to good." Safe remains good "across all services apart from Maternity which is rated as requires improvement" (p.69). Detailed findings not carried into the trust's summary include: 33 medical outliers on the day of inspection and mixed-sex breaches on the medical day case unit, where staff "were unaware that this was a mixed sex breach" (p.75); "staff told us that winter pressures led to patients being moved through the department due to space constraints before sepsis treatment had begun, which increased clinical risk" (p.108); the paediatric ED area "was not secure as per Facing the Future Standards" with "doors at either end of the department… not secured" (p.109); "Medical gases were not managed safely. We saw oxygen cylinders not stored safely" (p.116); and in medical care "some staff felt senior leaders in the organisation did not listen or support them" (p.97).
- **Agency and bank both over plan.** Agency £0.8m in June vs £0.5m plan (75% over), £0.9m over plan YTD; bank £1.9m vs £1.7m plan, £1.1m (22%) over plan YTD; agency over price cap 38.1% in June, up from 34.3% (p.180, p.184–185, p.145).
- **Capital.** 2026/27 capital plan £21.7m including PFI lifecycle, requiring £10.4m of capital PDC; the June board noted "capital constraints continue to impact business-as-usual delivery, with limited ability to address backlog pressures in the short term" (p.11, p.180, p.187).
- **Audit and Assurance Committee (June).** "Corporate governance and support capacity was noted as stretched, impacting delivery"; "Performance against internal audit actions was slightly below the 68% target" (p.18).
- **Fit and Proper Person Test.** Full compliance reported for the year to 30 June 2026, checks "completed for the 2025-26 reporting period with no adverse findings"; return sent to the NHSE Regional Director on 20 May 2026. "Two Board members left during the reporting period" — not named (p.212–213).
- **Leadership.** Rukshana Kapasi OBE took up post as substantive chair in July 2026, succeeding Graham Ward after 11 years; Steve Banks served as acting chair in the interim (p.22, p.7).
- **DSPT.** Independent audit by 360 Assurance (report dated 29 June 2026) gave a low-risk assurance opinion and high confidence rating, four low-risk actions, no high or medium findings; no reportable data security SIRIs in 2025/26 (p.218–251).
