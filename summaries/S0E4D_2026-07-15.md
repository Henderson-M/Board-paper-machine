# NHS Thames Valley ICB board — 15 July 2026

Context: Thames Valley ICB is a newly-formed body created from the close-down of the former BOB (Buckinghamshire, Oxfordshire and Berkshire West) and Frimley ICBs, now standing up its own operating model, budgets and governance. CEO is Nick Broughton; CFO Rich Chapman; chair Dr Priya Singh.

## Top lines

[LEAD] New ICB is running a large supplier-invoice backlog serious enough to trigger a joint NHSE national/regional "executive cell", weekly exec oversight and reactive media handling including a local radio interview.
[Evidence] "We now have 13,661 invoices outstanding [of which c.10,000 are over 30 days]. This means the total number of invoices is down 20% on the peak last month. Within CHC/LDMHA cohort the figures are down 25% from 9,807 to 7,451." — CEO & Chief Officers' Report, para 19 (p.3 of 6); executive cell "to bring together senior decision makers from NHSE regional and national teams, SBS (the system supplier) and the ICB" para 18; "media responses have been issued, including a local radio interview" para 20.

[LEAD] Financial sustainability is the ICB's only board-assurance-framework risk rated out of appetite, scored 20 (5x4) after mitigation against a threshold of 12, owned by the CFO.
[Evidence] "2A ... Financial Sustainability ... Rich Chapman ... 5 5 25 5 4 20 OPEN / 12 Out" and risk narrative: the risk "materialises owing to failure to deliver in-year financial balance and recurrent financial sustainability and/or secure sufficient capital and revenue resource ... including delivery of the new hospital" — [Board Assurance Framework 2026/27, Item 13], risk 2A, "Current Risk Score: 20".

[LEAD] The month 2 finance position was signed off only via a deliberately "high-level" process agreed with NHSE because the invoice backlog and dual-organisation wind-down made a full month-end impossible.
[Evidence] "It was agreed with NHS England that Thames Valley would complete a high-level month end process at month 2 and report on plan. The culmination of the complexities of closing down the two predecessor organisations, the setting up of the new TV ICB and the issues that have arisen with the financial system and the impact of the backlog of invoices, it was not possible to complete a thorough month end process." — [Quality, Performance and Finance / Month 2 finance report, Item 8], executive summary. ICB reports break-even YTD and forecast outturn; 2026/27 capital allocation £13.7m (incl. £7.2m strategic capital "to help support left shift investments").

[LEAD] The ICB is asked to approve a delegation agreement under which Frimley Health FT transfers employment of staff in five ICB functions, framed as necessary to live within a "revised financial envelope" set by NHSE.
[Evidence] Item 12 (Frimley Health NHS FT Delegation Agreement) is listed on the agenda "(to follow)"; the BAF discloses the substance: "The agreement transfers the employment of staff in the following functions: - Medicines Optimisation - All Age Complex and Continuing Care - Thames Valley Workforce Training and Education Hub - Berkshire Community Equipment Services - Associated Finance Support Staff ... allows the ICB to fulfil its role as a strategic commissioner whilst living within the revised financial envelope provided by NHSE as the regulator of ICBs." — [BAF 2026/27, risk 2A]. Requires a Joint Board Committee "in place before 1 February 2026" and gateway reviews at 6 and 12 months.

[LEAD] The ICB signals it will actively decommission low-volume clinical services on safety/sustainability grounds as it opens a wave of clinical service reviews and moves toward integrated health organisation (IHO) lead-provider models.
[Evidence] "We will shortly be embarking on a number of clinical service reviews with a view to testing our future service configuration landscape ... Where the ICB has concerns around the safety or sustainability of low volume services it will take active steps to decommission these in order to improve patient outcomes and experience." — CEO Report para 32; para 31: "in active dialogue with multiple local NHS provider organisations around potential lead provider opportunities as a precursor to the establishment of IHO ways of working."

[LEAD] Thames Valley ICB will host the new Office of Pan-ICB Commissioning (OPIC) for the whole South East region, absorbing direct-commissioning functions transferring from NHSE in April 2027.
[Evidence] "Thames Valley ICB has agreed to host the OPIC on behalf of the SE Region and this has been endorsed by the NHS England national team. The OPIC Programme is a joint venture between NHS England and Thames Valley ICB ... transition of staff and services in-scope from NHS England to the OPIC directorate within Thames Valley ICB." — [CEO Report appendix / OPIC paper], with model described as a "'hosted ring-fenced unit' ... administrative anchor and legal wrapper" and OPIC carrying its own BAF risk (3C).

[LEAD] Oxford University Hospitals FT was one of 12 trusts named in Baroness Amos's national maternity/neonatal investigation and received an individual trust report; the ICB is now reviewing required actions.
[Evidence] "Oxford University Hospitals NHS Foundation Trust (OUH) was one of twelve NHS trusts selected to participate in the investigation. Alongside the national report, an individual trust report was published which sets out findings specific to OUH, including concerns raised by women, families and staff regarding communication, culture, workforce pressures, safety." — [Public Quality Report, Item 8], p.7 of 8. (CQC separately rated OUH maternity Good on 6 June 2026, up from requires improvement, but John Radcliffe's "safe" domain remains Requires Improvement with "7 breaches in regulations", p.8 of 8.)

[LEAD] Board is asked to approve the ICB's approach to implementing the Lord Mann Review's 36 recommendations on antisemitism and racism — via existing EDI structures rather than a new programme.
[Evidence] "The review makes 36 recommendations, centred on five key themes ... The proposed approach is to embed the Lord Mann Review within existing equality, diversity and inclusion governance ... rather than establish a separate programme of work." — [Implementing Mann Review recommendations, Item 11], p.1 of 9. Requires boards to "fully understand staff survey data on the experience of racism within the organisation".

[WORTH WATCHING] A Never Event (wrong-site surgery) at Berkshire Healthcare FT was reported to the ICB.
[Evidence] "BHFT – has notified the ICB of an incident meeting the Never Event criteria for Wrong Site Surgery that occurred in April 2026. Learning has been identified and actions taken." — [Public Quality Report, Item 8], section 2.2.

[WORTH WATCHING] The new leadership has quietly closed out scrutiny of the former BOB ICB's decommissioning of Oxford Health FT CAMHS services with a retrospective review clearing the process.
[Evidence] "We note the recent interest in decisions taken by the previous BOB ICB with regards to the decommissioning of CAMHS services previously provided by Oxford Health Foundation Trust. The new leadership team of the Thames Valley ICB has completed a retrospective review of the decision making process and implementation approach to these changes and concluded that all appropriate steps were taken." — CEO Report para 35.

[Evidence] "On 1st July I also hosted an all staff briefing that covered the publication of the outcome of consultation report and the next steps in relation to the organisation change programme." — CEO Report para 4. *Request from Thames Valley ICB: the outcome-of-consultation report on the organisation change programme, including post/FTE reductions and running-cost-envelope target. [FOI] Independent review of the AJM Healthcare wheelchair contract. Why it matters: named contractor in enhanced oversight after service failed on assessment waits and delayed discharges.*
