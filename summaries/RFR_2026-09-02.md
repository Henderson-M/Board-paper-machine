[Evidence] "ESI's overarching conclusion is that at each stage examined by the Review, whether that was recruitment, line management, supervision or governance and oversight, there was a distinct lack of relevant policies or procedures in place. [Evidence] "A cluster of serious incidents following ERCP procedures, including patient deaths, occurred between 1 January 2020 and 18 November 2020.
