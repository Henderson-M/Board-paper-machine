## Top lines

[LEAD] UCLH opens 2026/27 £6m in deficit at month 2 — £4.4m worse than plan, driven by efficiency-savings shortfalls.
[Evidence] "The Trust reported a £1.7m deficit on a control total basis in May, £2.1m adverse to the plan of a £0.4m surplus. This result brings the Trust's year-to-date position to a £6.0m deficit, which is £4.4m adverse to plan." — Item 10, [Chief Financial Officer's report – Month 2 2026/27]

[LEAD] The trust is already forecasting a £37.3m shortfall against its £92.6m savings target, with most divisions missing the first identification milestone.
[Evidence] "On a full-year basis, the Trust is forecasting savings of £55.3m from efficiency schemes that have been identified to-date… it is a shortfall of £37.3m against the £92.6m savings target. Most divisions have failed to meet the target set by the FIRB of 30% identification of targets by the end of May." — Item 10, [Chief Financial Officer's report – Month 2 2026/27]

[LEAD] UCLH's £92.6m efficiency requirement is its largest ever, and 2025/26's target was only hit with £15.2m of one-off savings.
[Evidence] "The Trust delivered its overall efficiency target (of £76.6m) however £15.2m was delivered on a non recurrent basis." — Item 9/Q4 objectives, [Chief Executive and Senior Directors Team report]; "This represents the highest efficiency target to date and a substantial challenge." — Minutes of 26 March 2026, item 12 (M11 2025/26 finance)

[LEAD] Despite a "strong" reported position, UCLH's own five-year strategy concedes an underlying deficit it cannot close through income growth alone.
[Evidence] "While our reported financial position is strong, we have an underlying deficit. Closing this gap will take time and cannot be achieved through income growth alone." — Item 6, [UCLH Five-year strategy 2026-31, Ambition 5]

[WORTH WATCHING] A newly merged ICB is paying UCLH around £0.5m a month below the trust's expectation, with the contract still unagreed and escalation under way.
[Evidence] "The Trust has still to agree contract values with one of the largest newly merged ICBs, Central & Eastern, and escalation is taking place as the ICB's contract offer, and contract payments to-date, are significantly lower than the Trust's expectation by c.£0.5m a month." — Item 10, [Chief Financial Officer's report – Month 2 2026/27]

[WORTH WATCHING] A workforce committee report states UCLH is "currently an outlier for employment tribunals."
[Evidence] "UCLH are currently an outlier for employment tribunals but will be monitored closely." — Item 13.2, [Workforce committee report, 01 June 2026]

[WORTH WATCHING] Freedom to Speak Up concerns rose to 161 (from 130), with bullying and harassment among the top four themes and staff describing detriment after speaking up.
[Evidence] "Staff raised 161 concerns to the FTSU Guardians. This is an increase on the previous year where 130 cases were reported. The 4 most common themes for new concerns were Behaviour/Relationship (43), System and Process (36) Management Issues (34), and Bullying and Harassment (24)." — Item 4, [Freedom to Speak Up Guardian Service Annual Report 2025/26]

[WORTH WATCHING] Diagnostic waits hit their worst level of the year, with barely seven in 10 patients seen within six weeks against a 90% target.
[Evidence] "At the end of Q4, 73% of patients waiting for a diagnostic procedure were waiting less than six weeks. This is the worst performance from FY2526 and there has been a continued deterioration in performance since Jul-25." — Item 9/Q4 objectives, [Chief Executive and Senior Directors Team report]; May position 72.5% — Item 7, [Performance report]

[FOI] A KPMG audit into UCLH's year-on-year rise in complaints is referenced but its scope and findings are not disclosed.
[Evidence] "This action is being progressed through established governance arrangements, including oversight at QSC, QPRM and through the KPMG audit process. The findings have been identified as part of wider organisational change and audit activity…" — Action tracker, item 18 (Complaints). [Request: from UCLH, the KPMG audit terms of reference and report on complaints, and the complaints data showing the year-on-year increase referenced at the 27 Nov 2025 board.]
