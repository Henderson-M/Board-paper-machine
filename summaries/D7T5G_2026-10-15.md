Context: Second substantive public board of NHS Essex ICB (ODS D7T5G, created 1 April 2026 from Mid and South Essex plus the Essex parts of SNEE and HWE). CEO Tom Abell; Chair Prof Michael Thorne. The pack contains three NHSE 2025/26 annual assessment letters for the predecessor ICBs and an NHSE neighbourhood health letter with a one-page NHSE-authored system summary. Several items (MSEFT NOF 4, S29a/S31 notices, £14m plan risk, GP data-sharing letters) were flagged in the 16 July summary; they are repeated below only where the position has moved.
