## Top lines

[LEAD] Croydon Health Services forecasts a £12.0m closing underlying deficit at year-end, rated RED, dependent on recurrent CIP delivery and on an assumption that £14.9m of ICB fixed support income is "recreated next year".
[Evidence] Closing Underlying Position Y/E FOT "£12.0m" (RED); commentary: "Dependant on recurrent delivery of CIP programme… Note that this figure currently excludes CIP FYE and assumes other ICB fixed support income (£14.9m) will be recreated next year." — p.139, [Integrated Finance Report M2, scorecard 3a]

[LEAD] CHS's £41.2m cost improvement programme carries a delivery risk of up to £9.2m plus a £4.1m planning gap, with roughly £3.1m of savings schemes still unidentified two months into the year.
[Evidence] Health Risks: "£4.1m CIP planning gap; £1.6m-£9.2m CIP delivery; £2.4m Band 5 Nursing funding shortfall; £1.8m CNST rebate; £1.6m SWLP run rate cost pressures." — p.133, [M2 Finance Report coversheet]. "At present, ~93% (£38.1m) of schemes needed to address the identified gap have been identified… a further ~£3.1m in identified savings… Gap 7%." — p.144, [Appendix 1, CHS Efficiency Report]

[LEAD] Croydon University Hospital's urgent and emergency care remains rated "requires improvement" after the March CQC inspection, with the regulator flagging care in escalation spaces and crowding.
[Evidence] "Following the recent CQC inspection of urgent and emergency care in March, the service remains rated 'requires improvement', consistent with the previous inspection… The key areas identified for improvement are: Privacy and dignity, particularly in the context of care in escalation spaces; Ongoing crowding driven by high demand and discharge delays; Inconsistent patient communication regarding waiting times." — p.173, [Trust CEO & Place-Based Leader's Report, item 6]

[WORTH WATCHING] South London & Maudsley is forecasting a £21.4m underlying recurrent deficit with CIP delivery running at 40% of plan, and a "turnaround board" now identifying mitigations. [INFERENCE] figures are SLaM whole-trust, not Croydon-only.
[Evidence] CIP "YTD delivery is 40% of planned target. There is a forecast shortfall on a number of key schemes. Non recurrent mitigations are being identified by the turnaround board." Underlying Position M12 Forecast "(£21.4m)" (RED). — p.143, [Integrated Finance Report M2, scorecard 5a]

[WORTH WATCHING] Croydon Health Services is being designated the "Host Organisation" for Croydon place under SWL ICB's neighbourhood-health/integrator model, with an ICB–trust Memorandum of Understanding in development.
[Evidence] Paper covers "the development of a Memorandum of Understanding between the ICB and the Host Organisation for Croydon (Croydon Health Services NHS Trust)" and "delivery and oversight of Neighbourhood Health and Care in Croydon". — p.27, [Neighbourhood Health and Care, item 6]. Model defined as an Integrated Health Organisation: "a capitated contract commissioned by an ICB which transfers responsibility for the health outcomes of a whole population within a defined geography to a 'host' provider." — p.46, [Appendix 2, glossary]

[WORTH WATCHING] Croydon University Hospital bed occupancy hit 98.6% in May (above the upper control limit) with delayed discharges still losing substantial capacity.
[Evidence] "Bed Occupancy" charted at 98.6% May-26 — p.60; "474 Additional Delay Days… Avg. Delay Days 13.2" — p.58; "the average delay for May being 13.2 days which has come down from the 16.6 for the month of April." — p.53, [One Croydon Alliance Performance Report]

[WORTH WATCHING] Croydon has been named NHS England's London Women's Health Group Clinic Regional Centre of Excellence — but with no dedicated programme funding attached.
[Evidence] "The principal risk is the absence of dedicated programme funding. Delivery will therefore rely on maximising existing commissioning arrangements and partner collaboration to embed the model sustainably." — p.168, [Croydon Women's Health Regional Centre of Excellence, item 14]

## FOI leads

[FOI] Request from SWL ICB any correspondence with Croydon Health Services on whether the ICB will fund CHS's ERF/HCD overperformance and whether the £14.9m fixed support income is being recreated in 2026/27.
[Evidence] Finance scheme portfolio repeatedly notes "Currently unclear whether SWL ICB will fund overperformance" and the £12.0m underlying position "assumes other ICB fixed support income (£14.9m) will be recreated next year." — p.139, p.145, [Integrated Finance Report M2]. This is the single biggest swing factor in CHS's underlying deficit and turns on an unpublished ICB funding decision.

[FOI] Request from South London & Maudsley the terms of reference, membership and recovery plan of its "turnaround board", and the M12 forecast underlying deficit papers.
[Evidence] "Non recurrent mitigations are being identified by the turnaround board" against a forecast underlying deficit of "(£21.4m)". — p.143, [Integrated Finance Report M2, 5a]. Pack references a turnaround board but discloses no scope, membership or plan.

[FOI] Request from Croydon Health Services / LBC the procurement documents behind the intermediate-care recommissioning, including the earlier "unsuccessful procurement process".
[Evidence] Minutes: recommissioning "had been significant and complex, particularly following an earlier unsuccessful procurement process due to market capacity challenges." — p.11, [Draft minutes 13 May, item 8.3]. A failed NHS/council procurement is disclosed but not explained.

[FOI] Request from Croydon Health Services detail of the £2.4m "Band 5 Nursing funding shortfall" listed as an in-year financial risk.
[Evidence] Health Risks list: "£2.4m Band 5 Nursing funding shortfall." — p.133, [M2 Finance Report coversheet]. Specific unexplained nursing-funding gap.
