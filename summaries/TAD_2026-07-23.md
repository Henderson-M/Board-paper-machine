## Top lines

[WORTH WATCHING] CIP delivery risk "significantly worsened" at Month 3 — trust still forecasts breakeven but flags up to £9.2m worst-case deterioration, driven mainly by the Mental Health Care Group and out-of-area placements.
[Evidence] "The risk of delivering the CIP programme has significantly worsened due to: Out of area placements remaining at elevated levels; and Confidence on delivering 3% savings in Care Groups; Digital Services savings and the deteriorisation of c£6.4m, which requires robust plans to be developed to de-risk the Trusts financial delivery." — p.140, [Finance Report (Month 3 – June 2026)]
[Evidence] "The efficiency programme is offtrack at Month 3 by £80k and is forecast to under deliver by £2.4m for the year." Further risks "between £3.4m (best case); £6.4m (likely case) and £9.2m (worse case)"; "a further net £5.1m of mitigations are required to fully de-risk the position. This is a worsening position from Month 2 where likely risks were assessed at £2.5m." — p.140, [Finance Report Month 3]
[Context] YTD position is a £1.98m deficit, £14k better than plan; full-year forecast breakeven; NOF rating segment 2; contingency already "partially deployed" at M3 — p.146/p.140.

## FOI leads

[FOI] Bradford's own information-governance incidents in 2025 — the CEO report references them but gives no detail.
[Evidence] "Following information governance incidents during 2025, the Trust strengthened its awareness activity through executive communications, targeted training and a Trust-wide Information Governance campaign." — p.11, [CEO Report]
[Request] To BDCFT: number and nature of substantiated unlawful/inappropriate patient-record access cases 2024–26; number of staff subject to disciplinary action, dismissal, professional-regulator referral or police referral; and copies of the internal investigation summaries. Grounds the local angle on the national Mackey "curiosity kills your career" campaign.

[FOI] Joint Chief People Officer appointment with Airedale NHS FT — terms not disclosed.
[Request] To BDCFT (and Airedale): the memorandum/agreement governing the shared executive appointment, cost-sharing split, accountability arrangements, and whether the post was openly recruited. A shared board-level director across two FTs is a governance/system-working point worth standing up.
