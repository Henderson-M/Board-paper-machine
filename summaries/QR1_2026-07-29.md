[WORTH WATCHING] BNSSG continuing healthcare / All Age Continuing Care overspending on rising Fast Track caseload (£1.6m YTD variance); mitigation is outsourcing care reviews.
[Evidence] "The Fast Track caseload increased over Q4 2025/26 which has led to higher than plan run rates in Q1 (£1.1m)… outsourcing additional capacity to provide care reviews… The YTD & FOT variance of £1.6m has been reported, further work will be performed across July to provide a robust FOT." — p.9, [Month 3 (June) Cluster Finance Report]
