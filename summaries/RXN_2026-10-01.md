## Top lines

[LEAD] NHS England is withholding the trust's Q2 deficit support funding; the trust is £2.2m adverse to plan at month 5 and says revenue cash support may be needed if savings slip further.
[Evidence] "The Trust has been advised that Q2 DSF will be withheld by NHSE. Mitigations had been approved by the Trust Board, however the cash position was managed through working capital management which avoided the need to defer the September PDC dividend payment. Any slippage on WRP will mean revenue cash support may be required if the slippage exceeds the amounts that can be reasonably achieved through working capital management." p.67, [9.1 IPR inc Finance and SIP, Productivity Alert/Advise/Assure]. Also: "The Trust is reporting a £2.20m adverse position to plan to the end of August 2026. The Trust has a YTD £16.18m deficit against a £13.99m planned deficit (including DSF funding of £3.85m). DSF has not been received for M4 and M5 (£1.5m)." p.67. Forecast is "developed based on non-receipt of DSF (best and likely cases include Q2-4 non-receipt, worst case includes further clawback of Q1)". p.67. Cash mitigations listed include "Restriction of supplier payments in accordance with the priority list of suppliers" and "Utilisation of internal capital cash for revenue purposes as a short-term measure". p.67.

[LEAD] By June 2026, 27.3% of the trust's funded obstetrics and gynaecology consultant workforce was unavailable for daytime duties and 36.4% for out-of-hours; elective gynaecology was cut and the board is asked to approve two obstetricians above establishment.

[LEAD] MOSS safety drill reveals only 68% of category 1 and 63% of category 2 caesareans meet target times, and 57% of inductions start within six hours; the trust says this data "has not previously been reported as part of the maternity dashboard".
