# University Hospitals of Northamptonshire (RNS) — Boards in Common, 11 June 2026

**Source pack:** UHL/UHN Joint Boards public papers, 11 June 2026 (350 pages). Trust input listed the meeting as 2026-06-09; pack title and agenda state 11 June 2026 — verify the date before publishing anything time-sensitive.

**Pack scope:** Boards in Common — UHN (Kettering General Hospital FT + Northampton General Hospital Trust) sitting jointly with University Hospitals of Leicester Trust. Performance data in IPRs is for April 2026 (Month 1). BAFs are Q4 2025/26 (closing-position) updates.

## 1. Top lines

**[LEAD] UHN's two finance BAF risks both at maximum score 25 ("extreme"); medium-term financial plan risk just escalated from 20 to 25 as 25/26 plan missed and 26/27 plan formally non-compliant.**
[Evidence] "Most risks within the BAF are within the 'significant' range, noting that the two financial risks are scored as 'extreme' (score of 25)" — p.307, [Paper O1 UHN BAF Qtr 4]. UHN16 (medium-term financial plan): "Risk Scores Qtr 3 25/26: 20 / Qtr 4 25/26: 25 / Target 10" — likelihood moved from 4 to 5 — p.326. UHN17 wording: "loss of deficit support funding resulting from non-delivery of the 2025/26 financial plan" scored 25 throughout — p.328. Executive commentary: "The current submission does not deliver the requested trajectory and discussions with NHS England remain ongoing" — p.327, [O1 UHN BAF].

**[LEAD] UHN finished 2025/26 with a £55m deficit at Month 11 (£24m KGH, £31m NGH), £42.2m adverse to plan — and Q4 deficit support funding withheld.**
[Evidence] "The financial position of UHN at Month 11 reports a deficit of £55.0m (£24.0m KGH, £31.0m NGH) which was an adverse position against plan of £42.2m (£17.9m KGH, £24.3m NGH) at this point in the year. This has increased from a break-even position against plan at Month 6 of the financial year. Deficit funding for Q4 [h]as been confirmed as withheld and this will have significant impact on both cash positions. Efficiency delivery to Month 11 was £59.4m across UHN which was £15.1m adverse to the planned position." — p.329, [O1 UHN BAF, UHN17 Q4 commentary]. Note: BAF is Q4 closing-position — board minutes/CEO report don't yet quote a full-year audited deficit; this Month 11 figure is the latest in the pack.

**[LEAD] 2026/27 plan is "non-compliant"; NHSE has withheld in-year Deficit Support Funding and is only partially approving the PDC support requested to fill the gap; supplier payments are now being staged.**
[Evidence] "Lack of in-year Deficit Support Funding (DSF) due to non compliant finance plan is resulting in planned deficit needing to be covered by interest bearing PDC support … Requests for PDC support to cover the planned deficit and settle creditor balances from 25/26 are only partially being supported by NHSE. The May request was £10.2m but only £4.0m has been approved so the balance has been added to the June request. Risk remains this will not be approved and more stark supplier payment management will be required" — p.143, [Paper E2, KGH Cash Flow]. NGH equivalent: "May request was £5.5m but only £5.0m has been approved" — p.144. Supplier payment runs being "tightly managed to ensure Salary payments are protected … Better Payment code compliance (30 day payment requirement) is dropping" — p.143. Board approved separate cash drawdown of £5.399m for KGH and £3.5m for NGH for June "which reflected the lack of deficit support funding given the non-compliant financial plan" — p.14, [Paper A, May 2026 minutes, 22/26/3].

**[LEAD] UHN finance committee gave only "Limited" assurance on the medium-term financial plan and workforce productivity — and warned the 7% CIP requirement is "already at the upper limit of deliverability".**
[Evidence] "The current 7% CIP requirement is already at the upper limit of deliverability. The need to identify clear routes to deliver additional in-year savings" and "Workforce levels remain above planned establishment" — p.146, [Paper F4, UHN Finance, Investment and Performance Committee escalation, 26 May 2026]. Same committee escalation flags "developing gap in Cost Improvement Programme (CIP) delivery from quarter 2 onwards" and "Associated risks to supply chain continuity if cash pressures persisted" — p.146. NGH "Prolonged OPEL 4 status, particularly at Northampton, indicating sustained system pressure" — p.146. Three of four assurance levels in the report are "Limited"; the May minutes also record that "the UHN 26/27 Financial Plan was already looking challenging" with "no plan for the workforce reductions required in 2026-27 to deliver the financial plan" — p.15, [Paper A].

## 3. FOI leads

**1. Coroner's Regulation 28 Prevention of Future Deaths notice, 1 April 2026 — UHL satellite dialysis unit.**
What to request: full text of the Reg 28 notice issued by Lincolnshire Coroner on 1 April 2026, the trust's formal response, the device-failure root-cause analysis, and any reports relating to the similar non-fatal event identified at another satellite unit in November 2025. From: UHL (and HM Coroner / Chief Coroner's office if the notice is not yet on the Judiciary website).
Why it matters: PFDs are public after the trust replies; satellite dialysis safety is a recurring sector concern; second event at another unit suggests systemic exposure.

**2. CQC enforcement notice covering UEC and medicine at NGH and maternity at KGH.**
What to request: full text of the enforcement notice/section 29A warning, the trust action plan, and any subsequent correspondence with CQC. From: UHN (or the CQC under EIR/transparency). The pack repeatedly references the notice but does not quote it or specify which type of notice it is.
Why it matters: An enforcement notice spanning three core services across both UHN sites is a major regulatory event not yet picked up in HSJ coverage that I can see.

**3. Coroner's verdict + cultural review behind regional oversight of UHN CYP urgent and emergency care.**
What to request: the coroner's verdict referenced; the terms of reference, completed report and recommendations of the "comprehensive cultural review"; and the regional oversight letter from NHSE. From: UHN and East Midlands regional NHSE team.
Why it matters: Pack says the review identified "leadership, psychological safety and team functioning" issues — that is a publishable governance story.

**4. NHSE letter withholding Q4 2025/26 Deficit Support Funding and only partially approving PDC support for May 2026.**
What to request: correspondence from NHSE confirming Q4 DSF withdrawal and the rationale; the May 2026 PDC requests for KGH and NGH; NHSE's decisions on each; and any subsequent agreement on the 2026/27 non-compliant plan. From: UHN, copy to ICB.
Why it matters: PDC-only support with partial NHSE backing is a sector-level squeeze on cash; the precise sums (£10.2m requested / £4.0m approved at KGH; £5.5m requested / £5.0m approved at NGH) make for a hard news number.

**5. £80m group CIP programme — risk papers, QIA, and "proposed headcount reductions".**
What to request: the underlying CIP programme document; the QIA framework; any schemes withdrawn for clinical-risk reasons; and any internal modelling of the headcount reductions referenced in the BAF. From: UHN.
Why it matters: BAF wording explicitly flags that "rushed implementation … heightening risk of harm" is a live concern. Specific scheme detail and the headcount number is missing from the public pack.

**6. UHL technology audit (limited assurance).**
What to request: the internal-audit report on Technology completed shortly before April 2026 that received a "limited" assurance opinion; the management action plan and committee response. From: UHL.
Why it matters: limited-assurance audits at England's "most digitally dynamic trust" (per HSJ list cited in the CEO report) are a story signal.

**7. KGH MatNeoIST programme — full milestone delivery reports.**
What to request: the full monthly MIA exception reports for January 2026 onwards (including the EDI deep-dive report due end May), the homebirth-service restoration EQIA, the action log of "milestone slippage" items, and the report from the Royal College of Obstetricians peer review referenced. From: UHN and NHSE East Midlands.
Why it matters: KGH formally entered MatNeoIST in January 2026 with end date January 2027 — a CQC enforcement notice (per BAF) is "expected" — substantial documentation exists but is not in the public pack.

**8. UHN external partner contract for 2026/27 group delivery support.**
What to request: name of supplier appointed via UHN/UHL procurement to support 26/27 delivery (start June 2026), contract value, term and deliverables. From: UHN.
Why it matters: External consultancy support to two trusts under turnaround-style financial pressure is a public-interest contract and routinely worth HSJ coverage.

**9. UHL Single Managed Insourcing supplier withdrawal.**
What to request: identity of the insourcing supplier that withdrew at contract stage, the procurement process used, value of the proposed contract, and the alternatives now being explored. From: UHL.
Why it matters: HSJ has previously covered insourcing market turbulence; named supplier withdrawal at contract stage is unusual.
