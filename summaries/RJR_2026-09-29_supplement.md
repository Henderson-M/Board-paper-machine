# Countess of Chester Hospital Foundation Trust board: 29 September 2026 (supplementary papers)

**Why this follow-up:** the 29 September pack alerted on 24 September listed the Integrated Performance Report (item 14) as "to follow". It has now been published as two separate files. Part 1 (the board cover sheet) has a text layer and is summarised below. Part 2 (the dashboard itself) is image-only, so its figures could not be read automatically.

**Tier counts:** 0 LEAD / 2 WORTH WATCHING / 0 FOI

## Top lines

### WORTH WATCHING

1. **Savings programme running at under a third of plan.** "At month 5, CIP delivery was £1.602m year to date against a £5.045m plan, an adverse variance of £3.443m. Divisional financial recovery plans have been presented to EDG and are being monitored." The cover sheet adds that "enhanced executive oversight of CIP delivery and divisional financial recovery plans will continue". Read alongside the main pack's finance material (BAF5, the unfunded escalation cost of £250k a month in the July minutes).

2. **Delayed discharges well above plan as four-hour performance slips.** "Patients not meeting the criteria to reside remain a significant constraint, with 4,730 delayed days in August, 462 more than July and 2,990 above plan." Four-hour performance "reduced to 61.0% against a plan of 64.2%", and August ED attendances were "14% (1,016 attendances) higher than the same period last year".

### Other points from the cover sheet (context, not leads)

- RTT 18-week performance 68.4% in August, 4.7 points above plan; no 65-week waiters. The waiting list "remained 2,288 pathways above plan", with "additional external validation support due to commence in September".
- Cancer: July FDS 87.4% (fifth consecutive month above plan), 62-day 83.3%; 31-day fell 1.8 points to 93.5% against a 96% target.
- Diagnostics 79.7%. Non-obstetric ultrasound "accounts for 42% of patients waiting over six weeks".
- VTE, ED screening and sepsis compliance flagged as requiring improvement. Mandatory training 89.0% (target 90%), appraisal 79.6% (target 80%).
- No MRSA cases and no never events reported.
