## Top lines

[LEAD] NHS England has discontinued LSC ICB's leadership and governance enforcement undertakings (in place since March 2025) with effect from 23 October 2026 — but the financial undertakings stay, and NHSE will issue *revised* financial undertakings separately.
[Evidence] "NHS England has confirmed compliance with, and discontinuation of, the associated undertaking. The leadership and governance undertaking has also been discontinued... Both discontinuations took effect on 23 October 2026... The financial elements of the undertakings remain in place and will continue to be addressed, with NHS England advising that revised financial undertakings will be shared separately." — p.5, paras 4.1.1–4.1.3, [Item 6 Chair's Board Report]

[LEAD] The ICB's own finance committee was "not assured" its £215m-mitigation recovery plan will be delivered, and members suggested the "most likely" forecast scenario was in fact the best case — the plan requires c.£215m of cost mitigations to land on a £34.9m deficit control total (which attracts deficit support funding), with £24.4m of risk-adjusted delivery risk at month 6.
[Evidence] "The committee whilst noting the position and ongoing actions was not assured as to delivery... It was suggested that the best case scenario was in fact that, and that the most likely scenario (the same figures) was optimistic." — p.3–4, [Item 10 Finance and Contracting Committee Escalation and Assurance Report, 24 September 2026]; "The ICB's 26/27 plan requires a minimum delivery of c.£215m cost mitigations 2026/27 to achieve the required financial position (resulting in a £34.9m deficit as per NHSE control total which would attract deficit support funding)... the risk-adjusted assessment indicates £24.4m of delivery risk." — p.1, [Item 11 M6 Finance Report]

[LEAD] A clinical harm review of East Lancashire Hospitals' glaucoma waiting list found "significant patient harm", with some patients suffering irreversible sight loss because of delayed surgery; new referrals were temporarily closed.
[Evidence] "A comprehensive clinical harm review of the Trust's glaucoma waiting list identified significant patient harm associated with delays in receiving definitive glaucoma surgery. For several patients, delayed intervention resulted in progression of glaucoma and irreversible visual field loss, with some patients left with advanced visual impairment despite subsequent treatment." — p.22, [Item 12ii Performance Report]

[LEAD] The phased closure of two 24-bed wards at the Royal Lancaster Infirmary will go ahead without public consultation: Lancashire's Health and Adult Scrutiny Committee ruled the change is not a "substantial variation", and the board is being asked to agree it need take no further decisions on the scheme.
[Evidence] "Lancashire Health and Adult Scrutiny Committee has concluded that the proposed ward reduction does not constitute substantial variation and therefore formal public consultation is not required... Agree that ICB Board is not required to take further decisions on this RLI proposal and that bed reconfiguration remain the operational responsibility of UHMB." — p.4–6, [Item 17b RLI bed reconfiguration]. Same paper: "One in four beds at the Royal Lancaster Infirmary are occupied by people who no longer meet the criteria to reside in hospital" — p.4.

[LEAD] NHSE wrote to the ICB on 6 October confirming Ingleton and Bentham (North Yorkshire) will transfer from LSC to Humber and North Yorkshire ICB on 1 April 2027 — one of only three boundary changes nationally — and that decisions on future ICB footprints have slipped to September 2027, for implementation 1 April 2028.
[Evidence] "the timeframe for decisions on future ICB footprints has been extended and these are now expected to be taken by September 2027, for implementation on 1 April 2028... An area of North Yorkshire adjacent to Lancashire, including Ingleton and Bentham, currently within NHS Lancashire and South Cumbria ICB, will transfer to NHS Humber and North Yorkshire ICB." — p.3–4, paras 2.2–2.3, [Item 7 Chief Executive's Report]

[LEAD] Board leadership: Andy Knox and Andy Curran have been appointed permanent joint chief medical officers (from interim); recruitment to a permanent chief nurse is to start, with Jane Scattergood remaining interim chief nurse.
[Evidence] "A Cummins highlighted the permanent appointment of Andy Knox and Andy Curran as joint Chief Medical Officers, following a period in interim roles... advised that recruitment to the permanent Chief Nurse post would commence shortly." — p.5 (minute 99/26), [Item 4a Draft Minutes, 17 September 2026]. Tracey Bleakley also attended her first public board meeting as NED and Commissioning Committee chair (minute 96/26).

[WORTH WATCHING] Assurance on elective waiting-list harm at Blackpool Teaching Hospitals "remains limited": the trust is only harm-reviewing waits over 65 weeks instead of over 52 weeks as its own SOP requires, because of patient volumes, and actions from a May 2026 urogynaecology review are still outstanding.
[Evidence] "Current assurance regarding elective waiting list harm remains limited... harm reviews continue to be undertaken at pathway closure for waits over 65 weeks rather than over 52 weeks as set out in the standard operating procedure, due to the volume of patients waiting... the Trust-wide actions arising from the May 2026 urogynaecology review remain outstanding." — p.22, [Item 12ii Performance Report]. BTH also holds 1,097 (40.7%) of the ICB's 2,694 52-week waiters — p.21.

[WORTH WATCHING] The pack states L&SC Cancer Alliance has the second-worst proportion of late-stage cancers in England and ranks 19th of 20 alliances for early-stage diagnosis; UHMB gynae faster-diagnosis performance is 34% (ranked 119/123 trusts).
[Evidence] "As a Cancer Alliance, Lancashire and South Cumbria has the 2nd worst proportion of late-stage cancers in England. However, we are the most improved Alliance area through our focused work" — [Item 13ii Cancer Overview, 'Cancer Staging in L&SC' slide]; UHMB FDS lowest tumour group "Gynae Q4 34% 119/123 -9%" — [Cancer Standards Performance – UHMB slide]
