[Evidence] "The number of patients waiting outside ED for handover increased from 2,923 to 3,631 (+24%) and has been steadily increasing since Jul 26. This equates to 117 patients waiting per day. [LEAD] NHS 111 performance fell sharply in October: only 26.58% of calls answered within 60 seconds (38.47% in September), abandonment 17.88% and average answer time more than six and a half minutes; the finance director told the September board that NHS England reviews had concluded the service is under-resourced and that funding is fixed by contract
[Evidence] "111 performance deteriorated significantly in October, with calls answered within 60 seconds falling from 38.47% to 26.58%, average answer time increasing from 205 to 395 seconds, and abandonment rising above the upper control limit from 11.09% to 17.88%.
