# The Walton Centre NHS Foundation Trust board — 4 June 2026

- **Source:** https://www.thewaltoncentre.nhs.uk/download/trust-board-june-2026.pdf?ver=14481&doc=docm93jijm4n6639.pdf
- **Pack:** Single combined pack, 450 pages (1 PDF, ~15.5MB)
- **Analysed:** 2026-06-15
- **Note:** Prompt gave meeting date 2026-06-23, but the pack is the **public Trust Board of Thursday 4 June 2026** (next meeting listed as 6 August 2026). Walton Centre is a specialist neurosciences trust; this is a financially healthy, well-performing org, so most signals are "worth watching" / FOI rather than strong leads.

## Top lines

[LEAD] NHS England accepted the trust's medium-term plan only as "Compliant with Conditions", warning that underdeveloped savings and an unsigned commissioner contract are a "material financial risk" that "may result in enhanced oversight arrangements".
[Evidence] "The underdevelopment of CIP and unsigned contracts represent material financial risk... failure to make the required progress may result in enhanced oversight arrangements." Also: "As at the end of March 2026 your weekly Cost Improvement Programme (CIP) submission showed 7% of total CIP £9.1m is fully developed or implemented... The Trust must prioritise work to rapidly develop CIP." And: "As at 31 March the Trust did not have a signed contract with your main commissioner... The Trust must now move urgently to reach signature on all contracts." — pp.29–30, [CEO Report Appendix 1 — NHSE MTP acceptance letter, signed Chris Adcock, Regional Director of Finance (North West), 16 April 2026]

[LEAD] The trust's new finance system implementation is preventing it from producing a full finance report to its board and is hampering cash analysis at the start of the financial year.
[Evidence] "Implementation issues with the new finance system prevented a full finance report from being available." — p.83, [Business Performance Committee Key Issues Report, 26 May 2026]. Corroborated: "The ongoing implementation of the financial system is hindering a detailed analysis of cash variances at this point." — p.67, [Finance IPR M1] and CEO report para 23, p.25.

[WORTH WATCHING] Medical director / chief medical officer Andy Nicolson has left after 20 years; Mark Kellett started as CMO and Responsible Officer on 1 June 2026.
[Evidence] "Mark Kellett will start as Chief Medical Officer and Responsible Officer at the Trust on Monday 1 June replacing Andy Nicolson who left in May following 20 years as a consultant at the Trust." Leavers list also names "Mr Andrew Brodbelt, Consultant Neurosurgeon/Staff Governor left April 2026" and "Rekha Siripurapu Consultant Diagnostic Radiologist left 19 April 2026." — pp.24–25, [CEO Report June 2026]

[WORTH WATCHING] Resident (training-grade) doctors' core skills / mandatory training compliance is described by the Business Performance Committee as "unacceptably low" at 65% against an 85% target — the trust ranks lowest in the region.
[Evidence] "Completion rates of core skills training of doctors in training are unacceptably low (65% v. 85% target) and this has been escalated." — p.82, [BPC Key Issues Report]. IPR adds: "Lead Employer Resident Doctor mandatory training compliance remains low at 65.58%, the Trust ranking lowest for compliance in the region." — p.45, [IPR Workforce]

[WORTH WATCHING] Spinal services are the trust's one deteriorating elective specialty — flagged as the "primary focus" of improvement work and a "low outlier" holding down RTT — and are also driving a rise in complaints, prompting a thematic review.
[Evidence] "All specialities are demonstrating improvement with the exception of spinal services which becomes the primary focus of the Trust improvement work." — p.42, [IPR]. "The spinal sub-specialty is a low outlier preventing a higher overall performance." — p.82, [BPC]. "a thematic review is underway for complaints relating to spinal services as an increase has been identified." — p.78, [Quality Committee Key Issues Report]

[WORTH WATCHING] Freedom to Speak Up annual report records two clusters of staff raising the same concerns from the same area, with "inappropriate attitudes and behaviours" the single biggest theme.
[Evidence] "during quarter three 8 colleagues spoke up about the same issues from the same department and during quarter four 6 colleagues spoke up about the same issues from the same ward." Themes: "inappropriate attitudes and behaviours (22)" topped the list of 41 concerns. — pp.227–228, [Speak Up Service Annual Report 2025/26]

[WORTH WATCHING] The trust says it was shut out of the Cheshire & Merseyside system "quality accounts day" because the joint event did not run this year.
[Evidence] "We were not offered the opportunity to present our Quality Account to the NHS Cheshire and Merseyside 'joint day' where all trusts share their reports as the 'quality accounts day' did not go ahead this year as it had in previous years." — p.86, [Quality Account 2025/26 front sheet]

## FOI leads

[FOI] Request the full unredacted NHSE North West MTP acceptance letter and annex (ref 2026-04-16 CA TWC, 16 April 2026) plus any subsequent correspondence on "enhanced oversight". The pack reproduces the letter but the underlying CIP development data, contract status and the threshold for "enhanced oversight" are worth pinning down across other NW trusts. From: The Walton Centre / NHSE North West region.

[FOI] Request the further mortality review commissioned after a patient died of a cardiac arrest following elective spinal surgery, where the coroner's conclusion was "Misadventure — necessary surgery which has inadvertently caused spinal cord damage." A "further review was commissioned to understand the reasons for the sudden deterioration." From: The Walton Centre (Learning from Deaths / PSIRG). — pp.217–218, [Learning from Deaths Report]

[FOI] Request the paper clarifying the backlog of Datix incident reports unmanaged by medical staff from 2024, 2025 and 2026, which the Quality Committee escalated and asked to be quantified. From: The Walton Centre (Quality Committee). — p.79, [Quality Committee Key Issues Report]
