## What changed in V4

**The republish is not cosmetic. It adds the three papers that were marked "to follow" on the first issue, and one of them carries a new leadership story.**

V4 is 383 pages against 371 in the version analysed on 10 September. The 12 extra pages are three papers inserted in agenda order:

- **62-26 Chair's report, Angela McNab (pp.26-29)** – was "to follow"
- **65-26 Quality and safety committee assurance, Jo Emmanuel (pp.78-81)** – was "to follow"
- **66-26 Financial, workforce and operational performance assurance, Nick Page (pp.82-85)** – was "to follow"

Everything else in the bundle is unchanged. Page references from the earlier analysis shift by +4 for the CEO report and IQPR (old pp.26-73, now pp.30-77) and by +12 from item 67-26 onwards (old p.74 and beyond, now p.86 and beyond). Pages 1-25 are unaltered. Twenty separate verbatim strings from the earlier analysis were re-located at exactly those offsets, with no textual change.

The agenda page still carries the label "(to follow)" against item 66-26 even though the paper is now in the bundle (p.1).

## Top lines – new in V4

[LEAD] Acting chief executive Abigail Jago is leaving Queen Victoria Hospital to become chief executive of North Middlesex University Hospital, part of the Royal Free London group. The first issue of this pack recorded her departure but not her destination.
[Evidence] "This meeting also marks the final Board meeting of Abigail Jago as Chief Executive of QVH. Abigail leaves the Trust to take up the role of Chief Executive of North Middlesex University Hospital and Community Services within the Royal Free London Group." – p.28, [62-26 Chair's report]. Repeated in the report's key issues: "Recognition of Abigail Jago's contribution to QVH at her final Board meeting prior to taking up the role of Chief Executive at North Middlesex University Hospital and Community Services" – p.26. The CEO report in the same bundle still gives no destination (pp.30-36), and the BAF records only "Acting CEO to leave QVH in early October" – p.357, [BAF006 Leadership Capacity].

[LEAD] The finance and performance committee has been told a financial recovery plan is being drawn up to close the cost improvement gap and "address the underlying deficit", language that does not appear anywhere in the first-issue pack.
[Evidence] "The committee discussed the continuing challenge of achieving the full-year Better Value Programme and noted that a detailed financial recovery plan is in development to meet the 2026/27 financial plan and address the underlying deficit to secure long-term sustainability." – p.83, [66-26 Finance and performance assurance, committee meeting 1 September 2026]. Cover summary: "achievement of the full-year Better Value Programme remains challenging and a financial recovery plan is being developed to address the remaining efficiency gap and support long-term sustainability" – p.82. The committee's agenda for that meeting included "Financial recovery options 2026/27" – p.83. Context from the unchanged IQPR: "The forecast for the current schemes is projecting a shortfall of £1.5m" against a £7.5m programme – p.65, [64-26.1 IQPR M3 2026-27].

[LEAD] The finance and performance committee has recommended a business case and contract award for an MRI programme to the board and corporate trustee, with no value disclosed in the public pack and no MRI item on the public board agenda.
[Evidence] "The committee subsequently endorsed the preferred option, supported the progression of the scheme and recommended for approval the business case and contract award to the Board and Corporate Trustee." – p.84, [66-26]. "The committee noted that the programme represents a significant strategic investment that will improve patient experience, diagnostic resilience and service sustainability." "The committee noted the work being carried out to firm-up the outline cost estimates. The risk of the programme slipping into the following financial year if procurement is delayed was discussed." – p.84. The committee agenda lists "MRI Procurement and Refurbishment" – p.83. The public agenda for 16 September (items 58-26 to 80-26) contains no MRI item – pp.1-3 and pp.11-12.

[WORTH WATCHING] The quality and safety committee has backed cutting the quality strategic risk score from nine to six, which would be the first movement in any strategic risk score since the last board review.
[Evidence] "The committee supported a proposal to reduce the quality risk score from 9 to 6, subject to Executive Team consideration and continued monitoring of key quality indicators, controls and assurance measures." – p.81, [65-26 Quality and safety committee assurance]. Cover summary: "the committee reviewed the Quality BAF and supported a proposed reduction in the quality risk score from nine to six, subject to continued monitoring of key quality risks" – p.78. The BAF paper in the same bundle states "No strategic risk scores have changed since the previous Board review" and "Workforce and Quality score 9" – pp.337 and 338, [77-26 Board Assurance Framework].

[WORTH WATCHING] A waiting list assurance review is running at the trust, and delivery of its outstanding actions depends on adding analytical capacity the trust does not currently have.
[Evidence] "Waiting List Assurance Review – significant progress has been made, with strengthened governance, oversight and reporting arrangements. Delivery of remaining actions is dependent on increasing analytical capacity within the BIU, which remains a key risk, with additional support options being explored." – p.82, [66-26]. The committee agenda's first item was "Waiting List Assurance Action Plan update" – p.83. The review, its findings and the action plan are not in the pack.

[WORTH WATCHING] National programme teams and unnamed external specialists are providing assurance on the trust's patient administration system replacement, which is due to go live on 30 November.
[Evidence] "Risks remain relating to programme timelines, system readiness and programme funding beyond December 2026 and will be reviewed at the next meeting. National assurance support remains in place." – p.83, [66-26]. "Assurance was received that extensive oversight and assurance support is being provided through national programme teams and external specialists. The committee also noted continuing financial risks associated with sustaining programme resources beyond December 2026 and requested ongoing reporting regarding contingency arrangements and programme affordability." – p.84. Unchanged from the first issue: "PAS go live... 30/11/2026 Status remains at Red/Amber" and "Executive escalation has taken place with Altera" – p.71, [IQPR KSO4 project report].

[WORTH WATCHING] The trust has logged new organisational risks over its pathology laboratory system programme and an unsupported order communications system.
[Evidence] "New organisational risks relating to the Laboratory Information Management System (LIMS) programme and unsupported ICE system continue to be monitored through executive governance arrangements." – p.78, [65-26 Quality and safety committee assurance]. Unchanged from the first issue: Sussex Pathology Network "QVH LIMS/ICE due to go live in early-mid 2027" with managed service "contract award predicted - end Sept 2027" – p.73, [IQPR KSO4 project report].

[WORTH WATCHING] Governors are recruiting a QVH deputy chair alongside the group chair, a post not identified in the first issue of the pack.
[Evidence] "The Council of Governors' Appointments Committee is currently leading the recruitment process for the Group Chair and the QVH Deputy Chair." – p.28, [62-26 Chair's report]. "Alongside implementation of the partnership arrangements, work has also progressed in relation to the recruitment of the future Group Chair and QVH Deputy Chair. These appointments will be important in providing long term leadership and governance arrangements for both the partnership and the Trust." – p.27. Governors also sat on "stakeholder engagement panels" for the group chief executive and group chair appointments – p.28.

[WORTH WATCHING] The finance and performance committee has told executives the partnership benefits framework is still too high level to show what the three-trust group is actually worth.
[Evidence] "The committee emphasised the importance of moving from a high-level framework to the identification, quantification and prioritisation of benefits to ensure focus is maintained on those areas delivering the greatest value across the Group. The committee requested future reports provide clearer information on expected financial, operational and clinical benefits, associated risks, delivery timescales and ownership arrangements. Emphasis was placed on distinguishing between local organisational benefits and wider Group benefits, with robust arrangements required to track delivery, demonstrate early wins and monitor long-term benefit realisation." – p.84, [66-26].

[FOI] The waiting list assurance review and its action plan exist but are not published.
[Evidence] "Waiting List Assurance Review – significant progress has been made" and "Waiting List Assurance Action Plan update" – pp.82-83, [66-26]. **Request from Queen Victoria Hospital Foundation Trust:** the waiting list assurance review report, its terms of reference and who carried it out, the full action plan with RAG status, and the papers of the finance and performance committee meeting of 1 September 2026.

[FOI] The MRI business case, its cost and the contract award are held back from the public pack.
[Evidence] "recommended for approval the business case and contract award to the Board and Corporate Trustee" and "the work being carried out to firm-up the outline cost estimates" – p.84, [66-26]. **Request from the trust:** the MRI procurement and refurbishment full business case, the options appraisal, the capital value and funding source, the successful supplier and contract value, and the private board and corporate trustee minutes recording the award.

[FOI] The identity of the "external specialists" assuring the PAS implementation is not disclosed.
[Evidence] "extensive oversight and assurance support is being provided through national programme teams and external specialists" – p.84, [66-26]. **Request from the trust:** the names of all external firms and consultancies engaged on the Digital Transformation Programme and PAS implementation since 1 April 2025, contract values and deliverables, plus any national assurance or gateway review reports on the QVH PAS go-live.

[FOI] The committee has asked for work on "key service agreements and commissioned activity" without saying which ones.
[Evidence] "Further work is required to strengthen oversight and governance arrangements relating to key service agreements and commissioned activity, with an update requested within three months." – p.80, [65-26 Quality and safety committee assurance]. **Request from the trust:** the underlying report on service level agreements and commissioned activity considered by the quality and safety committee on 1 September 2026, and the list of agreements identified as requiring strengthened governance.
