## Top lines

[LEAD] Medway forecasts running out of cash headroom this quarter: October balance is £9,000 above the minimum, £20m of NHSE revenue cash support is "not guaranteed", and the unmitigated projection is a £16.4m deficit — before the trust loses Deficit Support Funding.
[Evidence] "The Trust was forecast to move into an adverse cash position during Quarter 3. The forecast October balance of £2.774m was only marginally above the £2.765m minimum. External revenue cash support of £12m for November and £8m for December was required and was not guaranteed." ... "the unmitigated most likely projection remained a £16.4m deficit before loss of Deficit Support Funding (DSF)" — p.148, [Finance, Planning and Performance Committee report, Item 4.0]. Revenue cash support attracts a 3.5% PDC charge "estimated at approximately £2m per year" (p.148). Month 6: YTD deficit £9.8m including DSF, £27.5m excluding, both £9.4m adverse to plan — p.77, [Integrated Finance Report: Month 6, Item 3.0]. FPPC: "Total monthly costs at a total Trust-level had not materially reduced since April" — p.148. NHSE "expects a Board-agreed Financial Recovery Plan to support the December revenue cash support application" — p.149.

[LEAD] Medway tells its board it has only LIMITED assurance on the Thirlwall (Letby) Inquiry recommendations — its plan for CCTV on neonatal insulin storage will miss the 30 November national deadline, and there is no agreed process for a joint agency response if a baby dies unexpectedly in hospital.
[Evidence] "Assurance level: LIMITED. Existing safeguards are reported, but their effectiveness is not yet demonstrated" ... "the insulin-storage CCTV plan exceeds the 30 November deadline" — p.53, [Thirlwall Inquiry: Medway's position and readiness, Item 2.6]. "There is no agreed process for initiating a joint agency response for a baby already in hospital"; "The out-of-hours contact route to the Designated Doctor for Child Deaths is missing from on-call rotas" — p.63. Safeguarding Level 3 training was 76% for medical/dental staff against an 85% local KPI — p.53. "The plan remains uncosted" — p.54. Three recommendations rated non-compliant, one not started, one not assessed (bereavement care) — p.61.

[LEAD] New chief executive Mark Orchard (started 1 October) completes a near-total executive rebuild: substantive CMO, CPO, COO and CFO all arriving between October and January, with interim CEO Siobhan Callanan retiring after 40 years' NHS service.
[Evidence] "Ritchie Chalmers joined us as Chief Medical Officer on 5 October. Barbara Britner, Chief People Officer, and James Pavett-Downer, Chief Operating Officer, will join us on 2 and 9 November respectively. Jazz Thind joins as Chief Finance Officer on 4 January." — p.28, [Chief Executive Officer's Report, Item 2.2]. Recruitment underway for Deputy Chief Executive/Chief Strategy Officer, final interviews November — p.169, [Board and Committee Membership and Designations, Item 5.0]. Orchard joined the Kent and Medway ICB "on an honorary contract" from 18 October — p.28. Helen Wiseman proposed as Deputy Chair; Mojgan Sani as SID from 1 April 2027 — p.172.

[LEAD] Medway's winter plan identifies a forecast capacity gap of 152–156 beds before mitigation — the residual 103-bed gap "remained unresolved" at October's finance committee — and rates social care capacity risk Red.
[Evidence] "Current modelling identifies a forecast winter capacity gap of approximately 152-156 bed equivalents if no mitigating action is taken" — p.131, [Winter Plan Update 2026, Item 3.2]; "Social Care Capacity — Red" — p.133; "the 103-bed winter capacity gap remained unresolved" — p.148, [FPPC report]; "The approved winter plan retains a 103-bed gap, with ward closures increasing the importance of reliable discharge" — p.91, [Integrated Quality Performance Report, Item 3.1]. [INFERENCE] The 152–156 figure is pre-mitigation and 103 the residual gap after mitigation; the pack does not reconcile the two explicitly.

[LEAD] Mortality outlier: SHMI has risen to 1.34 and is "higher than expected", a pneumonia mortality outlier has returned (190 deaths v 155 expected), and Medway's ED delay rates are more than double national peers.
[Evidence] "SHMI for the period of June 25-May 26 is 1.34 and higher than expected" ... "Pneumonia outlier has returned (190 deaths vs 155 expected)" ... "Medway report 51.5% of ED attendances with a 4-hour to DTA delay and a 38.4% with 12+ delays compared to 24.1% and 9.4% against national peers" — p.103, [Integrated Quality Performance Report, Item 3.1]. QAC: "SHMI remained above the expected range and continued to show an upward trend"; "Failure to recognise and escalate deterioration remained a recurring theme" — p.143, [QAC report, Item 4.0].

[WORTH WATCHING] Cyber exposure called out by Audit Committee: 11% of staff entered credentials in a phishing simulation, investment "compares poorly with peers", key digital leadership posts are unfilled and the pathology LIMS programme has no funded project team.
[Evidence] "investment and dedicated capacity compare poorly with peers, and 11% of recipients entered credentials during the phishing simulation" — p.151, [Audit Committee Assurance Report, Item 4.0]; "Key digital leadership posts were unfilled and the pathology Laboratory Information Management System programme did not have a funded project team" — p.148, [FPPC report].

[WORTH WATCHING] Risk-management grip failing: 76% of approved risks overdue for review, the escalation route "not operating effectively", and 27 of 35 internal audit actions overdue, with recurring concerns on single tender waivers and SFI breaches.
[Evidence] "Seventy-six per cent of approved risks were overdue for review" ...
