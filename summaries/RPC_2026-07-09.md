# Queen Victoria Hospital NHS Foundation Trust (RPC) — Board pack, 9 July 2026

Analysis for HSJ (correspondent: Alison). Single ~10.2MB combined public pack, 353pp. Papers reviewed in full: Chair's report (Item 31.26), CEO report (Item 32.26), Integrated Quality & Performance Report M1 (Item 33.26), Finance & performance assurance / Finance & Performance Committee report (Item 35.26), Strategy & culture assurance (Item 36.26), Audit & risk assurance (Item 37.26), Board Assurance Framework incl. FINANCE/REGULATION/SUSTAINABILITY templates (Item 40.26), draft minutes 14 May 2026 (Item 28.26). Note: small (~£90–100m turnover) specialist trust — reconstructive surgery / burns / max-fax / sleep. Core signals here are small-provider financial sustainability, specialised-service viability, RTT/elective recovery, and — dominant this month — a proposed strategic partnership/group with Royal Surrey and Ashford & St Peter's plus very heavy interim/acting leadership.

## 2. FOI leads

- **The strategic partnership / group MOU and governance package.** The pack repeatedly references the draft Memorandum of Understanding, the "arrangements for the first six months of leadership and governance", the "proposed timeline for partnership appointments" and a report to the private boards of Royal Surrey and Ashford & St Peter's "proposing expansion of the existing Group to include QVH" — but none of the substance is published. Request from QVH (and mirror to RSFT/ASPH): the draft/final MOU, the private-board strategic-partnership papers, the six-month leadership-and-governance arrangements, and the timeline for partnership appointments considered at the 23 July extraordinary board. This is the single highest-value follow-up. (Ref: Item 31.26 p.23–24; Item 36.26 p.179–180.)
- **The "gap" in the £7.5m Better Value programme.** CEO report and BAF005 confirm a residual unclosed gap in the cost-reduction programme with "more being delivered non-recurrently than planned," but the size of the gap and the schemes are not disclosed. Request the Efficiency Steering Group / Better Value programme scheme-level tracker and the value of the unidentified/non-recurrent element. (Ref: Item 32.26 p.25; BAF005 p.228–230.)
- **Additional licence conditions history.** NHSE confirmed in March 2026 that QVH's additional licence conditions were removed; the pack does not publish the original conditions or the removal notice. Request the original additional licence conditions imposed on QVH and the NHSE correspondence removing them. (Ref: BAF009 p.243.)
- **2024/25 financial control / governance "significant weakness".** The audit & risk report references a "significant weakness identified in the 2024/25 external audit VFM Value for Money assessment" arising from financial control and governance issues reported to the committee in November 2024. Request the external auditor's VFM report and the underlying finance/governance review that identified the weakness. (Ref: Item 37.26 p.181; audit narrative ~p.183.)
- **Children's / paediatric model of care.** The strategy & culture committee reviewed options for the future care of children and young adults at QVH and endorsed a "hybrid model", noting "system-level collaboration is needed to develop the long-term strategy for paediatric care." Request the Children's Model task-and-finish group options paper and the South Thames Paediatric Network input — a service-reconfiguration signal for a specialist site. (Ref: Item 36.26 p.178.)

## 5. Pack assessment

**Not routine — one clear lead plus solid supporting material.** The lead is the strategic partnership: QVH is being folded into the existing Royal Surrey / Ashford & St Peter's Group, with an MOU due before all three boards in July, a QVH extraordinary board on 23 July to approve the first six months of leadership/governance, the BAF already branded "Group Board", and a shared interim CFO with Royal Surrey — a small specialist trust consolidating for sustainability. Underneath sit genuine financial-sustainability pressures (loss of Deficit Support Funding, a £7.5m Better Value target with an unclosed gap, working-cash risk below £1m), an unusually thin substantive executive (acting CEO/CFO/CNO, interim chair and company secretary; board turnover flagged in the regulation BAF), NOF Segment 3 despite otherwise Segment-1-level delivery, and RTT/cancer/diagnostics slipping off plan at the start of the year (Plastics, OMFS and out-of-area Sleep demand from Kent). The withheld MOU/private-board group-expansion papers and the Better Value gap are the strongest FOI follow-ups. The removed additional licence conditions and 2024/25 VFM "significant weakness" give useful recent-history colour.
