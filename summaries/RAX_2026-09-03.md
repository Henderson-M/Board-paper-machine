## FOI leads

[FOI] **The mortuary services review.** *Request from Kingston and Richmond:* the full review report identifying the 11 major and 11 minor shortfalls, the reviewer's identity, the action plan and the weekly monitoring notes, and confirmation of whether all major findings were closed by the 17 September 2026 deadline. *Why it matters:* mortuary oversight is under national scrutiny following the Nottingham maternity review and NHS England's June 2026 letter; a review finding 11 major shortfalls at a named trust is a standalone story.

[FOI] **The new never event.** *Request from Kingston and Richmond:* the never event category, date and site, the patient safety incident investigation report, the PSOG papers reviewing it, and the duty of candour record.

[FOI] **The £44.8m underlying deficit and the FIP.** *Request from Kingston and Richmond:* the month 4 finance report annexes showing the underlying deficit calculation and its movement since month 1, the FIP scheme register split recurrent/non-recurrent with RAG ratings, and the schedule of non-recurrent measures the reported position depends on.

[FOI] **The external auditor's value for money commentary.** *Request from Kingston and Richmond:* the auditor's Annual Report / ISA 260 for 2025/26 including the VfM commentary on the Financial Improvement Programme, and the internal audit reports on FIP governance and risk management (Amber-Green).

[FOI] **The four new corporate risks.** *Request from Kingston and Richmond:* the risk register entries and supporting papers for management of abnormal radiology results, the cytology analysis service risk, planned caesarean capacity, and ED fire safety relating to equipment obstructing access routes — including scores, controls and mitigation dates.

[FOI] **The doctor's employment tribunal claim** listed for hearing in 2028. *Request from Kingston and Richmond:* the jurisdiction of the claim (discrimination, whistleblowing, unfair dismissal), legal costs incurred to date, and the trust's total employment tribunal caseload.
