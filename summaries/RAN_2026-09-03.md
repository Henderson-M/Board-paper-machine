## FOI leads

[FOI] **The July 2026 never event.** The IQPR records one never event but gives no category or account. *Request from RNOH:* the never event category, the patient safety incident investigation report or after-action review, the duty of candour record, and any correspondence with NHS England or the CQC about it.

[FOI] **The surgical site infection outlier review.** *Request from RNOH:* the 2025/26 retrospective IPC case review referred to on p.91, its findings and recommendations, the SSI Prevention Group and IPC Committee minutes considering it, and the trust's SSI rates against the national benchmark that generated the outlier status.

[FOI] **The chair vacancy.** The board is being chaired by the Senior Independent Director "in lieu of Trust Chair". *Request from RNOH and NHS England (London):* the date the chair post became vacant or the chair stood aside, any settlement or severance agreement, the recruitment timetable, and correspondence with NHSE about the appointment.

[FOI] **The closed board session.** Agenda item 5 is a "Summary of the items discussed in the closed part of the Trust Board meetings held on 2 July 2026", and no finance report appears in the public pack. *Request from RNOH:* the month 4 finance report and the closed board papers for 2 July and 3 September 2026, or a schedule of the items taken in private with the exemption relied on for each.
