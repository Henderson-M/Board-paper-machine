## Top lines

[LEAD] Medway forecasts a £35.4m in-year deficit (before deficit support funding) and its own finance team rates delivery of the 2026/27 plan "high risk", warning finances "will deteriorate later in the year unless spending is reduced".
[Evidence] "The full-year forecast is breakeven, supported by deficit funding. Excluding this funding, the forecast deficit is £35.4m." … "The assessment is that delivery of the 2026/27 plan is high risk. The largest risk to the Plan is the delivery of the savings target." — p.139 & p.141, [Finance Report Month 2, Item 3.4]

[LEAD] The trust closed 2025/26 with a £54.9m deficit — £50m adverse to plan — and had to borrow an extra £40m, per the CEO/CFO account in the May minutes.
[Evidence] "Simon stated the Trust ended the financial year with a £54.9m deficit, £50m adverse to plan, primarily due to the loss of deficit support funding and ambitious savings targets that were not met. The Trust had to borrow an additional £40m, incurring ongoing interest costs." — p.7, [Minutes of 6 May 2026, Item 3.2.1]

[LEAD] Medway is carrying a backlog of ~428 open patient safety investigations (113 high-level), averaging 192 days old, with 59 overdue Duty of Candour conversations and 46 investigations more than six months overdue — a position the trust says creates "a risk of false assurance" and CQC/regulatory exposure.
[Evidence] "Approximately 428 open investigations across the Trust … Of these, ~113 incidents require high-level investigation … Average investigation age 192 days (target <60 working days) … DoC overdue 59 … >6 month overdue investigation 46 … Risk of false assurance." — p.367-369, [Patient Safety Incident Backlog and PFD Update, Item 6.6]

[LEAD] A Turnaround Director (Ian O'Connor) is now in post and the trust has £48m of CIP schemes identified against a £38.8m target (£50.3m stretch), but only ~50% of the year-to-date savings requirement has been delivered.
[Evidence] "£48 million of schemes identified that are risk adjusted to £33.2 million … CIP delivery for the YTD is £3.4m … against a plan requirement of £6.6m … The shortfall will need to be addressed urgently." Author: "Ian O'Connor, Turnaround Director". — p.148 & p.135, [2026/27 Cost Improvement Plan, Item 3.5; Finance Report Item 3.4]

[LEAD] External consultancy PA Consulting and a System Financial Improvement / System Delivery Director are embedded in Medway's turnaround, advising on ward closures and drafting a "Deep Dive Paper"; NHSE South East has issued a formal financial accountability letter.
[Evidence] "The sufficiency of approach to achieve the financial plan … with recommendations in the Deep Dive Paper prepared by the System Financial Improvement Director. The expected position was reinforced in the April NHSE SE Financial Accountability Meeting (24/4 letter from Karen Geoghagan, NHSE SE CFO)." — p.135, [Finance Report Month 2, Item 3.4]. Also: "External consultants, including PA Consulting and the System Improvement Director, have provided capacity, frameworks, and recommendations, particularly around ward closures" — p.7, [Minutes 6 May 2026, Item 3.2.5]

[LEAD] Medway has appointed Mark Orchard (currently Group Deputy CEO at Portsmouth/Isle of Wight, ex-HFMA President) as substantive Chief Executive from autumn 2026, and is recruiting to four further substantive executive director posts.
[Evidence] "Mark Orchard has been appointed as the Trust's new Chief Executive, starting this autumn … Currently Group Deputy Chief Executive for Portsmouth Hospitals University NHS Trust and Isle of Wight NHS Trust … Recruitment to four substantive Executive Director positions is under way." — p.24, [Chief Executive's report, Item 2.2]

[WORTH WATCHING] Medway's Freedom to Speak Up service logged 124 concerns in 2025/26 (23 red-rated), with 44% of users saying they went to the external service because prior concerns were not listened to; patient-safety concerns clustered in Emergency & Acute medicine.
[Evidence] "124 concerns were raised … 23 were rated red … 44% of staff using The Guardian Service have reported doing so due to a perception that they have raised their concerns before but not felt listened to." Emergency & Acute: highest at 32 concerns, 14 patient-safety. — p.184-191, [Freedom to Speak Up Annual Report, Item 5.1]

[WORTH WATCHING] The trust's backlog maintenance liability has jumped to £147.5m (this paper) — up 24% in two years — while the risk register puts "critical infrastructure risk requiring immediate intervention" at £163m; a £163m estate risk has sat open since 2024.
[Evidence] "the Trust's provisional backlog maintenance requirement has increased to £147.5 million. This represents a 23% rise over a two-year period" — p.373, [Strategic Asset Management Plan / Backlog Maintenance, Item 6.7]. Risk 2158: "identifies £65m of critical infrastructure risk requiring immediate intervention … worsened from £120 to £163million" — p.209, [Risk Register, Item 5.2]

[WORTH WATCHING] Medway's SHMI mortality risk has escalated from a score of 6 to 20 on the risk register, sitting "outside the expected range", with several mitigating actions already overdue.
[Evidence] "The Trust SHMI is outside the expected range … The risk was originally raised 3 years ago, with an initial risk score of 6, this increased to a score of 16 in June 2025 and again to 20 in February 2026, which indicates that the actions taken to-date have not had an impact on risk mitigation." — p.207, [Risk Register, Item 5.2]

[WORTH WATCHING] A neurology consultant workforce collapse is logged as a "significant issue": of four budgeted consultant posts the service was down to one consultant for a period, hitting outpatient and inpatient care.
[Evidence] "Of the four budgeted consultant posts, one consultant is on maternity leave until July 2026, and two consultants are confirmed to leave in December 2025 and January 2026. This will leave the service with only one consultant between January to March 2026." — p.214, [Risk Register — Approved Significant Issues, Item 5.2]
