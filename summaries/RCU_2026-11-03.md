[LEAD] Sheffield Children's worst-case forecast is now an £8.6m deficit against a breakeven plan, with risk-adjusted savings delivery £5.2m short of its £17.7m target and the forecast underlying deficit "just under £7m".
[Evidence] "The M6 iteration of this model highlights a best case FOT of £1.3m deficit against plan, a mid/likely FOT of £5.0m deficit and a worst-case scenario of £8.6m deficit...
