[LEAD] The trust's underlying deficit has risen from about £36m to £46m, driven by lower-than-expected voluntary redundancy uptake.
[Evidence] "APPROVED revision of the Trust reported underlying deficit position… recognising that the increase primarily reflected reduced recurrent benefit from lower-than-expected voluntary redundancy uptake. The Committee noted that, after current mitigations, the underlying deficit had increased from approximately £36m to £46m." — p.236, [Finance & Operational Committee Report Item 10.9]

[LEAD] Only 122 of 582 voluntary redundancy applications were approved (114 progressing), cutting 81.77 WTE for a £4m recurrent full-year saving from 2027/28 — short of what the recovery plan needs.
[Evidence] "From the 582 applications 114 are progressing as approved… we were only able to approve 122 applications (some have since withdrawn)… The VR programme will reduce our funded establishment by 81.77 WTE. This represents a full-year recurrent salary saving into 2027/28 of £4m… we have not yet achieved the full workforce reduction needed to support our recovery plan." — p.19-20, [CEO Board Report Item 7.0]

[LEAD] In-year breakeven depends on further NHSE cash support that has not been agreed; the trust reported a £9.6m deficit at month three and took £7.0m of revenue cash support in June.
