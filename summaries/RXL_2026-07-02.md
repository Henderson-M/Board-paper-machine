## Top lines

[LEAD] Trust forecasts a £28.8m underlying year-end deficit for 2026/27 — £5.7m worse than its planned £23.1m exit run rate — with the gap blamed on an undeveloped efficiency programme.
[Evidence] "the current forecast exit run rate for March 2027 is a £2.4m deficit which extrapolated gives an annual exit rate for 2026/27 of £28.8m." / "This is £5.7m worse than the planned exit rate, at the end of 2026/27, of a £23.1m deficit. The reason for the gap is the efficiency programme which is not currently fully developed or implemented." — p.151, [Finance Report period ending 31 May 2026]

[LEAD] The Finance and Performance Committee has recorded that the Trust is "unlikely to exit tiering in the near term" — i.e. it does not expect to leave NHSE's constitutional-standards intervention regime soon, despite exiting the formal Recovery Support Programme in April 2026.
[Evidence] "RTT remains below plan, with capacity, workforce and data quality issues following PAS migration. The Trust is unlikely to exit tiering in the near term, despite beneficial GIRFT and Cancer Alliance support." — p.142, [Finance and Performance Committee Escalation Report, 16 June 2026]

[LEAD] Deficit support funding is now explicitly at risk of clawback: NHSE told trusts it will cut £1 of DSF for every £1 of adverse variance to plan, and all DSF received earlier in the year could be lost in Q4.
[Evidence] "In a National Webinar on the 22nd May it was stated that DSF available will reduce by £1 for every £1 variance to plan in the organisation outturn. In addition, DSF is subject to cumulative/retrospective adjustment, so it is possible to lose all the DSF received in earlier quarters in quarter 4 if the outturn variance is adverse and equal to or exceeds the DSF." — p.154, [Finance Report period ending 31 May 2026]

[LEAD] Month 2 (May) position is already £2.5m behind plan year-to-date, driven by WRP under-delivery and lost RTT income; the £34.9m 2026/27 savings target is £1.5m behind at just two months in.
[Evidence] "YTD the actual deficit of £4.7m is £2.5m worse than the £2.2m planned deficit... The key drivers of the YTD variance to plan excluding DSF are predominantly £1.5m under-delivery of WRP against plan, £2.9m loss of income from RTT under delivery, £0.5m Industrial Action." — p.151, [Finance Report period ending 31 May 2026]
