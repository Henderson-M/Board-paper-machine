## Top lines

[LEAD] Countess of Chester remains under NHSE enforcement undertakings; regulator has improved the trust's provider capability rating to amber-red and signalled the undertakings may be revised, after its NHS Oversight Framework ranking jumped from 133/134 (Q1) to 98/134 (Q4).
[Evidence] "Louise Shepherd, NHS England Regional Director wrote to the Trust on the 30th June 2026 to confirm that the Provider Capability rating of the Trust (originally assessed in October 2025) has been improved to amber-red… We are anticipating a subsequent update to the NHSE enforcement undertakings placed on the Trust." — p.28, [CEO Report Item 7]

[LEAD] The trust submitted a medium-term plan to NHSE containing performance trajectories that do not meet urgent and emergency care standards in years one and two, and NHSE has now asked it to revisit that non-compliant position.
[Evidence] "The Trust submitted a medium-term plan with non-compliant UEC performance trajectories in years 1 and 2." — p.45, [BAF3, Item 9a]; "Update provided on discussions with NHS England with a request to revisit the Trust's medium term plan non-compliant position for UEC." — p.120, [Finance & Performance Committee Chair's Report Item 19]

[LEAD] Emergency plastic surgery care has ceased at the trust because of workforce constraints, with activity redirected to Mersey and West Lancashire NHS Trust; the F&P Committee is now tracking the "sustainability of the plastic surgery service" as a risk.
[Evidence] "In Plastics services, the workforce constraints have resulted in the cessation of emergency care, with activity redirected to Mersey and West Lancashire NHS Trust." — p.19, [Minutes 19 May 2026, Item 18]; "risk of sustainability of the plastic surgery service." — p.121, [F&P Committee Chair's Report Item 19]

[LEAD] Withdrawal of distressed system funding (DSF) in Q2 has created a cash risk requiring a "cash preservation plan"; future DSF is conditional on CIP delivery, and the month 2 position already carried £688k of pressures (under-delivered CIP, industrial action, unfunded pay award) offset only by non-recurrent measures.
[Evidence] "Cash risk associated with DSF withdrawal (Q2) established. Cash preservation plan developed." — p.55, [BAF5, Item 9a]; "Month 2 position includes £688K pressures including under delivered CIP, industrial action and unfunded pay award pressures which have been mitigated through non-recurrent benefits. Future DSF is dependent on delivery of financial plans and progress of CIP identification and delivery which remains a risk." — p.120, [F&P Committee Chair's Report Item 19]

[LEAD] Maternity services have been formally stepped down from NHSE's Joint Oversight and Support (JOS) process following an exit visit on 24 June 2026 — the trust's exit from enhanced maternity oversight put in place after it left the Maternity Safety Support Programme in July 2024.
[Evidence] "The visiting team, led by Catherine McClelland, confirmed that maternity services will be formally stepped down from the JOS oversight process. The team highlighted the importance of remaining mindful of capacity pressures and workforce risks that could impact the sustainability of improvements." — p.30, [CEO Report Item 7]

[WORTH WATCHING] The Thirlwall Inquiry report is now anticipated after the parliamentary summer recess; the trust's inquiry costs were funded by NHSE for 2025/26 with 2026/27 funding still under discussion.
[Evidence] "We continue to understand, share and embed learning from the Inquiry, with the Inquiry report now anticipated after parliamentary summer recess." — p.57, [BAF8, Item 9a]; "Inquiry funding supported by NHSE for 2025/26 and discussions underway for 2026/27." — p.55, [BAF5, Item 9a]

[WORTH WATCHING] An interim non-executive director with "extensive NHS finance experience" has been appointed and installed as Finance & Performance Committee chair while a full recruitment exercise runs — approved at an extraordinary private governors' meeting on 3 June 2026.
[Evidence] "We are pleased to welcome Mark Oldham, Non-Executive Director to our Board of Directors… joined the Trust on an interim basis whilst a full recruitment exercise takes place." — p.34, [CEO Report Item 7]; "An Extraordinary Private Council of Governors meeting was held on 3rd June 2026 to formally approve the appointment of an interim Non-Executive Director, who will be the Finance and Performance Committee Chair." — p.161, [Council of Governors Report Item 26]

[WORTH WATCHING] Ten risks sit on the significant risk register at 15+, including a red-rated risk of "corridor care" from medical patients managed outside the bed base, a non-compliant fire alarm system requiring a multi-million-pound replacement, and non-criteria-to-reside patients running at c.24%.
[Evidence] "High number of medical patients being managed outside of the Urgent Care bed base… Risk remains significant for effective high quality patient care, through risk of corridor care and usage of non-ward-based care for patients requiring admission." (4x4=16) — p.68, [Significant Risks Report Item 9b]; "Trust Fire Alarm System - Non-Compliance… risk remains high due to nature of remaining work." (4x4=16) — p.66; "NCTR performance remains challenged (circa 24% in March, improving to c.21% in April against a target of ≤15%)" — p.14, [Minutes 19 May, IPR]

[WORTH WATCHING] An internal audit of controlled drugs found unresolved stock discrepancies, errors in stock deductions and wastage recording, and controlled-drugs cupboard keys not stored in line with policy; separately the annual CD report notes a Q4 rise in "unaccounted for losses" in the emergency department.
[Evidence] "weaknesses were identified in stock management and governance processes, including unresolved stock discrepancies, errors in stock deductions and wastage recording, and one instance where controlled drugs cupboard keys were not stored in accordance with policy." — p.122, [Audit Committee Chair's Report Item 20]; "ED reports the greatest number of unaccounted for losses (a notable rise was highlighted in Q4 potentially due to lack of prompt investigation)." — p.114, [Controlled Drugs Annual Report Item 15]
