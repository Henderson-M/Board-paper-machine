## Top lines

[LEAD] Sheffield Teaching is £11.21m behind plan two months into 2026/27, reporting a £6.35m deficit where it had planned a £4.86m surplus.
[Evidence] "The Trust financial position to the end of May 2026 is an actual I&E deficit of £6.35m against a planned surplus of £4.86m, therefore giving an adverse variance against plan of £11.21m (3.9%)." Drivers listed as "Undelivered 2026/27 P&E £8.54m", "Deterioration in Directorate positions of £0.71m (excluding P&E)" and "Costs relating to the Resident Doctor Industrial Action carried out in April of £1.19m" — p.7, [Paper D — Integrated Performance Report]. The April industrial action costs are described as "not being covered from central funds" — p.3, [Paper L — Finance and Performance Committee Meeting Assurance Report]

[LEAD] The trust has identified barely half of its £65m savings target and its own finance committee says it is not yet assured the plan can be delivered.
[Evidence] "P&E plans submitted on 5th June are £37.74m against a full year target of £65.00m (£27.26m / 41.9% shortfall)." Year-to-date delivery is "£2.29m against a target of £10.83m (£8.54m / 78.8% shortfall)" — p.31, [Paper D — Integrated Performance Report]. Committee escalation: "the Committee wants to flag to the Board of Directors the existing gap in the Trust's P&E plan and the plans to close that gap and that at present the Committee is yet to be assured on whether it can be delivered having factored in ongoing issues in hitting plan with RTT and the PTL" — p.2, [Paper L — Finance and Performance Committee Meeting Assurance Report]

[LEAD] A change to the national capital allocation formula has cut Sheffield's operational capital by around £6m, leaving its four-year capital plan over-committed by £5.2m and 2027/28 over-committed by £6.9m.
[Evidence] "the change to the OCA calculation has resulted to a reduction in an OCA allocation of circa £6m less than last year, putting pressure on the capital programme for the next 4 years." And: "the more concerning issue is the £6.9m over commitment in 2027/28 which is only likely to get worse with slippage in 2026/27." 2026/27 funding is £58.2m against forecast spend of £62.2m, "an over-commitment of £4m (£1.7m this time last year)" — p.1 and p.3, [Paper K — Approval of Four Year Capital Plan and Capital Programme Update]

[LEAD] NHS England's plan sign-off letter — published in full in the pack — rated Sheffield "compliant with conditions" and made its workforce-reduction funding conditional on evidenced establishment cuts.
[Evidence] "Acceptance Status: COMPLIANT WITH CONDITIONS" — p.7, [Paper C — Chief Executive's Matters, Appendix A, letter of 9 April 2026 from NHSE North East & Yorkshire]. Conditions include "Fully developed board-approved sickness reduction plan by end of Q1 2026/27", "RTT delivery to be a minimum of 70.5%" and "NHSE Region to be observer on Trust Cancer Board". On cost-of-change money: "This support will be provided by NHS England on the condition that robust criteria is in place for accessing this funding... The organisation will need to demonstrate that the establishment of the organisation has been reduced recurrently to support sustainability and there is a reasonable payback period for the funding provided." — p.8, same paper

[LEAD] The trust told NHSE its chief finance officer had committed to eliminating unidentified savings by the end of June — a commitment the July papers show has not been met.
