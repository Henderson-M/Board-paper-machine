# Calderdale and Huddersfield NHS FT (RWY) — Public Board, 9 July 2026

Source: Combined Public Board of Directors pack, Thursday 9 July 2026 (single ~18MB PDF). Page references are to the CEO/finance/quality papers as titled; the combined pack repaginates, so paper titles are given to disambiguate.

## 1. Top lines

**LEAD** Trust is a national outlier on never events — nine in 12 months against a national average of 2.3, including a cluster of four linked to theatre processes; external support being brought in.
[Evidence] "Nine Never Events recorded (May 2025 – May 2026 reporting period)... Cluster of four recent events and one further never event declared on 8 May 2026 linked to theatre processes... Benchmarking data: Organisation identified as an outlier compared to peers... National data between April 2025 – April 2026 reported four never events and the average was 2.3." — Quality Committee highlight report / previous minutes, [Item 11 Quality Committee Chair Highlight Report]. Two further never events declared in May 2026 (IPR): "There were two never events declared in May. This is a clear area of focus for the Board." — [CEO Report, para 3.23]

**LEAD** Trust starts 2026/27 £5.9m adverse to a £20m planned deficit; full-year breakeven now hinges entirely on securing £20m of NHSE Deficit Support Funding that NHSE can claw back.
[Evidence] "At Month 2, the Trust reports an underlying year-to-date deficit of £5.0m, representing an adverse variance of £5.9m against plan... The forecast year-end position is a £20m deficit, achievement of this position will secure receipt of £20m Deficit Support Funding (DSF) to allow the Trust to reach an overall breakeven position." — [Month 02 Finance Report, Key Points to Note]. "any payment of DSF can be recovered by NHS England if the DSF is not achieved." — [CEO Report, para 6.4]

**LEAD** Delivery of the plan depends on a £36.9m CIP — already £2.1m behind at Month 2 — and a sharply increased workforce-reduction target, with a new FTE Reduction Oversight Group set up to drive it.
[Evidence] "Delivery of the financial plan is dependent on a £36.9m Cost Improvement Programme... The Trust acknowledges that the original planned workforce reduction was insufficient in the context of the current wider financial position and CIP challenge. In response, there is a planned net reduction in FTEs from 53 to 213. The Trust has established an FTE Reduction Oversight Group (ROG), chaired by the Director of Corporate Affairs... The recurrency level of the plan for 26/27 is 69%." — [Item 15 Finance and Performance Committee Chair Highlight Report]

**LEAD** Reconfiguration Full Business Case cleared HM Treasury/DHSC and the CRH build contract has been signed with Laing O'Rourke; trust capital plan for 2026/27 is £77.9m.
[Evidence] "our Full Business Case for the new clinical build at Calderdale Royal Hospital was approved in May and we signed the contract with Laing O'Rourke in June. This is a huge milestone..." — [CEO Report, paras 2.4, 5.1]. Capital plan Forecast 26/27 £78.29m against plan £77.87m — [Month 2 Consolidated Group finance, KEY METRICS table]

**LEAD** CQC well-led rating held at "good" but urgent and emergency care at Huddersfield Royal rated "requires improvement", with a regulatory breach on premises/equipment issued at both outpatient sites.
[Evidence] "Our Well-led inspection resulted in a rating of good... Urgent and Emergency Care at HRI was rated as requires improvement, with immediate actions already underway... A regulatory breach relating to premises and equipment was issued at both sites." — [CEO Report, Key Points / paras 3.3, 3.6]. Further CRH UEC and medical care reports still awaited.

**WORTH WATCHING** Substantive Director of Workforce and OD post vacant since interim cover; recruitment under way with July interviews — Director of Corporate Affairs holding the new statutory sexual-safety exec lead role in the interim.
[Evidence] "Recruitment process launched for Director of Workforce and OD on 13 May 2026 with interviews planned for July. The Chief Executive formally thanked Jason Eddleston for covering this role on an interim basis." — [May minutes, Item 55/26]. "Vicky Pickles, Director of Corporate Affairs will undertake this role until the substantive Director of Workforce and OD is appointed." — [CEO Report, para 4.8]

**WORTH WATCHING** Trust made its first mandatory NHSE regional submission of colleague-on-colleague sexual misconduct cases on 30 June 2026.
[Evidence] "The first submission was made on 30 June 2026 with details of the number of ongoing cases identified where there have been incidents of colleague-on-colleague sexual misconduct." — [CEO Report, para 4.7]

**WORTH WATCHING** System under acute flow pressure: bed occupancy circa 96%, with two never events and four patient safety incident investigations declared in May.
[Evidence] "Flow through the system is constrained by high bed occupancy (circa 96%)..." — [Item 15 F&P Highlight Report]. "we have seen cases of infections, pressure ulcers and falls increase. This is an indication of pressure on hospital services and the bed capacity." — [CEO Report, para 3.24]

**WORTH WATCHING** NOF override keeps trust in segment 3 despite improving to 1.85; NOF for 2026/27 rebuilt with 14 new scoring metrics and mandatory Staff Standards from Q1.
[Evidence] "Our National Oversight Framework score, published in June, improved in quarter 4 2025/26 from 1.98 to 1.85... We remained in segment 3 due to the financial override." — [CEO Report, para 3.15]

## 2. FOI leads

- **DSF conditional letter and the trust's self-assessment against it.** The CEO report references "a letter from NHS England outlining the areas that they will assess in determining whether the Trust is eligible for DSF" and a completed self-assessment against those criteria, plus a RAG-rated assessment considered at the June F&P Committee ([CEO Report para 6.3–6.4]; [F&P Highlight Report]). Request from CHFT: the NHSE DSF conditional/eligibility letter and the trust's full assessment/RAG rating against each condition. Why it matters: reveals exactly what NHSE is demanding in exchange for £20m and where the trust is failing conditions.

- **Never events external review / WYAAT safety discussion.** Papers say "An externally supported piece of work was being considered regarding safety process optimisation" and that selected never events were taken to a West Yorkshire Association of Acute Trusts safety meeting ([Quality Committee Highlight]). Request from CHFT: terms of reference, provider name and contract value for any external safety review; the quality summit outputs (22/23 May 2026); and the WYAAT never-events benchmarking pack. Why: names the firm and quantifies the outlier position.

- **Turnaround executive CIP progress report.** F&P Committee "approved the Turnaround executive CIP progress report" with detailed CIP and workforce plans ([F&P Highlight Report]). Request from CHFT: the Turnaround executive CIP progress report and the workforce-reduction trajectory paper. Why: confirms whether a formal turnaround regime/director is in place and the scale of planned post reductions (net FTE reduction figures cited but under-specified as "from 53 to 213").

- **Ockenden Board Assurance Statement (mortuary/post-death care).** NHSE system letter of 26 June 2026 requires a Board Assurance Statement by 31 July, to be signed off by Chair's Action ([CEO Report para 3.11]). Request from CHFT: the NHSE 26 June system letter and the trust's submitted Board Assurance Statement. Why: documents local compliance position on mortuary oversight post-Ockenden/Fuller.
