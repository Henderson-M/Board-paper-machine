# Royal Free London NHS Foundation Trust board — 3 June 2026

- **Source:** https://www.royalfree.nhs.uk/about-us/trust-board/trust-board-meetings
- **Pack:** 1 combined PDF (410pp; "Bundle Trust Board (Part 1) Public 3 June 2026")
- **Analysed:** 2026-06-05T00:00:00Z

> Note: This is a **group board** covering the Royal Free London group — Royal Free Hospital, Barnet Hospital, Chase Farm Hospital, and North Middlesex University Hospital (NMUH, which joined the group on 1 January 2025). Group chief executive: Peter (Pete) Landstrom; group chair: Mark Lam; CFO: Vicky Clarke. Pack page references below are PDF page numbers.

## Top lines

[LEAD] Group sets a £10.5m deficit plan for 2026/27 hinged on an "exceptionally challenging" £151m efficiency programme — but only £100m of schemes identified and £47m fully assured by May.
[Evidence] "The 2026/27 plan commits RFL to delivering it RFPL across the next three years – a £10.5m deficit in 2026/27, and breakeven in 2027/28 and 2028/29... delivery of an exceptionally challenging efficiency programme, beginning with delivery of £151.0m of efficiencies in 2026/27... RFL has agreed to deliver £129.9m of recurrent FIP, and £21.1m of non-recurrent FIP." — p.82, [BAF Risk 7 – Finance Activity and Productivity]. "The committee noted that £100m of FIP opportunities had been identified against a requirement of £151m, representing progress but leaving a material gap. £47m of schemes were fully assured reflecting issues with scheme maturity and pace of delivery." — p.293, [FISC Assurance Highlight Report, May 2026]

[LEAD] Group ran a £37.7m deficit at 2025/26 year-end, "favourable to plan" but reliant on non-recurrent funding, with recurrent CIP under-delivered.
[Evidence] "The committee noted a year-end deficit of £37.7m, favourable to plan and slightly improved from Month 11, largely due to nonrecurrent funding support. Underlying cost pressures remained significant... The trust delivered its overall FIP target; however, a shortfall in recurrent delivery was noted, creating additional pressures for 2026/27." — p.289, [FISC Assurance Highlight Report, April 2026]

[LEAD] Month 1 (April 2026) already over plan: £11.9m YTD deficit, £2.9m adverse, driven by unfunded resident-doctor strike costs that NHSE has confirmed it will not fund.
[Evidence] "At the end of April 2026 (month 1) the Group reported a year-to-date deficit of £11.9m, against the RFPL and excluding deficit support funding. The group was adverse to the year-to-date RFPL plan by £2.9m. This reflected the cost impact of unplanned industrial action, arising during the period 7 to 13 April 2026... NHSE has confirmed that Industrial action cost pressures will not be funded and that providers must mitigate these pressures in order to continue to receive deficit support funding." — p.108, [Integrated Performance Report, May 2026]

[LEAD] Cash is flagged as the most immediate risk; if savings slip the group says it will reprofile supplier payments and pause capital projects.
[Evidence] "Cash balances ended month 1 with a balance of £75m, £7m adverse to plan... The maintenance of sufficient cash is predicated upon the delivery of the £151m FIP and its cash releasing savings. Should FIP delivery fall behind plan, we will need to instigate refreshed cash management protocols which would include reprofiled supplier payments and pausing capital projects." — p.117, [Integrated Performance Report – Finance, May 2026]

[LEAD] Mortuary failures across the group's PFI sites and a wrong-body release at North Mid — a year on from the Fuller Inquiry's concluding report.
[Evidence] "We have experienced a total of 30 incidents of equipment failure across the RFL in 2025 with 29 of these being within PFI providers" (Barnet and North Middlesex). "There was one HTA Reportable Incident on 19th of December in which there was a release to funeral directors of the wrong deceased patient. This was recognised on January 2nd 2026 and has been reported to the Human Tissue Authority as a HTARI. A rapid review took place and an investigation is ongoing." — p.320, p.325, [Annual Mortuaries Report 2025]

[LEAD] Three never events so far this financial year — two wrong-site surgery, one retained guidewire.
[Evidence] "There have been three never events since the beginning of the financial year. Two related to the category of wrong site surgery and one to retention of an object namely a guidewire. Work is underway to introduce human factors training into specific areas such as theatres." — p.153, [Quality Committee Assurance Highlight Report, 19 May 2026]

[WORTH WATCHING] North Middlesex University Hospital under a "formal internal turnaround approach" within the group, with external support (Kingsgate) embedded.
[Evidence] "Corporate services, particularly estates and facilities, were identified as materially off plan and subject to a formal internal turnaround approach." — p.291, [FISC Highlight, May 2026]. "Further external support continues to be in place in Q12026/27 to rapidly mature areas of high priority (medical optimisation, nursing optimisation, theatre productivity, delivery of the workforce plan) in NMUH. Insights/learning from Kingsgate support is currently being embedded into business-as-usual practice." — p.83, [BAF Risk 7]

[WORTH WATCHING] Royal Free Hospital itself reported a "significant overspend" in month 1; commissioner contracts for 2026/27 remain untriangulated and unagreed beyond WNL ICB.
[Evidence] "Health unit performance was variable: Barnet performed positively, North Mid delivered close to balance (excluding industrial action), while Royal Free reported a significant overspend driven by activity and workforce pressures." — p.293, [FISC Highlight, May 2026]. "RFL has aligned its contracting and income assumptions with WNL; however, RFL's position is untriangulated with other commissioners as we have yet to receive formal offers... Any unagreed contracts will be escalated to the ICB and NHSE." — p.82, [BAF Risk 7]

[WORTH WATCHING] Two patient deaths in North Mid's emergency department "within a short time of each other," under PSIRF investigation; severe-harm/death incident data at North Mid unreliable due to merger IT.
[Evidence] "Two patient deaths occurred within the ED department at North Mid hospital within a short time of each other. There will be investigated through our PSIRF processes." — p.153, [Quality Committee, 19 May 2026]. "Attention was given to a reported spike in severe harm and death incidents at North Mid, with concerns raised regarding data reliability... inconsistencies between local and centrally reported data, including the re-grading of incidents... and the use of multiple reporting systems during the merger transition, had contributed to this position." — p.149, [Quality Committee, 21 April 2026]

[WORTH WATCHING] Internal audit finds "limited assurance" on medical device management; audit committee "not assured" current arrangements provide sufficient control.
[Evidence] "The committee received an update highlighting limited assurance in medical devices management, with variation across sites and gaps in governance, systems and training oversight. The committee was not assured that current arrangements provide sufficient control, noting the absence of a consistent organisational framework for equipment management and assurance." — p.394, [Audit Committee Assurance Highlight Report, May 2026]

[WORTH WATCHING] Group remains in NHSE Oversight Framework segment 3 and cannot exit while in deficit; effectiveness/experience-of-care domain dropped from 2 to 3.
[Evidence] "The RFL remains in segment 3 overall. RFL is ended 25/26 at a deficit and organisations with financial deficits cannot score higher than segment 3, even if some clinical or operational metrics are strong... effectiveness and experience of care (3) – down from 2 in Q2." — p.118, [Integrated Performance Report – NHS Oversight Framework]

## FOI leads

[FOI] Request the Annual Mortuaries Report appendices and the HTA correspondence on the North Mid wrong-body release. The pack says the 19 Dec wrong-deceased release was reported to the Human Tissue Authority and "an investigation is ongoing" but gives no findings. Request from RFL (and HTA): the HTARI report, the rapid review/investigation outcome, and any HTA correspondence or conditions on licences 12013/12562. Also request details of Risk 958 (no CCTV at North Mid mortuary, against Fuller recommendations) and the June 2025 unauthorised contractor access incident.

[FOI] Request the Kingsgate engagement details at NMUH. The BAF names Kingsgate as providing external support to North Mid (medical/nursing optimisation, theatre productivity, workforce plan) but gives no scope or value. Request from RFL: the contract value, term, deliverables, and any review report produced.

[FOI] Request the £151m Financial Improvement Programme breakdown and the "formal internal turnaround approach" for corporate estates and facilities. The pack states corporate estates/facilities are "materially off plan and subject to a formal internal turnaround approach" but does not describe it. Request the FIP scheme list (identified vs assured), and the terms/scope of the estates and facilities turnaround.

[FOI] Request the medical devices internal audit report. The audit committee escalated "limited assurance" on medical device governance from the Trust Quality Group and requested a comprehensive action plan. Request the full internal audit report, the limited-assurance findings, and the action plan.

[FOI] Request the £0.8m accounting adjustment in Royal Free London Property Services Ltd. FISC noted an "accounting adjustment of £0.8m within Royal Free London Property Services Ltd... requiring audit follow-up." Request the audit follow-up and explanation of the adjustment.
