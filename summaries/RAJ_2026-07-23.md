## FOI leads

- **External audit pack (to MSE, and to KPMG's client via the trust):** the full ISA 260 year-end report, the Auditor's Annual Report including the three VfM significant weakness findings, the draft audit opinion and the signed letter of representation for 2025/26. The pack confirms all four exist and were recommended to board on 19 June 2026 (p.240–242) but publishes none of the text. Matters because a triple VfM weakness modification is unusual and the underlying findings on governance are not otherwise public.
- **Sepsis review (to MSE):** terms of reference, commissioning body, name of the external reviewer, and the final report. Referenced twice as "independent and external" but never described (p.18).
- **Catering / soft FM market testing (to MSE):** list of services being market tested, the options appraisal, indicative contract values and timescales, plus any correspondence with Unison (p.21).
- **Safeguarding internal audit action (to MSE and Mid and South Essex ICB):** the internal audit report containing the safeguarding action, the action's original deadline, and correspondence with the ICB explaining the delay — the audit committee believes the trust may be outside risk tolerance because of it (p.246).
- **Section 31 conditions (to MSE and CQC):** the two current Section 31 notices and the Section 29A paediatrics notice, plus the trust's exit submission for the Broomfield maternity Section 31 (p.131, p.244, p.257).
- **Cancer harm reviews (to MSE):** the number of cancer harm reviews outstanding, the age profile of the backlog, and any harm identified to date (p.261).

## Notes and inference

- [INFERENCE] The pack contains no separate agenda item for board approval of the annual report and accounts — the audit committee recommended them to board on 19 June 2026 (p.245) and the July agenda (p.1–3) does not list an ARA item, suggesting approval happened at an earlier or extraordinary session. Worth checking with the trust when and where the ARA was formally adopted and when it will be published.
- [INFERENCE] The £63.6m 2025/26 outturn and the £78.7m 2026/27 planned deficit are both stated in the pack; the pack does not itself compare them, and no comparison is drawn here.
- The trust's own papers describe it as "operating within a high level of national oversight since April 2024" (p.80, [10 ii OTIP Executive Meeting report]). A non-executive director's question recorded in the previous minutes refers to the trust being "in NOF tier 5" (p.14, [4 DRAFT Board Minutes 28 05 2026]) — the segment is not otherwise confirmed in this pack.
- Auditor change: the previous minutes record that "Grant Thornton was appointed as the Trust's statutory external auditor for an initial contract term of 5 years with an option to extend for two more years" (p.17), while the 2025/26 audit reports summarised in this pack are from KPMG (p.242).
- Other items noted but not developed: theatre chiller failures causing "excessive temperatures and humidity levels, affecting theatre utilisation" (p.261); corridor care "remains above trajectory" (p.216); ambulance handovers 33 minutes in May against a 23-minute plan and 38 minutes in June (p.30); 2,788 twelve-hour A&E waits in June (p.30); Synnovis cyber incident response (p.243); Synlab pathology contract transition escalated to board (p.246); 249 FTSU concerns raised in 2025/26 with 40% of staff choosing not to escalate (p.151).
