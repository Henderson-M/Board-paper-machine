[LEAD] Suspected theft of codeine across four named wards was reported to the police and investigated by counter-fraud, with no individual identified; CCTV has been installed in every medicines room in the trust.
[Evidence] "One incident involved suspected misappropriation of codeine tablets following the identification of significant discrepancies between stock supplied and patient administration records on a ward. This triggered a wider investigation, which identified similar significant discrepancies across a number of wards, including Caton, Dott, Chavasse and Cairns. The investigation was undertaken with support from MIAA Counter Fraud and the Controlled Drugs Liaison Officer; however, an individual responsible could not be identified." — p.286, [Controlled Drugs Accountable Officer Report 2025/26, Consent Agenda Item 16.3]
[Evidence] "This incident was reported to the Regional CD Intelligence Group and to the Police." — p.284, same paper; "CCTV was invested in and installed across all Trust medicines rooms" — p.287, same paper. Overall assurance level: "Overall, partial assurance is provided" — p.284.

[LEAD] The Liverpool pharmacy site that supplies the Walton Centre's controlled drugs has gone eight years without an MHRA inspection, and a commissioned mock inspection found deficiencies.
[Evidence] "due to the national inspection backlog following the COVID-19 pandemic, the Aintree site has not undergone an MHRA inspection for eight years. During this period, regulatory expectations and inspection standards have become increasingly stringent. Recognising the potential risk to service continuity should the licence be suspended or revoked, the Chief Pharmacist commissioned an independent mock inspection by the Regional Quality Assurance Team… The inspection has identified a number of deficiencies requiring action which are in progress." — p.289–290, [Controlled Drugs Accountable Officer Report 2025/26, Item 16.3]
[Evidence] On the CQC's national CD recommendation to resource the accountable officer properly: "The WCFT at point of publication is not compliant with this requirement however the further investment in the SLA will mitigate this risk." — p.290, same paper.
