**Pack note before you read on.** The 16 September meeting is a Coventry and Warwickshire / Herefordshire and Worcestershire ICB "meeting in common" chaired by Crishni Waring with a single joint chief executive, Simon Trickett. Only three documents were published at the time of this scan: the two-page agenda, the governance log spreadsheet, and the chair-approved draft notes of the 15 July 2026 meeting. The substantive September papers listed on the agenda (CEO report, Spotlight: Planned Care, Neighbourhood Health Framework Review, integrated quality and performance report, Financial Plan and Delivery 2026-27, board assurance framework, committee escalation) were **not** on the page. Almost everything below is therefore lifted from the July minutes and the governance log, both of which are formally part of the September pack.

## FOI leads

1. **NHS England's 2025-26 annual assessment of both ICBs.** The CEO report is said to have carried "positive headline findings emerging from the NHS England (NHSE) annual assessment process" while "recognising that some areas required further improvement" (p.5). Neither the findings nor the improvement areas are set out. Request from NHS England (Midlands) and from the ICB: the full annual assessment output and covering letter for Coventry and Warwickshire ICB and for Herefordshire and Worcestershire ICB, 2025-26.

2. **The merger submission to NHS England.** Request from the ICB: the full submission recommending merger from April 2027, including the options appraisal, the boundary options considered and rejected, the workings behind the "estimated additional £1m of running cost savings", and any correspondence with NHS England, local MPs, councils and the West Midlands Combined Authority about it. Also request NHS England's decision, which per HSJ's 19 June story was due in September.

3. **The clustered operating model staff consultation.** Final consultation closed 15 July, structure confirmed in August, and the BAF now carries an increased risk "reflecting the impact of voluntary redundancies and on going organisational change" (pp.5 and 10). Request from the ICB: the consultation outcome document, posts in scope, posts deleted, number of voluntary redundancies agreed and total exit cost, and the final structure chart.

4. **George Eliot Hospital ophthalmology clinical harm review.** Request from George Eliot Hospital Trust and from the ICB: the terms of reference and any completed report of the comprehensive clinical harm review, the number of patients in the review cohort, the number of confirmed harms by grade, and the size of the ophthalmology follow-up backlog.

5. **The NHS England neighbourhood health review.** The minutes refer to "the positive feedback received following the recent NHSE neighbourhood health review" (p.5) without describing it. Request from the ICB and NHS England: the review report, feedback letter and any scoring or assessment framework applied to this cluster.

6. **Right to Choose ADHD and autism spend in Herefordshire and Worcestershire.** No figure is disclosed in the pack. Request from the ICB: monthly Right to Choose ADHD and autism assessment expenditure for 2024-25, 2025-26 and 2026-27 to date, broken down by provider, plus any quality assurance reports, audits or contract concerns relating to those providers.
