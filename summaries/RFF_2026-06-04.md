## Top lines

[LEAD] Trust opened 2026/27 £0.5m behind plan after six days of resident doctor industrial action it could not mitigate, and is banking on £22m of efficiencies to break even.
[Evidence] "In M1 Trust delivered an underlying deficit of (£1,917k) vs a planned deficit of (£1,416k)" … "The adverse variance of (£501k) has been driven by 6 days of Resident Doctors industrial action, the impact of which has not been mitigated" … "We are forecasting to deliver a break even position assuming £22,000k of EPP savings will be delivered in year [and] Cost pressures, including Industrial Action will be mitigated" — p.343, [Finance Report M1 (April) 2026/27]

[LEAD] The 2026/27 plan assumed a 2% pay award but a 3.3% award has landed, an unfunded pressure carried in month 1.
[Evidence] "3.3% pay award has been include in M1, the plan assumed a pay award of 2%" — p.343, [Finance Report M1]

[LEAD] Trust closed 2025/26 with a £6.08m deficit against a planned £158k deficit — only brought back to NHSE-adjusted breakeven "after releasing further flexibilities in month".
[Evidence] "As at Month 12 the Trust has a consolidated deficit of £6,084k against a planned deficit of £158k. NHS England (NHSE) adjusted performance is £0k variance, after taking into account granted asset income (-£399k), depreciation in respect of donated assets (£56k), granted assets (£99k) and £6,329k revaluation impairment against an NHSE adjusted plan of breakeven. However, this is after releasing further flexibilities in month." — p.351, [IPR Executive Summary, April 2026]

[LEAD] Two board members — chief operating officer Lorraine Burnett and associate NED Mark Strong — attended their final board meeting in April; two further senior leavers (FTSU Guardian Theresa Rastall and Diane Edwards) are also departing.
[Evidence] "The Chair thanked LB and MS for their dedication over the years, noting that this marked their final Board of Directors meeting." — p.5, [Draft Minutes 2 April 2026]; "the Board is asked to note the significant contribution to the People Committee of Theresa Rastall and Diane Edwards, both of whom will shortly be leaving the Trust and for whom this was their last meeting." — p.171, [People Committee Chair's Log]

[LEAD] A proposed cut to the trust's "constitutional standards" risk score was blocked by the finance committee, which referred the question of patient harm on the waiting list to the quality committee.
[Evidence] "In relation to constitutional standards (Risk 2592), although a reduction in score had been proposed based on the absence of recorded patient harm, this was not supported by the Finance and Performance Committee, which instead referred the matter to the Quality and Governance Committee for further assurance on patient harm reviews for those on the waiting list." — p.9, [Draft Minutes 2 April 2026]

[WORTH WATCHING] Trust has issued notice to terminate elements of its oral and maxillofacial surgery (OMFS) service, with the service reverting to "purely oral" from June 2026 and all cancer patients transferred to Sheffield Teaching Hospitals.
[Evidence] "Notice to terminate elements of the service that cannot be delivered has been issued. It is anticipated that the risk will be resolved following the conclusion of the notice period in June 2026. There is plan that the service will revert to purely oral from June 2026." … "All cancer patients are being transferred to STH." — p.321, [Corporate Risk Register, Risk 3014, May 2026]

[WORTH WATCHING] Cancer 62-day performance sits at 65.3% against an 85% target, with urology and colorectal named as the worst-performing tumour sites.
[Evidence] "Performance against the 62-day treatment standard of 75% was not achieved at 65.3%. Urology and Colorectal being the largest under achieving tumour site." — p.351, [IPR Executive Summary]

[WORTH WATCHING] A turnaround programme led by a Turnaround Director now sits behind the trust's two highest-scored finance risks (in-year plan delivery and 2–5 year sustainability, both 20).
[Evidence] "This risk is also linked to the financial turnaround programme led by the Turnaround Director and is more closely aligned with two Board Assurance Framework (BAF) risks, both scored at 20: Risk 1713, relating to the inability to deliver the in-year financial plan, and Risk 2845, relating to the inability to improve the Trust's financial sustainability over the next 2–5 years." — p.315, [Corporate Risk Register, Risk 1199]

[WORTH WATCHING] Staff reports of discrimination from patients and relatives are rising, flagged in the 2025 NHS Staff Survey at 7.16%.
[Evidence] "areas for improvement include raising concerns, colleague-to-colleague relationships, access to nutritious food, and a rising trend in discrimination from patients and relatives (7.16%)." — p.8, [Draft Minutes 2 April 2026, item 26/10 Staff Survey Final Results]

[WORTH WATCHING] Resident-doctor exception reports/fines jumped in Q1 2026 after the new contract, with the committee querying an apparently "significant increase in headcount/budget costs".
[Evidence] "a more marked increase in Q1 2026. This is due in large part to the implementation of the new contract. Residents continue to struggle with workload intensity, patient acuity and the impact of digital systems on efficiency… The Committee sought clarification on what appears to be a significant increase in headcount/budget costs, seeking to understand if this represents a real increase in headcount." — p.172, [People Committee Chair's Log, Guardian of Safe Working Q1]

[WORTH WATCHING] E. coli bacteraemia flagged as a high special-cause concern — 14 hospital-acquired cases in April against a target of 4.
[Evidence] "During the same reporting period, 14 hospital acquired cases of Escherichia coli bacteraemia were identified. Of these; 4 had a hepatobiliary source and after action reviews will be undertaken" — p.358, [IPR Quality: Infection Control]

## FOI leads

[FOI] Request the OMFS termination notice and the South Yorkshire ICB service-sustainability paperwork. The pack confirms notice has been served and the service is reconfiguring, but does not disclose which elements are being terminated, the income/cost impact, or the volume of patients transferred to Sheffield. Request from BHNFT (and SY ICB Sustainable Clinical Services programme): the termination notice, the January 2025 Executive Team paper recommending "service failure and referral to Acute Federation", and the September 2026 commissioning review scope. — p.320–321, [Corporate Risk Register, Risk 3014]

[FOI] Request the departure terms for the outgoing COO and NEDs. The board recorded April 2026 as the final meeting for COO Lorraine Burnett and associate NED Mark Strong, and the People Committee noted Theresa Rastall and Diane Edwards leaving — none with stated reasons. Request from BHNFT any settlement/COT3 agreements, leaving dates and successor arrangements for these roles. — p.5, [Minutes]; p.171, [People Committee Chair's Log]

[FOI] Request the waiting-list patient-harm review behind Risk 2592. The finance committee declined to lower the constitutional-standards risk score and referred patient-harm-on-waiting-list assurance to the quality committee — request the harm-review methodology, numbers of patients reviewed and any harm findings. — p.9, [Minutes]

[FOI] Request the ICB-approved capital overspend detail. The IPR says the £22.069m capital programme is £0.350m above plan and that "This overspend was agreed with the ICB and reflects system-wide capital slippage across the region." Request the agreement and which schemes/regional slippage are involved. — p.351, [IPR Executive Summary]
