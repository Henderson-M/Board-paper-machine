## Top lines

[LEAD] NHSE has lifted HIOW ICB's 2023 financial enforcement undertakings — but Portsmouth, Southampton and Isle of Wight trusts are getting a *revised* set of undertakings instead of release.
[Evidence] "The remaining providers with undertakings (Portsmouth Hospitals University NHS Trust, University Hospital Southampton NHS Foundation Trust and Isle of Wight NHS Trust) are in the process of having a revised set of undertakings agreed." Compliance certificates were issued to the ICB, South Central Ambulance Service, Hampshire Hospitals FT and HIOW Healthcare FT; Anne Eden's letter (15 September 2026) warns release "should not be interpreted as a conclusion that the System's underlying financial challenges have been fully addressed" — exec summary and enclosed NHSE letter, Item 10, [Undertakings Compliance Certificate]

[LEAD] The ICB's Finance Committee was told of a system financial risk of up to £40m sitting across Portsmouth Hospitals and University Hospital Southampton.
[Evidence] "The Committee also discussed that there now appeared to be a system financial risk of up to £40m located across Portsmouth Hospitals University NHS Trust and University Hospital Southampton NHS Foundation Trust." — section A (ADVISE), 22 September 2026 meeting, Item 12, [Board Assurance Report: Finance Committee]

[LEAD] HIOW ICB expects to be placed in Segment 3 of the new National Oversight Framework, with three alert-rated metrics.
[Evidence] The Executive Committee noted "one metric rated assured, seven advisory and three alert, and the indicative assessment that the ICB was likely to enter Segment 3" and "agreed the strategic ambition to… progress towards Segment 2" — section 3.a.v, Item 9, [Executive Committee Report]

[LEAD] The North East Hampshire transfer (from the disaggregated Frimley ICB, which carried an underlying deficit) is flagged as a risk that could materially move the ICB's year-end position.
[Evidence] "The Committee noted that the North East Hampshire transfer remained a significant financial risk and that a senior-level escalation meeting was planned for later that week. The outcome from the discussion could have a material impact on the year-end financial position." (ALERT) — Item 12, [Board Assurance Report: Finance Committee]; the M6 finance report cites "uncertainty associated with the North-East Hampshire transfer following the disaggregation of Frimley Integrated Care Board and its associated underlying financial deficit" — p.2, Item 11, [Finance Report Month 6]

[WORTH WATCHING] A Never Event at Portsmouth Hospitals has been escalated to the board, UHS remains in enhanced oversight, and UHS received an unannounced CQC inspection in October.
[Evidence] The Executive Committee "noted the National Oversight Framework red alert relating to long-standing complaints, ongoing enhanced oversight at University Hospital Southampton NHS Foundation Trust, and agreed escalation of the Never Event at Portsmouth Hospitals University NHS Trust" — section 3.a.iii, Item 9, [Executive Committee Report]; "the Trust received an unannounced inspection of services at the Trust in October 2026" — section 3.2b, Item 8, [Quality Report September 2026]. [WORTH WATCHING] The ICB's governance review has slipped and external governance support is being brought in to rescue the timetable.
[Evidence] "Progress has been slower than originally intended. Targeted external governance support is now being brought in to provide additional capacity and expertise and accelerate delivery of the wider Governance Review." — section 1, Item 15, [Governance Review Update]

[WORTH WATCHING] A Personal Health Budget investigation found no fraud but exposed oversight weaknesses — and may force a loss disclosure in the ICB's 2026/27 accounts.
[Evidence] "the case had highlighted weaknesses in historical oversight and monitoring arrangements relating to Personal Health Budgets… the Personal Health Budget matter raised at 1.1 in this report may, subject to completion of the ongoing review, require a loss disclosure within the 2026/27 Annual Report and Accounts" — sections 1.1 (ALERT) and 2.4, Item 13, [Board Assurance Report: Audit and Risk Committee]

[WORTH WATCHING] Acute providers are behind planned activity — partly blamed on the UHS fire — and the ICB's own breakeven forecast assumes a recovery it concedes may not happen.
[Evidence] "Providers have identified a number of contributory factors, including industrial action earlier in the year and the loss of capacity associated with the fire at University Hospital Southampton NHS Foundation Trust… current information and historical performance suggest that they may not fully recover the activity shortfall accumulated to date." — pp.2 and 6, Item 11, [Finance Report Month 6]. CIP delivery is £60.9m against a planned £76.5m (80%, a £15.6m shortfall) on a £145m programme — p.4, same paper.

[WORTH WATCHING] The ICB missed all three of its 2025/26 infection targets, including 25 MRSA bloodstream infections against a target of zero.
[Evidence] "25 methicillin-resistant Staphylococcus aureus (MRSA) bloodstream infections against a target of zero cases… 586 Clostridioides difficile infections, 65 above the annual target… 1,480 Escherichia coli (E. coli) bloodstream infections, 230 above the annual target, which is an increase compared to 2024/25". Overall assurance: "Partial". — exec summary / p.8, Item 8, [Infection Prevention and Control Annual Report 2025/26]
