## Top lines

[WORTH WATCHING] 2025/26 accounts published: £3.3m surplus is driven *entirely* by NHSE deficit support funding; underlying recurrent position is a £4.2m deficit.
[Evidence] "The Trust has achieved a £3.3m surplus for the year; this represents a £3.3m favourable variance against a breakeven plan... The surplus is driven in full by Deficit Support Funding awarded to the Trust by NHSE." and "The recurrent position is a £4.2m deficit with key issues being the recurrent 2025/26 QIPP gap, unfunded local authority inflation plus pay award, employers' national insurance and continence product cost pressures." — p.16, [Annual Report 2025/26, AGM pack]

[WORTH WATCHING] Board asked to formally adopt the IHRA definition of antisemitism and the Government definition of anti-Muslim hostility by the 31 July 2026 national deadline in response to the Lord Mann review; the trust currently has no dedicated anti-racism policy.
[Evidence] Nine immediate actions rated "2 actions Green... 6 actions Amber... 1 action Red – requiring formal Board decision before the national deadline of 31st July 2026" — the Red action is "Adopt the government definition of anti-Muslim hostility and the... (IHRA) working definition of antisemitism". Gap noted under Action 3: "No dedicated Anti-racism policy" — p.218, p.222, p.226, [Lord Mann Review: Trust Position Against the Nine Immediate Actions, item 3.3.2]

[WORTH WATCHING] Month 3 (June) finance: reported breakeven but an underlying £2.9m deficit from unidentified CIP against a £14.3m savings target; break-even forecast "reliant on delivery of the cost improvement programme and signature of 2026/27 income contracts."
[Evidence] "The underlying financial position is a deficit of £2.9m due to current unidentified CIP. The CIP target for 2026/27 is £14.3m; CLCH is forecasting a shortfall of £2.9m at M3 and a recurrent shortfall of £2.9m." and FSTC: "the year-end break-even forecast was reliant on delivery of the cost improvement programme and signature of 2026/27 income contracts. The associated risk of reserves utilisation if not delivered, was highlighted." — p.36–37 & p.32, [Integrated Finance & Performance Report M3 / FSTC assurance report]

[WORTH WATCHING] Staff Survey 2025 shows deterioration on morale, burnout and development — 56 of the questions declined in sentiment; burnout worsening — though CLCH still scores above regional/system comparators.
[Evidence] "56 questions concluded a decrease in sentiment, 32 remained within the same range, and 11 questions presented an increase." Burnout: "never/rarely feel burnt out... scored 34.3% in 2024, decreasing to 30.8% in 2025." "Often/always look forward to going to work" fell 63%→59.5%; staff recommending CLCH as a place to work 66.7% (down from 68.93%). Scored lower than 2024 on 6 People Promise elements incl. Morale and Engagement. — p.150–151, [Staff Survey 2025 results, item 3.3.1]

[WORTH WATCHING] All-Age Continuing Healthcare and Complex Care services for West and North London transferred to CLCH on 1 July 2026 — a service expansion to be folded into trust reporting from Month 4.
[Evidence] "On 1 July 2026 the All Age Continuing Healthcare and Complex Care services in West and North London transferred to CLCH." — p.13, [CEO's report 2.2a]

[WORTH WATCHING] New Chief People Officer Ema Ojiako started 29 June 2026 — she leads the Lord Mann action plan.
[Evidence] "I would like to welcome Ema Ojiako, our new Chief People Officer, to the Trust. Ema started with us on 29 June 2026." — p.15, [CEO's report 2.2a]
