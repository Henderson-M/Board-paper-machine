[LEAD] The board paper's "National Update" attributes specific new detail on the NHS England/DHSC transfer to the health secretary directly: regional NHS England teams will NOT move into DHSC after all, instead sitting in a "temporary Special Health Authority" — described in the paper as "the biggest departure from earlier expectations." [Flag: worth checking independently whether this framing/detail is already public elsewhere before treating as exclusive — it is reported here as the trust's own briefing note on what the Secretary of State confirmed.]
[Evidence] "The Secretary of State for Health and Social Care, Yvette Cooper, confirmed that NHS England will still be brought back into the Department of Health and Social Care (DHSC) on 1 April 2027, subject to legislation passing Parliament. However, she has modified the delivery model... Regional NHS England teams will NOT move into DHSC. This is the biggest departure from earlier expectations. The seven regional NHS teams will instead sit within a temporary Special Health Authority" — Chief Executive's Report, System Update / National Update

[Evidence] "NHS England checkpoint review was positive and support confirmed, with submission of the full business case by December 2026 and completion of the transaction achievable for 1 April 2027...
