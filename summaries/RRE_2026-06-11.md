## Top lines

[LEAD] Trust's reported £7.1m year-end surplus masks a £20.8m underlying deficit, with the surplus largely built on late non-recurrent funding including £4.9m of NHSE deficit support funding.
[Evidence] "The Trust is reporting an adjusted surplus of £7.1m at the financial year end, against the planned break-even position... It is important to acknowledge that the surplus achieved over the break-even position is substantially attributable to non-recurrent funding received late into the financial year... The normalised position for the Trust, excluding these strategic non-recurrent transactions, is an operational deficit of £2.3m. The Trust continues to report an underlying deficit of £20.8m." — p.82, [Financial Performance 2025-26 – M12, Enc M]

[LEAD] The £7.1m surplus relied on a £4.9m late NHSE deficit support funding allocation plus c£9.5m released central contingencies, £4.1m corporate restructure enabling costs, £2m ICS reimbursement and £1.2m mental health prescribing funding; excluding the DSF the adjusted surplus falls to £2.2m.
[Evidence] "When excluding deficit support funding (DSF) of £4.9m, the adjusted surplus would be £2.2m. Other strategic non-recurrent items affecting the year-end position include a £2m funding reimbursement to support the Staffordshire ICS position, funding of £1.2m to support mental health prescribing pressures, the release of central contingencies amounting to c£9.5m, and corporate restructure enabling costs of £4.1m." — p.87, [Financial Performance 2025-26 – M12, Enc M]
