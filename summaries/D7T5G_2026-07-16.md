## Top lines

[LEAD] NHS England has rated Essex ICB's medium-term plan only "Partially Compliant" and given conditional acceptance, demanding a "targeted and detailed recovery plan" for elective care.
[Evidence] "Acceptance Status: Conditional… Partially Compliant; conditions to achieve full compliance are set out in Annex One" and "Specifically in elective care a targeted and detailed recovery plan is required to mitigate the current shortfalls" — NHSE East of England regional director Clare Panniker, 22 April 2026 — p.25-27, [CEO Report Appendix B: 2026/27 planning close-down letter]

[LEAD] The ICB's own three-year plan shows its elective waiting list growing, not shrinking — projected to finish 2028/29 at 216,013 against a target of 145,164, a 48.81% overshoot — with 18-week RTT compliance planned at just 63.2% next year against the 70% national target.
[Evidence] "Total Waiting List Mar-27 223,102 [target] 236,659 [plan] 6.08%… Mar-29 145,164 [target] 216,013 [plan] 48.81%"; "Percentage of RTT waiting list within 18 weeks (national target 70%) Mar-27 69.6% [target] 63.2% [plan] -6.39%" — p.29, p.31, [CEO Report planning annex – headline/supporting metrics]

[LEAD] Community services 18-week performance is planned to collapse across the period — 59.4% against a 78% target in 2026/27, falling to 39.7% against 80% by 2028/29 (a 40-percentage-point miss).
[Evidence] "Percentage of people on waiting list for Community Services… waiting 18 weeks or less Mar-27 78.0% [target] 59.4% [plan] -18.64%… Mar-29 80.0% 39.7% -40.33%" — p.30, [CEO Report planning annex – headline metrics]

[LEAD] Mid and South Essex FT remains in the lowest NHSE oversight segment (NOF 4) with quality summits in place, and is subject to multiple CQC enforcement notices — a Section 29a for children's services and Section 31 notices for Basildon urgent/emergency care and Broomfield maternity.
[Evidence] "MSEFT… Remain in NOF 4 – with quality summits in place… Section 29a in place for Basildon & Broomfield Children & Young People (CYP)… Section 31 in place for Basildon Urgent Emergency Care and Broomfield maternity" — p.110, [Finance and Quality Assurance Report – System Quality Position Q1]

[LEAD] The new ICB has already identified £14m of unmitigated risk in year one of a breakeven plan that requires 2.5% efficiencies a year.
[Evidence] "The ICB has a plan to break-even, in each year of the planning round. This will require efficiencies of c2.5% annually and in year one unmitigated risk of £14m has been identified" — p.101, [Commissioning, Quality and Resource Committee – Executive Director Report, Key Financials]

[LEAD] The Lampard Inquiry has refocused its July 2026 hearings on "EPUT Engagement and Compliance Issues" and the Chair is prepared to make interim recommendations; Essex ICB is leading the three-ICB legal response, with Mills & Reeve LLP and Samantha Broadfoot KC instructed.
[Evidence] "The July 2026 hearings will now focus on… Essex Partnership University NHS Foundation Trust (EPUT) Engagement and Compliance Issues"; "The Chair has confirmed she is prepared to make interim recommendations on observations and the use of technology, and resuscitation"; "Mills & Reeve LLP have been appointed as Legal advisers. Samantha Broadfoot King's Counsel (KC) is appointed" — p.79, p.76, [The Lampard Inquiry Update]

[WORTH WATCHING] All four of the new ICB's strategic-objective risks were scored 16 (Red) and "Outside Appetite" at its first assurance report — including on financial and clinical sustainability.
[Evidence] "There is a risk that NHS Essex is unable to secure the long-term financial and clinical sustainability of services across Essex… Current Risk Score: 4 x 4 = 16 (Red)… Risk Appetite Position: Outside Appetite" — p.91 (and identical 16-Red/Outside-Appetite scores at p.52, p.72), [Strategic Assurance Framework]

[WORTH WATCHING] A safety-related ambulance death and EPUT patient deaths are logged in the quality report: EEAST "fatal harm" from a failure to recognise a cardiac rhythm, and three death incidents relating to patients known to EPUT.
[Evidence] "EEAST – Fatal harm reported to ICB - failure to recognise VF rhythm- thematic review underway"; "EPUT… 3 incidents raised relating to deaths of patients known to EPUT" — p.111, p.110, [Finance and Quality Assurance Report – System Quality Position Q1]

[WORTH WATCHING] 20 Essex GP practices have signalled potential withdrawal from data-sharing agreements under BMA collective action — flagged by the audit committee as a risk to integrated neighbourhood teams.
[Evidence] "The ICB has received standard letters from 20 practices across Essex regarding potential withdrawal from data sharing agreements. This follows the British Medical Association (BMA) discussions regarding Collective Action"; audit committee "Alert" on "GP withdrawal from data sharing agreements" — p.61, [Neighbourhood Report]; p.121, [Audit, Risk and Compliance Committee Summary]

[Evidence] Three consecutive performance slides marked "DUMMY DATA" under "7. ICB National Oversight Metrics" and "ICB Operating Plan Metrics" — p.106-108, [Finance and Quality Assurance Report].
