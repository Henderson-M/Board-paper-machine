## Top lines

[LEAD] BWC, as lead provider of the West Midlands CAMHS Provider Collaborative, reports that Ivetsey Bank Hospital is under Level 3 enhanced oversight with PICU admissions paused and two CQC Section 29 warning notices issued.
[Evidence] "Ivetsey Bank Hospital remains under Level 3 enhanced oversight with admissions to its psychiatric intensive care unit paused and two CQC Section 29 warning notices issued. A multi-agency Quality Improvement Group and enhanced on-site oversight are in place... The Board is asked to note the seriousness of the provider risks... until the collaborative transfers to a new lead provider in March 2027." — p.29, [Quality Committee Highlight Report, 19 May 2026]

[LEAD] The trust's £30m efficiency programme is running materially behind trajectory, with only about £1.2m delivered by month two against a £5m straight-line requirement (May committee: c.£0.5m vs a £2.5m monthly requirement).
[Evidence] "Although approximately £1.2m of efficiency had been reported by month two, delivery remained materially below the £5m straight-line requirement." — p.196, [Finance and Performance Committee Highlight Report, 25 June 2026]; and "Efficiency delivery was approximately £0.5m against an average monthly requirement of £2.5m, and a significant proportion of the £30m programme remains dependent on schemes still being developed." — p.184, [Finance and Performance Committee Highlight Report, 21 May 2026]

[LEAD] Waiting-list validation found roughly 600 non-obstetric ultrasound referrals had never been added to the waiting list, plus a cohort of inpatient non-RTT patients who should have had active RTT clocks — both reported to NHS England.
[Evidence] "The Committee reviewed three 65-week breaches, a material deterioration in six-week diagnostic performance following the discovery that approximately 600 non-obstetric ultrasound referrals had not been added promptly to the waiting list, and 86 operations cancelled during March." — p.30, [Quality Committee Highlight Report, 19 May 2026]; and "Ongoing validation of the Trust's waiting lists has identified a cohort of patients on the inpatient non-RTT waiting list who should have active RTT clocks. This issue has been reported to NHS England..." — p.195, [Quality Committee Highlight Report, 23 June 2026]

[LEAD] A patient safety investigation is under way into a delay accessing emergency obstetric theatre capacity at Birmingham Women's Hospital; capital funding has been prioritised to equip a third theatre.
[Evidence] "The Committee reviewed a new patient safety investigation involving a delay in accessing emergency obstetric theatre capacity. Capital funding has been prioritised to equip a third theatre space, although sustainable operation will depend on staffing." — p.196, [Quality Committee Highlight Report, 23 June 2026]

[LEAD] The Public Sector Decarbonisation Scheme (PSDS/Salix) is slipping and constraining capital: the Birmingham Children's Hospital scheme has moved from November 2026 to at least February 2027, contractor Dalkia has submitted £1.4m of claims under challenge, and the required National Grid electrical capacity for the Women's Hospital scheme may not be available.
[Evidence] "Completion of the Birmingham Children's Hospital scheme has moved from November 2026 to at least February 2027, while the Birmingham Women's Hospital timetable is uncertain because the required National Grid electrical capacity may not be available. Dalkia has submitted claims totalling £1.4m, which remain under challenge, and the Bring Energy contract and carbon-emissions requirements are unresolved." — p.194, [Major Projects Committee Highlight Report, 17 June 2026]

[LEAD] The 2026/27 Genomic Medicine Service operating model proposes consolidating five wet laboratories into two high-throughput laboratories in Birmingham and Oxford across four partner trusts, supported by time-limited national capital.
[Evidence] "The Committee supported the proposed consolidation of five wet laboratories into two high-throughput laboratories in Birmingham and Oxford, with analysis and reporting remaining distributed across the four partner Trusts... supported by NHS England and access to time-limited national capital." — p.196, [Finance and Performance Committee Highlight Report, 25 June 2026]

[WORTH WATCHING] The People and Inclusion Committee has escalated bullying, undermining and discrimination within Obstetrics and Gynaecology to the board, commissioning a focused update on actions.
[Evidence] "The Committee considered staff survey findings, trainee survey feedback and wider discussion regarding bullying, undermining, discrimination and confidence in speaking up, particularly within Obstetrics and Gynaecology. This is being escalated because the themes raise concerns regarding organisational culture, staff experience, psychological safety, and workforce sustainability." — p.190, [People and Inclusion Committee Highlight Report, 14 May 2026]

[WORTH WATCHING] The Children's Hospital Alliance (14 members, BWC included) successfully lobbied for the paediatric four-hour A&E standard to be deferred to the end of 2026/27.
[Evidence] "It also successfully advocated for implementation of the paediatric four-hour Emergency Department standard to be deferred until the end of 2026/27." — p.26, [CEO's Report, Appendix 1: Children's Hospital Alliance End-Year Report]

[WORTH WATCHING] A Maternity Oversight and Support System (MOSS) Level 2 signal was triggered following eight perinatal deaths, subject to internal and external review.
