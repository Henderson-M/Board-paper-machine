The meeting passed to the analyser as 25 June; treat the substance as the 4 June Joint Board pack. Worth a quick check whether a separate 25 June meeting exists.

> **Joint-board arrangement — read first.** From 1 April 2026 Stockport NHS FT (SFT) and Tameside & Glossop ICFT (TGICFT) operate under a single **Joint Board, joint Chair (David Wakefield), joint CEO (Karen James), joint executives and joint NEDs**, branded the **"South East Sector Healthcare Partnership."** This is the first Joint Board meeting. The Medium Term Plan describes it as having "evolved" toward integration "underpinned by joint leadership posts." Almost every paper is a "Joint Report." Two separate NHS foundation trusts, two separate sets of accounts, two control totals — but one board running both. This group/joint-board model is itself the through-line of the pack (cf. the HUTH/NLAG joint-board precedent).

## Top lines

[LEAD] Two-trust group running combined underlying deficits of more than £100m as it goes into 2026/27, propped up by ~£70m of deficit support funding.
[Evidence] "the underlying position for 2026/27 starting at a £51.5m deficit for TGICFT and £49.9m for SFT." Both "are in receipt of Deficit Support Funding (DSF) and have been set the target of achieving a break-even position at the end of 2030/31." DSF in 26/27: £30.2m (TGICFT) + £39.4m (SFT). — p.8 of [Final Medium Term Plan 2026-27 – 2028-29] (PDF p.35–36); SFT DSF figure p.10 of same paper / Finance Report p.4 (PDF p.117)

[LEAD] Plan's 5% recurrent efficiency target implies a c.21% cut to the substantive workforce that has not been modelled — and most of the saving is still unidentified.
[Evidence] "CIP included at recurrent 5% of operating expenditure recurrently year on year. This equates to c.21% reduction in substantive workforce. This required reduction in WTE to meet the financial plan has not been modelled into the workforce plan as no detail is known." Also: "For 2026/27, a large proportion of the CIP remains unidentified within the submission." — p.3 of [Final Medium Term Plan] (PDF p.31)

[LEAD] DSF is conditional — NHSE says Q2 deficit support depends on 100% of the risk-adjusted savings being found by end of June, but the trusts have identified only around a quarter.
[Evidence] "NHSE have stipulated that receipt of DSF in QTR 2 is dependent on 100% of the risk adjusted value of CIP being identified by the end of June 26. The Trust has currently only identified 45% (24% when risk adjusted) of the full year TEP plan." (TGICFT). SFT equivalent: "currently only identified 40% (23% when risk adjusted)." Combined efficiency targets: TGICFT £20.6m, SFT £26.8m. — p.3–4 of [Finance Report Month 1] (PDF p.116–117)

[LEAD] Contract income dispute with Derbyshire ICB has been escalated to national level, with up to ~£10.5m of assumed income at risk across the two trusts.

[LEAD] Tameside warns it cannot hit the 4-hour A&E standard unless the system funds extra beds it does not currently have — and says it has the lowest acute bed base of any acute provider in the North West.

## FOI leads

[FOI] The conditions attached to NHSE/GM ICB income and DSF — request the letter/conditions document.
- **What:** The "planning conditions" and "enhanced oversight" terms NHSE/GM ICB attached to the £5.0m (SFT) and £3.0m (per M1 narrative) / £5.0m non-contractual income and to DSF release. The finance report repeatedly says conditions were "shared alongside this report" but they are not in the public pack.
- **From whom:** Both trusts, and/or NHS Greater Manchester ICB.
- **Why:** Would reveal what regulatory strings are attached to keeping two trusts solvent — i.e. the real oversight regime behind the breakeven plan. — p.3–4 of [Finance Report Month 1] (PDF p.116–117)

[FOI] The Derbyshire ICB contract dispute now "escalated to a national level."
- **What:** Correspondence and the national escalation re: the Derby & Derbyshire ICB contract income (£3.9m–£6.6m at risk per trust).
- **From whom:** TGICFT, SFT, Derby & Derbyshire ICB, NHSE.
- **Why:** A cross-ICB income dispute referred up to NHSE, directly underpinning two breakeven plans. — p.4 of [Finance Report Month 1] (PDF p.117)

[FOI] The £51.5m / £49.9m structural-deficit analysis and the PFI premium finding.
- **What:** The 2023/24 "piece of work" that "highlighted that both Trusts had a significant structural issue… and specifically for TGICFT the premium associated with the Trust's PFI."
- **From whom:** TGICFT/SFT (joint).
- **Why:** Quantifies how much of TGICFT's deficit is its PFI — a recurring HSJ theme on PFI-burdened trusts. — p.8 of [Final Medium Term Plan] (PDF p.36)

[FOI] Alleged detriment after speaking up at Tameside.
- **What:** Details of "One case of alleged detriment following speaking up… identified at Tameside and Glossop ICFT… currently being managed through formal organisational processes."
- **From whom:** TGICFT (outcome / category, not personal data).
- **Why:** FTSU detriment cases are a standing HSJ governance signal; note the National Guardian's Office closes 30 June 2026 with functions moving to NHSE. — p.2, p.[Item 22] of [Freedom to Speak Up Report] (PDF p.259)
