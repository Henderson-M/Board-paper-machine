## Top lines

[LEAD] Royal Cornwall's underlying deficit has jumped to £54.4m at month 4, from £43.5m two months earlier — because it now assumes only half its savings plan will land.
[Evidence] "At Month 4, the assessed **underlying deficit is £54.4m, compared with £43.5m at Month 2. The deterioration principally reflects the revised assumption that 50% of the 2026/27 FSP will be deliver[ed]**" — [Month 4 Finance Report, underlying position]

[LEAD] Internal forecasting points to a potential £24.1m year-end overspend, and the board's finance members recorded "significant concern".
[Evidence] "**Internal forecasting indicated a potential year-end overspend of £24.1m, with run-rate analysis indicating a £22.6m risk**, driven principally by a forecast FSP shortfall of £15.6m, escalation and flow costs, temporary staffing, outsourcing and activity-related income risks. **Members expressed significant concern** that o[n current trajectory...]" — [Finance and Performance Committee report]
[Evidence] "[FSP delivery is forecast at] £26.9m against the **£42.5m target, representing a potential shortfall of £15.6m, with recurrent delivery forecast at approximately 30.5%.** The principal areas of forecast under-delivery remain Operational Productivity (£8.9m), Medical Value for Money (£5.4m), Nursing Value for Money (£3.1m), Estates (£1.9m) and Commercial & Income (£1.5m)." — [same]

[LEAD] The reported on-plan position has been held together by pulling £2.14m of savings forward out of the second half of the year — and it still slipped.
[Evidence] "At the end of month 4, the Trust has recorded a deficit of c£4.82m which, is £0.4m above plan. **The Trust has moved above plan despite bringing forward c£2.14m of mitigations from the second half of the fina[ncial year]**" — [Month 4 Finance Report]
[Evidence] At month 3 the same device was used: "The year-to-date position remained on plan at a deficit of £3.8m, although **this had been achieved through the early implementation of mitigating actions valued at £1.64m.**" — [Finance and Performance Committee report]
[Evidence] "Based on the month 4 position **the Trust expects to move back to level 3**" [in NHS England's escalation tiering] — [Month 4 Finance Report]

[LEAD] Two never events in April involving retained objects — a retained vaginal swab, and three separate incidents of guidewires left in nasogastric tubes.
[Evidence] "In **April 2026, two incidents met the national criteria for Never Events involving retained foreign objects after invasive procedures.** Immediate actions have been taken, including Trust-wide safety communications, reinforcement of checking and docum[entation]" — [Quality report, action 14: "Understanding controls and addressing any common themes associated with Never Events", Chief Nurse Officer]
[Evidence] "[The first never event, a] **retained vaginal swab** has been presented to the Patient Safety Incident Review and Oversight Group (PSIROG) on the 16/07/2026. **The second Never Event relates to 3x incidents involving guidewires retained in Nasogastric Tubes (NGT).** This report has been extended to ensure it is robust with sustainable actions and is due to be presented at PSIROG on the 20/08/2026." — [same]

[LEAD] The trust is being run by an acting chief executive after the previous chief executive's departure, with an acting chief nursing officer and an interim director of nursing beneath.
[Evidence] "Noted the recent Committee activity had focused on **executive leadership arrangements following the departure of the previous Chief Executive and oversight of the Acting Chief Executive arrangements**" — [Remuneration Committee report to board]
[Evidence] "We are also pleased to announce that **Sally Brittain has been appointed Interim Director of Nursing, Midwifery and Allied Health Professionals. She will provide interim cover while Bernadette George undertakes the role of Acting Chief Nursing Officer.**" — [Chief Executive's report]
[Evidence] The trust's head of EDI has also gone: the 2026/27 EDI action plan could not be consulted on with the Freedom to Speak Up Guardian and the JCNC "due to the **Head of EDI leaving the Trust** before this took place." — [Workforce Equality report]

[WORTH WATCHING] Four NHS England letters land in this pack in six weeks, including two from Sir Jim Mackey.
[Evidence] "On the **7 July a letter was received from Sir Jim Mackey, Chief Executive of NHS England**, asking Trust board members to put a renewed focus on educating staff whilst also **implementing a tough approach in response to those who breach patient trust in accessing patients records without legitimate reason.**" — [Chief Executive's report, item 2 Preventing Unlawful Access to Patient's Records]
[Evidence] "On the **3 July, the Trust received a letter from Sir Jim Mackey**, Chief Executive of NHS England, **setting out new minimum standards for patient experience on elective pathways.** The standards emphasise clear communication, timely updates and keeping patients informed" — [same, item 3]
[Evidence] "On the **11 August NHS England and the Department of Health and Social Care wrote to NHS trusts regarding the Resident Doctors Workforce Reform** confirming that NHS England is moving from managing industrial relations issues to implementing a major workforce reform programme for resident doctors and locally employed doctors (LEDs)." — [same, item 4]
[Evidence] "[The] Regional Director of Nursing / interim Regional Chief Nursing Officer wrote to all NHS and Foundation Trusts noting that in December 2025, NHS England wrote to all NHS Trusts to take further actions to **identify and act against potential perpetrators of sexual misconduct in the NHS**" — [same]

## FOI leads

[FOI] **The chief executive's departure.** *Request from Royal Cornwall Hospitals:* the date and terms of the previous chief executive's departure, any settlement or special severance payment and Treasury/NHSE approval sought for it, the acting arrangements and their remuneration, and the recruitment timetable for a substantive appointment. *Why it matters:* the pack refers to the departure only obliquely, via a remuneration committee report.

[FOI] **The two never events.** *Request from Royal Cornwall Hospitals:* the patient safety incident investigation reports for the retained vaginal swab and the three retained nasogastric guidewire incidents, the PSIROG papers of 16 July and 20 August 2026, and the duty of candour records.

[FOI] **The £24.1m overspend forecast.** *Request from Royal Cornwall Hospitals:* the internal forecast and run-rate analysis presented to the Finance and Performance Committee, the FSP scheme register showing the £15.6m shortfall by workstream, and the list of mitigations brought forward from H2 into the first four months.

[FOI] **NHS England escalation level.** The pack says the trust "expects to move back to level 3" on the strength of the month 4 position. *Request from Royal Cornwall Hospitals and NHS England (South West):* the correspondence setting the trust's escalation level, the criteria applied, and the "further supporting information for Trusts at Level 3" referred to.

[FOI] **The two Sir Jim Mackey letters** (3 July on minimum patient experience standards for electives; 7 July on unlawful access to patient records) and the trust's gap analyses and action plans in response. *Request from NHS England and Royal Cornwall Hospitals.* *Why it matters:* the letters are national and, on the record-access one, carry an explicit enforcement expectation.

[FOI] **The eRoster limited assurance audit.** *Request from Royal Cornwall Hospitals:* the full internal audit report, the management response and the improvement programme, plus the quantified impact of late roster closure on temporary staffing spend.
