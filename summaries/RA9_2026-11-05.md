[LEAD] Torbay Hospital's Tower Block is under formal enforcement by both the fire service and the CQC, and the board is being asked to endorse keeping it open as a "dynamic and conditional risk decision" while wards are decanted
[Evidence] "On 02 October 2026, Devon and Somerset Fire and Rescue Service (DSFRS) issued an Enforcement Notice relating to evacuation arrangements on levels 4 to 8 of the Tower Block. Separately, the Care Quality Commission issued a Section 29A Warning Notice on 07 October 2026 relating to our oversight of fire safety." Compliance deadline extended from 06 November to 18 December 2026; respiratory inpatients move from Midgley Ward, Hutchings Day Unit moves to the Horizon Centre, one service moves to Totnes Community Hospital; £9.8m secured (£8m + £1.8m) for compartmentation works running to February 2028; NHP replacement remains Wave 2, "current planned construction window of 2032 to 2035" — p.123–134, [Torbay Hospital Tower Block: Fire Safety Improvement Programme]. Two dedicated fire marshals now patrol the block 7pm–7am with a minimum seven-person overnight response team (p.130).

[LEAD] A patient died after a cardiac arrest while being cared for in the ED corridor in September — at a trust running an "eliminating corridor care" programme
[Evidence] "[Death] Patient brought to ED by ambulance, receiving care in the corridor, had a cardiac arrest, subsequently died" — one of 1 death and 10 severe-harm incidents reported in September; 9 corridor-care incidents reported in the month — p.132 of 362, [Integrated Performance and Quality Report, Information Pack]

[LEAD] NHSE told the trust that failing to forecast breakeven would trigger "national escalation and a formal leadership review" — the trust held its breakeven forecast only by agreeing recovery actions it is told to extend to "unpalatable" options, including a vacancy freeze, a 199-WTE workforce cut by March and raising charges to staff for car parking and catering
[Evidence] "A non-breakeven forecast, or a breakeven forecast which NHSE did not consider robust and deliverable would result in national escalation and a formal leadership review. The Trust submitted a forecast to breakeven, including some significant and difficult recovery actions... and the Trust has not had DSF withdrawn or gone into national escalation." — p.191–192 of 362, [Monthly Finance Report, Information Pack]. FOC chair flags "Trust-wide reduction of 199 WTE (c£3.7m) between M7 – M12" under "Fragility of breakeven delivery" — p.162, [Finance and Operations Committee Chair's Report]. Breakeven plan rests on £57.8m Deficit Support Funding; base-case delivery risk £6.4m, downside £14.9m; cash of £3.2m at month 6 (£15.1m below plan) with a £10.193m PDC revenue cash support application submitted — p.180–181, [Monthly Finance Report]. [LEAD] Trust's own mortality review found that for elderly patients waiting more than eight hours in ED for a bed, mortality has increased — statistically significant in over-90s and sustained over several years
[Evidence] "whilst overall mortality has improved and is now at pre-Covid levels, for elderly patients waiting for more than eight hours for a bed mortality has actually increased and is statistically significant in patients over 90 years. This increase has been sustained over the last few years." — p.203, [September 2026 Mortality Scorecard]. Crude death rate 3.13% vs 2.12% regional / 2.13% national; 38 of 76 structured judgement reviews outstanding; "The Trust is not assured that all cases requiring a structured judgment review are being reviewed" (p.196); no CUSUM alerts available since the Dr Foster contract ended in April with the replacement HED system not yet in place (p.196, 209).
