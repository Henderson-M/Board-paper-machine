## Top lines

[LEAD] NHS finished 2025/26 with a £563m underlying system deficit, with five named systems accounting for 88% of it.
[Evidence] "5 systems with the largest deficits (Kent and Medway, Cheshire and Merseyside, Nottingham and Nottinghamshire, Humber and North Yorkshire and Hampshire and Isle of Wight) are responsible for 88% of the total system deficit." Systems reported "a £563 million deficit (£400 million down from 2024/25 on a like for like basis)"; "15 systems reporting deficits (including DSF) compared to 17 in 2024/25." — [Month 12 Financial Position 2025/26], Revenue paras 6–9

[LEAD] NHSE delivered a £70m national underspend without drawing on its Treasury reserve claim — but systems missed their efficiency target by £842m.
[Evidence] "£70 million underspend (0.035%) at a national level" achieved "without drawing on a reserve claim from HM Treasury" (Summary para 3); systems delivered "£10.2 billion (92%) efficiencies, a shortfall of £842 million to the planned level of £11.2 billion" — [Month 12 Financial Position 2025/26], Revenue para 7

[LEAD] More than 700 staff have already left NHS England under voluntary redundancy, with 3,700+ applications approved and departures running to March 2027.
[Evidence] "over 3,700 applications approved and more than 700 staff having left in the first cohort" (Section 2); departures anticipated "up to March 2027" (Section 1). Members noted "the current period of organisational transition and uncertainty was contributing to increased anxiety among staff groups" (Section 10) — [Board committee updates – People Committee]

[LEAD] Three of NHSE's eight strategic risks now sit in the "high" band (current score 20/25) — workforce capacity, capital, and the risk of a significant data breach — with the data-breach risk explicitly worsened by DHSC integration and ungoverned AI rollout.
[Evidence] SRR6 Workforce capacity current score "20 (L4xI5)"; SRR4 Capital "20 (L5xI4)"; SRR7 Data breach "20 (L5xI4)". On data breach: "Organisational change, including DHSC integration, is increasing complexity in data handling, insider risk and records management, while workforce capacity pressures and reduced audit capability limit assurance coverage and response resilience. The rapid expansion of AI use, without fully established governance arrangements, introduces additional and evolving risks." — [Risk Report, Annex 1 Strategic Risk Register]

[WORTH WATCHING] NHSE's own risk papers flag staff attrition during the DHSC merger as a newly-escalated operational risk, plus a re-escalated pandemic-preparedness risk.
[Evidence] New operational risk: "risk to capacity and capability prior to integration of NHS England into the Department of Health and Social Care"; "Pandemic preparedness (ORR 11941): newly escalated"; adult secure inpatient capacity risk increased "from 16 to 25 following capacity closure" — [Risk Report] (Note: Annex 2 Operational Risk Register PPTX failed to download — 0 bytes — could not verify scores directly.)

[WORTH WATCHING] Nine acute providers recorded higher-than-expected mortality (SHMI) for the year to Dec 2025; four were also outliers the previous year.
[Evidence] Named: County Durham and Darlington FT, East Cheshire, East Lancashire Hospitals, Medway FT, Blackpool Teaching Hospitals FT, Queen Elizabeth Hospital King's Lynn FT, Rotherham FT, Morecambe Bay FT, North Midlands. Several flagged "possible data quality issues". Caveat in paper: a higher count "should not immediately be interpreted as indicating poor performance… a 'smoke alarm'". p.18, [Integrated Performance Report]

[WORTH WATCHING] Community-services 52-week waiters rose 15.3% year on year to 89,630, overwhelmingly children — 83.8% in community paediatrics.
[Evidence] "As of March 2026, 89,630 community health services waiting times are reported as over 52 weeks… an increase of 11,918 (15.3%) from March 2025… 83.8% of all >52 week waits are observed in the community pediatrics service line." p.~28, [Integrated Performance Report] *(relevant to your monthly CHS waiting-times tracker — published data)*

[WORTH WATCHING] NHSE seeking board sign-off for directions letting it use existing patient data to identify and directly contact clinical-trial recruits by letter, email and SMS.
[Evidence] The Digitrials service will "identify appropriate cohorts to be invited to take part in research, using data which has already been collected by NHS England" and contact eligible individuals "through letters, emails, and SMS." — [Digitrials recruitment service directions 2026] (Item 7, for decision)

## FOI leads

- **VR scheme breakdown.** Pack states 3,700+ redundancy applications approved and 700+ already gone, but gives no cost, grade or directorate split. *Request from NHS England:* total cost of the approved redundancies, breakdown by pay band / directorate / region, and how many were at very senior manager / board level.
- **10-Year Workforce Plan delay.** SRR6 says risk reduction depends on "timely publication of the 10-Year Workforce Plan"; it remains unpublished. *Request from NHSE/DHSC:* current intended publication date and any drafts shared with ministers.
- **Quality Strategy delay.** SRR5 cites "delays to Quality Strategy publication" as a constraint on managing quality risk during transition. *Request:* the planned publication date and reasons for slippage.
