## Top lines

[LEAD] Trust's own board assurance framework warns the underlying deficit, if unaddressed, would leave it "unviable" and is forcing system-wide investments to be "curtailed or cancelled"
[Evidence] "The Trust has a significant underlying financial deficit, which if left unaddressed would leave the Trust in an unviable financial position. The current position means that important system wide investments are being curtailed or cancelled to fund the deficit. This puts at risk the ability for the organisation to continue in its current form, albeit progress made in the last 12 months has reduced this risk." — p.332 [BAF Appendix 3 — Finance, loss of control] (red-rated risk, current score 12, "Partial" assurance)

[LEAD] BAF explicitly links failure to reach financial balance to the case for the new West Suffolk Hospital and the future of services on the current site
[Evidence] "if the organisation cannot break even in a reasonable time frame, then the case for the new hospital and the viability of services on this site are open to question." — p.334 [BAF Appendix 3 — Finance, C2]

[LEAD] Dragados Sociedad Anonima named as preferred/primary construction partner for the c.£1.2–1.5bn new West Suffolk Hospital, with the scheme having "struggled to attract a competent, capable partner"
[Evidence] "The Trust have been collaboratively allocated Dragados as its primary construction partner. This represents a significant step forward and removes any risk of our scheme struggling to attract a competent, capable partner. The next step will be the formation of a contract by 31st July 2026." — p.302 [Item 6.1 Future System Project]; capital budget "between £1.2 and £1.5bn based on a new build space of 97k sqm", construction start "2027/28".

[LEAD] New hospital outline business case slips two months (August to October 2026) and Deloitte commissioned to assess the design's impact on productivity and running costs
[Evidence] "OBC submission is now expected in October 2026 (was August)." ... "Deloitte management consultants have been commissioned to independently assess the impact that the proposed hospital design may have on future productivity and the ongoing cost of operation." — p.303 [Item 6.1 Future System Project]

[LEAD] Trust agreed a £12.8m deficit plan for 2026/27, funded entirely by deficit-support funding to reach a reported break-even; April (M1) already £545k worse than plan
[Evidence] "We have agreed a deficit plan of £12.8m in 2026/27 which will be funded with deficit support funding and therefore our reported position is against a plan to break even." ... "this position includes deficit support funding of £12.8m for 26/27, of which £1.1m is included in the M1 position. Therefore, our underlying monthly deficit without this funding would be around £1.3m." — p.271–272 [Item 5.2 Finance Report M1]

[WORTH WATCHING] Trust has cut 305 WTE (6.0%) since April 2024 as part of financial recovery
[Evidence] "Since April 2024, we have reduced our staffing levels by 305 WTEs (6.0%)." — p.274 [Finance Report]

[WORTH WATCHING] New finance ledger and transactional-procurement system contracted to NHS SBS, go-live October 2026, alongside a finance-function restructure
[Evidence] "Contract signed with NHS SBS to provide financial account and transactional procurement processes with a go live date estimated at October 2026" — p.335 [BAF Appendix 3 — Finance, C2 actions]

[WORTH WATCHING] Freedom to Speak Up: patient-safety concerns ran at 28% of cases, above the national 18%; 209 concerns raised in 2025/26
[Evidence] "Patient safety concerns comprised 28% percent of concerns raised, which is higher than in recent quarters and higher than the national figure of 18%." — p.177 [Item 4.2 FTSU Q4]; "The total number of concerns raised this year 25/26 was 209." — p.174

[WORTH WATCHING] CEO claims West Suffolk is "the fourth most improved Trust in the country" across a broad range of metrics
[Evidence] "I'm delighted to say we are the fourth most improved Trust in the country across the broad range of metrics." — p.28 [Item 1.7 CEO report] (no source/benchmark cited)
