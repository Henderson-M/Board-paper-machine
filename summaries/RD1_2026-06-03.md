## Top lines

[LEAD] NHSE South West accepted the BSW Hospitals Group medium-term plan only "with conditions", warning of "significant concerns" over deliverability of a £117.34m efficiency programme and threatening regulatory escalation — the regulator's letter is published in full in the pack.
[Evidence] "Although the BSW Hospitals Group has submitted a breakeven plan across all three years, we have significant concerns regarding the current deliverability of the financial plan… The plan includes a £117.34m efficiency requirement (7.1%), of which 18.2% remains unidentified and 54.7% is assessed as high risk, significantly undermining confidence in deliverability." — p.38, [Close Down Letter, NHSE SW, 22 April 2026, signed Sue Doheny, Regional Director]

[LEAD] NHSE warns RUH/the Group that failure to deliver will trigger "the full range of regulatory options".
[Evidence] "It is important to note if you are unable to deliver against your commitments, this will lead NHS England to consider the full range of regulatory options available to us as part of any subsequent escalation." — p.38, [Close Down Letter, NHSE SW]

[LEAD] CQC published its UEC inspection report in May 2026 rating urgent and emergency services "Requires Improvement", downgrading "Caring" from Good, and finding three regulation breaches (12, 16/17, 18); Trust must submit an action plan to CQC by 8/9 June 2026.
[Evidence] "The CQC published the inspection report in May 2026 giving a rating of 'Requires Improvement' for Urgent and Emergency Services. The CQC downgraded the rating for 'Caring' from 'Good' to 'Requires Improvement.' The safe, responsive, and well-led domains were also rated as 'Requires Improvement'…" — p.165, [CQC UEC Inspection, Item 13]; breaches of Reg 12, 17(2)(a), 18(1) — p.165

[LEAD] CQC found unsafe staffing and blocked fire exits in RUH's emergency department, with overnight senior medical cover below Royal College of Emergency Medicine levels.
[Evidence] "high vacancy and sickness rate for nurses… The number of senior decision makers fell below the Royal College of Emergency Medicine recommendations, leaving gaps in senior medical cover overnight." and "the environment, particularly when crowding and other storage problems resulted in the blocking of fire exits." — p.171, [CQC LAP Assessment Report LAP-01996]; "unsatisfactory and unsafe numbers of resident doctors as senior decision makers overnight" — p.166

[LEAD] CQC documented an older patient held in an ED cubicle for five days awaiting a mental health bed, and 17.5% of mental health patients staying in ED over 12 hours (August 2025).
[Evidence] "we identified an older patient who was in a small cubicle for 5 days waiting for a decision about their admission to a mental health bed." and "In August 2025, 17.5% of mental health patients remained in the ED for over 12 hours." — p.172, [CQC LAP Assessment Report]

[LEAD] RUH cash position fell below its minimum sustainable working balance; recovery is dependent on a sale-and-leaseback transaction requiring regional sign-off.
[Evidence] "The actual cash balance has fallen below the previously proposed minimum working balance of circa £19m." — p.58, [Finance & Performance Committee Upward Report]; "Cash [Risk 3289 – Score 20] - £12.1m closing balance. Reduction of £11.1m in the month… below £19m sustainable cash levels." and "Recovery by the first quarter… is dependent on the sale and lease back transaction. Sign off from the region will be required" — pp.58–61, [IPR / F&P Committee]

[LEAD] Two of RUH's most senior clinical leaders are leaving simultaneously: CNO Toni Lynch retires June 2026 (Jason Lugg appointed successor); Interim CMO Kheelna Bavalia's secondment ends June 2026 (Dr Rebecca Maxwell appointed substantive CMO, starting August).
[Evidence] "Toni Lynch, Chief Nursing Officer, will retire in June 2026 after 5 years… Jason Lugg, currently Deputy Chief Nursing Officer, has been appointed as her successor." and "Dr Kheelna Bavalia's secondment as Interim Chief Medical Officer, concludes in June 2026… Dr Rebecca Maxwell has been appointed as substantive CMO and will join later in August 2026 with interim cover arrangements being finalised." — p.30, [CEO/MD Report Item 6]

[WORTH WATCHING] RUH closed 2025/26 with a £20.5m deficit (against a forecast £34m); group partners SFT and GWH face £32.5m and £47.2m efficiency requirements respectively, GWH using external financial turnaround support.
[Evidence] "RUH closed 2025/26 with a year-end deficit of £20.5m against a forecast £34m… SFT have an efficiency programme of £32.5m that is fully identified but high-risk in delivery… GWH has commenced with external financial turnaround support arrangements to assist with the delivery of a challenging £47.2m efficiency requirement." — p.17, [CEO/MD Report]

[WORTH WATCHING] NMC told RUH (and all trusts) of a 12-year failure to follow register screening processes; 421 nursing and midwifery professionals are being contacted for further assessment.
[Evidence] "The NMC have identified that systems and processes relating to the screening of applicants requesting to join the NMC register had not been followed for 12 years… As of 27 May, the NMC is contacting 421 nursing and midwifery professionals who will be asked to provide further information…" — p.21, [CEO/MD Report] *(national NMC issue, not RUH-specific)*

[WORTH WATCHING] BSW Hospitals Group statutory governance framework (Scheme of Reservation/Delegation, SFIs, Committees in Common) put to the board for approval to enable Group Board going live 1 July 2026; governance advice supplied by Teneo.
[Evidence] "Approval will ensure a clear, consistent and legally robust governance framework is in place to support safe and effective Group working from 1 July 2026." and prepared by "Simon Hackwell, Governance Advisor, Teneo" — pp.323–324, [Statutory Governance Framework Report Item 18]
