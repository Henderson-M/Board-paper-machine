## Notes and caveats

- The public chief executive report carries the box "REPORT EXEMPT FROM PUBLIC DISCLOSURE: Yes. This report contains sensitive information and would need to be edited prior to it being shared with the public", yet is published in the public pack. [INFERENCE] This reads as a form-filling error rather than a deliberate disclosure, but it is worth checking whether a fuller version exists.
- The provider ranking table on slide 5 of the IPR is internally inconsistent: the "Elective proportion waiting within 18 weeks" column is headed "Rank out of 73" but shows ranks of 92 and 110. Rankings from that one column have not been used above.
- The audit and governance committee insights report gives the meeting date as 17 September 2026 in its header but says in the body, and the insights cover report confirms, that the extraordinary meeting was held on 7 September 2026.
- Speaker notes on the neighbourhood health deck record that several slide visuals "were generated with Microsoft Copilot".
