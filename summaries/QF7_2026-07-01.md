## Top lines

[LEAD] South Yorkshire ICB cuts to 483 WTE from 850 — a 51% operating-cost reduction — and confirms compulsory redundancies were approved by NHS England.
[Evidence] "The changes will see the organisation reduce to 483 whole time equivalents (wtes) from 850 wtes through a 51% reduction in operating costs. The reduced impact on staffing has been achieved by focussing the reduction on more senior roles." … "NHS England have also approved a small number of compulsory redundancies for those colleagues who were displaced." — p.3, [Enclosure 07 Chief Executive Report]

[LEAD] Publication of the ICB's Target Operating Model consultation outcome was paused after staff-side organisations raised a joint grievance about the consultation process.
[Evidence] "the consultation outcome document relating to the ICB Target Operating Model had not yet been shared due to a joint grievance raised by staff-side organisations regarding the consultation process. As a result, publication of the consultation outcome and associated documentation had been paused while next steps were being considered." — p.2, [Enclosure 21 System Leaders Executive minutes, 17 March 2026]

[LEAD] The ICB's £119.5m efficiency programme is only 66% deliverable — £78.6m — with £42m of residual efficiency risk; the £25m of contract-review savings remain "low in confidence".
[Evidence] "the financial deliverability is 66% (£78.6m of £119.5m target)" — p.4, [Enclosure 19 Month 2 Finance Report]. "Efficiency Risk (Amber) £42m" — p.8, [Enclosure 19]. "contract reviews - identified as a significant component of the savings plan - remained low in confidence and required further detailed work." — p.4, [Enclosure 26a Turnaround Committee Reports]

[LEAD] The whole £21.5m forecast break-even rests on as-yet-unconfirmed Deficit Support Funding; without action the unmitigated overspend would be £89m.
[Evidence] "The forecast at Month 2 is break even due to limited information being available and assumes the full DSF of £21.5m is awarded in year." — p.4, [Enclosure 19]. "If the ICB did not take any action the unmitigated forecast would be £78.4m overspend which would increase to £89m due to the loss of deficit support funding." — p.8, [Enclosure 19]

[LEAD] ADHD/autism Right to Choose was a £15.5m cost pressure in 2025/26, with roughly 31,000 patients awaiting assessment on NHS pathways at end-March.
[Evidence] "this had been a major cost pressure in 2025/26, with forecast expenditure of £15.5 million against a significantly lower original budget. For 2026/27, the budget had been increased to £14 million" … "approximately 31,000 patients awaiting assessment on NHS pathways at the end of March and acknowledged that total demand including Right to Choose pathways was likely to be significantly higher." — p.3, [Enclosure 26a Turnaround Committee Reports]

[LEAD] The ICB will stop acting as system convenor — cutting committees from 16 and dissolving its Place Committees — as NHS England takes over the "system convenor and performance management space".
[Evidence] "The Board, in extant governance, is supported by 16 Committees … This is neither desirable nor deliverable in the refocused Strategic Commissioner ICB." — p.1, [Enclosure 16 ICB Change Programme]. "whilst the ICB will continue to be a significant partner at place, it will no longer act as convenor." — p.3, [Enclosure 16]. "NHS England occupies the system convenor and performance management space." — p.3, [Enclosure 16]

[WORTH WATCHING] Two of six South Yorkshire paediatric audiology services were initially assessed Red Risk in a national review, with one requiring a look-back case review for possible harm.
[Evidence] "National review of paediatric audiology services has noted that two out of six services in South Yorkshire were initially assessed as Red Risk ratings, with four services assessed as Amber. One service required a look back case review to establish if harm has been caused." — p.8, [Enclosure 09 BAF and Risk Register]

[WORTH WATCHING] All other South Yorkshire trusts sit in NOF segment three of four; the ICB carries a 25% vacancy rate in its Continuing Healthcare team.
[Evidence] "All our other trusts are in segment three (of four)." — p.5, [Enclosure 07 Chief Executive Report]. "currently operating with vacancy levels of approximately 25%." — p.6, [Enclosure 19 Month 2 Finance Report]

[WORTH WATCHING] Oncology workforce pressure is a residual risk scored 16, with temporary breast, head-and-neck and lower-GI services stood up to mitigate patient-safety risk.
[Evidence] "A temporary breast oncology service, head and neck service and lower Gastrointestinal (GI) service have been implemented locally to mitigate patient safety risks." — p.7, [Enclosure 09 BAF and Risk Register]
