## Top lines

[LEAD] NWAS is one of 16 NHS organisations selected for second-wave assessment for Advanced Foundation Trust status under the 10-Year Health Plan.
[Evidence] "The Trust has been selected as one of 16 NHS organisations nationally to undergo assessment for AFT status... We are part of the second wave of NHS Trusts selected for assessment" — p.30 & p.38, [Chief Executive's Report]

[LEAD] The trust's ethnicity pay gap widened sharply in 2025/26 — the median gap nearly doubling — even as the gender pay gap narrowed to its lowest since 2020.
[Evidence] "The mean hourly pay gap increased from -4.38% to -5.65%, while the median hourly pay gap increased from -6.28% to -11.23%... White staff earned an average hourly rate of £20.62, compared with £19.46 for BME staff... At the median, White staff earned £19.30 per hour compared with £17.13 for BME staff, a difference of £2.17 per hour" — p.133–134, [Workforce Equalities Monitoring Data (WDES, WRES, Pay Gaps)]

[WORTH WATCHING] Internal Audit gave the trust's cyber security / Data Security and Protection Toolkit framework a "Low" assurance rating — the lowest of the quarter — at the same meeting the board proposes cutting its strategic cyber risk score from 20 to 15.
[Evidence] "Cyber Assessment Framework aligned Data Security and Protection Toolkit — Low Risk [assurance]" (Internal Audit, Q1 2026/27) — p.118, [Audit Committee 3A report, 24 July 2026]; "SR08 (Cyber Security) has decreased in risk score from 20 to 15" — p.43, [Board Assurance Framework Q1 2026/27]

[WORTH WATCHING] The board is raising its strategic Urgent & Emergency Care risk from 10 to 15, with performance deteriorating across all response categories in June and NHS 111 call answering below standard for a fifth consecutive month.
[Evidence] "SR03 (Urgent and Emergency Care Performance) has increased from a score of 10 to 15... strategic foresight has been revised from Improving to Deteriorating" — p.43–44, [BAF]; "Call answering within 60 seconds deteriorated for the fifth consecutive month to 53.29%, with abandonment at 8.53%" — p.206, [Integrated Board Report]; "C2 mean response time increased from 24m51s to 27m07s... C2 90th percentile rose to 54m15s and remained outside target across all sectors and ICBs" — p.197, [Integrated Board Report]

[WORTH WATCHING] The trust flags a financial and skill-mix risk from paramedic *oversupply* — turnover has come in lower than the attrition its workforce plan assumed.
[Evidence] "PES lower than anticipated turnover is a risk as workforce plans were based on higher attrition assumptions causing skill mix difficulties and potential financial risks from paramedic oversupply" — p.213, [Integrated Board Report]

[WORTH WATCHING] BME staff remain around 2.5x over-represented in formal discipline and 2.5x less likely to be appointed after shortlisting, with BME appointments falling year-on-year for the first time.
[Evidence] "BME staff therefore accounted for 18% of formal disciplinary cases, while representing approximately 7% of the workforce" — p.132/153; "White applicants were around two-and-half times more likely to be appointed from shortlisting compared to BME applicants (2.53 2025/26, 2.34 2024/25)... the overall number of BME appointments fell significantly, representing the first year-on-year decline" — p.151–152, [WRES report]

[WORTH WATCHING] The 2025/26 audited accounts and Annual Report were signed off with a clean (unqualified) audit opinion — but the accounts document itself is not in this pack (approved at a separate 24 June session).
[Evidence] "External Auditors presented the Audit Completion Report... The report set out the intention to issue an unqualified opinion on the trust's financial statements... It was noted the Trust met all of its statutory duties" — p.117, [Audit Committee 3A report, 24 June 2026]

## FOI leads

[FOI] A strategic-level "Operational/People" risk scored 20 (the joint-highest on the register) is withheld from the published board pack as a "Sensitive Risk" — request the risk description and mitigation.
[Evidence] BAF SR05 operational risks table lists "317 Operational/People — Sensitive Risk — [initial score] 20 High" (also SR02 risk 821 Financial/People 16, SR03 risk 799 Operational/Emergency Preparedness 20→15, all redacted) — p.52, 54, 58, [BAF]. Request from NWAS: the description, cause and current controls for corporate risk 317 (and 821, 799).

[FOI] The trust reported 197 data breaches in 2025/26, six of them externally reportable to the ICO, with one still under active ICO investigation over patient-record "markers" — request the incident detail and ICO correspondence.
[Evidence] "There were 6 data breaches which met the threshold to be reported externally via DSPT to the ICO... Confidential patient information disclosed by mistake because of markers of other patients attached to a health record... The data breach was reported to the ICO — further investigation ongoing" (DCIQ 54469) — p.113–114, [SIRO Annual Report 2025/26]. Request from NWAS: the ICO correspondence and outcome for DCIQ 54469.

[FOI] The People committee notes rising employment tribunal claims and "variations in call avoidance cases by ethnicity" plus sexual-safety and substance-misuse casework, without detail — request the underlying figures.
[Evidence] "Ongoing concerns include sexual safety and substance misuse cases, variations in call avoidance cases by ethnicity, rising Employment Tribunal claims in line with national trends" — p.125, [Trust Management Committee 3A report]. Request from NWAS: number of live/settled ET claims 2024/25–2025/26 and any with discrimination elements; and the "call avoidance by ethnicity" analysis.
