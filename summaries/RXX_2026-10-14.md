[LEAD] The trust discovered over the summer that a cohort of registered general nurses had been employed in registered mental health nurse and learning disability nurse roles, working "outside of their scope of practice"; all regulators were notified, a PSIRF harm review is under way and Duty of Candour is flagged.
[Evidence] "a review of our nursing skill mix over the summer, as part of safe staffing and workforce planning, identified a cohort of Registered General Nurses (RGN's) employed in Registered Mental Health Nurse (RMN) and Registered Learning Disability Nurse (RNLD) roles. An immediate plan was put in place to mitigate any risks identified as result of the nurses working outside of their scope of practice. This has included revised duties, supernumerary status and redeployment to suitable roles, and backfill arrangements whilst colleagues are given the opportunity to undertake the necessary qualifications to revise their registrations. All regulatory bodies were notified and confirmed assurance by the Trust's response...
