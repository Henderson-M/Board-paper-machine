## Top lines

[LEAD] Chief Nurse Professor Louise Morton retires on 14 July after four years; Bev Thomas (currently Director of Nursing, Barnet and Chase Farm, Royal Free London) starts 10 August.
[Evidence] "This is the last Board meeting for Professor Louise Morton before she retires on 14 July… The Trust will welcome Bev Thomas, to the role of Chief Nurse on 10 August, whose most recent role has been as Director of Nursing for Barnet and Chase Farm Hospitals, part of the Royal Free London NHS Foundation Trust." — p.26, [Chief Executive Report]

[LEAD] Trust ended 2025/26 with a £1.7m surplus but faces a £14.237m full-year cost improvement requirement for 2026/27, with delivery of recurrent savings named the central strategic risk.
[Evidence] "The Trust ended 2025/26 in a positive position, delivering a £1.7m surplus, but the 2026/27 financial environment is materially more challenging. At Month 2… a full-year cost improvement requirement of £14.237m. Delivery of recurrent savings and productivity improvement therefore remains a key strategic risk and a central focus for the Executive Team and the Finance and Performance Committee." — p.27, [Chief Executive Report]

[WORTH WATCHING] Savings programme is materially un-derisked at the start of the year: a "significant proportion" of identified savings is high risk and only 63% of the requirement is developed.
[Evidence] "At Month 2, the programme had delivered 97% of the monthly target and developed savings had increased from 56% to 63%; however, a significant proportion of identified savings remains high risk and several workstreams depend on workforce consultation, quality and equality impact assessment… the programme continues to require active executive grip, transparent benefits tracking and further de-risking through Q2 and Q3." — p.27, [Chief Executive Report]

[WORTH WATCHING] Cybersecurity/IT resilience BAF risk has deteriorated (current score 20, up arrow), the highest-rated risk on the framework alongside estates; a cyber-attack BCP "table top" exercise is targeted for Sep-26 and one mitigation action is already overdue ("Due July").
[Evidence] BAF 003 "Cybersecurity and IT Resilience… cyber attacks or legacy system failures severely disrupt services" — Current Risk Score 20, risk movement up; action 1 "Improve cybersecurity environment following linkage with EPIC infrastructure" Target Aug-26 marked "Due July". — p.32–33, [Board Assurance Framework]

[WORTH WATCHING] Estates/infrastructure BAF risk scored 20 (target risk date 2030), with stated controls gaps including isolation rooms that "do not provide adequate isolation as required in the Health Technical Memorandum".
[Evidence] BAF 004 "Estates and Infrastructure Risk… limited national capital funding prevents addressing backlog maintenance… catastrophic failure of buildings and or infrastructure" — Current Risk Score 20; related risk "ER644 – Risk that TSB Isolation Rooms do not provide adequate isolation as required in the Health Technical Memorandum, with risk to patient safety and failure to meet regulations". — p.32, p.34, [Board Assurance Framework]

[WORTH WATCHING] Incident closure timeliness is well below target — only 50% of incidents closed within 30 days against an >80% target — and 24 incidents remain at an "unknown" harm rating.
[Evidence] "Incident Closed within 30 Days — Latest Month 50% — Target >80%" (red); Trust total 24 incidents at "7 Unknown" harm. — p.64, [Integrated Quality and Performance Report]

[WORTH WATCHING] EPIC system defect closed RTT pathways when referrals were closed in ERS; a separate EPIC issue cut Friends & Family Test responses from ~1,200/month to ~150 before resolution.
[Evidence] "Revalidated RTT 18-week performance was 72.0% and narrowly missed the challenging 72.9% internal target, which was affected by an EPIC system defect that caused closure of RTT pathways when referrals were closed in ERS"; "the issue involved a reduction from about 1200 responses per month to about 150 responses in December 2025, and was resolved at the beginning of January 2026." — p.18, p.20, [Minutes of 7 May 2026 board]
