## Top lines

[LEAD] NHS England has imposed additional licence conditions on East Kent under Section 111 of the Health and Care Act — a formal regulatory intervention — and placed the trust in the new Intensive Recovery Programme as one of five named trusts.
[Evidence] "NHS England's imposition of additional licence conditions under Section 111 represents a serious regulatory intervention. It reflects concerns about Board stability, leadership capacity, governance, quality and safety, operational delivery and financial grip." — p.45, [Interim Chair's Report]
[Evidence] "EKHUFT is one of five trusts to be placed in the new Intensive Recovery Programme (IRP), and the subsequent decision by NHSE to notify the Trust that we may be in breach of our license (section 111), NHSE has downgraded the assessment of leadership from amber-red to red. A Director for the IRP has been appointed and started this week… an independent organisation has been appointed to conduct a leadership capability review." — p.53, [Acting CEO Report]

[LEAD] The trust's chief executive has resigned; East Kent is led by an acting CEO and interim chair, and is recruiting a substantive chair and additional non-executive directors amid quoracy risk on the board.
[Evidence] "Support executive leadership continuity following the Chief Executive's resignation and progress the recruitment of a substantive Chair." / "Restore Board stability and governance grip, address constitutional and NEDs vacancy/quoracy risks." — p.48, [Interim Chair's Report]
[Evidence] [INFERENCE] Register of interests lists Des Holden as Acting CEO/CMO (first appointed acting roles Dec 2025/Jan 2024), Olu Olasode as Interim Chair (appointed 23 April 2026), and Helen Mackie as Acting CMO — pp.1–2, [Register of Director Interests]. Substantive CEO Tracey Fletcher remains on the register as "Chief Executive". — pp.1–2

[LEAD] The CQC issued East Kent a Warning Notice over urgent and emergency care at the Queen Elizabeth The Queen Mother Hospital, following two inspections; the trust received the outcome of its representations against the notice on 18 May.
[Evidence] "On Tuesday 18 May 2026 we received an outcome letter from the CQC following a review of representations against a Warning Notice." / Jan inspection prompted a "Letter of Intent that required submission of an urgent action plan"; further inspection 6–7 May. — p.58 & p.54, [CQC QEQM UEC Inspection Update]
[Evidence] CQC findings (May visit): "Care and treatment was not always provided in a safe and timely way in non-care spaces"; "Safe and timely alcohol withdrawal protocols were not followed"; "antibiotic prescribing that was not in line with guidelines"; "guidelines which were not in date." — p.58, [CQC QEQM UEC Inspection Update]

[LEAD] The trust requires a further £76m of savings in 2026/27 and is planning a £35m deficit funded only by non-recurrent NHSE deficit support; April (M1) CIP delivery was already £0.9m behind target.
[Evidence] "non-recurrent Deficit Support Funding (DSF) totalling £35m would be received in 2026/27. This non-recurrent allocation reduced the Group's planned deficit from £35m to a breakeven plan." / "CIP achievement in April was £3.7m, £0.9m below the CIP target of £4.6m." — p.124 & p.121, [Month 1 Finance Report]
[Evidence] [Context] At M11 2025/26 the trust missed an £80m savings target and lost £19.2m of deficit support, closing the year with a Group deficit of £77.3m pre-DSF — pp.10, [April board minutes 26/011.2]. SRR 3664 records "Failure to deliver the Trust financial plan for 2026/27." — p.121, [Month 1 Finance Report]

[LEAD] Cancer and diagnostic performance deteriorated sharply in April, reversing recent gains, with the trust remaining under NHSE Tier 1 elective oversight.
[Evidence] "Cancer performance deteriorated in April, with the faster diagnosis standard (FDS) decreasing to 64% from 73% and 62-day to 69.3% from 70.9% in March… 65-week waits rising to 81 (61 in March), largely driven by Cardiology." — p.52, [Acting CEO Report]
[Evidence] "DM01 performance deteriorated in April, driven by booking workforce shortages and reduced diagnostic capacity due to equipment failures… structural capacity gap exists in both cardiac CT and general MRI." — p.52, [Acting CEO Report]

[WORTH WATCHING] The extended perinatal mortality rate has risen above threshold on the back of a rise in stillbirths, and a Maternity Outcomes Signal System Level 2 alert was triggered at QEQM; an external independent stillbirth review was due to complete in May 2026.
[Evidence] "The extended perinatal mortality rate has increased above threshold (5.50), driven by a rise in stillbirths. Stillbirth rate remains above target, including an increase in term stillbirths." — p.63, [Integrated Performance Report]
[Evidence] "Maternity Outcomes Signal System (MOSS) alerts, including previous Level 1 and new Level 2 signal linked to term stillbirths at Queen Elizabeth the Queen Mother Hospital (QEQM)… expansion of external independent review incorporating most recent case, with completion now expected in May 2026." — p.11, [April board minutes 26/013]

[WORTH WATCHING] The board's revised assurance framework retains a standing risk that the trust will not sustain improvements arising from the Independent Investigation into East Kent Maternity Services.
[Evidence] "There is a risk that the Trust won't continue to improve the experience of women and their families following the actions taken in response to the Independent Investigation into East Kent Maternity Services and other reviews." — p.179, [BAF Appendix 1]

[WORTH WATCHING] Patient flow remains a constraint, with patients ready to leave (No Criteria to Reside) running well above plan, and the IPR records continuing never events and mixed-sex accommodation breaches.
[Evidence] "No Criteria to Reside (NCTR) at 181 against a trajectory of 149." — p.51, [Acting CEO Report]
[Evidence] "Ongoing Never Events, mixed-sex accommodation breaches, and delayed flow (particularly in critical care) highlight continued patient safety risks linked to operational pressures." — p.62, [Integrated Performance Report]
