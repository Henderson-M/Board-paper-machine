## Top lines

[LEAD] Trust's draft 2025/26 accounts show a £12m year-end deficit against a break-even plan, with the external auditor to make a mandatory s30 referral to the Secretary of State over the overspend.
[Evidence] "After technical adjustments the Trust delivered a deficit of £12m against a break-even plan… The Trust received £47.3m deficit support funding during the year… The Auditor would make a mandatory s30 referral to the Secretary of State regarding the annual overspend." — p.107 & 108 (combined), [Audit and Assurance Committee Escalation and Assurance Report, 13 May 2026]

[LEAD] NHSE has written to the trust with recommendations on financial delivery and the trust is standing up a "Financial Improvement Taskforce".
[Evidence] "The CFO summarised the financial outturn drivers noting the Trust was non-compliant from a system perspective and had received a letter from NHSE setting out recommendations for the current year; an improvement task force would be established to implement these." — p.107 (combined), [Audit and Assurance Committee Report]; and "The Trust is currently mobilising a Financial Improvement Taskforce to deliver sustainable financial improvement at pace and strengthen financial leadership and accountability." — p.30 (combined), [Integrated Performance Report, Best Use of Resources]

[LEAD] National Maternity Outcomes Signal System (MOSS) generated a level 1 signal for Worcestershire Royal Hospital on 31 March 2026 — the trust's first MOSS critical safety check — flagging a potential intrapartum safety issue; the trust self-rated AMBER.
[Evidence] "On 31st March 2026 the Maternity Outcomes Signal System (MOSS) generated a level 1 signal for Worcestershire Royal Hospital… This means that there could be a potential safety issue with the intrapartum service at Worcestershire Royal Hospital." — p.45 (combined), [Response to Maternity Oversight Signal System]; rating "AMBER - There is at least one known safety issue in our intrapartum service, with a plan of action already in place but it is not progressing as planned." — p.63 (combined). "We have had no previous MOSS critical safety checks at the Trust." — p.49 (combined)

[LEAD] In one of the six perinatal deaths behind the MOSS signal, the trust concedes the mother should have been advised to take aspirin from booking and this was omitted.
[Evidence] "We have been open and honest with the family of the baby who died in March 2026 that Mum should have been advised to take aspirin from booking and this was omitted – whether this would have affected the outcome is uncertain." — p.51 (combined), [MOSS pre-completion check]. Six deaths reviewed, "three have been recently referred to MNSI including the case from the 03/26." — p.47 (combined)

[LEAD] A Regulation 28 (prevention of future deaths) letter has been issued to the trust over the staffing establishment in its emergency department.
[Evidence] "Inquest outcome… A Regulation 28 letter were issued to the Trust regarding the staffing establishment in ED. This would be fully resolved in May." — p.40 (combined), [Quality Governance Committee Escalation and Assurance Report, Chief Nurse Escalations]

[WORTH WATCHING] Month 1 deficit £2.7m (£0.6m worse than plan), CIP already under-delivered, and the trust says it will need cash support in June with deficit support funding at risk.
[Evidence] "In M1 we report a £2.7m deficit against a £2.1m deficit plan… CPIP £2m delivered against £2.6m target… Cashflow modelling indicates cash support will be required in June. There is a risk of reduced access to Deficit Support Funding which would further challenge cash availability." — p.28–30 (combined), [IPR Financial Performance]. 2026/27 plan assumes £42.2m non-recurrent system deficit support and £57.1m CIP (6.8% of operating expenditure) — p.28.

[WORTH WATCHING] Independent NHSE review of the trust's use of the Safer Nursing Care Tool under way through May–July 2026.
[Evidence] "To note: An independent review is underway of the Safer Nursing Care Tool (SNCT) methodology and application by the NHSE CNO safer staffing faculty lead through May, June and July 2026 with a document submission having been completed." — p.78 (combined), [Safer Staffing Report – Nursing]

[WORTH WATCHING] Two confirmed measles cases and a Citrobacter outbreak on the neonatal unit (one of two confirmed cases died) declared at the trust.
[Evidence] "Citrobacter outbreak declared on NNU 2 cases confirmed as linked by typing, one case has died other case is well." and "Two patients with confirmed measles have been diagnosed at the Trust. Contract tracing has been completed for staff, patients and visitors…" — p.16 (combined), [IPR Infection Prevention & Control]

[WORTH WATCHING] Audit committee reviewed lessons from a cancelled Alexandra Hospital land sale that had a negative financial impact on the trust.
[Evidence] "The report set out key lessons from a review of a cancelled land sale in 2023/24 that had a negative financial impact. Lessons included the importance of robust, detailed, aligned clinical and estates strategies and the need for a more commercial approach." — p.107 (combined), [Audit and Assurance Committee Report — Review of Alexandra Hospital Land Sale]
