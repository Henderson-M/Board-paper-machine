## Top lines

[LEAD] Trust expects to stay in NHS Oversight Framework Segment 4 for 2026/27 and is transitioning into NHSE's National Provider Improvement Programme (NPIP) — its intensive regime for the most challenged providers.
[Evidence] "The Trust expects to remain in NHS Oversight Framework (NOF) Segment 4 for 2026/27. In parallel … NPIP support may apply where a provider's capability assessment is rated red, i.e. where NHS England determines there are material and/or long-running concerns about organisational capability to improve." Purpose line: aligned to "the National Provider Improvement Programme (NPIP) requirements for the most challenged providers." — p.49–50, [Single Improvement Plan Update]

[LEAD] Trust's underlying deficit ran at £50.3m for 2025/26 and the medium-term plan submitted to NHSE shows a £23.1m underlying deficit at end 2026/27 — £3.2m above the £19.9m control limit it has agreed.
[Evidence] "The 2025/26 outturn excluding DSF was a £34.4m deficit. The underlying exit run rate at the end of 2025/26 was a £50.3m deficit… The medium term financial plan submitted to NHS England incorporated an underlying exit run rate at the end of 2026/27 of a £23.1m deficit. This is £3.2m above the 2026/27 plan control limit of £19.9m." — p.209, [Finance Report (M1 Briefing Paper)]

[LEAD] 2026/27 break-even plan rests on £34.9m of waste-reduction (savings) schemes plus £19.9m deficit support funding, but only £28.0m of schemes are developed — a £6.9m gap — and the risk-adjusted value is just £13.6m. PA Consulting has been brought in to help close the gap.
[Evidence] "The WRP requirement that was included within the Trust's financial plan for 2026/27 was £34.9m. Against this, plans have been developed that currently total £28.0m, a gap of £6.9m." Risk-adjusted total "£13.6m". "work is on-going at pace, with support from PA Consulting … to bridge this gap and also secure sign off of all PIDs as a priority." — p.210, [Finance Report (M1 Briefing Paper)]

[LEAD] Clinical coding backlog has made the Trust a national outlier on mortality indicators (SHMI/HSMR) despite no evidence of deteriorating care; backlog will distort reported mortality data for an estimated 12–18 months.
[Evidence] "The Trust continues to experience a significant clinical coding backlog, leading to unreliable SHMI/HSMR mortality indicators, with the Trust currently appearing as a national outlier despite no evidence of deterioration in care quality. The backlog will affect reported mortality data for an estimated 12–18 months." Listed as a red ALERT to QAC. — p.55, [Quality Assurance Committee Escalation Report]

[LEAD] New Never Event: a wrong-site eye injection.
[Evidence] "The committee were notified of a NEVER Event which occurred due to a wrong site eye injection. The committee were reassured that process is being followed to learn from the incident via PSII." — p.56, [Quality Assurance Committee Escalation Report]

[LEAD] Estates subsidiary Atlas flagged for statutory-compliance and governance failings — including ventilation and water safety gaps — with recovery running to January 2027.
[Evidence] "Significant compliance and governance concerns were identified within the Atlas-delivered estates service, including gaps in statutory compliance (ventilation, water safety) and KPI underperformance. Recovery plans are in place but extend through to January 2027." — p.174, [Finance and Performance Committee Escalation Report]

[LEAD] BAF financial-control risk escalated to 20 (from 16); Trust "remains in tiering for all constitutional standards".
[Evidence] "Risk 1.1 – Exceeding the financial control limit (Risk Score 20) … elevated to a 20 in light of the need to fully work up WRP schemes by the end of June 2026 (75% by end of May)." "Risk 3.1 – Achieving access standards (Risk Score 16) … the Trust remains in tiering for all constitutional standards." — p.31, [Board Assurance Framework]

[WORTH WATCHING] April saw a clear reversal of the Trust's emergency-care recovery: ED 12-hour breaches rose from 1,123 to 1,420, ambulance handovers over 60 minutes rose from 20 to 71, and bed occupancy hit 94.3%.
[Evidence] "12 hour performance has worsened, with: Breach percentage increasing from 19.7% to 24.2% | Breach numbers increasing from 1,123 to 1,420 patients"; ">60 minute delays increased significantly, from 20 to 71 cases"; "Bed occupancy remains above safe thresholds and has increased, now 94.2%". A&E Type 1 mental-health 12-hour performance at 27.1%. — p.183, [Operational IPR — UEC & Flow]; bed occupancy 94.3% at p.186

[WORTH WATCHING] Cardiac/cardiothoracic capacity gap being plugged by outsourcing — mutual aid from Manchester FT (20 cases) and a contract with Spire Manchester — while the Trust recruits surgeons and perfusionists.
[Evidence] "Cardiothoracic have been offered mutual aid from MFT, this will start at 20 cases from Mid-May 26… Contract in for outsourcing with Spire Manchester continues… Exploring Mutual aid with LHCH (Liverpool) for both Cardiac and Cardiology." — p.192, [Operational IPR — Elective]. Cardiac physiology staffing (risk 5190) and cardiology outpatient capacity both score 20/16 on the Corporate Risk Register, p.44.

[WORTH WATCHING] Inpatient volunteer activity suspended in clinical areas following a safeguarding incident; reinstatement targeted for w/c 8 June 2026.
[Evidence] "A suspension of volunteer activity in clinical areas is in effect, implementation of strengthened controls (sign-in/out, ward awareness). Further governance improvements are to be made before reinstatement of inpatient volunteer activity wc-08.06.26." Also flagged to WAC alongside a DBS/safer-employment review. — p.57, [QAC Escalation]; p.213, [Workforce Assurance Committee Escalation]

[WORTH WATCHING] Core skills (mandatory training) compliance target cut from 95% to 90%, and Level 3 safeguarding training compliance has fallen.
[Evidence] "The Committee approved the proposal to reduce the Trust target for core skills compliance from 95% to 90%… members were clear that this must not dilute personal accountability." — p.215, [WAC Escalation]. "a reduction in compliance with Level 3 safeguarding training… an area requiring renewed focus." — p.57, [QAC Escalation]
