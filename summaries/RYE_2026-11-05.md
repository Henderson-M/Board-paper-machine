[LEAD] SCAS's June 2026 heatwave major incident is linked to 21 patient deaths: of 39 cases that met the Duty of Candour threshold, 30 (76%) involved severe harm or death, almost all delayed ambulance arrival. An independent After Action Review has made 14 recommendations, and the trust's data centre overheated mid-incident, taking down reporting systems and degrading the CAD.
[Evidence] "Patient Harm Recorded On Datix • 21 cases (53%) death • 9 cases (23%) severe harm • 6 cases (16%) moderate harm • 3 cases (8%) low or no harm. "During the incident period, SCAS experienced its highest-ever number of calls waiting overnight on 25th June … Additional challenges arose when the SCAS data centre overheated overnight on 25th June, causing data reporting systems to become unavailable and affecting the performance of the CAD system."
