[Evidence] "114 ERs to date in 2026-27" and "NORTHUMBRIA has seen an increase in ERs since the change. Feb – May 2026 saw an approximate 3 fold increase. Board asked to note "the impact of the revised national Exception Reporting processes on reporting activity and associated financial costs" — p.56.
