## Top lines

[LEAD] Cancer 62-day performance stuck at 65.1% in May — well below the 80% standard and its own trajectory — with the pack naming the trust a regional outlier on lung cancer.
[Evidence] "Newcastle Hospitals is an outlier in the region for its low Lung 62-day performance. The treatment end of the pathway is contributing to this" — p.98, [Integrated Board Report]. Metric table: Cancer 62-day May-26 actual 65.1% vs trajectory 69.3% vs 80% target; backlog concentrated in Lung, Upper GI, Lower GI, Urology — p.95, p.98, p.101, [IBR]. Escalated as the only "Alert" from the 18 May Finance & Performance Committee: "62-day cancer metric with the latest unvalidated data showing a 10% deterioration since December 2025" — p.38, [Committee Triple A Reports]; and again at the 22 June F&PC: "In April 2026, performance against the 62-day standard was 65.0%, below both trajectory and standard" — p.40.

[LEAD] Trust recorded 10 Never Events in 2025/26 and has already logged two in the first two months of 2026/27, prompting a dedicated safety-standards project board.
[Evidence] "Two Never Events have been recorded in 26/27, 10 Never Events were recorded in 25/26." Two 2026/27 cases detailed: April 2026 "Wrong route medication administration" (Acute Medicine); May 2026 "Unintentional connection of a patient requiring oxygen to an air flowmeter" (Major Trauma) — p.76, [IBR]. Trust response is a PSIRF priority to implement NatSSIPs2 "led by a Project Board with dedicated resources" — p.76.
