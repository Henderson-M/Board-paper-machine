# Warrington and Halton Teaching Hospitals NHS FT (RWW) board — 3 June 2026

> **Note on scope:** This is the first public Trust Board (Part 1) of **North Cheshire and Mersey NHS Foundation Trust (NCM)**, the new integrated organisation formed on **1 April 2026** when Warrington and Halton Teaching Hospitals (RWW) **acquired** Bridgewater Community Healthcare (RY2). It is a single trust with one board, not a joint/shared board — predecessor data still reported separately as "WHH" (acute) and "BCH" (community). This pack covers both `RWW` and `RY2`.

- **Source:** https://northcheshireandmersey.nhs.uk/about-us/how-we-work/board-meetings-and-papers/
- **Pack:** 1 combined PDF, 259pp ("Trust Board Part 1 Agenda and Papers - 03/06/26")
- **Analysed:** 2026-06-05T00:00:00Z

## Top lines

[LEAD] NCM is one of 16 trusts nationally sent an NHS England letter demanding evidence it has explored ways to improve its £30.5m deficit plan — covering loss-making services, outpatient reform, temporary staffing and CIP.
[Evidence] "Letter received from NHSE given our plan is non-compliant (1 of 16 trusts in the country) requesting evidence of discussion around opportunity to improve the plan, investments, loss making services, outpatient reform, temporary staffing and CIP delivery including productivity." — p.2 of June FSPC assurance report [Committee Assurance Report BM/26/06/29cii, FSPC 22 May 2026], pack p.131

[LEAD] The trust is paying PwC roughly £40,000 a month to monitor its finances — an unbudgeted cost set to continue into Q2 — and is applying to NHSE for up to £14.781m of cash support for Q2.
[Evidence] "PwC monitoring to continue through Q1 and potentially into Q2 with a cost of £40k per month which is a cost in excess of plan." — June FSPC report p.2, pack p.131. Cash support recommendation: "Approve cash support of up to £14.781m from NHSE for Quarter 2 2026/27." — IPR p.4, pack p.49

[LEAD] NCM opened 2026/27 with a Month 1 (April) deficit of £5.8m, £0.9m worse than plan, against a full-year £30.5m deficit plan for which it received no deficit support funding.
[Evidence] "At Month 1 the Trust has recorded a deficit position of £5.8m which is £0.9m worse than plan. The difference of £0.9m is due to a £0.9m impact of industrial action." / "The Trust submitted a deficit plan of £30.5m. The Trust was not allocated any deficit support funding (DSF) this year." — IPR pp.4 & 9, pack pp.49 & 54

[LEAD] Predecessor WHH closed 2025/26 £40.7m in deficit — £12m worse than plan — with an underlying deficit of £45.5m; Bridgewater (BCH) finished £4.4m in deficit, £2.9m worse than plan.
[Evidence] "Month 12 deficit position is £12m worse than plan at £40.7m (before deficit support funding (DSF)) due to stretch CIP not delivered (£11.2m) and unfunded integration costs (£0.8m). The Trust underlying deficit is £45.5m..." / "Month 12 deficit position is £2.9m worse than plan at £4.4m due to stretch CIP not delivered." — April FSPC report p.2, pack p.126

[LEAD] NCM ranked 117th of 118 trusts nationally for 12-hour waits in the emergency department and is reporting more than a quarter of type 1 attendances as corridor care, with a pledge to eradicate it by winter 2026.
[Evidence] "NCM currently ranked 117/118 for 12 hour time in department nationally and NCTR remains high (24.6% at 11 May)." / "The trust is reporting 28.5% for instances of corridor care in ED as a proportion of Type 1 attendance, data has been refreshed and an improved performance of 16.2%." / "Trust is committed to eradicating corridor care by Winter 2026..." — June FSPC report, Corridor Care hot topic, pack p.128

[LEAD] Chief Nurse Ali (Alison) Kennah will retire later this year after eight years at the trust.
[Evidence] "Ali Kennah, Chief Nurse, will retire later this year following more than 30 years of service in the NHS... joining the Trust in 2017 as Head of Nursing before being appointed Chief Nurse in 2024." — CEO Report p.3-4, pack pp.36-37

[LEAD] The trust received an NHFD outlier alert for hip-fracture mortality and a mortality review found a quarter of fractured-neck-of-femur deaths lacked an ASA grade, "very likely" understating the recorded risk of death.
[Evidence] "National Hip Fracture Database data has demonstrated a deterioration in mortality in Q2 and Q3 2025/26 resulting in an outlier status alert being received by the Trust." / "This review has identified that 9 of 35 (25.7%) of deaths did not have an ASA grade submitted to the NHFD. This is very likely to have resulted in significant underestimation of risk of death in these patients by the NHFD casemix adjusted mortality algorithm..." — Fragile Clinical Services pp.4-5, pack pp.140-141

[WORTH WATCHING] Staff confidence that concerns will be acted on has fallen sharply across both arms of the new trust and sits below the national average on all four speak-up questions; FTSU disclosures have surged.
[Evidence] Acute: "If I spoke up about something that concerned me, I am confident my organisation would address my concern – 44% ... 8% deterioration." Community: "...47% ... a 16% deterioration in comparison to 2024." / "Both sectors are also below the national average for their respective comparator groups... across all four questions." / FTSU disclosures: acute 108 in 2025/26 (vs 87, 31, 42); community/dental 107 (vs 36, 43, 14). — FTSU report pp.5-6 & 3-4, pack pp.229-230 & 227-228

[WORTH WATCHING] The trust has launched a further Mutually Agreed Resignation Scheme and needs a 324.7 WTE cut to deliver its pay-related savings after NHSE raised the bank-reduction target from 10% to 15%.
[Evidence] "During the period, the Trust launched a further Mutually Agreed Resignation Scheme (MARS)..." — CEO Report p.4, pack p.37. "Reduction of 324.7 WTE required to deliver pay CIP following change from 10% to 15% bank reduction." — May FSPC Workforce Plan deep dive, pack p.128

[WORTH WATCHING] An external review of the chronic pain service found concerns over compliance with NICE guidance on injection rates and opioid prescribing; the trust now plans to decommission the hospital service and recommission a community model.
[Evidence] "Escalation indicated following external service review commissioned by the Medical Director – concerns regarding compliance with NICE Guidance, injection rates and Opiate prescribing standards." / "Agree process and programme timeline with commissioners to decommission current hospital-based service and recommission future community based reablement service." — Fragile Clinical Services p.7, pack p.143
