## Top lines

[LEAD] Trust is off plan four months into 2026/27, reporting a £1.7m year-to-date deficit against a planned £0.9m deficit, and is running an agreed £12.8m deficit plan for the year funded entirely by deficit support funding.
[Evidence] "as of the end of June, we are not currently on plan, with an in-year position of £1.7m deficit against a planned £0.9m deficit" — p.31, [CEO report]; "We have agreed a deficit plan of £12.8m in 2026/27 which will be funded with deficit support funding" — p.187/190, [Finance Report M3]; "Deficit YTD £ 1.7M / Variance against plan YTD £ -0.9M Adverse" — p.191, [Finance Report M3]

[LEAD] The trust's underlying recurring deficit is running just below £1.5m a month, masked by roughly £1.1m a month of deficit support funding — i.e. the reported position is being held up by monthly bailout cash.
[Evidence] "Our recurring deficit position continues to be just below £1.5m without the monthly deficit funding (of almost £1.1m per month)." — p.190, [Finance Report M3]

[LEAD] Cost improvement programme is significantly short: a gap of £9.2m/£11.4m (unweighted/weighted) remains, with only 69%/62% of the CIP target identified.
[Evidence] "A gap of £9.2m/£11.4m remains when considering the unweighted / weighted CIP position respectively. This represents 69% or 62% of CIPs identified against target." — p.177, [Finance & Performance CKI, 17 June 2026]

[LEAD] The finance and performance committee gave elective recovery its lowest ("Minimal") assurance rating, blaming overrunning theatre refurbishment; 564 patients were waiting 52+ weeks in June against a forecast of 221, and elective/diagnostic waits is the trust's top-scored organisational risk (current score 16).
[Evidence] "Elective Recovery ... 4 Minimal ... The deficit in elective activity is significant; this is due to the continuation of overrunning refurbishment works in main theatres." — p.175, [F&P CKI]; "our June 2026 performance for those patients waiting 52 weeks is off plan at 564 against our forecast of 221" — p.32, [CEO report]; RSK-224 elective/diagnostic waits, current risk score 16 — p.244, [Org Risk Register]

[LEAD] Trust names Dragados Sociedad Anonima as construction partner for the new West Suffolk Hospital (New Hospital Programme), with £12.2m of NHP capital in the 2026/27 plan.
[Evidence] "The Trust has now been matched with its construction partner and can confirm Dragados Sociedad Anonima as the organisation who will work alongside us." — p.35, [CEO report]; New Hospital Programme full-year forecast £12,247k — p.203, [Capital progress report]

[WORTH WATCHING] Trust sits in NHSE oversight segment 3 (in financial deficit) and its patient safety domain is rated "4 – Low performing" in the Q4 2025/26 National Oversight Framework (published 11 June 2026); ranked 66 of 134 acute trusts.
[Evidence] "Segment 3 – Below average and/or financial deficit ... Patient safety: 4 – Low performing ... Trust in financial deficit? Yes ... Trust rank 66 out of 134" — p.58, [IQPR May 2026, NOF slide]

[WORTH WATCHING] 2025/26 annual report and accounts laid before Parliament on 14 July 2026 with an unqualified external audit opinion; the 2025/26 deficit outturn was £15.0m and the accounts will be published/presented at the AMM on 24 September. (The ARA document itself is not attached to this pack.)
[Evidence] "The annual report and quality accounts were submitted ahead of deadline to NHS England on 25 June 2026. These have been approved and laid before parliament on 14 July 2026." — p.233, [Governance report]; "External Auditor opinion – reported unqualified opinion on accounts, with no material errors" — p.205, [Audit & Risk Committee CKI, 23 March 2026]; "a redistribution of central system funding resulted in a deficit of £15.0m" for 25/26 — p.182, [Insight Committee CKI]

[WORTH WATCHING] Internal audit issued two negative assurance opinions — on Partnership Working and on Mental Capacity Act/DoLS — now escalated to board committees.
[Evidence] "Internal Audit progress report – completion of 3 audits (including 2 negative assurance opinions: Partnership Working; Mental Capacity Act/DoLS)" — p.205, [Audit & Risk Committee CKI]

[WORTH WATCHING] Trust is a poor payer of NHS supplier invoices: only 46% of NHS bills (by number) were paid within the 30-day Better Payment Practice Code target.
[Evidence] "NHS ... Percentage of bills paid within target 46% [by number] / 56% [by value]" against a 95% standard — p.202, [Finance Report M3, BPPC]

[WORTH WATCHING] Executive Medical Director Richard Goodwin holds extensive declared private-sector radiology/AI interests while the trust drives a diagnostics-expansion agenda.
[Evidence] "Director of RWMH Ltd ... (private medical services). Consultant to Radnet Management Inc., a subsidiary of Radnet Inc of which DeepHealth is also a subsidiary. DeepHealth provide Saige Lung, Saige Brain and Saige Prostate AI tools. Radnet Inc are also majority shareholders of HeartLungHealth, a teleradiology provider to the NHS." — p.249, [Register of Directors' Interests]
