# Tameside and Glossop Integrated Care NHS FT (RMP) board — 4 June 2026

> **Joint/shared board.** This is the first formal "Joint Board" of the **South East Sector Healthcare Partnership** — a single board in common covering **Tameside & Glossop Integrated Care NHS FT (RMP)** and **Stockport NHS FT (RWJ)**. From 1 April 2026 both trusts operate under joint governance (joint chair, CEO, NEDs and executives) with one shared agenda; each board still takes its own decision. Most papers are joint or "joint summary with separate trust report". Lines below are tagged SFT / TGICFT where the data is trust-specific.

- **Source:** https://www.southeastsector-hcp.nhs.uk/
- **Pack:** 1 combined PDF (444 pages)
- **Analysed:** 2026-06-05T00:00:00Z

## Top lines

[LEAD] Both trusts' final 2026/27 plans only break even because they assume Deficit Support Funding; underlying deficits are £51.5m (TGICFT) and £49.9m (SFT), with break-even not due until 2030/31.
[Evidence] "the underlying position for 2026/27 starting at a £51.5m deficit for TGICFT and £49.9m for SFT" and "Both Trusts are in receipt of Deficit Support Funding (DSF) and have been set the target of achieving a break-even position at the end of 2030/31, year 5 of the planning cycle." — p.36 & p.33 (MTP pp.8 & 5), [Item 9b Medium Term Operational Plan 2026/27–2028/29]

[LEAD] Receipt of DSF in Q2 is conditional on the trusts identifying 100% of risk-adjusted CIP by end of June 2026 — and at Month 1 they had found only 40–45% (23–24% risk-adjusted), with the joint Finance & Performance Committee formally alerting the board to a possible "DSF clawback".
[Evidence] "NHSE have stipulated that receipt of DSF in QTR 2 is dependent on 100% of the risk adjusted value of CIP being identified by the end of June 26. The Trust has currently only identified 45% (24% when risk adjusted) of the full year TEP plan." (TGICFT, p.116, [Item 15b Finance Report Month 1]) and "a material proportion of savings remains unidentified, limiting confidence in the deliverability of the plan and creating exposure to an in-year deficit support funding (DSF) clawback" — p.74–75, [Item 13 Joint F&P Committee AAA Report]

[LEAD] A contract-income dispute with NHS Derby & Derbyshire ICB — worth £6.6m to SFT and £3.9m to TGICFT in 2026/27 — has been escalated to national level.
[Evidence] "£6.6m income assumption from Derbyshire ICB – this contract dispute has now been escalated to a national level." (SFT, p.117) and "£3.9m income assumption from Derbyshire ICB – this contract dispute has now been escalated to a national level." (TGICFT, p.116), [Item 15b Finance Report Month 1]

[Evidence] "UEC performance is severely impacted by the bed deficit on the site which has been acknowledged by GMICB and NHSE… The system has agreed to resolve the Trust acute bed deficit by Q4 2026/27… Failure to resolve the bed deficit position will result in failure to achieve the 4-hour standard." and "the Trust has the lowest acute bed base of any acute provider in the North West compared to Type 1 ED activity." — p.31 & p.35 (MTP pp.3 & 7), [Item 9b Medium Term Operational Plan]

[LEAD] Both trusts' plans are underpinned by "high risk" assumptions agreed with GM ICB, and non-contractual income from GM ICB/NHSE (£5.0m TGICFT, £3.0m SFT to deliver the control total) is itself conditional on hitting the financial plan and accepting "enhanced oversight".
