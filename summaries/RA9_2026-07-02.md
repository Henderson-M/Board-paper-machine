## Top lines

[LEAD] Trust dropped from Segment 3 to Segment 4 of the NHS Oversight Framework on the latest Q4 data, falling from 83rd to 102nd (joint) in the national league table.
[Evidence] "In this latest update, we have moved from Segment 3 to Segment 4, with a score of 2.60, having previously been between 2.41 and 2.53. This is reflected in our league table position, where we have moved from 83rd to 102nd (joint) out of 134 trusts." — p.27, [Chief Executive's Report, para 45]

[LEAD] The trust's finance risk on its new corporate risk register sits at the maximum score of 25 and is rising, against a stated underlying deficit of "c.£100m".
[Evidence] BAF Finance domain: "There is a risk the Trust does not achieve financial sustainability given the underlying deficit of c.£100m" — p.52/p.58, [Board Assurance Framework, BAF-FN]; CRR heatmap shows Financial score "25 … ▲ Increasing … Above appetite" — p.64, [Corporate Risk Register Summary Heatmap]

[LEAD] The trust has a £54.4m savings target for 2026/27 — more than 7% of budget — with only £3.4m delivered at Month 2, and is forecasting delivery risk of up to £23.6m.
[Evidence] "we have an incredibly challenging plan to deliver in 2026/27 including a savings programme of £54m which is more than 7% of our budget" — p.24, [CEO Report, para 18]; "Year to date at Month 2 the Trust has recognised CIP delivery of £3.4m against a £4.2m target (£0.8m adverse to plan)" and "forecasting delivery of the planned outturn but recognises forecast risks to delivery of between £9.3m in the base case and up to £23.6m in the downside risk assessment" — p.129–130, [Month 2 Finance Report]

[LEAD] Tower Block fire safety risk was "materially expanded" on the BAF, with a new major gap in control/assurance over the lack of embedded fire safety arrangements.
[Evidence] BAF change log, Strategy and Business: "** risk materially expanded to include tower fire safety: this includes: new control for tower fire safety management; new major gap in control and assurance on lack of embedded fire safety arrangements; new actions including oversight group, programme, risk register, summit, and external agency engagement." — p.46, [Board Assurance Framework]; BAF-ST C5 rated "Limited" — p.61

[LEAD] Director of Corporate Governance and Trust Secretary Emily Long left the trust on 30 June 2026 following a restructure of executive and senior management arrangements.
[Evidence] "following a restructuring of our executive and senior management arrangements, Emily Long, Director of Corporate Governance and Trust Secretary, left us on 30 June 2026 to pursue new opportunities." — p.24, [CEO Report, para 11]

[LEAD] A voluntary redundancy scheme is cutting around 75 WTE, with settlement agreements being finalised in early July.
[Evidence] "we have been able to support a reduction of around 75 WTE, contributing to our overall workforce reduction requirement and delivering recurrent savings, while maintaining safe services. The programme is now in its final stages. Colleagues confirmed their decisions in mid-June and we are currently finalising settlement agreements, with completion expected in early July." — p.25, [CEO Report, paras 25–26]

[LEAD] Hospital mortality (HSMR+) is 107.6 and statistically higher than expected, a 4-point increase representing a change in banding, with four new CUSUM alerts this quarter.
[Evidence] "The latest HMSR+ for September 24 to August 25 is 107.6 (101.0 – 114.6) and statistically higher than expected. This represents a change in banding compared to the last report produced in January 26 and a 4-point increase in HSMR+." — p.147, [May 2026 Mortality Score Card]; "four new CUSUM alerts this quarter" — p.150

[WORTH WATCHING] The EPR (Epic) is internally assessed at "WATCH status" with high-impact performance metrics not returning to baseline — while the same report recommends the committee take SATISFACTORY/significant assurance.
[Evidence] "TSD has been assessed at a WATCH status. Key rating drivers are: Pockets of dissatisfaction amongst key user groups; High impact performance metrics not returning to baseline; Personalisation and proficiency data; Failure to establish a confirmed, long-term Governance structure" — p.133–134, [EPR Status Report]; recommendation: "take significant assurance that Epic Go-Live has been navigated successfully" — p.135

[WORTH WATCHING] Mortality review work shows elderly patients (over 90) waiting more than eight hours for a bed in ED have a statistically significant, sustained increase in mortality.
[Evidence] "in elderly patients waiting for more than eight hours for a bed mortality has actually increased and is statistically significant in patients over 90 years. This increase has been sustained over the last few years." — p.153, [Mortality Score Card, 3.1 Urgent and Emergency Care]

[WORTH WATCHING] Capital is severely constrained — only £1m available against a year-zero backlog of £54m.
[Evidence] BAF-FN Gap in Assurance: "Only £1m capital available against a year zero backlog of £54m" — p.58, [Board Assurance Framework, Finance]

[WORTH WATCHING] Cancer 28-day faster diagnosis performance was 50.4% in April against an 80.5% national standard; the trust submitted a recovery plan to NHSE on 18 May.
[Evidence] "The 28-day faster diagnosis was not met in April with performance at 50.4% against the national target of 80.5% … On the 18th May the Trust submitted a recovery plan and trajectory to NHSE" — p.125, [Operational Performance Report]; A&E 4-hour at 64.88% in May, 12-hour breaches 11.7% — p.121–122

[WORTH WATCHING] The Peninsula Pathology Network has been unable to agree a single peninsula-wide operating model.
[Evidence] "It has not been possible to agree a single peninsula wide model and further work is required to develop a clinically safe, sustainable and deliverable approach including: The target operating model; financial assumptions and proposed savings; governance arrangements." — p.34, [CEO Report, para 107]

## FOI leads

[FOI] Request the NMC's correspondence to the trust on affected nursing/midwifery registrants. The CEO report states 421 professionals nationally must provide further information and that affected staff at the trust may exist — request how many TSDFT registrants fall in the cohort and any related internal action.
[Evidence] "The NMC has now contacted 421 nursing and midwifery professionals who need to provide further information … We will offer wellbeing support to any of our nurses who have been affected" — p.39–40, [CEO Report, paras 157, 160]. Request to: NMC and TSDFT (numbers affected at the trust; correspondence received).

[FOI] Request the two perinatal patient safety incident investigations (PSIIs) and the MNSI referrals. The mortality report confirms increased neonatal deaths, two internal PSIIs underway, and a January 2026 case investigated under "delays to escalation in care".
[Evidence] "Perinatal services have seen an increase in neonatal deaths … 2 internal PSII's are underway related to perinatal mortality cases supported with external perinatal expertise from MNIST" — p.153–154; "treated for sepsis at birth, transferred to Derriford, died 2 days later … investigating this case as a patient safety incident investigation (PSII) under the local maternity category of delays to escalation in care" — p.154, [Mortality Score Card]. Request to: TSDFT (PSII reports, terms of reference) and NHSE/MNSI.

[FOI] Request the contract/engagement detail for external consultant Kaajal Chotai, named in the May minutes as having supported the risk management framework, and the trust's "external risk expert" referenced in the EPR report. Request the contract value, term and deliverables.
[Evidence] "Thanks were placed on record to … Kaajal Chotai (external consultant) who had supported this work" — p.9, [May minutes, 51/05/26]; "The approach to risks associated with Epic has been reviewed by the Trusts external risk expert" — p.134, [EPR Status Report]. Request to: TSDFT (consultancy contracts).

[FOI] Request the private-session paper on the Section 75 adult social care transition. The CEO report states a more detailed paper is being held in private "reflecting the complexity and sensitivity of this work".
[Evidence] "A more detailed paper is being considered in private session, reflecting the complexity and sensitivity of this work." — p.37, [CEO Report, para 129]; transition risk scored 4x4=20 on BAF-OT — p.62. Request to: TSDFT (private paper, risk and financial disaggregation detail).

[FOI] Request the structured judgment review (SJR) assurance position. The mortality report states the trust is "not assured" that all cases requiring an SJR are being reviewed, and that 31 ME-referred deaths were flagged for review.
[Evidence] "the medical examiners referred thirty-one deaths to the Trust for further review via SJR … The Trust's patient safety team is not currently assured that An SJR is being completed for all appropriate cases." — p.156, [Mortality Score Card]. Request to: TSDFT (SJR completion data, backlog).
