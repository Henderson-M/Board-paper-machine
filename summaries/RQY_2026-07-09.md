**Pack:** Trust Board Part A July 2026 meeting papers (239pp, ~12.5MB). **Part A (public) only** — Part B (confidential) items, including the Complex Care business case, advanced Foundation Trust roadmap and the external Charity governance review, were not available. Meeting held Thursday 9 July 2026, Springfield Hospital.

Key people: Ann Beasley (Chair), Vanessa Ford MBE (CEO), Philip Murray (Chief Finance & Performance Officer), Ashton Ntuli (COO), Dr Billy Boland (CMO), Sharon Spain (CNO).

## 1. Top lines

**LEAD — Coroner issues Prevention of Future Deaths report over inpatient suicide at the trust; separate April spike of 12 suspected suicides triggers CMO/CNO review**
"VF highlighted the Prevention of Future Deaths report relating to the sad death of Lajos Mandrik by suicide on Ellis Ward. This was noted as reinforcing the importance of being able to clearly evidence improvement" — p.18, [Minutes of 14 May 2026 board, CEO's report item A26/027]. Separately: "36 incidents were reviewed in April, including an increase to 12 suspected suicides compared to the previous period... The increase in suspected suicides was acknowledged as significant" — p.68, [QSAC Chair's Report]. "The CMO and CNO have commissioned a more detailed review to identify common external factors, quality of care prior to the incident" — p.113, [Q&P Report May 2026, Patient Safety].

**LEAD — Mental health patients waiting up to 17 days for an acute bed; 12 waited over 120 hours in urgent care, double the previous month**
"There were excessive individual waits with some patients waiting up to 169 hours... 12 patients waited over 120 hours in urgent care settings. This was double the reported numbers compared to March... the longest recorded at 17 days, reinforcing the need for coordinated system-level action" — p.68, [QSAC Chair's Report, waits for admission / external beds]. Average waits rose to 53 hours in April; 44 incidents involving 21 patients, "a majority of which were within the Lotus Unit."

**LEAD — CQC inspected the Adult Acute Pathway; ligature risks and documentation flagged as "already known" issues being addressed**
"We welcomed the Care Quality Commission (CQC) to inspect services within the Adult Acute Pathway. Early feedback highlighted respectful, dignified care... Opportunities for improvement focused on learning from incidents, risk management and documentation... Some issues, including ligature risks and documentation, were already known and are being addressed" — p.45, [CEO's Report]. CEO's own framing question: "Following CQC inspection feedback, how do we ensure that learning from incidents, inspections and patient and staff experience is translated into consistent improvements" — p.42.

**LEAD — 615 children waiting more than 104 weeks; ADHD is the "primary driver" of 52-week breaches across adult and children's pathways**
"Number of Children with >104 waits (April 2026): 615" against a target of 550 (reducing 50/month) — p.97, [Q&P Report, Annual Planning Metrics]. "ADHD remains the primary driver of 52-week breaches in the adult pathway, leading to increased waiting times and projected to continue to rise" — p.91; "There is a consistent negative upward trend in 52 weeks with current position considerably above upper control limit" — p.105.

**LEAD — Trust is a national outlier on temporary staffing: "2nd highest bank to pay cost ratio in London"; on track to breach NHSE bank cap**
"we are an outlier with the 2nd highest bank to pay cost ratio in London and also an outlier in our use of bank to support ETOC" — p.119, [Q&P Report, Finance Domain]. Bank spend was £29.2m in 2025/26; NHSE has set a £21.3m limit for 2026/27 — "significant reduction in spend needed... The Trust is at risk of not achieving the target" — p.189/193, [M2 Finance Report]. "The current run rate would put the Trust above the NHSE cap for 2026/27" — p.120.

**WORTH WATCHING — £16.5m savings target with £4.7m at risk; recurrent CIP delivery at just 21% against an 80% requirement**
"CIP Target: £16.5m inclusive of internal overprogramming target of £1.5m... Forecast delivery is £10.3m, leaving a £4.7m risk. Recurrent Delivery: Forecast at 21% (£3.1m) against the 80% requirement — urgent improvement needed" — p.199, [M2 Finance Report]. Trust reports M2 breakeven on plan but "continues to operate with an underlying deficit" — p.93. Note improvement: regional submission at start of June carried a c£8m risk.

**WORTH WATCHING — Deaf CAMHS inpatient unit (Corner House) closed to admissions on 30 June under NHSE Tier 4 review; national inpatient model scrapped**
"NHS England's review of Tier 4 Deaf CAMHS has confirmed a move from a national inpatient model to enhanced regional community services... Corner House closed as an inpatient service on 30 June, with funding reinvested in expanding specialist Deaf CAMHS community teams across England" — p.45, [CEO's Report]. 16 Deaf CAMHS 52-week breaches reported — p.105.

**WORTH WATCHING — Two NEDs (incl. Vice Chair / Senior Independent Director) leaving in the autumn; trust recruiting clinical and audit NEDs plus a digital associate NED**
"we are currently out for recruitment for two new NEDs to replace Sola Afuape and Jonathan Warren who will be leaving us in the autumn. We are recruiting a Clinical NED and an Audit NED... interviewing at the end of July" — p.35, [Chair's Report].

**WORTH WATCHING — Trust remains NHSE Segment 2 (ranked 18th of 61 MH trusts); sickness absence a deteriorating outlier (ranked 9th–10th of 61)**
"the Trust maintaining a segment 2 position ranked 18th out of 61 MH Trusts (up from 21st in Q3)... sickness absence has increased by 0.2% with Trust ranked 9 out of 61" — p.91/96, [Q&P Report]. Trust-wide LoS 70 days vs 51-day year-end target; older adults 122 days vs 87 target — p.50 [CEO Report] / p.97 [Q&P].

**WORTH WATCHING — Risk-assessment audit finds only 61% of records current and just 33% of safety plans co-produced with patients**
"Only 61% of records are current and comprehensive... there is a significant gap in patient involvement, with only 33% of records demonstrating co-produced risk and safety planning" — p.70, [QSAC Chair's Report, Trust Quality Risk Assessment Audit]. Six Patient Safety Incident Investigations remain incomplete with "concerns raised regarding delays, variable report quality" — p.68.

## 4. Assessment

Not a routine pack, but not a scandal pack either — this is a financially clean (unmodified external audit opinion, Level 2 internal audit opinion, breakeven-on-plan) but operationally stretched mental health trust. The publishable HSJ material sits in patient safety and access: the named Prevention of Future Deaths report combined with the April suicide spike; the 17-day waits for an acute bed; the CQC adult acute inspection; and the children's 104-week/ADHD waiting-list picture. The bank-cost-outlier line ("2nd highest bank to pay cost ratio in London") is a solid finance-desk data point. FT-status roadmap and Complex Care business case were held in Part B and are worth a follow-up.
