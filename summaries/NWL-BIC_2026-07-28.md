## Top lines

[LEAD] Imperial has ~1,200 legacy mortuary "reportable incidents" back to 2015 still to be reviewed, and all four NWL trusts are only partially compliant with the David Fuller Inquiry recommendations as the NHSE 31 July board-assurance deadline lands.
[Evidence] ICHT "assessed themselves as partially compliant in 10 recommendations… full compliance cannot be declared until all legacy cases (since 2015) have been reviewed against HTARI standards. This is underway with approximately 1200 incidents to review." — p.35, [3.1 Mortuary Board Assurance Statement Review]

[LEAD] The mortuary review sits under a live regulator action: the Human Tissue Authority is running a sector-wide "Evidential Compliance Assessment" after finding "critical failures" in incident reporting at Nottingham University Hospitals, with a mandatory declaration due from all licensed post-mortem establishments by 18 September 2026.
[Evidence] HTA update 7 July 2026 (004/2026): "following a recent Post Mortem sector inspection which identified critical failures in the reporting of HTA reportable incidents (HTARIs) at Nottingham University Hospitals NHS Trust (HTA Licence number 12258), the HTA will be conducting a sector-wide Evidential Compliance Assessment (ECA)." — p.32, [3.1 Mortuary Board Assurance Statement Review]

[LEAD] The group is £7.8m behind its own deficit plan just two months into 2026/27, with all four trusts in the red.
[Evidence] "a year-to-date deficit of £19.5m, against a year-to-date deficit plan of £11.7m, reporting a £7.8m adverse variance… All four trusts report year to date adverse variances." Driven by resident doctors' industrial action (£5.5m, unfunded) and £7m efficiency under-delivery. — p.148 & p.152, [4.3.2 NWL APG Finance Report Month 2 (May 2026)]

[LEAD] The group's headline 2025/26 surplus was entirely a one-off national bonus; without it all four trusts only broke even.
[Evidence] "the year-end outturn was £18.1m surplus, with £18.1m bonus deficit support funding received in month 12 driving this. All four Trusts achieved their planned breakeven position, excluding this additional funding." — p.210, [8.1 Group CEO report, 3.2]

[LEAD] LNWH chief executive Pippa Nightingale is leaving to run Bedfordshire Hospitals; Mark Titcomb (MD of Ealing/Central Middlesex and the NWL Elective Orthopaedic Centre) steps up as interim CEO.
[Evidence] "Pippa Nightingale, Chief Executive at London North West University Healthcare is leaving after four years to take up a new role as Chief Executive of Bedfordshire Hospitals NHS Foundation Trust later this summer… Mark Titcomb has been appointed interim Chief Executive of LNWH." — p.25, [2.1 Chair's Report]; p.209, [CEO report 2.4]

[WORTH WATCHING] A member of Hillingdon ED staff was stabbed by a patient; Imperial reports a separate ED assault that led to a criminal conviction, plus "historical under-reporting" of violence.
[Evidence] THHFT: "The Committee members received a staff story following a serious violent incident in the Emergency Department, during which a member of staff was stabbed by a patient." — p.219, [8.2b THHFT TSC Chair's Report]. ICHT: incident "which resulted in a successful prosecution. The Committee noted historical under-reporting…" — p.227, [8.2d ICHT TSC Assurance Report]

[WORTH WATCHING] Two never events at named sites this cycle.
[Evidence] ICHT: "the May 2026 never event involving an unintentionally retained guidewire following insertion of a central venous catheter during an emergency procedure at Charing Cross Hospital" — p.228, [ICHT TSC 2.6]. CWFT: "a Never Event relating to a cataract procedure" — p.223, [CWFT TSC 2.1]

[WORTH WATCHING] Outbreak of water-associated organisms at Ealing Hospital, alongside rising E. coli and Pseudomonas at LNWH.
[Evidence] "an outbreak of water-associated organisms at Ealing Hospital, for which mitigation measures and ongoing surveillance were in place." — p.215, [LNWH TSC 2.6.2]

[WORTH WATCHING] Imperial has been picked for the second wave of NHSE's Advanced Foundation Trust programme; Hillingdon has appointed Laing O'Rourke as builder for its New Hospital Programme redevelopment.
[Evidence] "Imperial College Healthcare is one of 16 trusts in the second wave of the Advanced Foundation Trust Programme, due to begin assessment in 2026/27." / "Progress continued on redevelopment of Hillingdon hospital with Laing O'Rourke appointed as the builder." — p.209, [CEO report 2.3, 2.2]

[WORTH WATCHING] LNWH escalates the risk of acute trusts increasingly holding mental health patients with commissioning responsibility unresolved.
[Evidence] "Increasing operational, safety and financial risk arising from acute trusts absorbing care for mental health patients, with unresolved issues around commissioning responsibility and regulatory expectations." — p.217, [LNWH TSC 4.5]

## FOI leads

[FOI] Imperial legacy mortuary-incident review — once the HTA Evidential Compliance Assessment declaration is completed (due 18 Sept 2026), request from ICHT how many of the ~1,200 legacy incidents were previously unreported to the HTA, and the ECA declaration itself. Why it matters: quantifies the retrospective mortuary-governance failure the Fuller/Nottingham work has exposed. — evidence p.35.

[FOI] London Fire Brigade enforcement notice at Imperial — request the enforcement notice and remediation plan for the Mint Wing (referenced but not detailed). — p.230, [ICHT TSC 2.16].

[FOI] Violence against staff — request from all four trusts the number of physical assaults on staff (and RIDDOR reports) in 2025/26, plus the incident report for the Hillingdon ED stabbing. "Historical under-reporting" is admitted at ICHT. — p.219, p.227.

[FOI] Radiology reporting backlog on the Head & Neck pathway at Hillingdon — request the harm-review outcomes and number of patients affected. — p.220, [THHFT TSC 2.7].

[FOI] Fleming Centre "Principal Supply Chain Partner" appointment at Imperial (recommended to the Board in Common) — request the contract value, term and deliverables. — p.227, [ICHT TSC 2.3].

## Pack assessment

- Routine: false
- Top lines: 11 (LEAD: 5, WORTH WATCHING: 6, FOI: 5)
- Colour quotes: 4
- Annual accounts / annual report present: no — the statutory accounts are not in this pack (ICHT TSC notes an extraordinary audit committee on 25 June 2026 to sign off final annual accounts, "no significant issues anticipated," p.230). The pack does disclose the 2025/26 year-end outturn: APG £18.1m surplus driven entirely by £18.1m one-off deficit-support funding (underlying breakeven across all four trusts), p.210; ICHT £4.924m surplus including a cash-backed national bonus, p.229. Worth requesting the audited accounts / ISA 260 once signed.
- Note: the NOF "new metrics" paper (pp.177–187) and the Amos/Ockenden maternity paper (pp.107–114) are the trusts' own analyses of national frameworks — useful background, not standalone leads.
