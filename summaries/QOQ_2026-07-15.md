## FOI leads

- **The regulatory notice on the ICB deficit.** Minutes state "A regulatory notice was expected due to the deficit position; this was being managed through the Audit Committee." Request from HNY ICB (and NHSE NEY region) any correspondence/notice issued to the ICB regarding its 2025/26 deficit position and the withdrawal of deficit support funding, and the Audit Committee papers managing it. Matters because it converts a stated deficit into a documented regulatory intervention.
- **The 50% cost-reduction operating model.** The public Q&A confirms a "circa 50% recurrent cost reduction" and 133+ voluntary redundancies but not the establishment numbers or savings target. Request from HNY ICB the Model ICB operating model blueprint, target running-cost figure (£), FTE reductions by stage, and total redundancy/settlement cost.
- **Northern Lincolnshire Risk Summit (2 July 2026).** Chaired by the Regional Director of Nursing on NLAG pathways. Request the Risk Summit terms of reference, action plan and outputs from HNY ICB / NHSE — the pack references it but does not summarise findings.
- **GIRFT support offer for Humber Health Partnership.** Request the GIRFT support offer / three-month support plan for the HHP stroke and UEC pathways from HNY ICB.
- **16 Never Events / HHP safety.** Request from Humber Health Partnership the Never Event StEIS reports and the HHP Safer Surgery and Interventions Group minutes for 2025/26 (14 of 16 system Never Events at HHP).
- **Bridlington bed closures.** Request from York and Scarborough the business case / equality and quality impact assessment for closing the Bridlington escalation beds, and the East Riding Health Scrutiny Committee correspondence.
