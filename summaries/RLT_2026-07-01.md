[LEAD] Both trusts already off their breakeven plans at Month 2, with the April 2026 resident-doctor industrial action and CIP slippage named as drivers
[Evidence] "At Month 2, SWFT reported a YTD deficit of £4.7m, which is £1.5m adverse to plan, and GEH reported a YTD deficit of £3.2m, which is £1m adverse to plan.
