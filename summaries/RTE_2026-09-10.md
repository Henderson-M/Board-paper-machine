## Top lines

[LEAD] Trauma and Orthopaedics and Cardiology are both under NHS England-mandated support at the same time, with the external Royal College review into Cardiology still outstanding.
[Evidence] "Trauma and Orthopaedics is in mandated support following concerns across the pathway, including theatre productivity, sterile instruments, governance, workforce and culture." Also: "Cardiology remains under mandated support and enhanced oversight, with concerns principally relating to culture, behaviour, process, governance and pathway improvement. The formal Royal College report had not yet been received." — p.234-235, [Quality and Performance Committee Report]

[LEAD] The trust's own Finance and Resources Committee says its new finance and procurement system carries a "governance and potential fraud risk", with the fix now slipping to January or April 2027.
[Evidence] "The Committee remained concerned about the procurement element of the new finance and procurement system. This significant weakness (previously reported) creates a governance and potential fraud risk. A solution has been agreed but the implementation date will now slip to either January or April 2027." — p.411, [Finance and Resources Committee Report]

[LEAD] KPMG has been engaged to support the trust's financial sustainability programme, which still has a £14m gap in "red/unidentified" savings schemes nine months into the year.
[Evidence] "there remained an unidentified gap of £14m in red/unidentified schemes in the Sustainability plan which would need to be recovered in the remaining nine months... details of the outcome of the KPMG supported plan – including scheme timelines, complexity and delivery risk." — p.412, [Finance and Resources Committee Report]

[LEAD] The trust's underlying deficit is £66.5m even assuming full delivery of next year's savings plan, with a £6.2m-£6.4m recurrent savings gap still unidentified at month 4.
[Evidence] "The underlying position for the Trust is £66.5m deficit which assumes full delivery of 26/27 recurrent FSP... there remains a gap of £6.2m and the Trust is now showing an in-month shortfall in delivery in month 4." — p.278/280, [Integrated Performance Report]

[LEAD] The trust has asked NHS England for £9.1m in capital cash support because of reduced depreciation cash, and admits its estate asset registers cover only half its assets in enough detail to support investment decisions.
[Evidence] "The Committee approved a request to NHSE for £9.1m capital cash support required as a consequence of a reduced level of depreciation cash." And: "Existing Estate Asset Registers record only 50% of assets in sufficient detail to support informed decision making." — p.412, [Finance and Resources Committee Report]

[LEAD] The latest CQC maternity inspection (published 27 August) found a new regulatory breach on medicines management, even as the trust pushes to have its Section 31 enforcement conditions on maternity at Gloucestershire Royal lifted.
[Evidence] "The latest CQC inspection report was published on 27 August 2026 and rated maternity services Requires Improvement. It identified one regulatory breach relating to medicines management, for which an action plan is required by 7 September 2026." — p.34, [CQC Section 31 Conditions Report]

[LEAD] Maternity Incentive Scheme safety actions are rated red on five of six measures even as the trust self-assesses all eight Section 31 conditions as met and seeks their removal.
[Evidence] "Maternity Incentive Scheme Year 8 compliance remains a significant residual risk, with Safety Actions A, B, D, E and F rated red and Safety Action C rated amber." — p.234, [Quality and Performance Committee Report]

[WORTH WATCHING] The trust is treating hourly fetal-monitoring peer review ("fresh eyes") as a met Section 31 condition despite compliance running at 75%, still below its own 85% local target.
[Evidence] "The latest audit result is 75% against the Trust's 85% local target... Current compliance of 75% remains below the Trust's 85% local target." — p.35, [CQC Section 31 Conditions Report]

[WORTH WATCHING] Midwifery and neonatal nursing fill rates fell in Q1, with neonatal nurses' "Qualified in Specialty" compliance at just 57% and 45 staffing red flags recorded.
[Evidence] "Midwifery fill rates reduced to 87% in June, 45 staffing red flags were recorded, and reliance on temporary staffing remains significant... Neonatal nursing fill rates remain at 70%, Qualified in Specialty compliance is 57%." — p.131-132, [Perinatal Quality Oversight Model Report – Q1]

[FOI] Request the Royal College report into Cardiology at Gloucestershire Hospitals from the trust and/or the relevant Royal College — the Quality and Performance Committee report confirms it exists but had not been received as of 10 September.
[Evidence] "The formal Royal College report had not yet been received." — p.235, [Quality and Performance Committee Report]

[FOI] Request the scope, contract value and findings of the KPMG-supported review of the trust's Financial Sustainability Programme, due to be detailed to the Finance and Resources Committee in September.
