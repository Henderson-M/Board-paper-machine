# Wrightington, Wigan and Leigh Teaching Hospitals NHS FT board — 17 June 2026

- **Analysed:** 2026-06-15

## Top lines

[LEAD] Trust warns of "potential national tiering" as 65-week waits deteriorate after planned closure of two theatres.
[Evidence] "The Committee noted a deterioration in long wait performance, particularly 65-week waits, driven by reduced theatre capacity from the planned closure of theatre 5 and 6, prioritisation of urgent and cancer activity, and wider capacity and productivity constraints. This presents a risk of regulatory escalation, including potential national tiering, and associated reputational impact. Recovery actions… are likely to require utilisation of independent sector capacity, with consequent financial implications." — Finance & Performance Committee AAA (27 May 2026), [Item 11.3]

[LEAD] WWL reports a £6.2m technical deficit in its 2025/26 annual accounts, despite headlining an adjusted £3.3m surplus.
[Evidence] "At year end, we are reporting an adjusted surplus of £3.3m… The technical deficit in our annual accounts is £6.2m." — Trust Finance Report M12, Financial Performance slide 5, [Item 12a]

[LEAD] Trust says its underlying run-rate is a £14.5m deficit at year-end — behind its own planned £11.9m exit position — and forecasts it will need external cash support in Q2 2026/27.
[Evidence] "At Q4, the underlying deficit was £14.5m — demonstrating a continued improvement, but behind the forecast exit assumption of £11.9m." / "The current underlying run rate would indicate we will require external cash support in Q2 2026/27." — Trust Finance Report M12, slides 5–6, [Item 12a]

[LEAD] Month 1 2026/27 opens with a £2.1m deficit and a "Red"-rated revenue plan, CIP, and elective activity.
[Evidence] "We are reporting a deficit of £2.1m for April 2026. This is an overspend of £0.4m driven by the costs of industrial action, reduced levels of activity relative to plan and not achieving our CIP plan in month." Revenue plan, CIP and API income all rated "Red". — Trust Finance Report M01, slides 3–4, [Item 12c]

[LEAD] £31.8m CIP target for 2026/27 flagged as a "significant delivery risk", with productivity (1.7% in M1) running below the 2% NHSE target and well below the trust's own 5.5% goal.
[Evidence] "CIP Delivery & Productivity – The Committee highlighted a significant delivery risk associated with the Cost Improvement Programme, given the scale of savings required, alongside continued underperformance in productivity relative to national expectations and peer organisations." / "Implied productivity growth for month 1 is 1.7% versus 24/25 which is below the NHSE target of 2%. The Trust target is 5.5%." — F&P Committee AAA [Item 11.3]; Trust Finance Report M01 exec summary [Item 12c]

[WORTH WATCHING] Internal audit returns a "limited assurance" opinion on job planning, with two high-risk findings on pay accuracy and Working Time Directive compliance — escalated as a People Committee "alert".
[Evidence] "Job Planning Internal Audit (Limited Assurance) – The Committee considered the outcome of the internal audit, which returned a limited assurance opinion, including two high-risk findings relating to system interoperability impacting pay accuracy, and compliance with Working Time Directive requirements." — People Committee AAA (19 May 2026), [Item 11.2]

[WORTH WATCHING] Trust remains in the lowest NOF segment (Segment 4) for sickness absence; People Committee flags the position as "a concern".
[Evidence] "The Committee noted that the Trust remains in the lowest segment (Segment 4) for sickness absence… the Committee agreed that the current position remains a concern and should be clearly flagged." — People Committee AAA, [Item 11.2]

[WORTH WATCHING] Resident doctor industrial action continuing — CEO notes the trust is managing "the sixteenth" period of action; exception reports up 21% year-on-year and overtime claimed up 57%.
[Evidence] "The organisation is currently managing a four-day period of industrial action by our Resident Doctors, the sixteenth one to date." — CEO Report [Item 8]. "416 exception reports submitted compared with 345 in the previous year… Total overtime recorded increased from 244 hours and 20 minutes to 384 hours and 20 minutes." — GOSWH Annual Report 2025/26 [Item 2.3]

[WORTH WATCHING] Trust running an interim Freedom to Speak Up service after its ICB-provided arrangement ceased; flagged as a board-level risk pending a new provider.
[Evidence] "There is currently an interim FTSU service in place, following the cessation of the previous ICB-provided arrangement… the Committee recognised the risk associated with a temporary gap in substantive provision, and agreed this should be highlighted to the Board." — People Committee AAA [Item 11.2]; corroborated in FTSU Reflection Tool report [Item 14].
