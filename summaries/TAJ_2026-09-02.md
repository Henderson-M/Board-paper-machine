## Top lines

[LEAD] GPs withdrawing from psychotropic prescribing is pushing work onto the trust, and the board is warning of becoming a "prescription-only" service.
[Evidence] "**GP Withdrawal from Psychotropic Prescribing.** The Committee noted the **significant operational and clinical impact arising from GP collective action regarding prescribing responsibilities. This is creating additional pressures on Trust services and requires commissioner, ICB and provider collaboration to develop a sustainable commissioned care pathway and avoid secondary care becoming a "prescription-only" service.**" — Overall p.144, [Quality and Safety Committee Escalation and Assurance Report — listed under "Alert"]
[Evidence] The committee asked for the risk to be re-examined: "the Committee requested further review of existing risks, particularly: **The GP withdrawal from psychotropic prescribing risk.**" — Overall p.147, [same]

[LEAD] Seven CQC requirement notices are still open at the trust's adult working age services.
[Evidence] "**Adult Working Age Quality and CQC Compliance.** The Committee reviewed progress following the CQC inspection and noted that whilst significant improvements had been made, **further work is required in care planning, risk assessments, learning from incidents, staff supervision, training compliance and environmental standards. Seven requirement notices remain subject to delivery of improvement actions. The Committee specifically challenged the pace of improvement in supervision recording and assurance arrangements.**" — Overall p.144, [Quality and Safety Committee report — listed under "Alert"]

[LEAD] One person chairs two Midlands mental health trusts: Philip Gayle chairs Black Country Healthcare while remaining chair of Birmingham and Solihull Mental Health.
[Evidence] "Philip joined the Trust on an interim basis at the beginning of December 2024. He brings deep NHS and commercial experience to the role. **He has been at Birmingham and Solihull Mental Health Foundation Trust since 2019, first as a Non-Executive Director before becoming Deputy Chair in 2022 and substantive Chair in March 2023, a position he will continue in.**" — [Our Trust Board Members: Philip Gayle, Trust Chair]

[LEAD] The trust is still in segment 4 of the oversight framework despite reporting break-even, and NHS England has flagged concerns about how its savings are being reported.
[Evidence] Listed among areas requiring continued oversight: "**Ongoing segment 4 position within the National Oversight Framework.**" — Overall p.144, [Quality and Safety Committee report]
[Evidence] "The Committee noted that the Trust remained on plan and reported a **break-even position at Month 3. Members recognised this as a significantly improved position compared with previous years. NHS England has acknowledged the improved financial position, although concerns remain regarding CIP delivery reporting and future recurrent savings requirements.**" — Overall p.150, [Finance and Digital Committee Escalation and Assurance Report, 30 July 2026]

[WORTH WATCHING] The trust is modelling what happens when mental health block contracts are taken apart.
[Evidence] "A **Trust Financial Strategy will be completed by the end of September.** This forms part of **Best for Black Country** work and NHS finance accreditation requirements. **Work is underway to understand the implications of deconstructing block contracts and proposed future payment mechanisms within mental health services.**" — Overall p.150, [Finance and Digital Committee report]
[Evidence] The committee is being renamed to reflect a wider remit: "The Committee supported: **Renaming the Committee to Finance, Productivity and Performance Committee.** Amended Terms of Reference to strengthen oversight of performance, productivity and contracts." — [same]

[WORTH WATCHING] Children and young people are waiting more than two years, and urgent crisis response is below the national standard.
[Evidence] Areas requiring continued oversight: "Older Adult length of stay. **CYP waits over 104 weeks. Urgent crisis response performance below national standards.** Workforce metrics including sickness absence, vacancies and turnover." — Overall p.144, [Quality and Safety Committee report]
[Evidence] For balance, the pack records: "72-hour inpatient follow-up remains above the national standard at 85.4%. Adult acute and PICU length of stay remains broadly aligned to trajectory." — Overall p.77, [Trust Integrated Quality Performance Report, August 2026 — July data]

[WORTH WATCHING] The board's own committee says several high-level risks have not moved for a long time despite the mitigations logged against them.
[Evidence] "**Risk Management Arrangements. Members expressed concern that several high-level risks have remained static for prolonged periods despite mitigation activity. The Committee requested review of risk scoring, tolerance levels, ownership and timelines to ensure risks better reflect the current position and progress made.**" — Overall pp.144–147, [Quality and Safety Committee report]
[Evidence] Also queued for review: "The appropriateness of current risk scores and risk tolerances across the High Risk Register. **The newly broadened risk relating to adherence to clinical standards and record keeping.**" — Overall p.147, [same]

[WORTH WATCHING] A carer's story to the board described her father being discharged from community mental health services for non-engagement shortly after leaving hospital.
[Evidence] "A particular concern was **her father's discharge from community mental health services due to non-engagement shortly after leaving hospital. Lisa felt this risked reinforcing his belief that he did not need support and expressed deep concern that without sustained intervention** [he would deteriorate]" — [Carer story]

[WORTH WATCHING] Smoke-free "tolerance zones" are being reviewed so they do not become permanent by default.
[Evidence] "[the Committee asked for a review of] tolerance zones to ensure alignment with national smoke-free ambitions and **avoid arrangements becoming permanent by default**." — Overall p.146, [Quality and Safety Committee report]

[WORTH WATCHING] The pack records the change of health secretary and the new flu vaccination ambitions.
[Evidence] "**Yvette Cooper became Secretary of State for Health and Social Care on 20 July 2026, with Karin Smyth appointed as a Minister of State.** We will continue to monitor any changes in national policy and their implications for local services." — [Chief Executive's report]
[Evidence] "The **NHS England Flu Vaccination Programme 2026/27 (flu letter) was published on 2nd July 2026. This letter sets out NHSE initial 3-year ambitions for the flu vaccination programme**, as well as what we need to do to deliver this progr[amme]" — [Flu vaccination trajectory report, item 15.1.1]

## FOI leads

[FOI] **The GP psychotropic prescribing withdrawal.** *Request from Black Country Healthcare and NHS Black Country ICB:* the risk register entry and supporting papers quantifying the additional prescribing workload transferred to the trust, the correspondence with the ICB and the LMC, the numbers of patients affected, and any proposed commissioned care pathway. *Why it matters:* GP collective action pushing prescribing into secondary care is a system-wide consequence with a named trust warning about becoming a "prescription-only" service.

[FOI] **The seven outstanding CQC requirement notices.** *Request from Black Country Healthcare and the CQC:* the inspection report and the requirement notices for adult working age services, the trust's improvement action plan, and the current closure status of each notice — particularly on supervision recording, where the committee challenged the pace of progress.

[FOI] **The segment 4 position.** *Request from Black Country Healthcare and NHS England (Midlands):* the trust's NHS Oversight Framework domain scores and NHSE's segmentation correspondence, and NHS England's stated concerns about "CIP delivery reporting and future recurrent savings requirements".

[FOI] **The dual chairmanship.** *Request from Black Country Healthcare and Birmingham and Solihull Mental Health FT:* the appointment terms, time commitment and remuneration for the chair role at each trust, the NHS England approval for the joint arrangement, and any conflict of interest protocol.

[FOI] **The Trust Financial Strategy and block contract work.** *Request from Black Country Healthcare and NHS Black Country ICB:* the Trust Financial Strategy due at the end of September 2026, and the analysis of "deconstructing block contracts and proposed future payment mechanisms within mental health services".

[FOI] **The CYP 104-week waits.** *Request from Black Country Healthcare:* the number of children and young people waiting over 52, 78 and 104 weeks by pathway (including neurodevelopmental assessment), and the recovery trajectory.
