## Top lines

[LEAD] SWL ICB is clustering with South East London ICB, confirming a shared executive team across the two ICBs and a jointly designed governance framework.
[Evidence] Three exec appointments named as spanning both ICBs: "Dinah McLannahan has been appointed as Chief Finance and Compliance Officer across both South East London and South West London ICBs"; "Ed Waller has been appointed as Chief Commissioning Officer and Deputy Chief Executive appointed across South East London and South West London ICBs" (joining from Kent and Medway in early September); "Elaine Clancy as Chief Nurse and Quality Officer across both SEL and SWL" — p.274, [Organisation Report, Item 7]. CEO Andrew Bland's own declaration records him as "Chief Executive of another ICB (within a Cluster arrangement) NHS South East London ICB" — p.3, [Declarations of Interest]. Governance framework "Developed alongside South East London ICB" — p.15, [ICB Governance Framework, Item 3].

[LEAD] The ICB is running a restructure to meet the model ICB blueprint, with a management response to staff consultation published and posts now being filled after voluntary redundancies.
[Evidence] "the organisation has published the management response to consultation… on the proposed future structure of NHS South West London Integrated Care Board… This forms part of the wider change and transformation programme… to… deliver the requirements of the model ICB blueprint… The organisation is now entering into the filling of posts process which will run until quarter 3 of this year." — p.273, [Organisation Report, Item 7]. Minutes record "operational continuity risks during transition, including gaps created by voluntary redundancy before new structures were in place" — p.9, [Minutes 29 April 2026].

[LEAD] The board has determined a substantial service change permanently relocating Guy's and St Thomas' specialist children's cardiac, respiratory and critical care (CRIC) inpatient services, with the ICB response signed off by Chair's action.
[Evidence] "Determination of a substantial service change relating to the permanent relocation of GSTT specialist children's cardiac, respiratory and critical care (CRIC) inpatient services and agreement that Chair's action be taken to approve the ICB response to NHSE." — p.12, [Decisions made in other meetings, Item 2].

[LEAD] Two never events at Epsom and St Helier (ESTH) since April — a retained foreign object and a wrong-site surgery — with SWL flagged as the second-highest never-event reporter in London.
[Evidence] "Since 1 April 2026 (and last report to board), 2 Never Events (NE's) have been reported by ESTH (a retained foreign object and a wrong site surgery). A total of 15 NE's were recorded for the year 2025/26 almost similar to year 2024/25 with 14 NE reported… SWL remains second highest ICB in London region for reporting NEs." — p.236, [Quality Report, Item 6.4].

[LEAD] CQC has rated Croydon Health Services' maternity services "Requires Improvement" and issued warning notices over its urgent and emergency care, where corridor care continues.
[Evidence] "Following the Trust's inspection in Oct 2025, the CQC final report has rated the trust's maternity services as Requires Improvement… Improvement actions are progressing following CQC warning notices for UEC services. Operational pressures in ED continue to impact corridor care." — p.237, [Quality Report, Item 6.4].

[WORTH WATCHING] SWL had the second-highest volume of 12-hour A&E admission breaches in London in April, with 2,276 patients waiting over 12 hours.
[Evidence] "2,276 patients waited over 12 hours from 'decision to admit' to admission in April. SWL had the second highest volume of 12-hour breaches in London, and the seventh highest nationally." — p.254, [Performance Report, Item 6.5].

[WORTH WATCHING] The ICB is forecasting breakeven on a £4.38bn budget with a £33.3m savings target, but flags Right to Choose spend as a significant, uncontrolled risk.
[Evidence] "Savings plan is £33.3m and is on plan to deliver. £5.8m of these are non-recurrent"; "Right To Choose providers are still expected to exponentially increase above the plan until the new pathways have been agreed"; running costs forecast "£26.5m" against a "not to exceed £26.5m" target. — p.214 & p.220, [Month 2 Finance Report, Item 6.2].
