## Top lines

**[LEAD]** Reported break-even forecast masks a "likely" £2.7m year-end deficit — worst case £9.5m — with the balanced position only held up by drawing down temporary central reserves.
[Evidence] Reported forecast is break-even "in line with plan… However as a result of material emerging financial pressures – particularly within DMH – the reported forecast is based on a best case scenario… As such it is critical that robust recovery plans are implemented as a matter of urgency." — Finance Report internal p.5 [Paper P, Finance Report Month 3 / June 2026] (combined p.167). Risk-adjusted outturn: **Likely FOT £(2,738)k, Worst case £(9,496)k** — Appendix G internal p.18 [Paper P] (combined p.180).

**[LEAD]** Mental Health directorate £1.06m overspent at month 3, and the plan's whole balanced position now rests on temporary reserve gains that will run out if the overspend is not cut.
[Evidence] "The Mental Health Directorate is overspending by £1,064k at the end of month 3. This includes medical locum pressures of £420k (14 locums currently utilised). Out of area beds budgets are overspending by £188k…" — internal p.4 [Paper P] (combined p.166). "…the current directorate overspend is already exceeding the surplus/deficit profile in the plan, with the difference only able to be offset by the bringing forward of temporary gains in central reserves. If the rate of directorate overspend does not reduce promptly, the central reserves gains will be fully utilised and the overall Trust position will need to be supported by trust wide recovery plan actions." — internal p.6 [Paper P] (combined p.168).

**[LEAD]** Nearly 8,000 people are waiting more than a year each just for an ADHD assessment — the longest for 381 weeks (over seven years) — and the trust's own trajectory shows the backlog still growing past 10,000 into early 2027.
[Evidence] "ADHD (18 week local RTT) – assessment waits over 52 weeks – No of waiters: **7,951**" (May-26; up from 7,860); "Longest waiter (weeks): **381**". Trajectory chart labelled "special cause variation of a concerning nature," rising toward ~10,000+ by 2027. — DMH Waiting Times Dashboard + ADHD assessment exception report [Paper Q, Integrated Performance Report June 2026 (M3)] (combined p.205 and p.213).

**[LEAD]** Community paediatrics assessment backlog also over a year for 7,684 children — longest waiter 229 weeks — and "All Neurodevelopment" treatment waits over 52 weeks stand at 1,578.
[Evidence] "Community Paediatrics – assessment waits over 52 weeks – No of waiters: **7,684**" (longest 229 weeks); "All Neurodevelopment (inc CAMHS, SALT, PAEDS) – treatment waits over 52 weeks: **1,578**" (longest 233 weeks). CYP 18-week compliance just **22.8%** vs plan. — FYPCLDA Waiting Times Dashboard [Paper Q, IPR] (combined p.211, p.223).

**[WORTH WATCHING]** NHS England is requiring trusts to cut the *growth* in corporate-service spend by 50%, being delivered across LPT and group partner NHFT via a "Group Value Programme."
[Evidence] "NHS England was expecting Trusts to reduce growth in corporate service expenditure by 50% and an update on progress towards a Group corporate and enabling service efficiencies across LPT and NHFT was presented." — Finance & Performance Committee AAA report, 18 June 2026 [Paper O] (combined p.160).

**[WORTH WATCHING]** The Finance & Performance Committee admits it lost sight of a commissioned service — the Adult Eating Disorder Service — because quarterly performance reports were stopped in 2025.
[Evidence] "FPC had little oversight of the performance of the Adult Eating Disorder Service since quarterly reports had been stopped in 2025. FPC agreed to introduce an annual report starting with presentation in August 2026." — Finance & Performance Committee AAA report [Paper O] (combined p.160).

**[WORTH WATCHING]** A community hospital ward was temporarily closed after drainage failures blocked patient and staff toilets "on a number of occasions, over the past four years."
[Evidence] Temporary closure of North Ward at Hinckley and Bosworth Community Hospital owing to drainage issues causing blocked toilets "on a number of occasions, over the past four years." — Managing Director's Report [Paper G] (combined p.55).

**[WORTH WATCHING]** Older-adult mental health inpatients are staying well beyond target, averaging 86.3 days.
[Evidence] "Average Length of Stay in Older Adult Acute MH Beds: **86.3** days" vs target ≤77.9 (adult acute & PICU 52.9 vs ≤52.5). Inappropriate out-of-area placement bed days 14 (target 0). — Operational Planning Dashboard [Paper Q, IPR] (combined p.193).

**[WORTH WATCHING]** Freedom to Speak Up cases hit an all-time high of 178, three-quarters of them about staff wellbeing/workplace culture — against a strong headline staff-survey score.
[Evidence] Worker Wellbeing the dominant theme (134 of 178 cases); "no detriment cases were reported during the year." National FTSU staff-survey sub-score fell to a five-year low of 6.37 (LPT 6.93). National Guardian's Office abolished end June 2026 following the Dash review / 10-Year Health Plan. — Freedom to Speak Up Annual Report 2025/26 [Paper N] (combined pp.150–158).

## FOI leads

**[FOI]** ADHD assessment provider procurement collapse — request the contract detail. The commissioned replacement ADHD service provider withdrew after being awarded, and a reserve provider is now signing the data-sharing agreement, against a 7,951-strong 52-week backlog.
- Request from **Leicester City Council / LLR ICB** (co-commissioners): name of the withdrawn provider, the original and reserve award decisions, contract values, and the timeline. "New provider awarded the contract withdrew, reserve provider now awarded contract and currently signing off the data sharing agreement." — ADHD 18-week assessment exception report [Paper Q, IPR] (combined p.213).

**[FOI]** Adult Eating Disorder Service oversight gap — request the stopped reports. Quarterly performance reporting to FPC ceased in 2025 with governance only being reinstated (as an annual report) from August 2026.
- Request from **LPT**: the last quarterly Adult Eating Disorder Service performance reports, and any correspondence explaining why quarterly reporting stopped. — F&P Committee AAA report [Paper O] (combined p.160).

**[FOI]** Fire alarm systems review — request the report. An independent review of fire alarm systems was commissioned and a risk added to the Corporate Affairs register.
- Request from **LPT**: the independent fire-alarm review report, its scope, and the mitigation plan. — Quality & Safety Committee assurance report (combined ~p.98).
