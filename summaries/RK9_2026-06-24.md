## Top lines

[LEAD] Trust set a 2026/27 breakeven plan only after NHSE/ICB agreed £35m non-recurrent funding plus £11m extra savings, converting a £46.5m planned deficit.
[Evidence] "The Trust originally submitted a plan for 2026/27 with a deficit of £46.5m as per the Operating plan approved by the Board in March 2026. Following discussions with NHSE and the ICB, the Board approved a revised breakeven plan at its May 2026 meeting. This is based on non-recurrent NHSE/ICB funding of £35m and £11m of increased Cost Improvement Programme (CIP) savings. The overall breakeven plan is now based on a CIP target of £91.6m (c8% of turnover)." — p.20, [Chief Executive's Report]

[LEAD] Trust remains in NHS Oversight Framework Segment 4 (lowest), ranked 105th of 134 acute trusts, with finance/productivity rated "low performing".
[Evidence] "The Trust will remain in NHS Oversight Framework (NOF) Segment 4 following the Quarter 4 2025/26 update… improving its national position from 119th out of 134 acute trusts in Quarter 3 to 105th (joint) in Quarter 4." — p.20, [Chief Executive's Report]; "Segment 4 / No change from previous quarter… Finance and Productivity / 4 – Low performing… Patient Safety / 3 – Below Average" — p.37, [Integrated Performance Report]

[LEAD] Trust ended 2025/26 with a £110m underlying deficit and is targeting a three-year route from a £125m underlying position to surplus.
[Evidence] "The Trust ended the year with an underlying deficit of £110m." — p.245 [Quality Account 2025/26]; "Move from an underlying deficit position of £125m to £0 in three years and into surplus in years 4 and 5… c50% from £125m in 2025/26, to £46m 2026/27." — p.245, [Quality Account 2025/26]

[LEAD] Royal Eye Infirmary rated "Requires Improvement" by CQC and designated one of three "pathways in distress"; committee took only partial assurance citing leadership and culture concerns.
[Evidence] "the REI service had been rated 'Requires Improvement' following the CQC inspection… REI had been designated as one of three 'pathways in distress' and would receive enhanced support, including targeted coaching and a rapid improvement programme… Noted – Partial assurance… assurance is limited due to ongoing concerns regarding leadership and culture within REI, potential similar risks in other services." — p.69–70, [Safety and Quality Chair Assurance Report]

[LEAD] Board flags multiple "fragile services" needing focused support — cardiology, neurology/stroke, urology and children's services, the latter via internal audit.
[Evidence] "additional services, including cardiology and neurology/stroke, had also been identified as requiring focused support… Concerns were also raised regarding potential risks in children's services identified through internal audit." — p.70, [Safety and Quality Chair Assurance Report]; "emerging risks within a small number of services, including cardiology, neurology, stroke and urology… a more formalised 'fragile services' approach." — p.11, [Minutes 27 May 2026]

[LEAD] Staff survey deteriorated, with 18 areas significantly worse and statistically significant declines in "voice that counts" and engagement; recommendation scores fell and sit below national.
[Evidence] "we significantly improved in 6 survey areas, had 75 with no significant difference and 18 significantly worse. 53.02% of colleagues responded they would recommend the Trust as a good place to work, down from 2024. Nationally, this score is 57.77%. 55.17% of colleagues would be happy with the standard of care provided to a friend or loved one, also down from 2024. Nationally, this score is 60.83%." — p.28, [Quality Account 2025/26]

[LEAD] CIP already £4.6m adverse at month 2, with the CEO naming industrial action, inflation and the nursing job evaluation process as additional in-year risks.
[Evidence] "The Trust position at month 2 is a deficit of £5.4m… The CIP position is £4.6m adverse to plan year to date… there are risks noted reflecting increase CIP delivery risk of the revised targets. Additional risks from industrial action, inflation and from the nursing job evaluation process has also been noted in month 2." — p.20, [Chief Executive's Report]

[WORTH WATCHING] Board Assurance Framework scores culture (SR3) and ageing infrastructure (SR4) at 20 — the two highest strategic risks for 2026/27.
[Evidence] "SR3: Creating a Healthy Culture / 20 / Neutral… SR4: Managing aging Infrastructure / 20 / Neutral" — p.299, [Board Assurance Framework]

[WORTH WATCHING] Cancer 62-day performance for 2025/26 was 63.1% against an 85% target; 31-day treatment standard also missed on theatre capacity.
[Evidence] "The Trust did not achieve the target to treat 85% of patients within 62 days of referral. Overall performance across the entire year was 63.1%." — p.204, [Quality Account 2025/26]; "The Trust did not achieve the target to treat 96% of patients within 31 days… Limited theatre capacity continued to be a challenge." — p.204, [Quality Account 2025/26]

[WORTH WATCHING] Six sexual misconduct incidents and 26 staff detriment/disciplinary/bullying cases recorded in 2025/26, against a year of declining staff experience.
[Evidence] "Number of Sexual misconduct incidents 2025/26: 6… Cases of staff detriment, suspicion and disciplinary action, bullying 2025/26: 26" — p.29, [Quality Account 2025/26]

[WORTH WATCHING] Hospital-onset C.difficile rose for a fourth consecutive year to 154 cases, despite minutes describing a "sustained reduction".
[Evidence] "Incidence of C.difficile… 92 / 108 / 124 / 154" (2022/23–2025/26) — p.250, [Quality Account 2025/26 Annex A]; cf. "A sustained reduction in Clostridioides difficile (C. difficile) cases was also highlighted." — p.11, [Minutes 27 May 2026]

[FOI] Internal audit reported "one area of limited assurance" in 2025/26, but the named area is not stated; Consultant Job Planning report still pending.
