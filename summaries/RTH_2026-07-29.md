[LEAD] OUH is one of 12 trusts named in Baroness Amos's national maternity and neonatal investigation (published 30 June 2026), and has issued an unreserved apology accepting the failings in care families described.
[Evidence] "On 30 June the National Maternity and Neonatal Investigation, led by Baroness Amos, published a national report and also individual reports about 12 NHS trusts, including OUH… an unreserved apology to the women, babies and families who suffered in our care… We accept what families have told the investigation, the failings in care they describe in the report, and our responsibility to act." — p.2, [CEO Report TB2026.57]

[LEAD] Sir Andrew Morris OBE, currently deputy chair and senior independent director at NHS England, appointed OUH trust chair from 1 August — stepping down from the NHSE board and his other roles to take a single-trust chairmanship.
[Evidence] "Sir Andrew Morris OBE has been appointed as our new Trust Chair – he will take up the role on 1 August. Sir Andrew is currently Deputy Chair and Senior Independent Director at NHS England… Plans have been agreed for him to step down from his current roles over the coming months." — p.4, [CEO Report TB2026.57, 3.1-3.3]

[LEAD] Never event in June: wrong-site ophthalmology treatment, declared a patient safety incident investigation; ophthalmology safety risk being escalated to the Risk Committee.
[Evidence] "Never Event: Current performance confirms one June PSII relating to wrong-site ophthalmology treatment, with no harm reported." — p.5, [IPR M3 TB2026.65]; corroborated: "The Risk Committee would review two additional risks – reputational risk related to maternity issues and safety risk in ophthalmology." — p.4, [Minutes 27 May TB2026.55, para 30]

[LEAD] Multiple staff under active investigation for inappropriate access to patient electronic records, linked by the interim CEO to national media reports.
[Evidence] "The Interim CEO addressed national media reports issue of inappropriate access to patient electronic records… Within the Trust, a number of potential instances of inappropriate access were under active investigation. If staff were found to have accessed data inappropriately, correct disciplinary processes would be followed." — p.3, [Minutes 27 May TB2026.55, paras 24-25]

[LEAD] Trust warns it is likely to miss its target to cut its underlying deficit by c£40m; only £59.4m (47%) of its £126.9m 2026/27 savings requirement is identified, leaving £67.4m (53%) unidentified at month 3.
[Evidence] "even with these actions the Trust is likely to miss its target to reduce the underlying deficit by c£40m." — p.16, [Finance Report M3 TB2026.66]; "total new 2026/27 CIP requirement of c.£120.8m… totalling £126.9m. As at Month 3, identified schemes total £59.4m, leaving £67.4m (c.53%) unidentified." — p.11, [Finance Report M3 TB2026.66]

[WORTH WATCHING] Substantive CEO Prof Meghana Pandit has returned only part-time, with interim CEO Simon Crowther holding the accountable officer role until 1 October 2026.
[Evidence] "It was noted that the Trust Board has received a note setting out the current interim arrangements in relation to the Chief Executive and Accountable Officer role. [WORTH WATCHING] Surgical Elective Centre first-patient date slips to October 2026 (from September); the 2-3 month delay is estimated at ~£2.3m of lost income / ~794 elective cases.
[Evidence] "We expect the first patient will now be treated in October 2026, rather than September as originally planned. [WORTH WATCHING] Cancer 62-day performance (63.5%, met plan) remains "significantly below national and Shelford comparators"; a GIRFT radiotherapy review is due in September.
[Evidence] "Cancer 62-Day Pathways: Performance met plan at 63.5% against an 61.2% plan, though we note that we are significantly below national and Shelford comparators. Actions include… radiotherapy (GIRFT review September)." — p.6, [IPR M3 TB2026.65]

[WORTH WATCHING] Continued non-executive churn: Dame June Raine CBE stepped down as NED for health reasons after ~6 months; audit committee chair Paul Dean is leaving during 2026; two NEDs are being recruited; the chair post has been filled on an acting basis since March 2025.
[Evidence] "Dame June Raine CBE, who started in post as a Non-Executive Director on the Trust Board on 1 December 2025, has stepped down for health reasons… Paul Dean… currently Chair of the Audit Committee, will be leaving the Board during 2026… We are currently recruiting two new Non-Executive Directors." — p.5, [CEO Report TB2026.57, 3.5-3.7]

[WORTH WATCHING] Board to abolish its Integrated Assurance Committee, redistributing functions to four new portfolio committees (Quality & Performance, Finance, People, Investment/Digital/Estates) — phased from August 2026.
[Evidence] "The key structural change proposed is the removal of the Integrated Assurance Committee (IAC), with its functions redistributed across a Quality and Performance Committee, Finance Committee, People Committee and Investment, Digital and Estates Committee." — p.2, [New Committee Structure Proposals TB2026.64]
