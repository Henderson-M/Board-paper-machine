[Evidence] "NHS England identified NELFT as one of six organisations whose segmentation improved in Q4 following changes to the financial override position." — p.106, [Integrated Performance Report, Item 16]

[WORTH WATCHING] NHSE London region has offered to relax the "triple lock" oversight process for the trust from Q2, conditional on it staying within plan.
[Evidence] "the region has offered that dependant on the Trust overall financial position staying within plan as at quarter 2, the triple lock process would be relaxed from an NHSE/ICB point of view in light of the sustainability of the Trust finances." — p.61, [Financial report: Month 3 2026/27, Item 13]

[WORTH WATCHING] Complaints handling has materially deteriorated, with a large rise in complaints open beyond 90 days year on year.
[Evidence] "assurance is limited by worsening response timeliness, 109 complaints remaining under investigation, and 232 complaints open for longer than 90 days compared with 65 in the previous year... This represents a material deterioration in responsiveness" — p.103, [Integrated Performance Report, Item 16]

[WORTH WATCHING] The cost-improvement (Delivering Value) programme is running c£2.6m short of target on a risk-adjusted basis, and the trust models a c£4.3m underlying deficit into 2027/28.
[Evidence] "the risk adjusted forecast delivery is c£25.9m which is c£2.6m off plan" against a "£28.5m" target; "Modelling... has highlighted a c£4.3m underlying forecast deficit, which is an improving position as c£7.6m deficit was reported as at month 1" — p.63, [Financial report: Month 3 2026/27, Item 13]

[WORTH WATCHING] Access is deteriorating across adult mental health, autism/ADHD assessment and community waits, dragging 18-week performance down; prolonged A&E waits for mental health patients remain a concern.
[Evidence] "sustained pressures in adult mental health access, neurodevelopmental pathways and community waiting times driving deterioration in 18-week performance"; "Prolonged A&E waits remain a major quality, safety and flow concern... particularly those exceeding 72 hours" — p.101, [Integrated Performance Report, Item 16]
