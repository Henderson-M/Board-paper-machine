## 1. Top lines

Mitigations listed include "HR provisions, the release of balance sheet flexibilities, delivery of cash releasing Cost Improvement Programmes (CIPs), and anticipated benefits from PFI unavailability deductions." — Finance Report M2, [Item 15, P87/26]

## 2. FOI leads

- **Out-of-area placement reduction review.** The pack repeatedly references a "detailed review" of OAPs "planned to provide a clearer trajectory for improvement" and the trust's position "within the national context", but does not disclose scope or findings. Request from the trust: the terms of reference, the completed review, and the reduction trajectory/actions. (Ref: minutes P59/26; IQPR Item 14.)
- **Committee assurance reports (Enc. 10a/10b–14).** Finance & Resource, Quality, Audit, People & Culture and Charitable Funds committee assurance reports are summarised only in headline form in the public pack. Request the full committee reports — particularly the Finance & Resource discussion of the CIP "gap to target" and the digital/IT support provider "deterioration in response rates". (Ref: minutes 58/26; agenda items 16–20.)
- **Private board items — secure patient transport tender and payroll services contract.** Two items "recommended for Board approval which would be discussed during Private Board relating to the secure patient transport tender and the payroll services contract." Request contract values, awarded suppliers, and term. (Ref: minutes 58/26.)
- **CIP scheme-level detail.** £1.0m of the £7.8m CIP is still "opportunities" and Community directorate is £1,664k behind its £1,834k target at M2. Request the scheme-level CIP tracker and the risk-rating of unidentified savings. (Ref: Finance Report M2, Item 15.)
- **Harplands water temperature management.** Estates issue where "a definitive resolution had not yet been achieved" after "a sustained period of limited progress". Request the estates report / risk assessment on water safety at Harplands Hospital. (Ref: minutes 58/26.)

## 4. Pack assessment

**Broadly routine, but not empty.** This is a small, high-performing MH/LD trust (CQC "Outstanding", positive CQC MHA inspection Feb 2026, strong access/RTT/CAMHS metrics) and the pack is heavy on good-news items (Nursing Times shortlisting, wellbeing grant, new Bridge and Beyond CYP service, quality-account showcase). No leadership departures, no regulatory intervention, no CQC downgrade. The genuinely newsworthy thread is financial: an underlying/unmitigated £1.1m deficit sitting behind a wafer-thin £154k planned surplus, and CIP delivery at under half of plan two months in — a soft version of the "control-total held only by mitigations" story HSJ runs on larger trusts. Best supporting angles are the 840 CYP waiting >104 weeks (against a 636 plan), rising violence and aggression / racist abuse against staff, and persistent bank overspend. The OAP reduction review and the two private-board contracts (secure patient transport, payroll) are the strongest FOI follow-ups. No single item is near-publishable on its own without further reporting.
