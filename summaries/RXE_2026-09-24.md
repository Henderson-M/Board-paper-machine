[LEAD] The trust proposes to stop adding new adult referrals to its autism assessment waiting list from 1 April 2027, routing them instead through a peer-led voluntary-sector triage service, and to contact every one of the c.2,300 adults currently waiting before Christmas 2026 — final decision due November.
[Evidence] "the proposal is that from 1 April 2027, all new adult referrals for autism assessment would be triaged through that service and would not be added to an RDaSH adult autism diagnostic waiting list" — p.297, [Autism Assessment Services, Paper V].
