# Wirral Community Health and Care FT / WUTH — Group Board (Public) — 3 June 2026

- **Source:** https://www.wchc.nhs.uk/wp-content/uploads/2026/05/Group-Board-of-Directors-in-Public-3-Jun.pdf
- **Pack:** 1 file, 290pp (14MB)
- **Analysed:** 2026-06-11
- **Note:** Joint Group Board covering Wirral University Teaching Hospital FT (WUTH) and Wirral Community Health & Care FT (WCHC); statutory transaction (merger) is in progress, targeted single organisation by 2027.

## Top lines

[LEAD] WUTH closed 2025/26 with a £49.5m deficit — a £27.3m adverse variance to plan (£27.4m per minutes) — and its 2026/27 medium-term finance plan has yet to be approved by NHS England.
[Evidence] "At the end of March 2026 (M12), WUTH is reporting a deficit of £49.5m. This is in line with the forecast that the Trust submitted to NHS England in February but, excluding DSF, is a £27.3m adverse variance to plan." — p.118, [Joint Finance and Business Performance Committee Chairs Report, 22 Apr 2026]. Minutes confirm: "the Trust has confirmed to NHSE a revised forecast outturn for 2025/26 of a £49.5m deficit, representing a £27.4m adverse variance against the plan" — p.10, [Minutes 1 Apr 2026]. "The Trust's financial plan is yet to be agreed with NHSE and therefore remains subject to revision" — p.70, [CFO Finance Summary WUTH]. "Note that the plan has not been approved by NHSE and that further measures are required to meet the mandated control total" — p.71.

[LEAD] WUTH is in NOF segment 4 and is operating under "financial undertakings"; the joint group's resubmitted three-year plan still leaves a £14.9m gap to the national control totals.
[Evidence] BAF Risk ID05 (finance): "WUTH in financial undertakings" and "Resubmitted plans have a combined gap to national control totals of £14.9m" — p.104, [BAF ID05]. Repeated "NOF segment 4 – WUTH / NOF segment 1 – WCHC" — pp.100, 102, [BAF ID03, ID04]. Risk rated 5x3=15 (high), target 5x2=10 not until March 2027.

[LEAD] Sterile services critical incident at WUTH resulted in a recognised loss of income reported through to M10; not previously detailed publicly.
[Evidence] "the most significant change compared to month 9 being the recognition of the loss of income due to the impact of the Sterile Services critical incident" — p.13, [Minutes 28 Jan/1 Apr 2026 — WUTH FBPC chair report]. No detail in pack on cause, dates or scale beyond that it materially worsened M10 position.

[LEAD] Staff describing the merger as creating "an uncertain environment" — WUTH staff survey response rate fell to 42% (below 47% national average), 5 of 9 People Promise elements significantly down at WUTH (3 of 9 at WCHC), and the board expects 2026 results to "deteriorate further".
[Evidence] "DS commented that organisational pressures and the merger was creating an uncertain environment for staff, while emphasising areas within both Trusts' control and the need to deliver the merger at pace" — p.19, [Minutes, Item 28 Staff Survey]. "Members commented that upcoming year and the 2026 staff survey results were likely to be challenging and may deteriorate further" — p.19. WUTH response rate 42% (vs national 47%), WCHC 51% (vs national 62%) — p.19.

[LEAD] WUTH is hosting UK citizens repatriated from the MV Hondius cruise ship at the request of NHSE/UKHSA following Hantavirus cases, on the Arrowe Park accommodation block — guests arrived 10 May 2026.
[Evidence] "We were asked by NHS England and the UK Health Security Agency (UKHSA) to house UK citizens repatriated from the MV Hondius cruise ship in the accommodation block on the Arrowe Park Hospital site to provide them with a safe place for their isolation period, following cases of Hantavirus… We welcomed the guests on Sunday 10 May 2026 and nobody showing any symptoms was transferred to the site" — p.23, [CEO Report].

[WORTH WATCHING] WUTH 2026/27 CIP target is £30.7m (6%) but risk-adjusted forecast for delivery is only £23.1m — flagged as "primary risk to achieving the 2026/27 financial position".
[Evidence] "Full CIP Delivery: The primary risk to achieving the 2026/27 financial position is delivery of our CIP target of £30.7m. The current risk adjusted forecast for delivery is £23.1m" — p.70, [CFO Finance Summary WUTH]. WCHC CIP target £6.0m (also 6%).

[WORTH WATCHING] WUTH cash balance £2.7m at end of April 2026 — at NHSE's minimum stipulated level, with no guarantee of continued revenue support; June application submitted.
[Evidence] "The cash balance at the end of M1 was £2.7m, in line with NHSE's stipulated minimum cash balance… The revenue support applications for April and May were approved, and the application for June has been submitted" — p.70. "There is no guarantee that this support will continue" — p.10, [Minutes 1 Apr 2026].

[WORTH WATCHING] FOI/Subject Access Request non-compliance flagged at WUTH by the Joint Digital Committee as an alert; improvement plan in train.
[Evidence] "FOI and Subject Access Requests – WUTH Non-Compliance: The Committee has received and supported an improvement plan, including additional resources, digital workflow improvements and performance dashboards, and noted the SIRO is providing a further update to the Group Board in May" — p.116, [Joint Digital Committee Chairs Report, 16 Apr 2026].

[WORTH WATCHING] WUTH failed national 31-day and 62-day cancer standards and ambulance handover non-compliant; Type 1 A&E attendances 15% higher than April 2025.
[Evidence] "The Trust underachieved against the 31 and 62-day standards. 62-day performance is forecast to be achieved in April 2026" — p.56. "Attendances in April were 15% higher than in April 2025, increasing operational pressure across the department" — p.56. "ambulance handover performance remains non-compliant against the standard" — p.56, [EMD Operations Summary WUTH].

[WORTH WATCHING] WUTH maternity has "two areas of partial compliance" — midwifery staffing below Birth Rate+ acuity and unimplemented recommendations from national reports; establishment increase approved and being recruited.
[Evidence] "highlighted there were two areas of partial compliance. These were related to midwifery staffing below Birth Rate+ acuity and recommendations from national reports not implemented" — p.16, [Minutes Item 23]. Maternity Incentive Scheme Year 8 progress noted — p.128.

## FOI leads

1. **Sterile Services critical incident at WUTH** — board paper only references "the recognition of the loss of income". Request: incident report, root cause investigation, dates of incident, scale of cancellations and value of income loss, copies of correspondence to/from NHSE about it.
2. **WUTH "financial undertakings"** — BAF ID05 mentions "WUTH in financial undertakings" and an "engagement with NHSE to define the metrics to remove all undertakings", with a meeting on 22 April 2026. Request: the letter or notice imposing undertakings, the undertaking text itself, the minute/agenda of the 22 April NHSE meeting, and the metrics framework subsequently agreed.
3. **NHSE non-approval of WUTH MTFP / £14.9m gap to national control totals** — BAF ID05 records the combined Trusts' resubmitted plan has a £14.9m gap and that NHSE considered the MTFP "non-compliant" (BAF ID03 p.100). Request: NHSE correspondence rejecting/non-approving the plan, and any conditions attached to revenue support.
4. **PWC support to NHS Cheshire and Merseyside ICB** — CEO report references "PWC support will remain throughout Q1" on ICB management of change and financial recovery. Request: PWC contract value, term, deliverables, scope.
5. **MIAA grip and control review (WUTH)** — referenced as "moderate assurance"; PWC also reviewed. Request: full MIAA report and PWC review document, with management responses.
6. **Voluntary redundancy scheme** — approved by Board "for specific, targeted areas" and requiring NHSE redundancy funding support. Request: scheme design, target areas, projected headcount reduction, full cost.
7. **Frontis site options review** — board has "paused the decision" on use of the Frontis site. Request: options review paper and surveyor / property valuation papers.
8. **WUTH Mortuary Services Annual Report 2025/26** — on the agenda for "assurance" but appendix not extracted in time budget. Request the unredacted report; look for any HTA findings, capacity, or coroner-related material.

## Pack note

Substantive pack with multiple finance-led leads. The four headline angles — £49.5m WUTH deficit / unapproved MTFP, financial undertakings + NOF segment 4, sterile services critical incident, and staff describing the merger as "uncertain" — are all directly attributable. Group/merger context is the connective tissue: the same paper that records the £49.5m loss describes the integration programme as advancing and a single organisation as the goal by 2027. Strongest near-publishable line is the unapproved 2026/27 plan paired with the financial-undertakings / segment 4 status.
