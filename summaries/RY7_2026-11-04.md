Note: this is the **Group Board of Directors in Public** for Wirral Community Health and Care FT (WCHC) and Wirral University Teaching Hospital FT (WUTH), which share a single joint board, joint executive and joint papers. Most of the signal in this pack relates to WUTH (the acute trust); WCHC itself is in surplus and in NOF segment 2. Page references are to the single combined PDF (~500 pages).
