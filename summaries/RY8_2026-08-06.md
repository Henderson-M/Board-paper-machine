[Evidence] "At month 3, efficiency delivery remained on plan but was heavily supported by vacancies rather than fully planned transformation." [LEAD] The trust's external audit opinion was delayed by outstanding Local Government Pension Scheme assurance, the board's audit committee logged reputational risk over it, and further delays are expected.
[Evidence] "A recurring and substantive issue discussed by the Committee was the delay in finalising the audit opinion resulting from outstanding third-party assurance relating to the Local Government Pension Scheme (LGPS)." The committee discussed "Reputational risk arising from the delayed audit opinion," "Impact on statutory reporting timelines and the Annual Members' Meeting," and "Comparison with other organisations that appeared to have completed audits earlier and why DCHS was unable to complete their audit." Auditors said "DCHS was more exposed due to the relative materiality of LGPS balances compared to larger trusts." The committee also concluded "Ongoing delays are likely in respect of Local Government Pension Scheme (LGPS) issues." External auditor is Forvis Mazars; internal auditor is 360 Assurance. — pp.229–230, [Audit and Assurance Committee Annual Report April 2025 – March 2026, Appendix 1 to Item 99/26]
