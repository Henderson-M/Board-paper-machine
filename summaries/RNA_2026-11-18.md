## Top lines

[LEAD] Sandwell and West Birmingham's maternity and neonatal services remain under NHS England "intensive support" following the Baroness Amos investigation, and the board has now commissioned an externally led review of all 2025 stillbirths at the trust.
[Evidence] "risks relating to culture, workforce capacity and operational performance within maternity and neonatal services, particularly at Sandwell and West Birmingham NHS Trust following the Baroness Amos investigation. Assurance was provided that intensive support from the NHS England Maternity and Neonatal Improvement Support Team had commenced... the decision to commission an externally led review of all 2025 stillbirths involving families who had received care from the Trust, providing additional independence, transparency and assurance" — Item 26/58.1, Group Perinatal Report

[LEAD] Sandwell and West Birmingham's neonatal mortality rate "continued to demonstrate special-cause concern" even as its overall perinatal mortality and stillbirth rates improved.
[Evidence] "At Sandwell and West Birmingham NHS Trust, the overall perinatal mortality rate had improved and stillbirth rates had reduced; however, neonatal mortality remained elevated and continued to demonstrate special-cause concern" — Item 26/58.1, Group Perinatal Report

[LEAD] Sandwell and West Birmingham's year-to-date deficit is £1.18m worse than plan, with an underlying deficit now assessed at £64.6m and workforce 158 wte over plan.
[Evidence] "a year-to-date deficit of £7.83m against a planned deficit of £6.65m, representing an adverse variance of £1.18m... the latest assessment indicating an underlying deficit of £64.6m... workforce reductions where number[s] were 158wte over plan" — Item 26/57.0, Finance Report Month 4, Sandwell and West Birmingham NHS Trust

[WORTH WATCHING] Dudley Group, by contrast, is forecasting breakeven and running £0.135m better than plan — the two trusts' financial positions are diverging sharply within the same Group Board.
[Evidence] "the Trust reported a cumulative deficit of £0.921m at the end of July 2026, which was £0.135m better than plan... continued to forecast delivery of its planned breakeven position for 2026/27" — Item 26/57.0, Finance Report Month 4, The Dudley Group NHS Foundation Trust

[WORTH WATCHING] Dudley Group also reported an increase in neonatal mortality earlier in 2026, still under review via a thematic investigation, despite overall rates remaining below national averages.
[Evidence] "The Dudley Group NHS Foundation Trust continued to report stillbirth and neonatal mortality rates below national averages, although the increase in neonatal mortality earlier in 2026 remained under close review through a thematic investigation" — Item 26/58.1, Group Perinatal Report
