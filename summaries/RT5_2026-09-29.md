[LEAD] NHSE's "Deconstructing the Block" exercise concludes LPT is being "overpaid by c. 16%" against national prices on block contracts that make up 89% of its income, ahead of activity-based contracts for non-acute providers from April 2027.
[Evidence] "A national 'Deconstructing the Block' exercise has recently been completed. Providers and commissioners worked together to analyse block income contracts (in the case of LPT this covers 89% of income)... The national prices have been subject to a 'productivity deflator' which seeks to adjust for perceived deterioration in post Covid NHS productivity. LPT – in common with the vast majority of non-acute Trusts – would currently be being overpaid by c. 16% if comparing current block income with national prices used in this exercise." — p.6, [Paper O, Finance Report Month 5]. "NHSE stated aim is to introduce activity based contracts for non-acute provider contracts in some form from 1st April 2027. The process has already been implemented for acute Trusts in the current financial year, albeit the negative impact on contract values has initially been capped at 2.5% of contract values." — p.6. BAF11 lists as an assurance gap "Outputs of deconstructing the block exercise & uncertainty around impact on future revenue receipts" with actions "Submit completed exercise – CFOs – Sept 26" and "CFOs have met with ICB CFO to review high level outcomes, pre submission" — [Paper Q, Group BAF, BAF11]. [INFERENCE] 16% of 89% of income is a material exposure; the pack does not quantify it in £.
