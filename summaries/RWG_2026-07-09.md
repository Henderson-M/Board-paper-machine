# West Hertfordshire Teaching Hospitals NHS Trust (RWG) — Board in Public, 9 July 2026

Source: "July 2026 Board Papers" (single pack, 509 pp). Meeting held at Watford General Hospital.
Analyst note: a strong-performing acute trust (NOF segment 1; top-5 nationally on A&E four-hour; on-plan finances). The pack is largely good-news; the genuine reporting threads are the leadership arrangement (Acting CEO while the substantive CEO sits 7th in HSJ's Top 50), the Watford/Mount Vernon New Hospital Programme scheme, and patient-safety detail (three Never Events in 2025/26).

## 1. Top lines

**WORTH WATCHING** — Trust running under an Acting CEO (Paul Bannister) even as its substantive chief executive is named 7th in HSJ's Top 50 — pack does not explain the arrangement.
[Evidence] Chair "welcomed the Acting Chief Executive and the Acting Chief Information Officer to their respective roles" and "drew attention to the notable recognition of the Chief Executive in the Health Service Journal Annual Top 50 Chief Executives list, in which he was placed 7th" — p.3, [Minutes of 14 May 2026, item 1.4 Chair's Report]. Paul Bannister signs the CEO report as "Acting Chief Executive" — p.24, [Chief Executive's Report, item 2.1]. [INFERENCE] The substantive CEO is not named or explained in the public pack; reason for the acting arrangement is undisclosed.

**WORTH WATCHING** — Combined Watford + Mount Vernon New Hospital Programme scheme advancing to a decision-making business case; £12m of NHP enabling works this year.
[Evidence] "We are working very closely with the New Hospital Programme (NHP) on the delivery of £12 million of enabling works on the Watford site for this financial year." Mount Vernon relocation consultation "closed on 29 March and we will be working together to review findings and complete a decision-making business case." — p.23, [Chief Executive's Report, item 2.1]. "The Trust is working closely with UCLH to assess the potential implications of combining the Watford and Mount Vernon schemes, should the DMBC be approved." — p.360, [Redevelopment and Infrastructure Committee assurance report, item 5.3].

**WORTH WATCHING** — Three Never Events reported in 2025/26 (two retained foreign objects after surgery, one wrong-route medication); one investigation still open.
[Evidence] "During 2025/26 the Trust reported three Never Events, two relating to retained foreign objects following surgery and one relating to the administration of medication through an incorrect route… Two investigations were completed during the reporting period, while one remains ongoing." — [Quality Account 2025/26, Learning from Never Events]. The ICB ran "a system-wide themed review of Never Events across Hertfordshire and West Essex."

**WORTH WATCHING** — Board cut its financial-sustainability BAF risk score from 16 to 8 on the back of a "historic" year-end surplus, but carries a £38.9m (6.5%) efficiency target for 26/27.
[Evidence] "the Finance and Performance Committee was recommending that BAF Risk 4 (financial sustainability) be reduced from a score of 16 to 8 to reflect the significantly improved financial position" — p.13, [Minutes of 14 May 2026, item 5.6]. Chair "noted the Trust had recorded a small financial surplus at year end, which she described as a historic achievement" — p.3, [item 1.4]. "The annual plan includes a savings target of £38.9m" / "6.5% efficiency delivery… represents a significant challenge for all divisions" — pp.26–27, [Finance Report, item 2.2].

**WORTH WATCHING** — Month 2 position on plan (£5.7m YTD deficit) but held up by income timing gains that will unwind, while savings run £1.9m behind.
[Evidence] "the overall financial position is in line with plan, it is currently supported by income over performance… therefore expected to unwind over the remainder of the year. The income over performance is offsetting the under delivery of savings which are currently £1.9m behind plan." — p.26, [Finance Report, item 2.2]. Q1 carries a £6.6m deficit plan with surplus plans in later quarters. Breakeven is the annual objective.

**WORTH WATCHING** — ~£100m St Albans City Hospital redevelopment progressing; Surgical Centre due to open July 2026; endoscopy to expand from 10 to 40 weekly sessions by 2029.
[Evidence] "we will be spending nearly £100 million to increase elective and diagnostic capacity" — p.23, [item 2.1]. "The unit will expand from 10 to 40 weekly sessions by August 2029… Subject to Trust Board approval, the Committee endorsed entering into contract with the contractor selected through a robust procurement process." — p.360, [item 5.3].

**WORTH WATCHING** — Trust reports NOF segment 1 and sector-leading urgent-care performance (its own benchmarking).
[Evidence] "86.8%… seen, treated and discharged or admitted within four hours in May… highest performing trust in the East of England region and ranked 5th of 121 acute trusts… in the country." RTT 69.6%, "1st in the region and 32nd nationally." — p.20, [item 2.1]. Strategy update slide records "Achieved segment 1 in league tables" — p.127, [Strategy Update, item 2.5].

## 2. FOI leads

- **Acting CEO arrangement.** Request from West Hertfordshire Teaching Hospitals: correspondence and board/remuneration-committee records covering the appointment of Paul Bannister as Acting Chief Executive, the reason the substantive chief executive is not currently in post, expected duration, and any settlement or leave arrangement. Why it matters: a trust ranked segment 1 with its named CEO 7th in HSJ's Top 50 is being run by an acting chief with no public explanation — either a temporary secondment/leave worth confirming, or a departure the pack does not disclose.

- **Watford/Mount Vernon decision-making business case (DMBC).** Request from the trust (and/or UCLH): the DMBC, the Mount Vernon relocation consultation findings report, and the appraisal of "the potential implications of combining the Watford and Mount Vernon schemes." Why it matters: a live New Hospital Programme capital decision with cancer-services reconfiguration implications for the East of England.

- **P23 / endoscopy contract award.** Request from the trust: the contract value, term and contractor name for the St Albans endoscopy unit the board endorsed entering into, plus the P23 construction-partner contract for the wider ~£100m St Albans programme. Why it matters: NHS construction contract of material value awarded via a framework; value not disclosed in the public pack.

- **Never Event still under investigation.** Request from the trust: the completed investigation reports (StEIS/PSIRF) for the three 2025/26 Never Events, and the ICB's Hertfordshire and West Essex system-wide themed Never Event review. Why it matters: two retained-foreign-object events and a wrong-route medication event; one investigation open; systemic contributory factors identified.

## 4. Routine pack note

Not a routine pack, but close to it: this is a well-run, strongly-performing acute trust and most of the pack is positive assurance (staff survey above the national acute average on all nine People Promise themes; segment 1; on-plan finances). No single near-publishable LEAD. The reportable threads are the undisclosed Acting-CEO arrangement, the Watford/Mount Vernon NHP capital scheme, and the Never Events — all filed as WORTH WATCHING / FOI pending further reporting.
