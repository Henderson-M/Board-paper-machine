## FOI leads

- **External Board Governance / well-led review.** The May minutes confirm "the final copy of the External Board effectiveness review had been received"; the July pack publishes only a hexagonal "suggested areas of focus" summary (clarity of strategy, committee refocus, divisional governance, exec-led governance), not the underlying report. **Request from Mid Yorkshire Teaching NHS Trust:** the full external board governance/effectiveness review report, terms of reference, the name of the reviewing firm, and the resulting board action plan. Why it matters: external governance reviews at named trusts are reliably newsworthy (cf. Doncaster & Bassetlaw, HUTH).

- **Voluntary redundancy scheme scale and cost.** The CEO report gives application/WTE numbers but not the total cost, exit-package values, or a service-by-service breakdown. **Request:** total forecast one-off cost and full-year saving of VR Phases 1 and 2, number of WTE approved by staff group/band, and any NHSE correspondence approving the redundancies. (The finance section cites "£20.0m one-off cost" and "£14.2m FYE / £6.6m PYE" for VR2 and a "Union ceiling to be expanded 200+340 WTE" — worth standing up.)

- **Chief of Planning departure.** Emma Hall's exit on 30 June 2026 is recorded only as a driver for redistributing duties. **Request:** any settlement/COT3 agreement, confirmation whether the departure was voluntary, and whether the post will be replaced (responsibilities were split among Deputy CEO and CFO rather than backfilled).

- **Resident doctor industrial action cost.** The finance paper attributes £0.9m of the M2 overspend to "unmitigated resident doctor industrial action in April '26" and RSK-979 (score 20). **Request:** total forecast 2026/27 cost of industrial action to the Trust and the mitigation assumptions.
