## Top lines

[LEAD] Dorset HealthCare named one of six first-wave "Advanced Foundation Trusts" in England; trust eyeing AFT "capital freedoms" to fund neighbourhood health centres.
[Evidence] "On 29 May Dorset HealthCare was announced as one of six first wave Advanced Foundation Trusts in England." Plus: "It is possible that the capital freedoms offered by Advanced Foundation Trust status will support the development of some of these facilities" [neighbourhood health centres] — p.4 & p.12, [4.1 CEO Report June 2026]. The signed AFT management-representations letter to NHSE is appended at 4.2 (see FOI).

[LEAD] CQC well-led inspection of Dorset HealthCare booked for 23-25 June, with NHS England joining to assess "financial and resource governance"; unannounced visits already running.
[Evidence] "On 31 March 2026 we were notified that CQC will carry out a Well Led inspection of Dorset Healthcare on Tuesday 23 June to Thursday 25 June. It was noted that members of the NHS England (NHSE) team will join the inspection to assess financial and resource governance." — p.11, [4.1 CEO Report June 2026]. Minutes note CQC "publicly stated its intention to increase inspection activity" with "additional inspections expected ahead of the well-led review" — p.7, [02b BoDiC Minutes 08/04/2026].

[LEAD] DCH's 2026/27 "break even" plan only balances with £2.6m NHSE Deficit Support Funding; Month 1 already £1.3m in deficit, with industrial-action costs unfunded.
[Evidence] "Dorset County Hospital NHS Foundation Trust (DCHFT) submitted a break even plan to NHS England (NHSE) on 18th April 2026 for the financial year 2026/27, noting the inclusion of £2.6 million Deficit Support Funding to reach balance. Month one delivered a deficit of £1.3 million... The driver of this position is largely due to the cost of supporting Industrial Action in month, which at this stage remains unfunded per NHSE advice." — p.1-2, [8.3.4 DCH Finance Report M1].

[LEAD] DCH carrying £6.7m (29%) of its £23.3m efficiency target still unidentified and a further 20% high-risk at Month 1 — against an NHSE deadline of full identification by end of Q1.
[Evidence] "NHSE has requested that by end of quarter one, no unidentified scheme levels remain, and with no more than 20% of identified schemes classed as high risk. At month one, the Trust has 29% unidentified (£6.7m) and 20% of high risk schemes (£4.7m)." — p.2, [8.3.4 DCH Finance Report M1]. DHC separately reports "£9m unidentified CIP" — p.3, [8.1 FPC Assurance Report].

[WORTH WATCHING] New "score 20" DCH risk: failure to meet statutory and clinical timelines for ADHD medication titration and follow-ups in paediatrics.
[Evidence] Risk 2309 "NEW", added 18/05/2026: "Failure to meet statutory and clinical timelines for ADHD medication titration and follow ups", current score 20, "More work needed on mitigations" — p.2 of risk appendix, [6.4 DCH Risk Report May 2026].

[WORTH WATCHING] DCH community paediatrics waiting-list safety risk (score 20), with 1,852 children waiting over 52 weeks; risk open since 2018.
[Evidence] Risk 472 "Patient Safety Concerns and Increased Risk of Adverse Outcomes Due to Prolonged Wait Times in Community Paediatrics" (opened 10/09/2018), current score 20: "Reduction in OWL - 2659 / 1852 waiting over 52 weeks" — p.3 of risk appendix, [6.4 DCH Risk Report May 2026].

[WORTH WATCHING] DCH estates compliance at 71% (vs DHC 95%), driven by fire-safety and emergency-lighting failings; estates risk (SR5) scored at maximum 20/20 unmitigated.
[Evidence] "DHC compliance is reported at 95% and DCH compliance at 71% which reflects challenges primarily within fire safety and emergency lighting, driven by estate complexity and ongoing asset validation." — p.5, [8.1 FPC Assurance Report]. DCH estates BAF risk SR5 shown at "Mitigated 4 x 5 = 20" — [5.3 DCH BAF Q4].

[WORTH WATCHING] Dorset ICB cluster signals a sharp shift of resource out of hospitals ahead of full ICB merger in April 2027; CEO quotes the ambition to "end hospital as the default setting for care".
[Evidence] Cluster strategic-ambitions document, per CEO, will "End hospital as the default setting for care, using acute settings only where they add greatest value" and "Rebalance resources at scale, reducing the acute share of spend"; aims include "full ICB merger in April 2027" and ">half of all contracts" on outcomes models by 2028/29 — p.6, [4.1 CEO Report June 2026].

[WORTH WATCHING] DHC mental-health beds running hot: adult acute/PICU average length of stay 92 days against a 73-day target; 43% of adults stayed over 60 days; adult acute occupancy 98.6%.
[Evidence] "Adult Acute & PICU Average LOS (3-mth rolling period) 92 days (target 73 days)... 43% of Adults had a LOS >60 days in April"; "Adult Acute Occ. Rates remain consistently high at 98.6%" — p.37, [8.2.1 DHC ICD Apr 26].

## FOI leads

[FOI] AFT application representations letter — request the full NHSE assessment correspondence and any peer-review reports.
The pack appends the trust's 20 April 2026 "letter of representation" for its Advanced Foundation Trust application, signed by chair David Clayton-Smith, confirming the board "disclosed... all material reports and peer review information... covering governance arrangements or the quality of services... within the last two years" — p.1-3, [4.2 Letter of Representation - DHC AFT application]. Request from NHS England (and DHC): the AFT assessment report/decision letter, the named peer-review and governance reports referenced, and the assessment criteria scoring for the six first-wave AFTs. Why it matters: AFT status carries new capital and operational freedoms; the evidence base for awarding it is not public.

[FOI] CQC well-led + NHSE financial-governance inspection (23-25 June) — request the report and NHSE's resource-governance findings once published.
Request from CQC/NHSE: the well-led inspection report for Dorset HealthCare and the NHSE financial-and-resource-governance assessment that ran alongside it. The pairing of a CQC well-led review with an NHSE finance-governance assessment, days after AFT award, is itself the signal — p.11, [4.1 CEO Report].

[FOI] Medium-term plan acceptance letters "accepted with conditions" — request both NHSE letters.
"On 22 April both DCH and DHC received their acceptance letters from NHSE... Both were accepted with conditions" including reducing savings-plan risk (both), Talking Therapies trajectory challenge (DHC) and productivity/workforce deliverability (DCH) — p.7-8, [4.1 CEO Report]. Request from NHSE: the two MTP/IDP acceptance letters and the stated conditions in full.

[FOI] DCH Regulation 28 (Prevention of Future Deaths) report and DHC's Q4 Reg 28 — request the coroner's reports and trust responses.
DHC "received one Regulation 28 report" in Q4; DCH's LfD pack references engagement with the coroner — p.6, [7.2.1 Learning from Deaths Q4] / [7.2.3 DCH Q4 LfD]. Request from HM Coroner / the trusts: the Reg 28 report(s) and the trusts' formal responses.
