## Top lines

[LEAD] MFT is £19.5m adverse to plan two months into 2026/27, with cash down to 0.9 days of operating cash (£8.0m) after a planned £36m of revenue support was not drawn down.
[Evidence] "In month 2, the Trust delivered a £10.6m deficit against a £3.1m surplus plan – a £7.5m adverse position. The year to date position is £19.5m adverse variance to the plan." — p.352, [CFO's report – M2]; "The Trust has 0.9 days of revenue operating cash days at the end of May... equates to £8.0m... Planned £36m revenue support cash was not drawn down." — p.356, [CFO's report – M2]. (Note: p.355 slide and p.348 Finance Committee report both state the plan was a £3.1m *deficit*, not a surplus — the p.352 exec-summary "surplus" wording conflicts with the trust's own tables.)

[LEAD] The trust's £175m Value for Patients savings programme depends on cutting a minimum of 1,188 whole-time-equivalent posts, but schemes worth only c.£110m have so far been identified.
[Evidence] "If the Trust is unable to deliver the planned workforce reduction of 1,188 Whole Time Equivalents (WTEs) linked to the Value for Patients (VfP) programme, workforce costs will remain above plan..." — p.81, [Board Assurance Framework]; "MFT will need to deliver finance balance in 26/27, including delivery of a £175m VFP. This includes a minimum reduction of 1,188 WTE headcount reduction compared to the exit run rate... Schemes to the value of c£110m have been identified to date" — p.82, [BAF].

[LEAD] An NHS England-commissioned independent patient safety investigation (Niche Consulting), ordered by the health and social care secretary, found earlier provider reviews into a former RMCH spinal surgeon ("Consultant Surgeon A") gave only "partial and uneven" assurance; the trust says its RMCH spinal service is now "currently fragile."
[Evidence] "In March 2025, following sustained concerns... the Secretary of State for Health requested that NHS England commission an independent review... NHS England subsequently commissioned Niche Consulting... earlier provider led investigations were inconsistent in methodology, use of clinical records, definitions of harm and approaches to patient engagement. As a result, assurance was partial and uneven" — p.195, [Spinal Surgery Independent Patient Safety Investigation Item 11.2]; "the RMCH spinal service is currently fragile, with significant capacity constraints and requiring transformation" — p.196. Report published on NHSE website 28 May 2026 (p.203); face-to-face reviews offered to any of Surgeon A's patients for a minimum of three years.

[LEAD] MFT's jump to NHS Oversight Framework segment 1 rests on a breakeven plan the trust is already £19.5m behind, and the finance committee warns a missed Q1 could cost it both the segment rating and its Advanced Foundation Trust ambitions.
[Evidence] "For quarter 4 MFT improved to segment 1 from segment 3... ranking of 19/134" — p.112, [IPR]; "NOF score recently uplifted to 1 from 3 based on submission of a compliant breakeven plan and a breakeven forecast outturn." — p.113, [IPR]; "discussion on the potential risks to and impact on MFT's NHS Oversight Framework segmentation and Advanced Foundation Trust ambitions, in the event that the Q1 position was not achieved" — p.348, [Finance Board Committee escalation].

[LEAD] Greater Manchester Fire and Rescue Service has issued a Fire Enforcement Notice covering the Oxford Road Campus PFI estate, and a contractor has "ceased all estate variations with minimal notice."
[Evidence] "a contractor has ceased all estate variations with minimal notice, requiring intensified oversight. The Fire Enforcement Notice issued by Greater Manchester Fire and Rescue Service remains a key area of focus" — p.364, [Digital and Estates Board Committee escalation].

[WORTH WATCHING] The trust's 2025/26 annual report and accounts were signed off with a clean (unqualified) opinion from Grant Thornton — but the accounts themselves are not in this public pack; they were approved under delegated authority by the audit committee on 23 June.
[Evidence] "The Committee approved, under delegated authority from the Board of Directors, the Trust's 2025/26 Annual Report, 2025/26 Annual Accounts... noted the proposed unqualified (clean) audit opinion." — p.360, [Audit and Risk Committee escalation]; "Grant Thornton confirmed... an unqualified (clean) audit opinion would be issued and that no significant weaknesses had been identified... commentary regarding financial sustainability, including the Trust's low cash position" — p.361. [Evidence] "potential productivity opportunities of up to £290m over a three-year period" — p.348; "the Capital Resource risk score was increased from 12 to 20 as whilst the Trust's capital allocation has increased... it remains insufficient to meet the scale of MFT's capital requirements" — p.348, [Finance Board Committee escalation]. 2026/27 capital plan £147.3m (p.357).
