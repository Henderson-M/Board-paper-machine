## Top lines

[LEAD] NHS England served Leeds Teaching Hospitals with formal enforcement undertakings under section 106 of the Health and Social Care Act 2012 in October 2025 — disclosed only in the buried corporate risk register, and seemingly at odds with the trust's own CQC annual assurance paper claiming "no enforcement action".
[Evidence] "Letter from NHS England (7 October 2025) setting out Enforcement Undertakings under section 106 of the Health and Social Care Act 2012. Leeds Teaching Hospitals NHS Trust Improvement Plan. Weekly Improvement Steering Group chaired by Chief Executive." — p.96, [Corporate Risk Register May 2026, risk CRRE2 (CQC Registration breaches – Well-led)]
[Evidence] Contrast: "The Care Quality Commission has not taken enforcement action against Leeds Teaching Hospitals NHS Trust during 2025/26." — p.2, [9.3 CQC Registration Annual Assurance 2025/26]

[LEAD] The trust is in NHSE's Integrated Quality Improvement regime and operating OUTSIDE its own board-set risk appetite on Workforce, Regulatory and Clinical (patient experience) risks while it works through CQC regulatory breaches.
[Evidence] "Whilst the Trust remains in the NHSE Integrated Quality Improvement process and taking actions to address the CQC regulatory breaches, the Trust is moving away from the risk appetite set by the Board for Workforce risk (Workforce Retention risk), External risk (Regulatory risk) and Clinical Risk (Patient Safety and Outcomes and Patient Experience risk)." — p.5–6, [8.3 Well-led Improvement Plan Update]; risk appetite table shows all three "Operating outside" — p.1

[LEAD] CQC issued a Section 29A Warning Notice over maternity staffing and downgraded maternity at both sites to "inadequate", neonatal to "requires improvement", and rated the trust-wide Well-led domain "requires improvement" with breaches of Regulations 16, 17 and 18; the board itself was found "not working as cohesively as it should".
[Evidence] "The maternity services… were inspected by the Care Quality Commission (CQC) with a report received in June 2025 which downgraded the services on both sites to inadequate. The neonatal services were inspected in January 2025… both sides of the city downgraded to Requires Improvement." — p.2, [8.3(ii) Perinatal Improvement Plan Report]
[Evidence] "the CQC rated leadership at Leeds Teaching Hospitals NHS Trust as requires improvement… breaches of: Regulation 16(2), complaints response times; Regulation 17(2)(a), good governance; Regulation 18(2)(a), staffing and training compliance… the Board was not working as cohesively as it should… leadership culture was inconsistent across services and at Board level." — p.3, [9.3 CQC Registration Annual Assurance]
[Evidence] Significant gaps in control listed: "Section 29A Warning Notice (midwifery staffing)" plus breaches of Regs 12, 15, 17, 18 — p.4, [8.2(ii) BAF April 2026]

[LEAD] Donna Ockenden will chair an independent review into maternity and neonatal services at the trust, expected to cover a 15-year period (1 January 2011 – 31 December 2025), including stillbirths, neonatal deaths, hypoxic injuries and maternal deaths.
[Evidence] "the Government announced that Donna Ockenden will chair the independent review into maternity and neonatal services at the Trust. The Terms of Reference… are yet to be agreed; however, we expect the review to involve case reviews of stillbirths, neonatal deaths and serious incidents, hypoxic injuries and maternal deaths over a 15-year period, from 1 January 2011 to 31 December 2025." — p.2, [7 Chief Executive's Report]

[LEAD] The trust counted 600 patients receiving over-45-minute corridor care in its EDs in just the first 10 days of May 2026 (471 at St James's alone), and confirms it received an "NHSE letter of action" on corridor care; full elimination is not targeted until 2029.
[Evidence] "The number of patients experiencing corridor care for over 45 minutes in LTHT EDs for May 1-10th 2026… Grand Total 600 [LGI 129, SJH 471]." — p.5, [9.5 Corridor Care]
[Evidence] "There has been a recent NHSE letter of action regarding corridor care. An Executive-owned action plan detailing the improvements required is detailed in this paper." — p.4; "improvement programme to eradicate corridor care in LTHT by 2029" — p.3
[Evidence] "The number of patients in LTHT with no criteria to reside remains persistently high and has increased over the previous two quarters." — p.4

[LEAD] The trust opened 2026/27 in the red: a Month 1 (April) year-to-date deficit of £6.6m, £3.5m adverse to the NHSE plan, against a breakeven plan that depends on a £95m waste reduction programme — a slip the finance team itself says would equate to an Oversight Framework score of 4.
[Evidence] "In April, the Trust reported a year-to-date deficit of £6.6m which is £3.5m adverse to the NHSE plan. The biggest drivers of the month 1 deficit are costs associated with the resident doctor's industrial action in April and pay expenditure being higher than planned… Under the NHS oversight Framework the latest published segmentation and league table gives the Trust a combined finance score of 3, based on Q3 financial performance. The M1 YTD adverse variance to plan of £3.5m (1.92% of turnover) would result in a NOF score of 4." — p.25, [12.1 IQPR May, I&E Position 2026/27]
[Evidence] "The financial plan submitted for 2026/27 is a breakeven position and includes a waste reduction programme (WRP) of £95m." — p.25; pay expenditure £111.6m, "£6.9m adverse to the NHSE plan" — p.25

[LEAD] The trust missed its year-end RTT recovery target, ending 2025/26 at 65.9% against a 70% trajectory, and remains in Tier 2 escalation on the 62-day cancer standard (64.9% in January vs an 85% target).
[Evidence] "it was confirmed that the target had not been achieved with the year-end position reported at 65.9% against a recovery target of 70%." — p.2, [14.1 F&P Committee Chairs Summary]
[Evidence] "62-day performance was reported at 64.9% for January against a target of 85%, and it was recognised the Trust remained in Tier 2 escalation for this standard." — p.3, [14.1 F&P Committee Chairs Summary]

## FOI leads

[FOI] Request the NHS England letter of 7 October 2025 setting out the section 106 enforcement undertakings, plus the trust's resulting NHSE Improvement Plan.
[Why] The pack confirms a formal NHSE enforcement action exists but does not quote or publish it; it is the strongest single document in the pack. Request from LTHT (and/or NHSE North East & Yorkshire). Ask also why the CQC annual assurance paper states no enforcement action was taken in 2025/26 despite this.

[FOI] Request the CQC's June 2025 Well-led inspection report and the maternity/neonatal inspection reports (published 20 June 2025), and the Section 29A Warning Notice on midwifery staffing.
[Why] The pack summarises ratings and regulation breaches but does not reproduce the notices/reports. Request from LTHT or CQC.

[FOI] Request the NHSE "letter of action" on corridor care referenced in paper 9.5, and the trust's executive-owned corridor care action plan.
[Why] Letter referenced but not quoted; specific to LTHT. Request from LTHT/NHSE.

[FOI] Request the NHS Alliance external Well-led review terms of reference, contract value and report; and the MSSP diagnostic report (100+ recommendations).
[Why] Named external firm engaged; review under way but findings/scope not disclosed. Request from LTHT.

[FOI] Request the medium-term plan "conditions" attached by NHSE and the private board paper L(iii).
[Why] "Compliant with conditions" is stated but the conditions and the Q1 negotiating parameters are held in the private session.
