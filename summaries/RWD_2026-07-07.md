Analysis for HSJ (correspondent: Annabelle). This is a single ~52MB combined agenda bundle for the **Lincolnshire Community and Hospitals Group (LCHG)** joint public board — United Lincolnshire Teaching Hospitals NHS Trust (ULTH, ODS RWD) and Lincolnshire Community Health Services NHS Trust (LCHS) meeting as a group. New Group Chair Rebecca Brown chairing (Elaine Baylis stood down); Group CEO Prof Karen Dunderdale. Papers read in full: Group CEO report (Item 6), Finance Briefing M2 (Item 8.3), Risk Reporting to the Board + appendices (Item 12.1), Group BAF (Item 12.2), Maternity/CNST report (Item 8.2/8.1), Integrated Performance Report dashboard narrative (Item 11), previous minutes (5 May 2026). ## 1. Top lines

**LEAD — LCHS downgraded a segment in the NOF: dropped from segment 2 to segment 3 in the Q4 2025/26 ratings published in June 2026; ULTH remains in segment 4 (up 4 places to 104th of 134).**
"The NHS National Oversight Framework (NOF) Quarter 4 ratings were published in June 2026... For this quarter United Lincolnshire Teaching Hospitals NHS Trust (ULTH) remains in segment 4 and improves its place by 4 points (104th place out of 134 peer Trusts). Lincolnshire Community Health Services NHS Trust (LCHS) has been placed in segment 3 from segmentation 2 last quarter (41st place out of 61 peer Trusts)." — CEO report p.6, [Item 6]. [Context: the Q3 position (same pack) had LCHS in segment 2 at 24th/61, so this is a deterioration for the community trust.]

**LEAD — ULTH placed a breast-cancer risk at the maximum "very high" score of 20 after the ICB served a breach-of-contract notice on its breast services.**
"4855 – Delayed diagnosis of breast cancer risk presented at RRC&C in April confirmed increase in score to 5x4:20 Very High Risk (from 4x4:16 High Risk) due to ICB breach of contract notice issued for Breast Services." — Risk report p.5, [Item 12.1]. Underlying risk: "As a result of insufficient staffing levels and limited clinic capacity within the Breast Service, Pathology and Oncology, there is a risk that patients will not be reviewed in a timely manner following referral, diagnosis of breast cancer will be delayed... which would result in increased patient harm." An "ICB risk summit" was held in April 2026 with fortnightly follow-up meetings. — Appendix A risk 4855, [Item 12.1]; minutes 424/26, [5 May 2026 minutes].

**LEAD — ULTH at material risk of failing the entire CNST Year 8 maternity incentive scheme — with "no opportunity for recovery" — over training-compliance shortfalls, a "significant organisational and financial risk".**
"The most significant risk to compliance is in achieving ≥90% multidisciplinary training compliance across all staff groups... there is a material risk of returning a position of non-compliance at the Trust-selected checkpoint for training compliance at the end of July. Failure to achieve the required standards at this point will result in non-compliance for the entirety of CNST Year 8, with no opportunity for recovery within the reporting period. This represents a significant organisational and financial risk." — Maternity/CNST MIS Year 8 report exec summary, [Item 8.1/8.2]. The report also records "four MNSI [Maternity and Newborn Safety Investigations]" within the Year 8 reporting period. — Neonatal Oversight upward report, p. ~1982.

**LEAD — Group is £8.1m in deficit at month 2 (in line with plan) and must "improve our run rate" to hit breakeven, carrying "a significant CIP requirement" and requesting board approval to overcommit the capital plan by up to £5m.**
"At Month 2 the year-to-date position for the Group is an £8.1m deficit, which is in line with plan... The Group are required to improve our run rate during the remainder of the year to return to a breakeven position." "The financial position remains challenging, with a significant CIP requirement across the Group." Board asked to approve "the overcommitment on the capital plan of up to £5m". CIP delivered £12.1m at M2 (£0.2m below plan); cash £54.1m, £14.2m below plan. — Finance Briefing M2 exec summary and p.5–6, [Item 8.3]. [Note: exec-summary table states £8.0m deficit; narrative states £8.1m.]

**LEAD — 16 "very high" risks (score 20-25) on the group register, 14 of them at ULTH; a new 5x5:25 risk logged over delay to the improvement programme, and multiple services flagged as fragile or unviable.**
"There are 16 (2%) risks considered to be very high (20-25)... LCHS: 1... ULTH: 14" and "69 (9%) risks considered to be high (15-20)"; 757 risks total. New very-high risk "5776 – Risk of delay in Improvement Programme delivery impacting quality, flow and financial performance... confirmed score 5x5:25 Very High risk." Named service-viability very-high risks include "5450 - Risk of Gastro service not being viable due to current fragility of consultant workforce", "5761 – Risk to IBD service" (mortality risk, worsened as "the lead ACP for the service is leaving"), and pharmacy staffing risks where the "pharmacy establishment... is 84 WTE below national benchmarks". — Risk report p.4–6 and Appendix A, [Item 12.1].

- **ICB breach-of-contract notice on breast services.** The pack confirms the ICB "issued" a "breach of contract notice... for Breast Services" that pushed the delayed-diagnosis risk to 20, but does not publish it. - **Cath lab patient-harm risk.** Request the Lincoln cath-lab business continuity plan, the business case for a third lab, and any recorded harm/incidents or deaths on the TAVI waiting list referenced in risk 5272. (Ref: Item 12.1, Appendix A risk 5272.)
- **PPM external audit.** The external audit of the Planned Preventive Maintenance programme "identified the need for a significant resource-supported action plan." Request the audit report and the resulting action plan / cost. (Ref: minutes ~440/26; Item 12.2.)

## 5. Pack assessment

**Not routine — several trust-level leads, mostly buried in the risk register and committee narratives rather than headlined.** The strongest, most reportable items: an ICB breach-of-contract notice served on ULTH's breast-cancer services (pushing a patient-harm risk to the maximum score, with a risk summit and recovery plan running) — a document exclusive + regulatory-action shape; the NOF Q4 segment downgrade for LCHS (2→3); and the maternity CNST Year 8 "no recovery" non-compliance warning with financial consequences. Finance is bread-and-butter but solid (£8.1m M2 deficit, breakeven pressure, significant CIP, £5m capital overcommitment). The risk register carries a cluster of patient-safety/service-viability very-high risks (cath labs death risk, IBD/gastro fragility, pharmacy 84 WTE below benchmark) and a new 5x5:25 improvement-programme risk. Leadership angle is limited (new Group Chair already in post). The breast-services breach notice and CNST Year 8 exposure are the highest-value follow-ups.
