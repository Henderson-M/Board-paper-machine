**Pack note.** One shared pack for the Coventry and Warwickshire ICB (QWU) and Herefordshire and Worcestershire ICB (QGH) boards "meeting in common" (cluster CW-HW); this summary is duplicated under both ODS codes. Page references are to the printed page number inside each paper. The finance appendices and the IQPR slide appendix carry no page numbers, so those are cited by section or slide title.
