## Top lines

[LEAD] Specialist inpatient services currently run by the independent charity St Andrew's Healthcare in Northampton — including medium and low secure care, the national D/deaf service and acquired brain injury services — are transferring to NHFT from mid-September 2026 under a deal brokered with NHS England.
[Evidence] "NHFT, St Andrew's Healthcare, and NHSE have reached an agreement for a number of specialist inpatient services currently provided by St Andrew's Healthcare in Northampton to transfer to the care of NHFT from mid-September 2026, subject to final contractual and financial arrangements. The agreement includes medium and low secure care, the national D/deaf service and acquired brain injury services… Patients will remain on the Northampton site, with services expected to be delivered from William Wake House and Fitzroy House in the longer term." — p.1, [E – Chief Executive Report]

[LEAD] NHFT has been confirmed in the first wave of NHS England's Advanced Foundation Trust (AFT) programme, and has separately been designated eligible to develop an Integrated Health Organisation (IHO) contract for Northamptonshire from April 2027 — initial focus on children/young people and mental health, learning disability and autism populations.
[Evidence] "Following the invitation for NHFT to enter the first wave AFT application process… our submission has been successful… it has been agreed by NHSE that NHFT has been designated as eligible to take forward an Integrated Health Organisation (IHO) contract for Northamptonshire… The next phase will focus on developing business cases and a roadmap towards initial IHO contractual arrangements from April 2027." — p.1–2, [E – Chief Executive Report]; AFT status also confirmed at FT/26/47, [A – Minutes 28 May 2026]

[LEAD] Required anti-ligature works and other environmental changes on the trust's acute mental health wards are being delayed by the trust's PFI company, with the board told there is no effective way to force the issue — the Group CEO is having to escalate it personally.
[Evidence] "The Estates Team reported an Alert as progress in delivering the required anti-ligature works and other major changes to the environment for the acute mental health wards continues to be delayed due to a lack of timely engagement from the PFI Company. Of particular concern is the position presented that there is no clear mechanism for escalation and no effective route to challenge or expedite the PFI response. This concern is the same as for the call bell issue and is being escalated by the Group Chief Executive." — p.4 (Health and Safety Committee section), [M – AAA Quality and Safety Committee]. A call-assist bell installation at the Welland Centre (a national requirement since 2022) also remains outstanding for the same PFI-engagement reason — p.1, [M].

[LEAD] A cluster of unexpected child and young-person deaths — a number of them suspected suicides — has emerged in Northamptonshire over the past six months, prompting multi-agency Public Health meetings and a deep dive into cases; the county Director of Public Health has also warned about open-water drowning risks.
[Evidence] "It is noted that there has been a marked increase in the number of unexpected child deaths in Northamptonshire over the past six months. A number of these deaths in young people are suspected suicides. Public Health has been chairing multi-agency meetings on a regular basis in response to the 'cluster' and has completed a deep dive into those cases and is looking at children who may be at risk of suicide." — p.13 (Appendix 2, Learning from Deaths Q1 2026/27), [N – Patient Safety and Learning Assurance Report]. [INFERENCE] Seven child deaths were reported in Q1; the report states none were attributable to NHFT care (p.18, [N]) — so this is a system/commissioning story, not an NHFT-care story.

[WORTH WATCHING] NHFT has taken over children's community healthcare across west Northamptonshire (child development centre, community nursing, community paediatrics, physiotherapy) from 1 July 2026 after the ICB decided on a single countywide provider — consolidating children's services under one provider.
[Evidence] "From 1 July 2026, several children's community healthcare services – the Child Development Centre, children's community nursing (west), community paediatrics (west) and children's physiotherapy (west) – transferred to NHFT… NHS Northamptonshire Integrated Care Board determined that a single countywide provider would improve consistency, reduce variation…" — p.1, [F – Managing Director's Report]

[WORTH WATCHING] NHS England has paused approvals of the trust's material capital bids ("MOU approvals"), and inadequate capital funding is the trust's most severe strategic risk (BAF10, red).
[Evidence] "NHSE late approval of material capital bids – NHSE advised there is a pause on MOU approvals… Escalation as required to NHSE until approval received." — p.14 (BAF10, red-rated I5/L4), [J – Group Board Assurance Framework]

[WORTH WATCHING] The trust is carrying a £24.4m (6%) in-year savings ("Value Programme") target and had not fully identified the schemes by NHS England's 28 May deadline; the finance risk narrative warns the spend run rate "is not reducing fast enough to deliver plan" with "reducing cash balances."
[Evidence] "The Value Programme has a 6% target (£24.423m); £1.814m was delivered year to date as at month 2 and plans to deliver the remainder… require further work to be undertaken to bring the schemes into delivery… The delivery of the value program continues to remain the biggest financial risk" — p.3–4, [I – AAA Performance Committee]; "CIP plan not fully identified… Not fully identified by deadline, revised internal target of 30 June" and "Spend run rate is not reducing fast enough to deliver plan / Reducing cash balances" — p.15 (BAF11), [J]. (Reported M2 position: £1.87m deficit against a £2.41m planned deficit — i.e. ahead of plan, p.2 [I].)

[WORTH WATCHING] Staff are being TUPE-transferred from Nottinghamshire Healthcare to Northamptonshire Healthcare to create a single East Midlands mental-health collaborative "hub" hosted by NHFT, with a new director to be appointed.
[Evidence] "We will TUPE Hub staff from Nottinghamshire Healthcare to Northamptonshire Healthcare to create a single East Midlands Hub" (June 2026); "We will appoint to a new Director to lead the single hub" (Sept 2026). — p.13 (East Midlands Alliance Plan 2026/27, appended), [E – Chief Executive Report]

[WORTH WATCHING] Verbal abuse of staff rose 34% year-on-year across the trust; a named police operation (Op Cavell) is running against assaults and racially aggravated offences on the trust's mental health wards.
[Evidence] "There were 407 incidents of verbal abuse reported last year. There have been 546 incidents of verbal abuse this year. This is an increase of 34.2%." (violence and aggression fell 8.5%) — Annual Safety Report, [M – AAA Quality and Safety Committee, Appendix 2]. Op Cavell (St Marys/Berrywood wards) has produced three court convictions and further ongoing investigations — same appendix.
