# Torbay and South Devon NHS Foundation Trust board — 3 September 2026

- **Pack:** 1 combined information pack, 328 pages (with a 169-page core papers section)
- **Analysed:** 2026-09-01

## Top lines

[LEAD] Torbay has dropped into segment 4 of the NHS oversight framework and is now ranked 102nd of 134 providers — down from 83rd a quarter earlier.
[Evidence] "Under the NHS Oversight Framework the Trust is currently in **Segment 4 for Q4 2025/26; with an average score of 2.60 and ranked 102 out of 134 providers** across the domain scores covering access to services, effectiveness and experience of care, patient safety, people and workforce, and finance and productivity. This is a **worsening position from the Q3 2025/26 position where the Trust was in Segment 3 with an average score of 2.49 and a ranking of 83.**" — [Board Assurance Framework / oversight report]
[Evidence] "As the Trust is reporting a financial deficit or in receipt of deficit support our organisation's segment can be no [better than 3 or 4]" — [same]
[Evidence] "Unfortunately the Trust had moved from National Oversight Framework level 3 to 4 and JT was invited to provide some background on this issue" — [Board minutes]
[Evidence] Forward risk flagged to the board: "**Epic implementation, reporting and National Oversight Framework Segmentation and League Table publication.** The Quarter 1 2026 NHS Oversight Framework (NOF) segmentation data is expected to be published online" — [Chief Executive's report]

[LEAD] The trust's "breakeven" plan is built on £57.8m of deficit support funding — strip it out and the month 4 position is a £27.5m deficit.
[Evidence] "The Trust set a breakeven plan for 2026-27 including **£57.8m Deficit Support (DSF)** and a CIP target of £54.4m. Trust's financial position at the end of July shows a month end position of £8.2m deficit, as planned. **The position excluding DSF is £27.5[m]**" — [Month 4 Finance Report, Executive Summary]

[LEAD] Forecast risk on the £54.4m savings programme runs from £11.6m in the base case to £27.2m on the downside.
[Evidence] "The Trust is forecasting delivery of the planned outturn but recognises **forecast risks to delivery of between £11.6m in the base case and up to £27.2m in the downside risk assessment** (with £7.2m risk to the ULP in the base case and £13.9m in the downsid[e])" — [Month 4 Finance Report]
[Evidence] "CIP plans' maturity continues (**now £49.2m against £54.4m target**), although risk of full delivery remains high with **CIP recurrent run rate behind plan** currently; overall base case outturn risk sits at £11.6m (also includes IY ASC and CHC overspends, plus provision for lost income)" — [Finance Committee assurance report]
[Evidence] Recurrent delivery is the weak point: "**Percentage forecast recurrent efficiency delivery against plan: Trustwide 65%**" against 92% for in-year delivery — [Finance scorecard]
[Evidence] BAF gap in control: "**CIP delivery risk — significant proportion of schemes unidentified or early stage.** Accelerate CIP identification; transformation-led efficiency programmes through Finance Delivery Group" — [Board Assurance Framework]

[LEAD] The finance plan builds in £10.2m of PDC deficit cash support, and the trust may need more from month 9.
[Evidence] "The **2026/27 finance plan includes £10.2m revenue PDC deficit cash support drawn in Q1-2 and repaid in Q4** however the Trust will aim to manage liquidity without drawing on this support, minimising PDC dividend expense." — [Month 4 Finance Report, Cash]
[Evidence] "The Trust is not forecasting a need for cash support however **risks have been identified that if not mitigated, may require PDC revenue support in M09-M12.**" — [Finance Committee report]
[Evidence] "The Trust is reporting a cash balance of £12.8m as at 31st July 2026, £7.7m lower than planned, primarily due to a delay in receiving **£6.2m regular contractual receipts from Torbay Council**" — [Month 4 Finance Report]
[Evidence] The finance committee's overall assurance rating on the financial position was set at "MODE[RATE]" — [Finance Committee report]

[LEAD] The trust's own winter modelling shows a shortfall of 170 beds at peak pressure.
[Evidence] "modelling shows that, without material change in demand, length of stay, discharge and alternatives to admission, **the acute site could face a forecast bed deficit of 170 during peak pressure.** The preferred response is pathway redesign, greater use of alternatives to admission, reduced No Criteria to Reside and maximised out-of-h[ours capacity]" — [Winter Plan]

[WORTH WATCHING] The board delegated approval of its mortuary assurance statement to a committee to hit NHS England's national deadline.
[Evidence] "In response to the **NHS England letter dated 26 June 2026 concerning post-death care and mortuary oversight**, the Board delegated authority to the Quality and People Committee to consider and approve the Trust's Board Assurance Statement, enabling submission by the national deadline of 31 July 2026. At its meeting on 28 July 2026, the Committee reviewed the supporting gap analy[sis]" — [Mortuary Reports item]
[Evidence] "Trusts are asked to cascade this letter to relevant teams, including estates, safeguarding, pathology, bereavement, mortuary, corporate services, **and your Freedom to Speak Up Guardians**." — [NHSE letter reproduced in the pack]
[Evidence] The trust reports a recovery on its own mortuary standard: "been addressed, we have been **reassessed and we have returned to a 5-star rating**" — [Mortuary report]

[WORTH WATCHING] Internal control over risk management is rated "Limited" in the trust's own assurance summary.
[Evidence] Summary of key controls: "C2 Risk management framework incl. strategy, policy, BAF and corporate risk register — ARC oversight; internal audit of risk management; Risk Group reports — **Limited**" (against "Significant" for board governance, regulatory compliance monitoring and Freedom to Speak Up) — [Board Assurance Framework, Summary of Key Controls]

[WORTH WATCHING] An independent review of the One Devon People Digital Programme is due to report this month.
[Evidence] "An **independent review of the One Devon People Digital Programme is underway and is expected to report in September.** In parallel, work continues on the delivery of 19 system enhancements to improve EVA's functionality and user experience" — [Digital report]
[Evidence] Risk rated "Critical": "Delay to programme rollout and inability to realise planned benefits. 1. **Independent review of the original People Digital Business Case.**" — [same]

[WORTH WATCHING] Histology delays are driving urology breaches and the trust has bought in outsourcing until the end of October.
[Evidence] "**Histology delays had impacted urology performance** and while additional consultant workforce has been appointed, **outsourcing has been agreed to support recovery until end of October 26.**" — [Integrated Performance and Quality Report]
[Evidence] "Diagnostic performance (DM01) remained a challenge throughout FY25/26, and this has continued into FY26/27." — [same]

[WORTH WATCHING] NHS England's own safer staffing review of the trust gave significant assurance and confirmed compliance with all 12 national standards.
[Evidence] "Significant assurance is supported by the positive external review, which **confirms compliance with all 12 national standards** and effective safer staffing governance and controls." — [Tab 5.2.4, NHSE End of Year Safer Staffing Report 2025/26]
[Evidence] "The Trust Executive Group is asked to note the NHS England findings, take significant assurance from the external review and endorse completion of the identified actions" — [same]

## FOI leads

[FOI] **The £27.2m downside risk assessment.** *Request from Torbay and South Devon:* the full base-case and downside forecast risk analysis behind the £11.6m–£27.2m range, scheme-level CIP delivery data with RAG ratings, and the correspondence with NHS Devon ICB on accelerating CIP delivery and narrowing the forecast range.

[FOI] **The deficit support funding conditions.** The plan assumes £57.8m of DSF. *Request from Torbay and South Devon and NHS England (South West):* the DSF award letters and conditions for 2026/27, quarter by quarter, and any correspondence about withholding or reassessing tranches.

[FOI] **The segment 4 designation and the league table.** *Request from Torbay and South Devon and NHS England:* the trust's full NHS Oversight Framework domain scores for Q3 and Q4 2025/26 showing the fall from 83rd to 102nd, NHSE's segmentation correspondence, and the improvement plan required as a result.

[FOI] **The independent review of the One Devon People Digital Programme.** *Request from Torbay and South Devon and NHS Devon ICB:* the terms of reference, the reviewer's identity and contract value, and the report when it lands in September; plus the original People Digital business case and the actual programme spend to date against it.

[FOI] **The mortuary reassessment.** *Request from Torbay and South Devon:* what was "addressed" to restore the 5-star rating, the preceding inspection or assessment report, the gap analysis considered by the Quality and People Committee on 28 July, and the board assurance statement submitted to NHSE.
