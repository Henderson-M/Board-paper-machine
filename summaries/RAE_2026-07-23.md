## Top lines

[LEAD] NHS England is withholding Bradford's £2.3m deficit support funding for Q1 and Q2 because of concerns about the trust's ability to deliver its plan.
[Evidence] "NHSE has notified the Trust that its planned DSF allocation of £2.3m for the year will be withheld for Quarters 1 & 2 due to concerns about the level of risk to delivery of the plan. This decision was based on Month 2 reporting and the board assurance statements submitted. The impact of this is a loss of £0.6m for Quarter 1." — p.200, [Month 3 Finance Report, Bo.7.26.12c]

[LEAD] Bradford is running an £8.2m deficit at month 3, £1.6m worse than plan, with breakeven now dependent on cost reductions "yet to be demonstrated".
[Evidence] "The Trust is reporting a Month 3 deficit of £8.2m, this is £1.6m worse than plan." … "The Trust is formally forecasting delivery of the breakeven plan, but this is contingent on achieving a significant and sustained reduction in the current cost run rate over the coming months, which has yet to be demonstrated." — p.200, [Month 3 Finance Report, Bo.7.26.12c]

[LEAD] The trust warns of a forecast nil cash balance and explicit liquidity risk to paying suppliers, and says it is not eligible for £9.1m of planned cash distress support.
[Evidence] "The June 2026 cash support request was reduced from £5.5m to £2.5m, £3.0m below plan, resulting in a forecast nil I&E cash balance at both September 2026 and March 2027. This increases liquidity risk, particularly in relation to the timely settlement of supplier payments." … "The Trust is not eligible for the £9.1m of planned cash distress support due to its forecast breakeven financial position and has instead been offered repayable working capital PDC." — p.201–203, [Month 3 Finance Report, Bo.7.26.12c]

[LEAD] The trust has commissioned an external "Drivers of the Deficit" review; its top financial risk (SR08) sits at 20 (extreme), with the BAF warning of possible NHSE sanctions.
[Evidence] "Drivers of the Deficit report commissioned – draft report received to indicate further opportunities to deliver efficiencies." — p.311, [Board Assurance Framework, Bo.7.26.16]. Risk 2926 (SR08), current score 20 (5x4): "IF the Trust does not deliver a balanced financial position in year, THEN the Trust will not achieve its agreed financial plan with NHS England RESULTING IN additional sanctions being placed on the Trust by NHS England reducing its ability to invest in services and being unable to maintain liquidity to meet its financial obligations." — p.313, [BAF, Bo.7.26.16]

[LEAD] Bradford is one of 12 trusts with an individual report in Baroness Amos' national maternity and neonatal investigation; the trust says its own report is "difficult to read in parts".
[Evidence] "Bradford Teaching Hospitals NHS Foundation Trust was one of 12 trusts asked to be part of Baroness Amos' national maternity and neonatal investigation… We accept the recommendations in full. Bradford Teaching Hospitals NHS Foundation Trust has not had any specific actions or recommendations in our report." — p.27, [CEO Report, Bo.7.26.6]. "The Bradford Report (Appendix 6 and 7) is difficult to read in parts, particularly where service users have described poor experiences of care." — p.90, [Maternity & Neonatal Board Assurance, Bo.7.26.9]

[WORTH WATCHING] Staff survey shows Bradford staff report discrimination on grounds of religion/belief and race well above the sector average — though the trust flags these questions as "non-comparable".
[Evidence] Grounds of discrimination, % answering yes vs comparator average: Religion or belief 21.49% vs 6.87% (−14.62% worse than average); Race 60.29% vs 52.0% (−8.29%); Sex 20.40% vs 15.75% (−4.65%). "466 colleagues stated that they had experienced discrimination at work." Trust caveat: "Questions relating to discrimination are also classed as 'non comparable' due to the different use of descriptors." — p.99–100, [NHS Staff Survey 2025 Benchmarked Results & Action Plan, Bo.7.26.10b]

[WORTH WATCHING] Maternity received a second MOSS Level 1 trigger in June on two term neonatal deaths, one an unexpected death now referred to the coroner and MNSI.
[Evidence] "The maternity service received a 2nd level 1, MOSS signal trigger on 21 June, triggered by 2 term neonatal deaths in addition to the rolling number of term stillbirths reviewed in the April critical safety check." … "The 2nd baby was an unexpected death of a baby born in good condition at 3 hours of age. This case has been referred to the coroner and to MNSI for investigation." Intrapartum services remain RAG-rated 'Green/Amber': "There is at least one known safety issue in our intrapartum service." — p.90, [Maternity & Neonatal Board Assurance, Bo.7.26.9]

[WORTH WATCHING] A high-level obstetric consultant workforce risk (score 15) persists, with a business case for 3.0 extra consultant WTE and an out-of-hours anaesthetic staffing gap flagged as a patient-safety risk.
[Evidence] "A high-level risk (Risk Register Ref. 3816) remains on the Women's Services risk register with a current risk score of 15. A business case… requesting an additional 3.0 WTE consultant." … relies on "consultants working [beyond established hours]… not sustainable and increases both workforce and patient safety risks." Board asked to support "the obstetric anaesthetic out-of-hours staffing business case… recognising the operational and patient safety risks associated with the current staffing model, including delays." — p.126–127, [Bi-annual Perinatal Workforce Report, Bo.7.26.11b]

[WORTH WATCHING] Bradford District & Craven is moving to a formal place-based provider partnership, with a Strategic Partnering Agreement put to the board for approval and Integrated Health Organisation contracts with West Yorkshire ICB targeted from April 2027.
[Evidence] "…establishing shadow Partnership Governance arrangements in 2026/27 which acts as a transitional year to support emerging Provider Partnerships as they prepare to sign an NHS / Integrated Health Organisation contract with NHS West Yorkshire ICB for the provision of in-scope services." Bradford District Care FT identified as host/lead provider (CEO report). — p.270, [Strategic Partnering Agreement, Bo.7.26.13a]; p.25, [CEO Report, Bo.7.26.6]
