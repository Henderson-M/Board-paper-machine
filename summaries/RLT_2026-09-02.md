# South Warwickshire University FT and George Eliot Hospital NHS Trust — Joint Board — 2 September 2026

- **Source:** https://www.geh.nhs.uk/download_file/2410/280 ("Public Joint Board Combined Meeting Pack 02.09.2026")
- **Pack:** 1 combined file
- **Analysed:** 2026-09-01
- **Note:** SWFT (RJC) and GEH (RLT) meet as a single Joint Board under a Foundation Group arrangement. This pack covers both trusts and this summary serves both meeting entries.

## Top lines

[LEAD] A non-executive director has formally recorded his continued lack of confidence in the group's joint electronic patient record programme, which is now delayed with material financial consequences.
[Evidence] "**Non-Executive Director (UZ) expressed ongoing concern regarding the level of assurance provided by the programme; noting that this had been further heightened by the material financial risks associated with the programme delay. Whilst recognising the efforts being made to stabilise the programme, he formally recorded his continued lack of confidence** f[or the record]" — [Audit Committee minutes, minute 26/55]
[Evidence] "**The Committee agreed to continue escalating its ongoing concerns to the Joint Board regarding the EPR programme; particularly in regard to the associated material financial risk and the need for continued assurance.**" — [same, minute 26/55/04]
[Evidence] Another member wanted it resolved: "to avoid the Joint Board revisiting the same issues in six months' time." — [same]
[Evidence] "A new Programme Director has been appointed with **Oracle implementation experience** and is due to start in September 2026. The recommendations from the **NHS England (NHSE) Tiger Team programme health check** and internal audit are being implemented." — [Joint Electronic Patient Records Programme Status Report]
[Evidence] "**The financial impact of the delay to the JEPR programme will need to be considered as part of the forecast financial position**" — [Audit Committee annual report]

[LEAD] Deloitte's annual auditor's report flags weaknesses in value for money relating to financial sustainability.
[Evidence] "**The external auditor's Annual Auditors Report highlighted weaknesses in the value for money (VfM) reporting which related to the Trust's financial sustainability.**" — [Audit Committee Annual Report to the Board for the year ending 31 March 2026]
[Evidence] The opinion on the accounts themselves was clean: "External audit services have been provided by **Deloitte LLP. The Appointed Auditor expects to issue an unmodified (clean) opinion on the Trust's accounts** and to date, has no matters to report by exce[ption]" — [same]
[Evidence] "The **Annual Report and Accounts 2025/26 (including the Quality Report 2025/26) were signed off by Deloitte and submitted to NHS England by the agreed deadline and have been submitted for laying before Parliament.**" — [same]

[LEAD] Both trusts are adrift on breakeven plans they have just committed to for three years.
[Evidence] "[Both SWFT] and GEH submitted **compliant net breakeven financial plans for each of the three financial years 2026/27-2028/29. At Month 2, SWFT reported a YTD deficit of £4.7m, which is £1.5m adverse to plan, and GEH reported a YTD deficit of £3.2m, which is £1m adverse to plan.**" — [Joint finance report]
[Evidence] The finance committee has begun contemplating external support: "The level of cash was also highlighted as a concern and the Committee received assurance that cash is being actively managed, **including consideration of the potential requirement for external cash support.**" — [Finance Committee report]
[Evidence] "The cash balance at the end of May 2026 was £16.9m. The receipt of 2025/26 Deficit Support Funding and Sprint monies from the ICB in April 2026, **totalling £6.1m**, improved the Trust's cash po[sition]" — [same]

[LEAD] Staff engagement fell at both trusts, with more staff at each looking for another job.
[Evidence] SWFT: "**decreased from 63.86% to 62.48%, and both the proportion of staff looking for a new job (16.25% to 17.70%) and those who often think about leaving (21.31% to 22.42%) increased.**" — [People Experience and Inclusion report]
[Evidence] GEH: "[engagement fell to] 6.95 and **morale from 6.22 to 6.12, while the proportion of staff looking for a new job rose from 17.22% to 18.76% and those thinking about leaving rose from 21.83% to 23.76%.**" — [same]

[WORTH WATCHING] NHS England has been consulted about the strain the joint board arrangement is putting on non-executive directors, and specifically on the audit committee.
[Evidence] "[With shared] Non-Executive Directors (NEDs) and a Joint Board of SWFT and GEH, the **increasing pressure on the NEDs has been a concern. Discussions have taken place with NHS England (NHSE) around this concern and in particular the impact on Audit Committee.** NHSE advised that, whilst it is technically good governance practice for only voting NEDs to be Audit Committee members and therefore coun[ted for quorum...]" — [Audit Committee Annual Report]

[WORTH WATCHING] A member of the public has been jailed for 18 months after an assault on staff, and the board was told staff had begun to see violence as normal.
[Evidence] "[it was reported that violence and aggression was] being normalised; however, this was strongly challenged, and staff were actively encouraged to report all incidents. It was further shared that **enforcement action had been undertaken, with one individual having received an 18-month custodial sentence following prosecution.** This was highlighted as evidence of the organisation's commitment to taking such matters seriously." — [Joint Board minutes / People report]

[WORTH WATCHING] South Warwickshire is taking over the chair of the Warwickshire Care Collaborative, which becomes one of its own board sub-committees.
[Evidence] "**SWFT's role as Warwickshire Integrator (formerly Lead Provider)** – health care commissioning and contracting update; the **Chairpersonship of the Warwickshire Care Collaborative (WCC) moving to SWFT from July 2026 and the WCC becoming a SWFT Board Sub-Committee**" — [Minutes of the Joint Board held in Public on 1 July 2026, minute 26.180]
[Evidence] A standing "Integrator (Lead Provider) Programme Update" is on this agenda with an assurance rating of "Significant Assurance – with minor improvement opportunities". — [Integrator (Lead Provider) Programme Update]

[WORTH WATCHING] Cancer and RTT performance are both below plan, with symptomatic breast two-week waits at 59.4%.
[Evidence] "Cancer 28 day referral to diagnosis confirmation *NHSE NOF*: target ≥ 80%, **76.8%**. **Cancer 2WW Symptomatic Breast: target ≥ 93%, 59.4%**" — [Integrated performance scorecard, May 2026 data]
[Evidence] GEH RTT: "performance of **64.4%, which is below the plan of 66.9%**. The total number of patients on the waiting list is 19,369, with 0.5% waiting for more than 52 weeks (101 patients). **SWFT achieved a performance rate of 69%**, which is above the planned target; the total number of patients on the waiting list is now 31,289" — [same]
[Evidence] Sickness absence is running at "**5.0%**" against a target of "< 4%" — [same]

[WORTH WATCHING] Patient experience reporting gave only partial assurance, and community waiting lists over 52 weeks have grown.
[Evidence] "It was noted that the report provided **partial assurance**. The Chief Nursing Officer explained that the level of assurance was not related to patients having a poor experience, as feedback suggeste[d otherwise]" — [Quality Governance Committees in Common, 17 June 2026]
[Evidence] "**Patients on Community Waiting list > 52 weeks *NHSE NOF*** — target 0 — SWFT: 128, 101, 114, 127, 149, 152, 168, 164" — [integrated performance scorecard]

[WORTH WATCHING] Named service fragility: paediatric community nursing and the early pregnancy unit.
[Evidence] "rated at 15 - **Reduced capacity within the Children's Community Nursing Team due to high rate of maternity leave, career breaks, and staff leaving. Vacancy freeze has been lifted to support recruitment** into the nursing team due to the increasing shortfalls within some care packages, still awaiting agreement on new service spec." — [Risk register]
[Evidence] "**EPAGU staffing SWFT is risk rated at 12 - Ongoing concerns with EPAGU cover moving through into the summer months. EPAGU has had to close on a few occasions with patients being seen in Same Day Emergency Care (SDEC) beds** on Beaumont Ward if available." — [same]

## FOI leads

[FOI] **The joint EPR programme.** *Request from SWFT and GEH:* the Joint Electronic Patient Records Programme business case and its revisions, the quantified financial impact of the delay, the internal audit report on the programme, and the **NHS England "Tiger Team" programme health check report** and its recommendations. Also the supplier and contract value. *Why it matters:* a named NED has formally minuted a lack of confidence in a major digital programme carrying material financial risk.

[FOI] **The value for money weaknesses.** *Request from SWFT and GEH:* Deloitte's Annual Auditor's Report and ISA 260 for 2025/26 in full, including the VfM commentary on financial sustainability and any recommendations, plus the management response.

[FOI] **The NHS England advice on joint board governance.** *Request from NHS England (Midlands), SWFT and GEH:* the correspondence about NED capacity across the joint board and audit committee quoracy, and any wider NHSE guidance on foundation group NED arrangements.

[FOI] **The cash position and any external cash support.** *Request from SWFT and GEH:* the finance committee papers assessing "the potential requirement for external cash support", the 2026/27 cash forecasts for both trusts, and any PDC revenue support application.

[FOI] **The Warwickshire Integrator arrangement.** *Request from SWFT and NHS Coventry and Warwickshire ICB:* the Integrator (Lead Provider) contract and delegation agreement, its value, the Warwickshire Care Collaborative's terms of reference following the move to SWFT, and the governance case for making a system collaborative a trust board sub-committee.
