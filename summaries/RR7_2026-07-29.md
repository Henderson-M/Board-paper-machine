## Top lines

[LEAD] External auditors have flagged a "significant weakness" in the trust's value-for-money arrangements on financial sustainability — for the second year running (first raised in 2024-25).
[Evidence] "Value for Money Arrangements – The External Auditors identified a significant weakness in relation to financial sustainability criteria. This was first reported in 2024-25... There remains, however, a risk in relation to longer term financial sustainability for which there is not yet full assurance over the mitigations and actions." — p.67, [Group Audit Committee escalation report, 22 June 2026]

[LEAD] The trust's underlying deficit is now forecast to worsen to £40.979m — above its own medium-term plan target exit deficit of £34.910m — with cost-reduction delivery running far behind plan.
[Evidence] "The Trust medium term financial plan set a target underlying 2026-27 exit deficit of £34.910m... To date the Trust is forecasting delivery of £11.084m recurring cost reduction schemes and £0.846m new recurring pressure... forecasting an underlying deficit of £40.979m." — p.216, [Consolidated Finance Report, Item 5b]

[LEAD] Cost-reduction programme is forecast to under-deliver by £11.388m against a £27.667m annual target — a shortfall escalated to the Executive Management Group.
[Evidence] "The annual achievement is forecast at an under-achievement of £11.388m and this risk has been escalated to the Executive Management Group to inform possible mitigations." — p.216; YTD CRP delivery "£2.265m against a target of £6.408m resulting in a year to date under achievement of £4.142m" — p.215, [Consolidated Finance Report, Item 5b]

[LEAD] Dr Sean Fenwick has been confirmed as substantive chief executive after nearly a year as acting CEO (since August 2025); Beth Swanson confirmed substantive chief nurse; Sheena Fish to join as chief operating officer in autumn 2026.
[Evidence] "Following an external recruitment process I am very pleased to confirm the appointment of Dr Sean Fenwick as our substantive Chief Executive. Sean has served as Acting Chief Executive since August 2025... Beth Swanson has been appointed as Chief Nurse... we look forward to welcoming Sheena Fish to the role of Chief Operating Officer in the coming months." — p.43, [Chair's Report]

[LEAD] The trust fell from 65th to 96th of 134 trusts in the acute-sector NHS Oversight Framework league table for Q4 2025/26, remaining in Segment 3 of 4.
[Evidence] "Our ranking in the acute sector NHS Oversight Framework (NOF) league table for Quarter 4 2025/26 dropped from 65th to 96th place out of a total of 134 trusts. We remain in Segment 3 of 4. The key drivers behind the changed position relate to operational pressures against the plan we submitted to NHS England, including four hour performance in A&E and our cancer position." — p.53, [Chief Executive's Report]

[LEAD] The trust has launched a voluntary Mutually Agreed Resignation Scheme (MARS), closing on the day of the board meeting.
[Evidence] "As part of this work we have launched a Mutually Agreed Resignation Scheme (MARS) for colleagues to consider. The scheme is entirely voluntary and will close on 29 July 2026." — p.51, [Chief Executive's Report]

[WORTH WATCHING] Cancer/breast services remain a "material constitutional and reputational" risk, with the faster diagnosis standard at 47.1% against an 80% target; the trust links breast capacity pressure to demand restrictions at neighbouring County Durham and Darlington FT.
[Evidence] "Faster Diagnosis Standard improved slightly to 47.1%, but remains substantially below the required 80% standard... 62-day standard remains at 65.2%" — p.223; risk 4752 cites "an evidenced capacity and demand imbalance contributed to by the service demand restrictions in place at CDDFT" — p.226, [Strategic Objectives & Constitutional Standards Report, Item 5c]

[WORTH WATCHING] Multiple board-level departures: group COO Jo Halliwell and QE Facilities MD Gavin Evans left after the May meeting; NED / senior independent director Martin Hedley has resigned to relocate to the USA (remaining until September), prompting recruitment of a finance NED.
[Evidence] "In June Martin Hedley resigned from the Board of Directors in order to relocate back to the USA. He will remain on the Board until September." — p.42, [Chair's Report]; "this will be the last Board meeting for Jo Halliwell and Gavin Evans who will be leaving the Trust shortly" — p.10, [Minutes 27 May 2026]

[WORTH WATCHING] NHS England conducted a "Workforce Deep Dive" on 6 July 2026 and was due to return roughly 10 days later for further review.
[Evidence] "NHSE Workforce Deep Dive – The NHS England team attended to carry out a Workforce Deep Dive on 6 July 2025 [sic] and are scheduled to return in 10 days' time to further review." — p.62, [People & OD Committee escalation report, 8 July 2026]

[WORTH WATCHING] Culture concern: the Freedom to Speak Up Guardian records "inappropriate behaviour" as a recurrent theme and a lack of action to manage it, with a two-year pattern of bullying/harassment concerns in Dietetics; the People & OD Committee also reports no meaningful improvement in violence and aggression, with actions overdue.
[Evidence] "Inappropriate behaviour remains a recurrent theme highlighted by teams across the organisation, and lack of actions being taken to manage bad behaviours." — p.175; "Concerns within Dietetics have shown a consistent pattern over the last two years, particularly relating to bullying, harassment, and inappropriate behaviours." — p.179, [Freedom to Speak Up Report, Item 4a]; "There has been no meaningful improvement in the overall violence and aggression score and a number of actions remain overdue." — p.61, [People & OD Committee escalation report]

[WORTH WATCHING] Healthcare-associated infections above trajectory: the Quality Governance Committee raised an alert it is "not comfortable" with; C.difficile rates run at 30.2 per 100,000 occupied bed days against target.
[Evidence] "The Trust has exceeded trajectory in several infection indicators, including CDI and bloodstream infections. The Committee is not comfortable with the current position" — p.57, [Quality Governance Committee escalation report, 17 June 2026]; "C.Diff rates remain significantly above target at 30.2 per 100,000 occupied bed days" — p.225, [Strategic Objectives & Constitutional Standards Report]

[WORTH WATCHING] Anxiety, stress and depression account for around 37% of sickness absence, with an estimated annual cost of £3.9m.
[Evidence] "Anxiety, stress and depression continue to account for approximately 37% of sickness absence, with an estimated annual cost of £3.9m." — p.224, [Strategic Objectives & Constitutional Standards Report]
