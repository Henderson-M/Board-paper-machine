## Top lines

[LEAD] NHS England Midlands rated UHCW's medium-term plan "Compliant with Conditions", citing non-compliance on finance in 2027/28 and 2028/29 and on activity submissions, and gave the trust until 31 August 2026 to demonstrate a route to financial balance.
[Evidence] "Your submitted plan has been reviewed against the expectations set out in the national guidance and has been assessed as **Compliant with Conditions due to non-compliance** in your activity submissions and non-compliance for finance in years 2027/28 and 2028/29." / "We expect you to progress your medium term plans and demonstrate a route to financial balance in both of these years by no later than 31 August 2026." — p.33–37, [NHSE letter from Dale Bywater, Regional Director, 2 April 2026 / Appendix 2 Financial Conditions]

[LEAD] UHCW's 2026/27 plan is a breakeven plan only because it includes £11m of deficit support funding; the trust opened the year £4.9m in deficit at month 1, £1.2m worse than its own deficit plan, driven by resident doctor industrial action.
[Evidence] "The Trust submitted a breakeven plan on 26th March 2026, including £11m deficit support funding." / "The month 1 year-to-date position shows a (£4.9m) deficit compared to the deficit plan of (£3.7m) with an adverse variance of (£1.2m)." / variance "due to industrial action by resident doctors" — p.125, [CFO Report Month 01]; p.129, [Integrated Finance Report]

[LEAD] The trust must find £74.5m (6.3%) of efficiencies in 2026/27 but had delivered almost none of the recurrent target by month 1, and its own risk-scored forecast puts £71.2m of the £74.5m at risk.
[Evidence] "The Trust has set a target of £74.5m target for the year, comprising of £39.5m recurrent and £35.0m non-recurrent." / "Recurrent savings account for £0.07m compared to a plan of £1.5m." / "The calculated score at month 1 is £55.2m which represents a £16.1m risk to the £74.5m delivery." / Waste Reduction table: "Full Year Variance (£71,230k)... [LEAD] NHSE's planning conditions require UHCW to cut 92 WTE in 2026/27 and to seek prior NHSE approval for any consultancy spend above £50,000.
[Evidence] "Your 2026/27 financial plan shows proposed workforce reductions of 92 WTE." / "Any consultancy expenditure above £50,000 requires prior approval from the NHS England regional team based on the agreed regional process." — p.37–38, [Appendix 2 Financial Conditions]
