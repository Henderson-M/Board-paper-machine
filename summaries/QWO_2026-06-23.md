## Top lines

[LEAD] West Yorkshire system closed 2025/26 £28m in deficit against a planned break-even, and lost part of its Q4 deficit support funding for missing the target.
[Evidence] "At Month 12 (year ending 31 March 2026), the West Yorkshire system reported a £28m deficit against a planned break-even position, representing an adverse variance of £28m. This reflects challenges in delivering planned efficiencies and pay assumptions, alongside the impact of industrial action. The final outturn also reflects a partial loss of Quarter 4 deficit support funding due to non-delivery of the break-even position." — p.13, [Chief Executive's Report Item 20]

[LEAD] The 2026/27 "compliant" plan submitted to NHSE relies on £38.7m of assumed deficit support funding.
[Evidence] "A compliant financial plan for 2026/27 has been submitted to NHSE, based on assumed deficit support of £38.7m." — p.13, [Chief Executive's Report Item 20]. The ICB's own break-even plan submitted 12 Feb 2026 is flagged on the risk register as at risk because of "the significant level of risk contained within organisational plans (including ability to efficiency, risk that non-elective (NEL) growth is in excess of the 1% planned for...)" — p.7, [Corporate Risk Register Item 22, risk 2596].

[LEAD] The board will formally receive "Palantir out of West Yorkshire NHS" petitions and issue a statement in response.
[Evidence] Agenda item 18: "Receipt of the 'Palantir out of West Yorkshire NHS' petitions — To receive the petitions and provide a statement in response." — Agenda p.3, [Agenda Item 18]. [INFERENCE] The wording implies organised public campaigning against Palantir/Federated Data Platform involvement in WY; the strategic-commissioner paper separately commits to "accelerate Federated Data Platform (FDP) readiness" — p.[slide 9], [ICB as Strategic Commissioner Item 26].

[LEAD] Multiple senior ICB departures confirmed alongside an unfilled substantive CEO post and three of four Director of Nursing post-holders exiting.
[Evidence] Chief Nurse "Beverley Geary has been appointed to the substantive role of Chief Nurse at Leeds Teaching Hospitals NHS Trust (LTHT) and formally stepped down from her role at the ICB at the end of April 2026"; CDIO "Shaukat Ali Khan... will leave the organisation on 15 September 2026"; "The recruitment process for the next Chief Executive of the ICB is now underway and applications have now closed." — pp.18-19, [Chief Executive's Report Item 20]. Risk register: "Three out of four current Director of Nursing post-holders are exiting the organisation due to retirement, secondment ceasing and a new job between February and April 2026." — p.18, [Corporate Risk Register Item 22, risk 2593, scored 16].

[LEAD] Around 240 staff left under the first ICB voluntary-redundancy round, with a further 61 approved in a second round, as the ICB restructures to a "strategic commissioner" by September.
[Evidence] "Around 240 members of staff left the organisation under the first round of the ICB voluntary redundancy (VR). A second VR round received over 180 applications. 61 applications were approved." — p.18, [Chief Executive's Report Item 20].

[LEAD] Out-of-area mental health placements have risen above baseline, with 55 WY patients placed out of area (42 from Leeds and York Partnership) and a wider NHSE stocktake counting 115 across all pathways.
[Evidence] "the West Yorkshire position as of 20 May 2026 shows that 55 people are receiving inpatient care outside their local area... The majority of these placements are from LYPFT (42), reflecting an increasing trend in recent months... This is above the baseline position of 46 reported in December 2025." A "broader NHSE stocktake in April 2026 identified 115 people from West Yorkshire receiving care outside their local area across all pathways." — pp.16-17, [Chief Executive's Report Item 20].

[WORTH WATCHING] ICB raises proposed financial-delegation thresholds and is benchmarking its delegation limits via Audit Yorkshire; board to receive verbal findings.
[Evidence] "The changes also increase the approval thresholds for commitment of new/repurposed expenditure in recognition that the ICB has a total spend in excess of £7 billion." A "more comprehensive benchmarking exercise is currently being undertaken by Audit Yorkshire, and the findings are expected in time to be presented verbally alongside this paper." — pp.1-2, [Changes to Financial Scheme of Delegation Item 29].

[WORTH WATCHING] IPS (employment support) under-delivery linked to a 2024/25 decision to divert ring-fenced mental-health money to plug wider system financial pressures.
[Evidence] "the most significant collective factor affecting overall IPS delivery was the decision in 24/25 to utilise some of the MHLDA funding to support wider system financial pressures which reduced IPS investment." — p.[MH summary slide], [Performance Update Item 21].

[WORTH WATCHING] NHSE working "directly" with two named WY trusts on A&E performance; Airedale stuck below 70% four-hour performance and flagged separately for RAAC.
[Evidence] "NHSE are working directly with MYTT and Airedale NHS Foundation Trust." A&E April 2026: "Airedale Hospital at 63.8% and Mid Yorkshire at 73.0%." — pp.13-14, [Chief Executive's Report Item 20]. Risk 2036 (score 15): "risk of disruption of service provision at Airedale Hospital due to structural RAAC deficiencies... There is also a risk of a collapse." — p.21, [Corporate Risk Register Item 22].

[WORTH WATCHING] Five Place Provider Partnerships to take on contracts from April 2027, each Trust-hosted, with block contracts to be "deconstructed" — a material commissioning-model shift.
[Evidence] "Each of the five PPPs will be hosted by a Trust on behalf of local partners, with the ICB holding outcomes-focused contracts with each partnership. PPPs will initially operate in shadow form during 2026/27... become operational from April 2027." "Large elements of community and mental health services continue to be funded by block contracts that lack incentives... we deconstruct those block contracts." — slides 15-17, [ICB as Strategic Commissioner Item 26].
