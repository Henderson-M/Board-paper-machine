## Top lines

[LEAD] CQC issued a Section 29A warning notice to New Cross Hospital's emergency department in February 2026, then withdrew it after the trust's representations — the CQC concluding its own notice failed the legal test for enforcement.
[Evidence] "On 28th May 26 we received communication from the CQC stating they have upheld the Trust Representations regarding the 29A Warning notice, issued to the Emergency Department (ED) at New Cross Hospital in February 26, concluding that the notice was not sufficiently detailed to meet the legal threshold for enforcement." — p.146 & p.150, [IPR – RWT, Enclosure 10.3, Managing Director / CMO summaries]. Also p.64, [Integrated Committee Chairs Report, Enclosure 7]: "Following representations by the Trust, the anticipated Section 29A Warning Notice was withdrawn."

[LEAD] Cluster of never events at RWT — two wrong-site surgeries in Ophthalmology (April and May 2026) plus a wrong-route medication error in Gastroenterology.
[Evidence] "There were 2 Never Events reported in month, one in the Ophthalmology Directorate under the description of wrong site surgery which resulted in the patient receiving bilateral PRP (Pan-retinal Photocoagulation) laser treatment on 11th April 26, the error was identified at follow-up on 11th June 26. A previous wrong site surgery in Ophthalmology was also reported in April 26. The second Never Event occurred in the Gastroenterology Directorate under the description of wrong route medication on 26th May 26, when oral medication was mistakenly administered via a Hickman line" — p.150, [IPR – RWT, CMO summary, Enclosure 10.3]. Ophthalmology also flagged for "significant challenges... regarding managing demand and capacity" — p.146.

[LEAD] Group faces an £81.3m (6.4%) cost improvement requirement for 2026/27, of which £36m of schemes are already rated high-risk of non/reduced delivery, while national deficit support funding of ~£60m is set to reduce each year.
[Evidence] "Total savings requirement for the Group of £81.3m... £36m of CIP schemes have been assessed as high risk of non/reduced delivery (£29m RWT, £6m WHT)" — p.61, [Integrated Committee Chairs Report, Enclosure 7]. BAF risk TCT3: "If we do not deliver the 2026/27 cost improvement programme of £81m (6.4%)... while national deficit support funding (£60m) reduces each year, then the Group will not live within its c.£1.5bn resource envelope... resulting in an unsustainable financial position... increased regulatory intervention and an adverse impact on the CQC well-led assessment." — p.75, [Group Board Assurance Framework, Enclosure 7.1]. Split: RWT £53.8m, WHT £27.5m — p.61.

[LEAD] A New Cross theatre block was closed down by the fire service in May 2026 under a fire enforcement notice.
[Evidence] "Progress has been made with regard to fire enforcement notice for Nucleus Theatres (Block 55) New Cross Hospital, which has been closed down by West Midlands Fire Service, following a review and inspection in May 26. All required works to ensure appropriate fire safety works were completed on time and to required standard." — p.147, [IPR – RWT MD summary, Enclosure 10.3].

[WORTH WATCHING] Group-wide clinical service consolidation and site reconfiguration under way, with "future site configuration of multiple services" flagged as a longer-term strategic decision.
[Evidence] "A structured assessment of service consolidation and reconfiguration opportunities has been completed... covering oncology, paediatrics, maternity, ophthalmology... Our agreed priority specialties for initial focus are ophthalmology, rheumatology, spinal surgery, community paediatrics, and Trauma & Orthopaedics; a medium-term pipeline (6–18 months) including development of the Bilston Community Diagnostic Centre, theatre capacity decisions at Cannock, and maternity service integration; and longer-term strategic decisions, principally the future site configuration of multiple services." — p.81, [Transforming Care Together Progress Report, Enclosure 8.1].

[WORTH WATCHING] Staff-survey confidence in speaking up at Walsall is falling and below national average, diverging from the trust's own upbeat FTSU feedback; senior-leader "Follow Up" training at WHT sits at 59%.
[Evidence] "Recent NHS Staff Survey results for Walsall Healthcare NHS Trust indicate a concerning downward trend in the proportion of staff who feel safe and confident to speak up... Performance across these measures remains below the national average" — p.42, [FTSU Annual Report, Enclosure 6.2]. WHT "Follow Up (Senior Leaders)" FTSU training compliance 59.17% vs 88% at RWT — p.40. FTSU concerns 2025/26: WHT 359, RWT 247 — p.35.

[WORTH WATCHING] 2025/26 annual accounts and annual reports for both trusts approved with clean audit opinions; Walsall's Value for Money conclusion carried an "amber" rating with improvement recommendations across all three criteria.
[Evidence] "The External Auditors reported they will give a 'clean opinion' on the 25/26 financial statements. The External Auditors assessment of Value for Money was an amber rating (No significant weakness, improvement recommendations made) across all three criteria: Financial Sustainability, Governance and Improving economy, efficiency and effectiveness." — p.225, [WHT Audit Committee Chair Report, Enclosure 11.1]. RWT: external auditors' "intention to give an unqualified opinion on the Trust's financial statements and value for money" — p.226. Auditor: Grant Thornton. (Full statutory accounts not in this pack — only the audit-committee exception reports; no surplus/deficit outturn figure stated.)

[WORTH WATCHING] Mortuary/HTA assurance: an open maternity-pathway action at Walsall follows incidents where deceased babies/foetal remains were not placed in mortuary facilities in a timely manner.
[Evidence] "One maternity specific targeted action relating to maternity pathways at WHT remains open following HTARIs involving deceased babies/fetal remains not being placed into mortuary facilities in a timely manner, and a breakdown in the pathway." — p.29, [Mortuary Board Assurance Statement, Enclosure 6.1]. Of 18 original HTA actions, 4 remain unclosed (2 major, 2 minor); HTA/trust met 29 June to discuss "deviations in the Maternity Service Pathway" — p.117.

[WORTH WATCHING] NHS Oversight Framework Q4 2025/26 (final under the current framework): both trusts in segment 3 — RWT ranked 90th of 134, WHT 58th.
[Evidence] RWT "Average Metric Score 2.47, Segment 3, League table position 90 (out of 134)"; WHT "2.21, Segment 3, 58 (out of 134)" — p.26, [Group CEO's Report, Enclosure 6].
