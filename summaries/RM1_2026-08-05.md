**Note on this board:** this is the Group Board (General Purpose Joint Committee) of Norfolk and Waveney University Hospitals Group, covering three separate foundation trusts — Norfolk and Norwich University Hospitals (NNUH, RM1), James Paget University Hospitals (JPUH, RGP) and The Queen Elizabeth Hospital King's Lynn (QEH, RCX). [LEAD] Two medical schools have suspended undergraduate medical student placements at The Queen Elizabeth Hospital King's Lynn for the whole 2026/27 academic year over concerns about the educational environment and governance.
[Evidence] "The University of Cambridge School of Clinical Medicine and Norwich Medical School at the University of East Anglia have notified the Trust that they have suspended undergraduate medical student placements at QEH for the 2026/27 academic year.
