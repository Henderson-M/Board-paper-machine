## Top lines

[LEAD] KPMG internal audit of Oxleas' core financial controls on pay returned only "partial assurance", with 14 of 25 sampled vacancies not put to the trust's vacancy panel — a rating internal audit called "highly unusual" for the trust.
[Evidence] "The review received an outcome of partial assurance with improvements required (amber/red). The focus of the review was vacancy controls, and of a sample of 25 vacancies, 14 were found not to have been presented to the vacancy panel… The committee heard that the amber/red rating was driven by a lack of management awareness, and this is not a typical outcome for Oxleas." — p.146, [Audit and Risk Assurance Committee report, Item 14]; also "Financial reporting and management: amber/red driven by the fact only audit was conducted in-year and this audit had an amber/red rating. Internal Audit made clear that this was highly unusual for financial governance audits" — p.147

[LEAD] Chief financial officer Azara Mukhtar is leaving Oxleas in August after six years, to become group CFO for the St George's, Epsom and St Helier Hospital Group; recruitment for a replacement is under way.
[Evidence] "We will be sad to say farewell to our Chief Finance Officer Azara Mukhtar when she leaves Oxleas after six years in August. Azara has been appointed as Group Chief Finance Officer for the St Georges, Epsom and St Helier Hospital Group. The recruitment process to appoint a new Chief Finance Officer at Oxleas is underway." — p.52, [Chief Executive Report, Item 7]
