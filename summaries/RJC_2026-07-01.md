## Top lines

[LEAD] Both trusts opened 2026/27 in deficit and adverse to plan at month 2, against full-year net breakeven plans, driven by temporary staffing, industrial action and slow CIP delivery.
[Evidence] "At Month 2, SWFT reported a YTD deficit of £4.7m, which is £1.5m adverse to plan, and GEH reported a YTD deficit of £3.2m, which is £1m adverse to plan. For both trusts, the adverse variance is primarily driven by expenditure on temporary staffing required to cover high levels of staff sickness, industrial action costs and CPIP slippage." — p.27, [IPR Item 9.1, Finance Executive Summary]

[LEAD] SWFT's perinatal mortality rate spiked above the national average in Q4 with six reportable losses, triggering a national MOSS safety signal and a culture review now in progress within perinatal services.
[Evidence] "In Q4, following the increase in cases over the last two months the rolling mortality rate has spiked above the national average rate at SWFT." / Stillbirth Rate "↑4.1 per 1,000 births" vs national 3.2 (MBRRACE); "Overall Perinatal Mortality Rate ↑4.5 per 1,000 births" — p.5, [Perinatal Quality and Safety Report Item 8.1]. "In February 2026 SWFT received a Level 1 signal from the MOSS… Due to the cluster of cases around the same time frame (total 6 mortality cases) all these cases have been reviewed as part of this alert." / "A culture review is also in progress within Perinatal Services." — pp.7–8, [Item 8.1]

[LEAD] SWFT recorded four Never Events year-to-date in the latest KPI reporting, against a standard of zero.
[Evidence] KPI table, "Never Events… Standard 0… SWFT… Year to Date vs Standard: 4" (GEH: 0) — p.37, [IPR Item 9.1, Trust KPIs]

[WORTH WATCHING] An open MNSI maternal death investigation at SWFT remains in progress, with the bereaved family's concerns about counselling, decision-making and "not being listened to" formally recorded.
[Evidence] "MI-041445 18/04/25 Maternal Death… Final draft received. Action plan in progress" — p.6, [Item 8.1]. SWFT MNSI update (separate March 2026 case): "concerns regarding counselling and decision making, not being provided with enough information and concerns over not being listened to and dismissed by medical staff." — p.7, [Item 8.1]

[WORTH WATCHING] E. coli bloodstream infection has been added to SWFT's corporate risk register as a "significant risk" entering 2026/27, with national thresholds not yet set.
[Evidence] "The Trust continues to face a challenging position in relation to E. coli bloodstream infections as it enters 2026–27, with this likely to remain a significant risk. A Trust-wide risk has therefore been added to the Corporate Risk Register (Risk ID 572)." — p.8, [IPR Item 9.1]

[WORTH WATCHING] Both trusts' cancer 62-day performance remains below the national standard, with SWFT at an unvalidated 68% and GEH falling to an unvalidated 60.7% in May, and histopathology and tertiary-referral delays named as the key risks.
[Evidence] "GEH saw a performance of 73.2% in April against the 62-day standard with an unvalidated position of 60.7% for May 2026… SWFT saw a performance of 63.6% in April against the 62-day standard with an unvalidated position of 68% in May 2026." / Risks: "Histopathology reporting delays / Delayed tertiary referrals beyond day 38" — p.20, [IPR Item 9.1]
