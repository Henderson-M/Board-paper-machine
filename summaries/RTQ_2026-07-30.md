## Top lines

[LEAD] Named mental health hospital (Wotton Lawn) is subject to a Section 42 large-scale safeguarding enquiry with CQC involvement, staff suspensions and HR investigations, and the board has put it under enhanced oversight.
[Evidence] "In Q4 of 2025/26 concerns related to patient safety at Wotton Lawn Hospital were raised with Gloucestershire County Council Head of Safeguarding and resulted in a Large-Scale Safeguarding Enquiry being established under Section 42 of the Care Act 2014 to assure that patients are appropriately safeguarded against abuse and neglect. A range of partners, including Care Quality Commission are part of the enquiry" — p.216, [Quality Committee GHC Quality Report 2025/26, item 22/0726]

[LEAD] The enquiry has triggered material workforce instability at the site — staff suspensions, redeployments, loss of substantive matron cover and rising off-framework agency use — with the trust conceding the unit's reputation as a place to work has been hit.
[Evidence] "Significant Workforce Pressures continues at Wotton Lawn and there is ongoing workforce instability associated with: A large-scale Section 42 safeguarding enquiry and associated HR investigations, staff redeployments and restrictions, elevated sickness absence and difficulties onboarding newly recruited staff. There is reduced attractiveness of the service as a recruitment destination during a period of heightened scrutiny." — p.98, [Quality Dashboard Report M3, item 11/0726]

[LEAD] The trust is facing a UNISON strike ballot over the TUPE transfer of reablement workers to private providers, which the CEO warns could cause system-wide disruption across August.
[Evidence] "This has caused disquiet amongst reablement workers and there is a ballot for strike action through UNISON... any industrial action before then will cause system-wide operational disruption that GHC will have a significant role in managing over August." — p.38, [Report from the CEO and Executive Team, item 08/0726]

[LEAD] The trust is triggering six of NHSE's 28 financial early-warning "red flag" metrics and sits at Intervention Level 2 ("enhanced monitoring and support").
[Evidence] "NHSE have developed 28 Metrics to identify emerging financial risks. The 6 in exception at month 2" — including "Forecast + Total Risk ... -4.47%", "High Risk Efficiency ... 37.57%" and "Cash metric ... -13.25%"; intervention category "Enhanced monitoring and support" — p.70, [Finance M3 Report, item 10.1/0726]

[WORTH WATCHING] More than a quarter of the trust's in-year savings target was still unidentified three months in, with a stated risk of a deficit next year.
[Evidence] "Cost improvement programme has delivered £3.881m of recurring savings up to month 3. Target for the year is £14.459m. (£5.144m, 28.68% was unidentified at month 3)"; risk register: "There is a risk that non delivering of the savings target in 26/27 will lead to financial pressure or a deficit in the following year" — p.60 and p.69, [Finance M3 Report]

[WORTH WATCHING] 2025/26 statutory accounts have been finalised at a £2.013m surplus with an unqualified audit opinion — but the annual report and accounts document itself is not tabled in this pack.
[Evidence] "Final accounts were submitted 25th June 2026, and the year-end performance position for GHC was a surplus of £2.013m" (p.57, Finance M3); "The Committee was pleased to note the external audit report which indicated that an unqualified audit opinion was to be given to the accounts, and that the auditors had not identified any significant weaknesses in systems of accounting and financial control" — p.172, [Audit & Assurance Committee Annual Report 2025-26, item 16/0726]

[WORTH WATCHING] The trust says the 1.19% tariff uplift may not cover the mandated Band 5 nursing job-evaluation review (c.514 nurses affected), flagging an unfunded cost pressure valued at £1.184m; delivery plan due to NHSE by 31 July.
[Evidence] "There is a risk that the uplift to the 26/27 contract tariff (1.19%) is insufficient to cover the band 5 job evaluation review as it is unclear how much has been allocated and guidance is unclear" (risk value £1,184k) — p.69, [Finance M3 Report]; "NHS England has requested that all NHS organisations submit a delivery plan by 31 July 2026... the review will include roles currently occupied by circa 514 Band 5 Nursing" — p.39, [CEO Report]

[WORTH WATCHING] Berkeley House (LD/autism ward) has been under a CQC Section 31 notice since October 2023; reinspected in July 2025 but the trust is still awaiting the final report a year on due to "an extended period of factual accuracy checking".
[Evidence] "In October 2023 Berkeley House... received an inadequate rating and the Trust was also issued with a Section 31 notice. The service was reinspected in July 2025, and we await the final report due to an extended period of factual accuracy checking with CQC." — p.216, [Quality Committee GHC Quality Report 2025/26]

[WORTH WATCHING] The trust has restructured into three service groups from 1 July, with the next operational leadership layer now in formal consultation.
[Evidence] "Our new service group structure commenced on 1st July. There are three groups... The next operational leadership layer of the organisation is now in formal consultation" — p.38, [CEO Report]
