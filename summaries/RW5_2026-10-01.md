Context for the correspondent: HSJ has already reported (a) Chris Oliver's move to Greater Manchester Mental Health FT ("New CEO for trust recovering from abuse scandal", 18 May 2026, story 7041735) and (b) the trust's guilty plea in the Lancashire Fire and Rescue prosecution over the Pendleview death ("Exclusive: Trust pleads guilty after patient fire death", 19 May 2026, story 7041744). The new material in this pack is what has happened since: the interim arrangements and recruitment timetable, the sentencing date and the trust asking the coroner to hold off, plus a deteriorating in-year finance position and a hard sickness-absence disclosure.
