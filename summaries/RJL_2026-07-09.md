# Humber Health Partnership — Trust Boards-in-Common (NLaG RJL + Hull University Teaching Hospitals RWA)

**Meeting: Boards-in-Common (Public), Thursday 9 July 2026, Diana Princess of Wales Hospital.** One shared pack covers both trusts. Group CEO Lyn Simpson; Group Vice Chair (chairing) Murray Macdonald; Group CFO Emma Sayner.

*Pack confirmed as the 9 July 2026 Boards-in-Common pack (agenda p.1, "meeting held on Thursday, 9 July 2026").*

## 1. Top lines

**LEAD — Group forecasting a deficit of up to £141.9m with a £41.9m "unidentified" gap; plan remains non-compliant.**
"A straight-line forecast based on the YTD deficit position would leave the group with a £128.5m deficit by year end. When adjusted for seasonality... this deficit increases to £141.9m." Mitigating actions "leaving an unidentified gap of (£41.9m) across the Group." — p. [Finance Report Month 2 / Public Purse appendix, ~pack p.17041-17055, 17228-17238]. Plan "remains non-compliant" — [Public Purse, BIC(26)165].

**LEAD — Both trusts are being restructured under NHSE's Intensive Recovery Programme with Carnall Farrar; consultation on Care Groups about to start.**
"That review is being supported by NHS England (NHSE) through the Intensive Recovery Programme (IRP), with independent input from Carnall-Farrar... The first phase of the Carnall-Farrar has concluded... The next phase has commenced and will continue over the summer." HHP "is about to commence a consultation on changes to how Care Groups are organised." — p. [Group Chief Executive's Briefing, BIC(26)162, pack p.2020-2048].

**LEAD — CIP badly behind: £6.1m delivered against a £15.3m YTD target (£9.2m adverse); full-year CIP forecast £44.7m adverse against a £119.1m efficiency target.**
"The Group has delivered £6.1m CIP year-to-date against a target of £15.3m, (£9.2m) adverse. The Group is forecasting CIP delivery of £74.4m (£44.7m) adverse to plan." "challenging £119.1m efficiency target (6.8% including FYEs), which at the point of reporting had a risk adjusted forecast of only £56.7m." — p. [Finance Report M2, pack p.17298-17299, 17232-17234].

**LEAD — Cash is the emerging concern; group on daily-monitored external cash support, receiving less than requested from NHSE.**
"The particular concern that is clearly emerging is the position in relation to the availability of cash which is monitored daily as we work through this escalation." Cash balance £56.7m at M2; "£6.35m of additional external cash support (Revenue Support PDC) was received in May... £5.40m has been approved for June... £2.58m less than requested. July requests totalling £9.79m... waiting for NHSE approval." — p. [Public Purse, BIC(26)165, pack p.3370-3372; Finance M2, p.17123-17125, 17519-17521].

**LEAD — Underlying deficit put at ~£114.9m against a planned £63.1m deficit; net risk to plan delivery of £133.5m.**
"The Group's underlying financial position is estimated at a deficit of (£114.9m)." "At Month 2 the Group reported a net risk to delivery of the (£63.1m) deficit plan of (£133.5m)... still material with risk to CIP delivery the key risk." — p. [Finance M2, pack p.17385, 17292-17293].

**WORTH WATCHING — CQC Well-Led review of the Partnership has taken place; outcome awaited.**
"we had the benefit of the Care Quality Commission (CQC) adding their review of the Partnership through a Well-Led review." Partnership "is under significant external review and whilst we await the outcome of these..." — p. [Group Vice Chair's Briefing, BIC(26)161, pack p.1946-1954]. HUTH still awaiting CQC re-inspection to move on from its 2022 report (pack p.3938-3939).

**WORTH WATCHING — Coterminous NED/board model formalised across both trusts; national recruitment under way for extra NEDs.**
Move to "coterminous appointments to both HUTH and NLaG" for Chair, Vice Chair, four NED committee chairs and two associate NEDs, "approved by our Council of Governors and NHSE in June." Julie Beilby, Jane Hawkard, David Sulch and Macdonald appointed to joint positions; Prof John Craig (University of Hull) nominated. — p. [Group Vice Chair's Briefing, pack p.1917-1943].

**WORTH WATCHING — 30%/£30m variable-pay reduction target agreed but delivery running at ~10%; headcount reduction plans "least developed."**
"Agreed a targeted 30% / £30m reduction in variable pay... further work required to deliver with c. 10% reduction YTD." "plans to reduce headcount are the least developed but remain a key area of focus and scrutiny." "this falls well short of the 30% targeted reduction. A straight-line projection would be (£20.1m) higher than target." — p. [Public Purse, pack p.3395-3418; Finance M2, p.17793].

**WORTH WATCHING — Consultation on the future of Goole and District Hospital; scrutiny panel seeking engagement.**
North Lincolnshire Council scrutiny chair notes "progress in developing proposals for the future of Goole and District Hospital... to better understand the impact of any proposals on patients... staff, performance... and on local health inequalities." — p. [Quality Account governor/stakeholder statements, pack p.14930-14934].

**WORTH WATCHING — One Regulation 28 (Prevention of Future Death) letter issued in May 2026 over ED sepsis triage at Hull Royal Infirmary.**
"There have been one Regulation 28 Letters issued in May 2026. in respect of the initial triage of a patient in the emergency department at HRI, who presented with an infection and developed sepsis. The concern relates to the need for a single validated triage assessment tool to be universally used." — p. [Monthly Patient Safety Update, BIC(26)164, pack p.2842-2851].
