# Lincolnshire Community Health Services (RY5) — Group Board in Public, 7 July 2026

- **Source:** https://lincolnshirecommunityhealthservices.nhs.uk/about-us/our-trust-board/trust-board-papers
- **Pack:** Single combined agenda bundle (~52MB) — "Bundle LCHG Board Meeting in Public Session 7 July 2026"
- **Analysed:** 2026-07-06
- **Note:** This is a **joint "Group" board** — Lincolnshire Community Health Services NHS Trust (LCHS, RY5) now operates as part of the Lincolnshire Community and Hospitals NHS Group (LCHG) alongside United Lincolnshire Teaching Hospitals NHS Trust (ULTH). CEO is Karen Dunderdale (Group Chief Executive); new Group Chair Rebecca Brown (first meeting May 2026, replacing Elaine Baylis). Papers cover both organisations; most finance/risk data is reported at Group level or split LCHS/ULTH.

## Top lines

[LEAD] LCHS (RY5) was downgraded a segment in NHSE's National Oversight Framework Q4 ratings, dropping from segment 2 to segment 3; ULTH remains in segment 4, the worst tier.
[Evidence] "For this quarter United Lincolnshire Teaching Hospitals NHS Trust (ULTH) remains in segment 4 and improves its place by 4 points (104th place out of 134 peer Trusts). Lincolnshire Community Health Services NHS Trust (LCHS) has been placed in segment 3 from segmentation 2 last quarter (41st place out of 61 peer Trusts)." — p.5, [Item 6 Group Chief Executive Report]. [Note: the earlier Finance & Performance Committee upward report of 28 May, discussing the Q3 position, recorded that "LCHS had moved from segment 4 to segment 2"; the CEO report gives the newer Q4 published position — p.4, [Item 8.4 F&P Committee Upward Report, 28 May 2026].]

[LEAD] The Group is running an £8.1m deficit at Month 2, in line with plan but reliant on an unspecified run-rate improvement, with roughly £9m of the CIP still unidentified.
[Evidence] "At Month 2 the year-to-date position for the Group is an £8.1m deficit, which is in line with plan. The Group anticipates an improvement in run rate over the remainder of the year as activity volumes increase and the efficiency programme delivers additional benefits." — p.5, [Item 8.3 Finance Briefing M2]. "The Committee noted the risks associated with the unidentified CIP at circa £9m however the maturity of plans was being reviewed" — p.6, [Item 8.4 F&P Committee Upward Report, 28 May 2026].

[LEAD] A breast-cancer patient-safety risk at ULTH was escalated to "Very High" (20) after the ICB issued a breach-of-contract notice for Breast Services.
[Evidence] "4855 – Delayed diagnosis of breast cancer risk presented at RRC&C in April confirmed increase in score to 5x4:20 Very High Risk (from 4x4:16 High Risk) due to ICB breach of contract notice issued for Breast Services" — p.6, [Item 12.1 Risk Reporting to the Group Board]. Underlying risk: "As a result of insufficient staffing levels and limited clinic capacity within the Breast Service, Pathology and Oncology, there is a risk that patients will not be reviewed in a timely manner… diagnosis of breast cancer will be delayed" — Appendix B, [Item 12.1, risk 4855].

[LEAD] The Lincolnshire system has formally exited NHS England's Urgent and Emergency Care Tier 2 oversight, with ULTH cited as the regional top performer on four-hour and ambulance handover metrics.
[Evidence] "During May, formal confirmation was received from NHS England that the Lincolnshire system has exited their Urgent and Emergency Care (UEC) Tier 2 oversight." — p.6, [Item 6 Group Chief Executive Report]. "ambulance handovers and 4-hour aits [sic] which had seen a 20% improvement with ULTH being the top performer in the region." — p.4, [Item 8.4 F&P Committee Upward Report, 28 May 2026].

[LEAD] ULTH faces a "material risk" of failing the entirety of CNST Maternity Incentive Scheme Year 8 if it misses a ≥90% multidisciplinary training-compliance checkpoint at the end of July — a "significant… financial risk."
[Evidence] "there is a material risk of returning a position of non-compliance at the Trust-selected checkpoint for training compliance at the end of July. Failure to achieve the required standards at this point will result in non-compliance for the entirety of CNST Year 8, with no opportunity for recovery within the reporting period. This represents a significant organisational and financial risk" — p.2, [Item 8.1 CNST MIS Year 8 Launch Report].

[WORTH WATCHING] The Group's 2025/26 staff survey scores fell — including Freedom to Speak Up and advocacy/reputation indicators — with the trust arguing it is "not an outlier"; LCHS's response rate was 49% versus ULTH's 68%.
[Evidence] "There had been a decline in staff survey data relating to FTSU indicators" — p.[May minutes], [Item 5 Draft Public Board Minutes May 2026]. "the 2025/26 Staff Survey results also indicated a lack of staff confidence in speaking up." — same. "when compared with regional and national peers, the organisation's results remained favourable, and both organisations were not outliers" — same. "ULTH achieving 68% and LCHS 49%" — same.

[WORTH WATCHING] LCHS's only "Very High" risk (rated 20) is that school-aged children are not receiving timely speech-and-language therapy, with the service "unable to meet the needs of CYP in Lincolnshire."
[Evidence] "849 - Risk of school aged children & young people not receiving speech and language support" rated "Very high risk (20-25)" — p.10-11, [Item 12.1 Risk Reporting to the Group Board]. "pressure on these pathways was extreme with the current risk rating for school aged service at 20. As a result, the service was unable to meet the needs of CYP in Lincolnshire." — p.8-9, [Item 8.1 Quality Committee Upward Report, 23 June 2026].

[WORTH WATCHING] ULTH received a National Emergency Laparotomy Audit mortality-outlier notification for the Lincoln site in April 2026, following an earlier outlier at Pilgrim.
[Evidence] "the Group had received a National Emergency Laparotomy Audit (NELA) mortality outlier notification for the Lincoln site in April 2026. This followed a previous outlier notification at Pilgrim which had now been addressed" — p.7-8, [Item 8.1 Quality Committee Upward Report, 19 May 2026].

[WORTH WATCHING] The board is being asked to approve a £5m over-commitment on its capital plan, with capital spend already £12m below plan at M2, principally on the Boston CDC project.
[Evidence] "the Group are requesting approval from Trust Board for the overcommitment on the capital plan of up to £5m" — p.5, [Item 8.3 Finance Briefing M2]. "The Group delivered capital expenditure of £6.3m (£12m lower than planned)." — p.4, [Item 8.3 Finance Briefing M2].

## FOI leads

[FOI] Request the ICB breach-of-contract notice for ULTH Breast Services. The risk register records that the ICB issued a breach-of-contract notice that drove the delayed-breast-cancer-diagnosis risk to "Very High" (p.6, [Item 12.1]) — request the notice, any contract-performance correspondence, and the recovery plan from the April 2026 "Risk Summit". From: NHS Lincolnshire ICB / Lincolnshire Community and Hospitals NHS Group (ULTH).

[FOI] Request the NHSE UEC Tier 2 exit correspondence. The CEO report says "formal confirmation was received from NHS England" that Lincolnshire exited UEC Tier 2 (p.6, [Item 6]) — request the exit letter and the supporting evidence/conditions. From: NHS England Midlands / LCHG.

[FOI] Request the 2025/26 National Staff Survey results and action plans for LCHS and ULTH, including the FTSU and advocacy/reputation sub-scores that declined (p. May minutes, [Item 5]) — the pack asserts the trust is "not an outlier" but does not publish the numbers. From: LCHG.

[FOI] Request the CNST MIS Year 8 training-compliance position at the end-July checkpoint and the November mandatory checkpoint, plus the maternity Bi-annual (BR+) staffing report showing the funded-vs-required midwifery gap (p.2, [Item 8.1 CNST MIS Year 8 Launch Report]). From: LCHG (ULTH maternity).

[FOI] Request the child sexual exploitation (CSE) screening-tool audit. The Quality Committee expressed "deep concern over reported lack of completed child sexual exploitation (CSE) tools during the whole calendar year" and commissioned an urgent look-back review, with a final report due September 2026 (p.6, [Item 8.1 Quality Committee Upward Report, 19 May 2026]) — request the review terms of reference and findings. From: LCHG.
