> **Context note on the "Group Board" (what the pack literally says).** The pack cover reads "Sandwell & West Birmingham NHS Trust / The Dudley Group NHS Foundation Trust / Board of Directors (Group) Meeting" (p.1). The July minutes describe the 22 July meeting as "the second meeting of the Group Board as a Committee in Common between Sandwell and West Birmingham Hospitals NHS Trust and The Dudley Group NHS Foundation Trust" (p.6, Enclosure 2a), so the first Group Board was the 20 May 2026 meeting. Diane Wake is "Group Chief Executive"; Sir David Nicholson chairs and declared he "was the shared Chair of Sandwell and West Birmingham NHS Hospitals Trust, Royal Wolverhampton NHS Trust and Walsall Healthcare Trust" (p.6); the Joint Provider Committee paper lists him as "Group Chair (DGFT, RWT, SWBT, & WHCT)" and Wake as "Group CEO (DGFT & SWBH)" (p.38). The Board Architecture approved in July was built "to support effective Group working while preserving the statutory responsibilities, accountability and sovereignty of each Trust Board" (p.18). The pack nowhere uses the word "merger" for SWB/DGFT. This arrangement was already recorded in the machine's July summaries (RXK_2026-07-22, RNA_2026-07-22), so it is not new; what is new this month is the shared-services hosting decision and the anchor/commissioning extension (top lines below).

> **Already public in HSJ (do not re-lead on these):** SWB's entry into NOF segment 4 in Q1 2026/27 (HSJ 11 Sep 2026, 8124286); SWB joining NHSE's MatNeoIST maternity support programme (HSJ 15 Jul 2026, 8123827); SWB's Provider Capability downgrade to Amber-Red (HSJ 7 Jul 2026, 8123763); the Baroness Amos investigation findings (June/July 2026).
