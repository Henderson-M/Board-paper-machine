[LEAD] CQC's initial well-led feedback says most improvements required after the 2023 well-led inspection have not been delivered, and identifies gaps in executive and non-executive leadership capability and insufficient board challenge
[Evidence] "the CQC identified significant areas requiring improvement, particularly in relation to organisational strategy and culture, leadership capability and stability, equality, diversity and inclusion, Freedom to Speak Up, governance and accountability, financial sustainability, partnership working and organisational learning.
