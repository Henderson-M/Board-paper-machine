[LEAD] Obstetric theatre capacity is "not sufficient": emergency caesareans up 42% since 2019/20, 36% of elective lists had at least one cancellation, 61 incidents linked to theatre capacity in the year; plus MIS Year 8 with five of six safety actions rated red.
[Evidence] "elective Caesarean sections increasing by 29% and emergency Caesarean sections by 42% since 2019/20... the review demonstrates that current capacity is not sufficient to consistently meet planned demand without disruption. Thirty-six per cent of elective lists experienced at least one cancellation, and a third theatre was required on at least nine occasions during the year. Sixty-one incidents were linked to theatre capacity pressures, including nine occasions where alternative theatre environments were required because an obstetric theatre was unavailable." p.144, [Perinatal Quality Oversight Model Report Q1, Item 11]. "Maternity Incentive Scheme Year 8 compliance remains a significant residual risk, with Safety Actions A, B, D, E and F rated red and Safety Action C rated amber." p.234, [QPC report, Item 16].
