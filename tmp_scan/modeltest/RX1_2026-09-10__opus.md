Two of the four repeat prior-year significant weaknesses that were not resolved (p.446). The Annual Governance Statement discloses **six control deficiencies** for 2025/26 against four for 2024/25 (p.440). [INFERENCE] The same p.440 table shows one year at significant assurance and one at moderate; given the verbatim 2025/26 opinion is moderate, the significant rating appears to be 2024/25, implying a downgrade, but the table layout is ambiguous in the PDF and should be checked against the original before use.
