## Audience and beat

HSJ is a specialist B2B publication. Readership is senior NHS managers (CEOs, finance directors, board members, ICB leaders, DHSC). Stories are paid, exclusive content — readers expect insight they cannot get from the open-access NHS England announcements or local press.

- Finance: deficits worse than plan; in-year deterioration; specific cost pressures (agency, drugs, energy); CIP slippage; unfunded pay awards.
- Performance: A&E 4-hour, 12-hour breaches; cancer 62-day; RTT 18-week; ambulance handovers; diagnostic waits; backlog activity vs plan.
- Workforce: vacancy rates rising; agency/bank spend up; sickness absence; strike impact; staff survey changes.
- Capital and estates: critical infrastructure risk; RAAC; backlog maintenance figures; capital programme slippage.
- Governance and leadership: CQC findings; exec changes; "extreme" risks added to the register; whistleblowing/freedom-to-speak-up trends.
- Quality and safety: never events; serious incidents; mortality indicators; HSMR/SHMI flags; maternity reviews.
- Strategy and policy: service reconfiguration; ICB-level decisions affecting providers; system financial reset action.

## What is NOT a lead

- Routine performance reports with no notable movement.
- Pre-announced national policy without local angle.
- Patient experience anecdotes (unless they expose a systemic issue).
- Anything already covered in HSJ in the last fortnight.

## Style guidance

- Lead with the most newsworthy fact, not a context sentence.
- Always quote a specific number / specific page / specific paper title from the pack.
- Pull verbatim "colour" phrases (defensive language, spin) — these are valuable alongside the factual line.
- Flag where the inference rests on Claude's interpretation vs. what the document explicitly says.
