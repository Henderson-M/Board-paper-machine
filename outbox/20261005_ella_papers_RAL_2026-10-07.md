## FOI leads

[FOI] **The North Middlesex "on hold no capacity" list.** *Request from Royal Free London:* the number of patients on the list at the point it was identified, by RTT and cancer pathway and by specialty; the longest wait; the outcome of the validation and harm review (numbers of patients assessed as having suffered low, moderate or severe harm); and any correspondence with NHS England London or NHS West and North London ICB about it. Also request the internal audit report on RTT activity data rated Partial Assurance (Amber Red) (p.375, p.378) and the scope and output of the "external review of waiting lists and recording accuracy" (p.83). *Why it matters:* the pack confirms a list of patients outside active management with harm reviews under way but gives no numbers.

[FOI] **The cash position and "tactical payment measures".** *Request from Royal Free London:* the 13-week and 15-month cashflow forecasts presented to the Cash Committee on 16 September 2026; the minimum cash balance and the cash-support trigger discussed; Better Payment Practice Code performance by month in 2026/27; and any correspondence with NHS England about revenue cash support in quarter four. *Why it matters:* the FISC report says cash support "could" be required in Q4 but the forecast itself is not in the pack.

[FOI] **The Hunter Healthcare contract.** *Request from Royal Free London:* the contract value, term, deliverables and payment schedule for Hunter Healthcare's urgent and emergency care programme support; the basis of the "ROI ~7:1" claim (p.80); the contract renewal decision and date; and the same for Kingsgate (p.87, p.95) and Deloitte (p.113). *Why it matters:* the trust admits reliance on an external firm to cover leadership gaps and a renewal decision is due.

[FOI] **The seven never events and the prevention of future deaths notice.** *Request from Royal Free London:* the date, site, specialty and category of each never event since 1 April 2026; the patient safety incident investigation report for each; the coroner's Regulation 28 report received in June 2026 (p.64) and the trust's response; and any correspondence from the CQC or NHS England about the increase. *Why it matters:* the pack cites "heightened awareness from the CQC and NHSE" without saying what was said.

[FOI] **The NHS England maternity Insight Visit and targeted support.** *Request from Royal Free London and NHS England London:* the report or letter from the 27 May 2026 Insight Visit, the terms of the Targeted Support Programme extension, the exit criteria for moving to the "sustainability phase" (p.312), and the identity, contract value and scope of the "external consultancy firm" leading maternity organisational development (p.313). *Why it matters:* the pack records the extension but not what NHSE found.

[FOI] **Information governance incidents.** *Request from Royal Free London:* the Audit Committee minutes of 11 September 2026 and the "recent high-profile information governance incidents" referred to (p.375), including any notifications made to the ICO in 2026/27 and the live incident relating to duplicate records after the One RiO merger (p.110). *Why it matters:* the committee asked for a future assurance report on IG controls "in light of wider concerns", which are not described.
