## Read this first: what the meeting was for, and when it became public

**The meeting has already happened.** It was held at 15:00 on Tuesday 23 June 2026 via Teams. This analysis is of papers only, not minutes, so it records what was put to the board, not what the board decided.

**Why an extraordinary board was called.** The agenda gives the reason explicitly and there is no ambiguity about it: the meeting was convened for statutory year-end closure and nothing else. Five substantive items, all sign-off:

1. Draft Annual Report and Accounts for 2025/26, including the Ernst & Young year-end report ("ISA260") and the Letter of Representations, "To approve the Trust's draft Annual Report and Accounts for 2025/6" (p.1, Item 26/27/105, owners E. Saunders / R. Lea / K. Byrne and E&Y).
2. Committee Annual Reports 2025/26 for all five assurance committees (p.1, Item 26/27/106).
3. Board self-certification of compliance with provider licence condition CoS7, Availability of Resources (p.2, Item 26/27/107).
4. Any other business (verbal).
5. Review of meeting (verbal).

There is no crisis item, no regulator letter, no leadership item and no single-issue trigger in the pack. On the face of the papers this is the routine annual accounts-approval board that many foundation trusts hold as a separate extraordinary meeting in late June, scheduled for 59 minutes in total.

**The papers were published late, and incomplete.** The agenda itself flags the accounts items as "Publication date: September 2026" (p.1, twice, in the Preparation column against both the Annual Report and Accounts and the ISA260 / appendix). The pack PDF carries internal creation metadata of 17 July 2026 and sits at a `/wp-content/uploads/2026/09/` path, so it went onto the trust website in September 2026, roughly two and a half months after the meeting.

**Crucially, the accounts themselves are not in the published pack.** The 79-page document contains the agenda, the five committee annual reports and the provider licence self-certification paper. It does not contain the draft Annual Report and Accounts, the EY ISA260 Audit Results Report, or the Letter of Representations, despite all three being the headline agenda items. There is no surplus or deficit figure anywhere in the pack.

**Two clerical points worth noting before quoting from it.** The agenda's section header over the accounts items reads "Year End Closure for 2024/25" while the items beneath it are all 2025/26 (p.1). And the date of next meeting is given as "Thursday, 2nd July 2025" (p.2), a year out.
