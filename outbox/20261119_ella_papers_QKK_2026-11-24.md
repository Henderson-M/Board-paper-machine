[LEAD] The same review says every maternity site in south London is loss-making, with a combined deficit of more than £140m, and recommends south east London reviews maternity and general paediatrics together.
[Evidence] "Maternity is spread across ten sites in South London. Deliveries have decreased by 14% since 2019 and fertility rates continue to decline. As volumes decline, more sites will fall below viable scale, and over half are projected to drop under the 2,500-delivery sustainability threshold by 2035. This will further exacerbate the current financial challenge as every maternity site in South London is loss-making, with a combined deficit of over £140m." — p.96, [Acute Services Review Summary Report, Item 4]. On paediatrics: "the PRUH, UHL and QEH each requiring less than 15 beds almost entirely for non-elective activity. At these volumes, the cost of 24/7 consultant cover may not be proportionate to activity." — p.96. Recommendation: "Maternity and general paediatrics should be jointly explored for transformation in South East London." — p.97. No sites are named for closure or change; the review states options appraisal was out of scope (p.85).
