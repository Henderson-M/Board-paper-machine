[LEAD] E Block at St Helier, which houses maternity, neonatal, gynaecology and assisted conception services, has no identified technical end-point for when its temporary water-contamination mitigations will stop being safe to rely on, and the board is weighing options including full or partial decant and dispersal of services across South West London.
[Evidence] "The key risk for the Board to manage is the impact on patient safety if the current temporary mitigations to water safety risks in E block are relied on for too prolonged a period and then fail. This would result in the emergency closure or decant of services in an unplanned way and is likely to be significantly more disruptive than a planned approach." — p.27, [Water Safety in St Helier E Block]
[Evidence] "Emerging findings suggest there is unlikely to be a definitive technical point at which current water safety mitigations can no longer be relied upon.
