## Top lines

[LEAD] Cash reserves forecast to fall to around nine days of operating cover by the end of the financial year.
[Evidence] "Current cash balances provide approximately 29 days of operating expenditure cover. Based on the latest cash flow forecast, this is projected to reduce to c.9 days of cover by March 2026/27. This forecast excludes the impact of planned mitigations and cash improvement initiatives currently under development." — p.220, [M4 Finance Report]

[LEAD] Trust's cost improvement plan is falling well short of the recurrence NHS England requires, with the shortfall described as needing "urgent improvement."
[Evidence] "Recurrent Delivery: Forecast is at 30% (£4.5m) against the 80% requirement... There is however a risk that the 80% target may not be achieved and urgent improvement is required." CIP target is £16.5m; forecast delivery £13.0m, leaving a £2.0m risk. — p.216, [M4 Finance Report]

[LEAD] Trust is at risk of missing NHS England's mandated bank-staffing reduction target, spending well above the London benchmark.
[Evidence] "NHSE, as part of their ambition to ultimately eliminate temporary staffing usage completely, requires Trusts to reduce Bank spend and have set the Trust a £21.3m limit for 2026/27... a significant reduction in spend needed." Actual bank spend in 2025/26 was £29.2m; cumulative 2026/27 spend of £8.8m is £431k above plan (10.0% of paybill). "The Trust is at risk of not achieving the target." — p.211 and p.207, [M4 Finance Report]

[LEAD] Board's own risk framework shows four of its five top strategic risks running above the Trust's declared risk appetite, with the adult acute care pathway risk deteriorating further.
[Evidence] "BAF06 (the risk of failing to meet increasing demand across adult acute care pathways whilst maintaining safe, timely and high-quality care) which deteriorated to a risk rating of 20 in July... Four of the five BAF risks are currently operating above the Trust's stated risk appetite." — p.63, [Board Assurance Framework]

[LEAD] Committee found only "limited assurance" over the accuracy of ward observation records after a CCTV audit and coronial proceedings exposed non-compliance, and admits an earlier chance to spot the problem was missed.
[Evidence] "The Committee received limited assurance regarding the reliability and accuracy of nursing observation practices and associated record keeping. Significant concerns had been identified through a Patient Safety Incident Investigation, coronial proceedings and subsequent CCTV audit work, including instances of inconsistent records and wider non-compliance with observation requirements." And: "learning from earlier local investigations had not been fully incorporated into the original learning response, representing a missed opportunity to identify the wider systemic concern sooner." — p.97, [Quality and Safety Assurance Committee Chair's Report]

[LEAD] Mental health patients bear the overwhelming brunt of long emergency admission waits across South West London, with waits lengthening month on month.
[Evidence] "Average waiting times increased to 66 hours in July (55 hours in June), with 76 patients waiting longer than 72 hours for admission. Mental health patients accounted for approximately 83% of all patients waiting more than 72 hours across South West London, demonstrating a significant and ongoing inequity." Mental Health Act assessment waits also rose, from 173 hours in June to 193 hours in July. — p.95, [Quality and Safety Assurance Committee Chair's Report]

[WORTH WATCHING] CQC carried out a focused inspection of adult acute wards and the PICU in June; initial feedback was broadly positive but flagged gaps in risk assessment, ligature-risk oversight and blanket restrictions — formal report still awaited.
[Evidence] "The CQC carried out a focused inspection of Ellis, Lilacs, Laurel and Ward 1 (PICU) from 16 to 18 June 2026... Areas requiring further improvement included: Consistency of risk assessment and risk management processes; Recording and oversight of ligature and environmental risks... Reducing the use of blanket restrictions... The Trust is awaiting the formal inspection report, expected in September 2026." — p.45, [Chief Executive's Report]

[WORTH WATCHING] Two non-executive directors are leaving in quick succession, with three NED posts in active recruitment.
[Evidence] "Jonathan Warren, who left the Trust at the end of August... Sola Afuape will also be leaving the Trust in October." Audit Chair and Digital Associate NED roles have candidates offered "going through the appropriate compliance and recruitment checks"; a second recruitment round for the Clinical NED role is planned for autumn. — p.34, [Chair's Report]; p.18, [Minutes of the previous meeting]
