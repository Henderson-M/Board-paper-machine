[LEAD] Trust's Nervecentre electronic patient record go-live has slipped to May 2027 after a problem was found in system testing, having been eight weeks away when the board last met.
[Evidence] "Since my last report, the EPR Strategic Board has agreed a revised implementation approach for our Nervecentre Electronic Patient Record programme, with the planned full go-live moving to May 2027. The decision followed the identification of an issue during system testing." — p.33, [Chief Executive Report, Enc 4]. The July board was told it "would go live in 50 working days" (p.9, [Minutes 14 July 2026]) and the July Finance, Investment and Performance Committee discussed "The proposed EPR (Electronic Patient Record) go-live, scheduled for two months' time... together with ongoing risk mitigations, including training and a dress rehearsal in August" (p.197, [FIPC Assurance Report, Enc 14a]). The EPR go-live was formally escalated to the board as an "Alert" by FIPC in July (p.198). Two other papers still assume the old date: the IPR says pressure ulcer tools issues "will be resolved with full Nervecentre deployment in September 2026" (p.166) and sepsis screening assurance rests on "the scheduled deployment of Nervecentre in September 2026" (p.167).
