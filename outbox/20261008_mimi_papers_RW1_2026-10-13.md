## What has changed since the 18 August pack

- The application to lift the 2023 enforcement undertakings (flagged August) has succeeded: NHSE South East issued a compliance certificate on 15 September.
- The CQC well-led appeal (flagged August as a LEAD) is not mentioned anywhere in this pack. The trust now describes the Requires Improvement rating as "important evidence of organisational capability" and the action plan is listed "to follow".
- The finance committee's August warning about a £1m month 6 to month 7 swing has become a formal "Route to Break-even" paper: the underlying deficit is now put at £39.2m and the forecast exit underlying deficit at £20.5m, £9.5m adverse to plan.
- Suzanne Rankin took over as chief executive on 3 September; this is her first CEO report.
- NHS Oversight Framework: August pack recorded segment 1 for Q4 2025/26; this pack records segment 2 for Q1 2026/27 under the revised framework.
