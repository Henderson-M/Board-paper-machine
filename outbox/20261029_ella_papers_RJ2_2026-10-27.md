## Top lines

[LEAD] LGT was put in NOF segment 4 by a 0.01 margin because of its own "avoidable" cancer-data submission error, and has called in KPMG to review its data submission processes.
[Evidence] "the Trust has an average metric score of 2.78 which results in a place at the top of segment 4 (A score of 2.77 would have placed the Trust in segment 3)"; NHSE London modelling shows the true position "would have an overall metric score of 2.69 (and would have been placed towards the lower end of segment 3)"; "management has commissioned KPMG, the Trust's internal auditors, to review our submission processes and governance arrangements for all metrics" — p.10, [National Oversight Framework Update, Enclosure B]

[LEAD] The trust has dropped its breakeven plan and is now pursuing an £8m deficit as its "best-case objective", with its own finance committee recording "significant concern" that the recovery plan is deliverable; the underlying deficit is c.£42m and cash has fallen £45.9m since March.
[Evidence] "the Committee considered the proposal to pursue an £8m deficit as the best-case objective, requiring £11.3m of formal expenditure recovery from a gross £14.1m mitigation pipeline"; the committee "recorded significant concern about deliverability and the potential effect of the required actions on quality and performance" — p.49, [FITC Committee Report, Enclosure D1]. Plan was breakeven: annual "REPORTED SURPLUS / DEFICIT POST D.A." budget = 0 — p.55, [M6 Finance Report]. "Excluding non-recurrent benefits, the underlying deficit is approximately £42m YTD"; "Cash balances reduced by £45.9m since March to £81.1m at the end of September"; efficiency delivery "£13.3m behind plan" (£16.1m delivered v £29.3m plan) — p.53 and p.58, [M6 Finance Report, Enclosure D2]

[LEAD] Carnall Farrar's SEL Acute Services Review, published in this pack, projects a £1bn acute system deficit by 2030/31 and recommends consolidating breast surgery (now on four sites, three sub-scale), elective surgical hubs, shrinking the acute dental footprint, and reviews of maternity, general paediatrics and non-tertiary cancer surgery.
[Evidence] "the acute system deficit projected to reach £1 billion by 2030/31 before efficiency delivery" — p.13, [Acute Services Review, Enclosure C]; "Breast surgery is delivered from four sites… Three of the four sites operate at less than half the national median provider volume" — p.26; "Deficit support funding is to be phased out from 2026/27" — p.20, [SEL Acute Services Review Summary Report]

[LEAD] The ASR says every maternity site in South London is loss-making with a combined deficit of over £140m, and over half are projected to fall below the 2,500-delivery sustainability threshold by 2035 — teeing up a South East London maternity reconfiguration review.
[Evidence] "every maternity site in South London is loss-making, with a combined deficit of over £140m"; "over half are projected to drop under the 2,500-delivery sustainability threshold by 2035"; "any changes in South East London should derive from planning on a South East London footprint" — p.29, [SEL Acute Services Review Summary Report, Enclosure C]

[LEAD] A new trust chair was introduced at the 27 October AGM — the pack never names them — following Mike Bell's departure after four years, with NED Nav Chana having acted up since the summer.
[Evidence] AGM agenda item 3: "Introducing our new Chair" — p.1, [Agenda]; "following the departure of Mike Bell from the Trust after four years as Chair, he was undertaking the role on an acting basis" — p.2, [Minutes of 28 July 2026 board]. [INFERENCE] The incoming chair's identity is not disclosed anywhere in the 222-page pack — worth a call to the trust press office.

[LEAD] The chief nurse has left the trust: Lisa Crosby's departure is treated as housekeeping (a committee thank-you), Jo Peck is acting chief nurse, and substantive recruitment will not even start until early 2027.
[Evidence] "The Committee noted that this was the Chief Nurse's final FITC meeting and thanked her for her contribution" — p.49, [FITC Committee Report, 22 Sept meeting]; Jo Peck listed as "Acting Chief Nurse" — p.74, [Quality and Safety Report]; "Recruitment to CNO role starting in early f 2027 [sic]" — p.208, [Workforce KPI Report, Chief Nurse Division]. [INFERENCE] No reason for the departure is given anywhere in the pack.

[LEAD] A national maternity early-warning (MOSS) level 1 alert was triggered at University Hospital Lewisham in July after an intrapartum stillbirth at term, and the trust's own critical safety check concluded AMBER — a known safety issue where the action plan "is not progressing as planned".
[Evidence] "In July 2026, there was a level 1 alert triggered at the UHL site. The index case for this alert was an intrapartum stillbirth at term" — p.159; conclusion: "AMBER - There is at least one known safety issue in our intrapartum service, with a plan of action already in place but it is not progressing as planned" — p.160, [Q2 Perinatal Quality Surveillance Report, Enclosure E5]. MNSI rejected the case review because family consent was not obtained — p.159.

[WORTH WATCHING] Maternity triage is non-compliant with national standards and midwifery/neonatal staffing fails Maternity Incentive Scheme year 8 requirements; a £2.2m business case for 29 extra midwives is still "awaiting Executive sign off" while the trust pursues an £8m-deficit recovery plan.
[Evidence] "The Maternity Triage service does not comply with national standards"; "The current midwifery and neonatal staffing framework, does not meet MIS 8 requirements" — p.154, [Q2 Perinatal Quality Surveillance Report]; "awaiting Executive sign off for an additional midwife in triage day and night and a total of 29 additional WTE across the service to meet our birth rate plus safer staffing report. The total cost is around £2.2 million" — p.113, [Quality and Safety Report, Quality Account Priorities]

[WORTH WATCHING] Black African women accounted for five of nine perinatal deaths in Q2 (56%) against c.16.5% of births — the trust itself calls it "a statistical overrepresentation" and has commissioned further analysis.
[Evidence] "5 of the 9 notified cases involved women from Black African ethnic backgrounds (56% of cases)… thus, there is a statistical overrepresentation in this quarter" — p.156, [Q2 Perinatal Quality Surveillance Report]. Caesarean rate has risen "from 39% in 2021/22 to 48% in 2025/26" — p.165, same paper.

[WORTH WATCHING] QEH fire safety and cladding are flagged to the board as areas of "limited assurance", with 51 open estates risks including eight scored at 20, and London Fire Brigade engagement ongoing.
[Evidence] "QEH fire safety and cladding remain significant areas of risk and limited assurance. Interim mitigations remain in place"; "The Committee noted 51 open estates risks, including eight scored at 20" — p.183, [ARCC Committee Report, Enclosure F1]. Public questions at the July board also probed PFI expiry health checks — p.7, [July minutes].

[WORTH WATCHING] Diagnostic waits have collapsed to 68.7% against a 91.7% plan — non-obstetric ultrasound at 61.2%, cystoscopy at 40.7% — and the trust has agreed to transfer 330 pathways to Circle under ICB-funded private capacity.
[Evidence] "The Trust's DM01 position deteriorated further in August, reducing from 72.4% in July to 68.7%, against an end-of-year plan of 91.7%"; "The Trust has agreed 330-pathway transfers to Circle as part of ICB funded private capacity" — p.117, [M7 Performance Report, Enclosure E4]. Bed occupancy "decreased slightly to 102% in August (vs 104% in July)" — p.119, same paper.

[WORTH WATCHING] An E. coli contamination at Darenth Valley Hospital (Dartford and Gravesham Trust) suspended its birthing pools and diverted women to neighbouring trusts including LGT — disclosed in passing in a safety champions walkabout note.
[Evidence] "the ongoing sector-wide response to the E. coli contamination at Darenth Valley Hospital, including the temporary suspension of birthing pools and diversion of women and birthing people to neighbouring trusts including LGT" — p.170, [Q2 Perinatal Quality Surveillance Report]
