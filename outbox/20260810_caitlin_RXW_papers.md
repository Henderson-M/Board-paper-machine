## Top lines

[LEAD] The CQC has removed the last of the 60 conditions originally imposed on Shrewsbury and Telford's registration, formally notifying the trust on 22 July 2026 — the final condition concerned 15-minute assessment of every patient arriving in the emergency department.
[Evidence] "On 22 July 2026, the CQC formally notified SaTH, under Section 31 of the Health and Social Care Act 2008, that it had removed the final condition as a service provider from the Trust's registration. The condition required the Trust to maintain an effective system ensuring that all patients presenting to the Emergency Department were assessed within 15 minutes of arrival… This was the last of the original 60 conditions, meaning no conditions now remain on the Trust's registration." — p.31, [Group Chief Executive's Report, Item 061/26]

[LEAD] SaTH is awaiting CQC reports on urgent and emergency care and medicine inspections at both its hospitals, which the group chief executive expects "over the coming weeks".
[Evidence] "We expect to receive the CQC reports on the Urgent and Emergency Care and Medicine inspections at both RSH and PRH over the coming weeks. I anticipate that the reports will recognise the progress made across these services… However, we recognise that there is still more to do to ensure consistently high standards across every service and for every patient." — p.31, [Group Chief Executive's Report, Item 061/26]

[LEAD] Shrewsbury and Telford is holding two fire safety enforcement notices from Shropshire Fire and Rescue Service, with the board told cultural change is needed on oxygen cylinder management and obstruction of "hospital street".
[Evidence] "PAC heard that SaTH's Fire Officer would be meeting Shropshire Fire and Rescue Service the following week to discuss progress with the work required by the two enforcement notices (365 and 366). The fire compartmentation survey will be completed by then, and the provision of fire detection is already underway. Ongoing issues are management of oxygen cylinders and prevention of building up of items in hospital street. PAC noted that in both cases cultural change was needed, so that systems are followed and staff are confident that systems will work." The committee "was concerned whether staff were adequately trained" on fire safety. — [Performance Assurance Committee report, Board Information Pack]

[LEAD] SaTH is £2.67m adverse to its deficit plan three months into the year, against a plan that only reaches breakeven with deficit support funding.
[Evidence] "The Trust submitted a finance plan to NHSE on 18th March which showed a deficit plan of £30.49m pre deficit support (DSF) which moves to breakeven with deficit support for the year. At the end of June (month three), the Trust has delivered a deficit position of £10.94m against a deficit plan of £8.27m pre deficit support funding, an unfavourable variance of £2.67m. This moves to a deficit of £3.31m against a planned deficit of £0.65m post deficit support." Drivers: "industrial action (£0.75m) and slippage against various efficiency schemes (£1.70m)". — p.87, [Integrated Finance and Performance Report]

[LEAD] Type 1 A&E performance at SaTH fell to 43.9% in June.
[Evidence] "Type 1 performance decreased to 43.9%, and Type 3 performance decreased to 87.9%." Ambulance handovers were better than plan, with "86.4% of ambulance handovers within 45 mins". — p.87, [Integrated Finance and Performance Report]

[WORTH WATCHING] Shropshire Community's NHS Oversight Framework score has deteriorated from 1 to 2 in the Q4 segmentation published on 11 June.
[Evidence] "NHSE published the Quarter 4 NOF segmentations on 11 June 2026 and SCHT was allocated an overall score of 2, which is a deterioration from the previous rating of 1 but still strong performance. SCHT performs well across 3 out of 5 domains, scoring above average or higher, and is ranked 29th out of 61 Trusts." — p.~10, [Minutes of previous meeting in public, 9 July 2026, Item 036/26]

[WORTH WATCHING] SaTH's capital programme for 2026/27 totals £163.8m, of which £138.26m is externally funded, but only £23.56m had been spent by the end of Q1.
[Evidence] "The Trust has set an operational capital programme of £25.54m (including IFRS 16 expenditure) and externally funded schemes of £138.26m in FY26/27, giving a total capital programme of £163.80m, spend at the end of Q1 totalled £23.56m. The Trust held a cash balance at end of June 2026 of £40.26m." — p.87, [Integrated Finance and Performance Report]

[WORTH WATCHING] The Welsh-resident waiting list at SaTH is materially worse than the English one, with 117 patients waiting over 52 weeks and 7 over 78 weeks, against zero on every English metric.
[Evidence] "The submitted Trust Position for June 2026: English is 0 x 104 weeks, 0 x 78 weeks, 0 x 65 weeks, 0 x 52 week (adult) and 0 x 40 weeks CYP. The unvalidated Trust Position for Welsh is 0 x 104 weeks, 7 x 78 weeks, 34 x 65 weeks 117 x 52 weeks." — p.87, [Integrated Finance and Performance Report]

[WORTH WATCHING] A board committee flagged NHS England's review of the Federated Data Platform and Palantir, citing HSJ's own reporting.
[Evidence] "Ms Milanec drew colleagues' attention to a recent article in the Health Service Journal (HSJ) on NHSE undertaking a review of both the FDP and Palantir, the platform provider." Phase 2 of the trust's FDP transition is complete and Phase 3 under way. — p.12, [Minutes of previous meeting in public, 9 July 2026]
