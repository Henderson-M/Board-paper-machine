**Context for the correspondent.** Central East ICB was established on 1 April 2026 from the merger of Bedfordshire, Luton and Milton Keynes ICB, Cambridgeshire and Peterborough ICB and Hertfordshire and West Essex ICB (West Essex having gone elsewhere — the metrics paper repeatedly notes "Current data includes West Essex" as a caveat).
